# Seven-day controlled campaign

**Document ID:** RB-10  
**Package edition:** 1.0 / 2026-10-06  
**Document state:** WRITTEN_IMPLEMENTATION_SPECIFICATION  
**Implementation and runtime evidence:** NOT_STARTED / NOT_RUN in this delivered package.

## Prerequisites

Complete installed gates, select finite low-risk resources and define real calendar start/end plus daily budgets. Make ownership and intervention contacts explicit.

## Procedure

Perform normal scoped tasks each day, including a fresh observation, a reviewed action or deliberate denial, and next-session continuation. Schedule controlled restart, changed-file, model-failure, revoked-grant and uncertain-outcome cases across the interval. Preserve the actual wall-clock record, resource growth and interruptions. Pause safely if the responsible session cannot continue.

## Required observed result

Only actual elapsed duration counts. Retain a checkpoint and open gate when incomplete. No claim of unattended background work is made without real active supervision and authorization.

## Fault and escalation route

When required evidence, hardware, permission or runtime is unavailable, preserve the current state and enter the affected operation's explicit blocked/recovery state. Record the exact error, scope and minimum intervention. Do not retry non-idempotent effects blindly, reset profiles, rewrite old results or weaken the operating profile. Continue unrelated safe work where permitted.
