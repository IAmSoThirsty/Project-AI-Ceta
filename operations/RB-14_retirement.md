## Prerequisites

Collect the owner-approved retention/deletion choice, model/profile boundaries, live tasks and any unresolved effects. Identify all installed entry points and owned services.

## Procedure

Stop owned work, reconcile where possible and export explicitly selected data under its policy. Revoke grants/keys that should retire; remove only owned installation assets. Preserve retained profile/history and unknown files. Apply payload/index deletion with documented backup limitations, and verify no legacy service resumes authority.

## Required observed result

Retirement produces a clear inventory of preserved, deleted, unresolved and externally held material. It neither kills unrelated processes nor pretends uncontrolled copies vanished.

## Fault and escalation route

When required evidence, hardware, permission or runtime is unavailable, preserve the current state and enter the affected operation's explicit blocked/recovery state. Record the exact error, scope and minimum intervention. Do not retry non-idempotent effects blindly, reset profiles, rewrite old results or weaken the operating profile. Continue unrelated safe work where permitted.
