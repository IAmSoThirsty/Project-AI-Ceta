## Scope and controlling responsibilities

## Rules

Observation, attributed statement, inference, hypothesis, verified result and unknown MUST remain distinguishable in records and presentation. Evidence integrity proves what was recorded under stated assumptions; it does not automatically prove the proposition expressed. Confidence requires a declared basis and is not manufactured from confident wording.

Every consequential claim MUST identify its supporting records, scope, valid time, source revisions and invalidation dependencies. Contradictory supported positions remain separately visible until a justified transition resolves them. Repetition, popularity, role authority and user preference do not upgrade epistemic status.

Receipts and independent readback receive different identities. The verifier reports method and independence honestly. Historical results remain historical after a model, policy, binary, fixture or dependency change. Unavailable evidence yields an explicit unknown or reduced claim, never an invented success.
