## Scope and controlling responsibilities

Enforcement owner: monitor + supervisor + evidence service. This policy derives from the ground-up master prompt sections 28, 35. It is a complete written implementation rule; source adoption, exact compiled policy bytes and runtime enforcement require the build gates.

## Rules

Telemetry MUST be scoped and structured, with operation, task, causation, configuration and effect status. Routine logs exclude plaintext secrets and unrelated private content. Diagnostic export is an explicit operation, not a background upload.

Assumption records bind evidence, validity, dependencies, revalidation trigger and required failure action. A broken required assumption invalidates dependent preparation, capability qualifications and permissions. Tests must exercise propagation, not only the initial alarm.

NIRL reports technical indicators with uncertainty. Established boundary violations trigger deterministic restrictions; weak language classifiers cannot label a person hostile or punish disagreement. Chimera is visibly isolated, never a fake success world presented as reality. Restoration requires the prescribed evidence and current authorization.

## Enforcement and evidence

Implement predicates at the stated control boundary and add rejection fixtures for each forbidden path. Record the exact policy version, inputs, evaluated rule IDs and result. The policy's text is not itself an executable guard. Preserve both a legal case and a boundary-violation case in the source-bound verification bundle.

## Change and exception handling

A conflict with current adopted source law blocks the affected activation and is entered in the decision register. Do not invent an exception to obtain a passing test. Proposed changes must preserve the audit trail, identify impacted requirements and re-run dependent tests. Emergency responses may only exercise pre-existing, narrower authority.
