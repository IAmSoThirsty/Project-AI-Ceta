## Context

The system must remain coherent across cognition, authority, execution and continuity. Historical components and similarly named roles can hide different meanings and trust assumptions. This decision supplies a single explicit engineering rule for the new design.

## Decision

Keep cognitive reflection, protected custody and distributed CBCC authority distinct. Resolve a reviewed confidential envelope/threshold release protocol.

## Consequences

Stable ciphertext must survive fresh authorization; local files or signatures alone do not satisfy distributed secrecy.
