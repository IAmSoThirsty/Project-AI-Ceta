# Typed error admission and effect uncertainty

State: engineering increment source-tested; whole contract and runtime remain
open. Executor: CURRENT_CODEX_EXECUTION_AGENT. Independent review and owner
release acceptance have not been performed.

The desktop mapped contract namespace now implements ErrorDefinition,
ErrorCatalog, ErrorAssessment and ErrorContract in rebuild_ios. Profile:
ceta-error-semantics-v1. Existing ErrorEnvelope version 1 and the original twenty
public record schemas are unchanged. Internal catalog metadata is not another
admitted public record. Composition must authenticate the injected definitions.

Registered bounded definitions are immutable, reject malformed or weakened
completion guards, and have an order-independent versioned canonical identity.
Direct construction validates guidance under the existing Unicode/canonical
profile. Error decoding preserves original bytes/commitment and checks exact
expected scope, task, operation and affected-resource scopes. Nullable IDs and
revisions remain unavailable; no invented identity or resource-absence proof.

Unknown/possible effects require RECONCILE. Known observed effects are not
verified success and cannot be repeated just because an error references new
authority. EFFECT_INDETERMINATE, PERMIT_CONSUMED and STOP_PENDING require
reconciliation; an indeterminate effect cannot assert NOT_STARTED. The
assessment also honors an explicit RECONCILE declaration for any registered
error, even when the current attempt has NOT_STARTED. Recovery guidance and
retry-policy references do not issue authority, refund a budget or dispatch work.

Evidence: desktop-contract-source-bound-08 first passed 94 tests. Additional
boundary probes produced two failures in run 09: direct definition construction
did not reject noncanonical Unicode guidance, and the assessment ignored an
explicit RECONCILE declaration for a NOT_STARTED attempt. Run 10 passed 94
tests after constructor validation and reconciliation predicate repair, with
zero failures/errors/skips and source unchanged. All three runs are preserved.

Runtime request authentication, diagnostic access/redaction, trusted effect
evidence, owned termination, independent observation, settlement, compensation
and durable budget/consume behavior remain mandatory. B016/B031/B064 and full
effect/cancellation scenarios remain NOT_RUN. This pure contract neither proves
the reported effect happened nor promotes an error to completed work.
