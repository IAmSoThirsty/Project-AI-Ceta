# Bounded original-record framing

State: engineering increment source-tested; whole contract ratification and
actual transport qualification remain open. Executor: CURRENT_CODEX_EXECUTION_AGENT.
Independent review and owner release acceptance have not been performed.

Choose ceta-record-framing-v1: a four-byte unsigned big-endian body length,
excluding the header, followed by exactly 1 through 1,048,576 original UTF-8
record bytes. This reuses the existing strict wire bound and all twenty record
definitions. Length is decoded without body allocation. Zero length, oversized
declaration, wrong header width, truncated/trailing/concatenated complete frame,
mutable input, malformed JSON/Unicode, duplicate key and unknown record fields
are rejected under their own stable errors. A new public record schema is not
introduced. Exact original payload bytes and record commitments are preserved.

The fixed framing prefix has no magic/version, identity or authority field.
The eventual authenticated transport must bind this profile/version to its
session and reject unsupported profiles. Its separate ARCH-02 envelope, peer
and lifetime evidence, receiver generation, message identity, replay state,
fences, deadlines, policy and resource admission remain unfinished. The codec
cannot undo an already allocated input or act as a streaming receiver.

Evidence: desktop-framing-narrow-01 passed 13 targeted tests. Source-bound
desktop-contract-source-bound-11 passed 107 pure contract tests with zero
failures, errors or skips and unchanged watched bytes. Known header vectors,
hostile declarations and parser-not-called assertions cover the bounded guard;
twenty existing record types round-trip without weakening their validators.
This is not OS IPC, peer authentication, replay protection, permission, native
containment, independent outcome observation or runtime availability evidence.

No sockets, handles, database, filesystem, keys, model or effect operation is
added. Fragment accumulation, maximum admitted connections/messages, EOF,
timeout, cancellation and resource reserves must be qualified at the actual
receiver. A future transport change requires a versioned contract amendment
and corresponding tests/compatibility evidence; do not auto-detect alternate
byte orders or silently accept padding/batches.
