# Minimum Handoff Record

```text
HANDOFF ID:
ORIGINATING ROLE:
RECEIVING ROLE:
OBJECTIVE AND SCOPE:
INPUTS AND SOURCE IDENTITY:
AUTHORITY AND PROHIBITED ACTIONS:
CURRENT STATE:
ASSUMPTIONS:
UNKNOWNS:
WORK PERFORMED:
EVIDENCE PRODUCED:
FAILURES:
BLOCKERS:
RISKS:
UNRESOLVED DISAGREEMENT:
ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA STATUS:
OPEN DECISIONS:
NEXT SAFE ACTION:
SAFE TO CONTINUE: yes/no
```

A handoff is an operational continuity record, not a marketing summary. It must describe what actually occurred.
