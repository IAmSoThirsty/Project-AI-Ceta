# S24 purpose

Use this construction skill to produce: **A separately produced verification report with reproduced results and explicit discrepancies.**

The required input is: Producer deliverables, exact source and environment references, acceptance criteria, and raw evidence.

The skill's distinguishing procedure is: Inspect actual bytes; reconstruct prerequisites; rerun critical checks independently; compare expected and observed outcomes; retain failed reproduction details; avoid editing the artifact under review without a new revision.

Success is measured by this oracle: **A distinct verifier reproduces the required outcome from the supplied artifacts and evidence.** The specifically adverse condition is: **The verifier approves by trusting the producer narrative or their own earlier implementation.** Preserve this distinction in planning, implementation, testing, and reporting. The skill does not expand the assigned task's scope, change constitutional rules, or grant execution authority.
