# S03 prerequisites

## Domain prerequisites

Canonical contract references, producer and consumer owners, local language conventions, and compatibility constraints.

## Required readiness

The task has an owner-approved goal, exact local inspection scope, mapped unit IDs, applicable contract revisions, observable acceptance criteria, and a named review owner. The local baseline and relevant source digests are available. Effectful work has current scoped permission and a recovery plan appropriate to its consequences. Test fixtures are isolated from owner production data.

## Admission decision

Verify these prerequisites rather than assuming them from conversation summaries or directory names. If a required source, environment, permission, or contract is unavailable, preserve existing evidence and identify the blocked step. Do not invent local implementation details, substitute a remote layout, or silently widen the task. The relevant domain failure to anticipate is: A consumer privately widens a security-critical field or assumes unknown states mean success.
