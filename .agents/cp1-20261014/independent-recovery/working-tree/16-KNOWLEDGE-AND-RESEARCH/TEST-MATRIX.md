## Component and subsystem tests

| Component | Integrated behavior | Invariant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [16.01](16.01-SOURCE-INGESTION/README.md) | Acquire authorized source bytes; preserve originals; classify format and trust; extract through bounded parsers; record dates and transformations; index after commit. | Publication date, event date, and acquisition date are stored separately. |
| [16.02](16.02-KNOWLEDGE-EXTRACTION/README.md) | Parse structure; extract attributed entities and relationships; normalize identifiers without losing originals; validate against source spans; retain uncertainty and conflicts. | Entity matching must not collapse distinct people, projects, or claims solely by name similarity. |
| [16.03](16.03-KNOWLEDGE-INDEXING/README.md) | Index committed authorized source versions; preserve citation boundaries; apply access controls; retrieve lexical, semantic, and graph candidates; rebuild reproducibly. | Derived indexes are rebuildable views and never the sole source of evidence. |
| [16.04](16.04-RESEARCH-REASONING/README.md) | Decompose research questions; compare sources and chronology; assess independence and support; seek disconfirming evidence; state supported findings and unresolved gaps. | Multiple copies of the same original report count as one evidence lineage, not independent corroboration. |
| [16.05](16.05-RESEARCH-OUTPUT/README.md) | Build source-linked findings; validate every citation; separate observation and interpretation; preserve research state; export an inspectable report with exact evidence references. | A citation must support the specific claim adjacent to it, not merely relate to the topic. |
