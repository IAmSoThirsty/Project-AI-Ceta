## Component and subsystem tests

| Component | Integrated behavior | Invariant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [15.01](15.01-CONVERSATION/README.md) | Accept exact messages and drafts; preserve order and identity; stream assistant responses; reconcile delivery and storage; render history with provenance. | A duplicate transport delivery cannot create a duplicate human instruction or task. |
| [15.02](15.02-VOICE/README.md) | Obtain microphone permission; capture bounded audio; transcribe with uncertainty; confirm consequential ambiguity; synthesize approved output; support interruption and cleanup. | Speech recognition confidence cannot substitute for confirmation of ambiguous consequential commands. |
| [15.03](15.03-CHANNELS/README.md) | Configure an optional channel; verify endpoint and sender identity; normalize incoming data; authorize outgoing content and recipient; record delivery uncertainty accurately. | Channel membership or possession of a message does not establish owner identity. |
| [15.04](15.04-ATTACHMENTS/README.md) | Stage bytes; validate type and size; inspect unsafe structures; store under scoped ownership; create source references; ingest only through qualified parsers. | Attachment names and declared content types are not trusted as proof of actual format. |
| [15.05](15.05-HUMAN-INTERVENTION/README.md) | Present the exact decision and consequences; authenticate the responding human; bind the response to the proposal revision; expire stale prompts; resume only authorized work. | Silence, interface visibility, or a generic acknowledgement does not approve unspecified effects. |
| [15.06](15.06-MULTIMODAL-INTERACTION/README.md) | Validate image or media input; extract authorized observations; preserve spatial and temporal source references; combine with dialogue context; expose uncertainty. | Extracted text and visual interpretations remain attributed observations, not authoritative instructions. |
