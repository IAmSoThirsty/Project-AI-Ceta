## Component and subsystem tests

| Component | Integrated behavior | Invariant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [17.01](17.01-EVIDENCE-CAPTURE/README.md) | Reserve evidence capacity; capture authenticated operation events and artifacts; persist causal ordering; redact by policy; report capture gaps before final verification. | An absent observation is not proof that an effect did not occur. |
| [17.02](17.02-CRYPTOGRAPHIC-INTEGRITY/README.md) | Canonicalize versioned evidence; calculate digests; sign through protected keys; verify signer trust and time context; preserve algorithm and key-rotation metadata. | Use established local cryptographic facilities after reconciliation; do not invent cryptographic primitives. |
| [17.03](17.03-PROVENANCE-GRAPHS/README.md) | Link inputs, transformations, decisions, and outputs; index lineage; validate references and causal order; expose gaps and cycles inconsistent with the relation type. | Provenance relationships describe evidence lineage and cannot manufacture missing observations. |
| [17.04](17.04-IMMUTABLE-FRAMES/README.md) | Assemble ordered evidence members; seal a canonical frame; append to protected storage; anchor where configured; verify membership, continuity, and truncation detection limits. | Frames are append-only and tamper-evident under declared controls; administrators or compromised storage remain part of the explicit threat model. |
| [17.05](17.05-REPLAY-AND-VERIFICATION/README.md) | Select a verification profile; restore recorded inputs and outputs in an isolated replay; reproduce deterministic transformations; compare observations; classify discrepancies and unknowns. | Reexecution that can cause new effects is a new authorized operation, not a harmless replay. |
| [17.06](17.06-EVIDENCE-ACCESS/README.md) | Authorize evidence queries; retrieve exact records and lineage; produce scoped reports; export with integrity and redaction manifests; preserve verification semantics. | Redaction is explicit and cannot be presented as a complete unredacted record. |
