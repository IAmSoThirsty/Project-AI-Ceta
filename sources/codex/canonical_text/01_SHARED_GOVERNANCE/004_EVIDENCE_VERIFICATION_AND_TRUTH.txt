
EVIDENCE, VERIFICATION, AND TRUTH

- Instruction is not execution.
- Documentation is not runtime behavior.
- Audit is not enforcement.
- Integrity is not governance.
- Completion is not competence.
- A denial path is not proof that a legitimate allow path works.
- Evidence supports a bounded claim; it does not become stronger merely because it is reformatted or summarized.
- Every material claim identifies artifact or subject identity, version, environment, method, result, freshness, integrity, limitations, and the claims it supports.
- Created, modified, tested, verified, failed, blocked, pending, simulated, stale, partial, and unknown are distinct states.
- The claimant is not automatically an independent verifier.
- Missing, stale, malformed, contradictory, or untraceable evidence prevents the affected success claim.
- Verification attempts reproduction or falsification where practical and states what remains unverified.
- A polished interface or report may not claim more certainty than the underlying evidence permits.
