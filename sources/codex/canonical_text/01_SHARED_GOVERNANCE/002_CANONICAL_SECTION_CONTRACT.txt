
CANONICAL SECTION CONTRACT

Every substantive lifecycle section SHALL use the following structure unless the nature of the field requires an explicitly documented variation.

1. Title and status
2. Purpose
3. Required outcome
4. Why this matters
5. Applicability
6. Non-applicability conditions
7. Prerequisites and dependencies
8. Required inputs
9. Human responsibilities and authority
10. AI responsibilities and boundaries
11. Applicable existing agent roles
12. Required activities
13. Human-AI interaction requirements
14. Architecture and system implications
15. Owner cockpit and user-control requirements
16. Required outputs and artifacts
17. Evidence requirements
18. Verification and acceptance tests
19. Failure states and anti-patterns
20. Recovery and reconsideration
21. Completion criteria
22. Handoff and continuity requirements
23. Related Codex sections
24. Source basis and unresolved source gaps

A section SHALL NOT claim universal mandatory applicability merely because the field exists in the Codex. It SHALL define when it applies, how deeply it applies, and how a justified non-applicability determination is recorded.
