SECTION 348 — Procurement

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Acquire goods, services, software, hardware, data, facilities, and suppliers through a fair, authorized, evidence-based process that protects value, rights, security, continuity, and people.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A procurement model with need, specification, market, evaluation, authority, conflict, price, contract, security, privacy, IP, delivery, acceptance, supplier risk, and exit.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Procurement choices create long-lived technical, financial, legal, and operational dependencies. Lowest price, polished proposal, or urgent demand does not prove suitability, integrity, resilience, or total cost.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when an organization selects, buys, renews, leases, contracts, or materially changes a supplier, product, service, asset, or license.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no purchase, supplier, contract, asset, cost, obligation, or dependency changes and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use strategy, requirements, legal, IP, licensing, security, privacy, architecture, finance, supply-chain, quality, and vendor baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Need, specification, budget, alternatives, suppliers, evaluation, conflicts, contract, security, privacy, delivery, acceptance, support, continuity, and exit.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Authorized procurement, finance, legal, security, technical, privacy, and business owners evaluate and approve. Humans manage conflicts, negotiate, accept risk, sign, and terminate.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may compare authorized proposals, extract terms, draft questions, and identify gaps. It must not select supplier, negotiate, make representations, expose confidential bids, or approve spend or contract without human authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Procurement Analyst, Business Analyst, Architect, Legal Advisor, Security Auditor, Privacy Reviewer, Financial Analyst, Researcher, Verifier, and Governance Reviewer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define need; specify; research; solicit; evaluate; check conflicts and rights; assess supplier risk; negotiate; approve; contract; receive; accept; monitor; renew or exit.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show supplier, requirement, proposal, source, evaluation, conflict, cost, risk, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for selection, spend, negotiation, contract, exception, and termination.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Capture supplier, product, dependency, data, security, support, SLA, license, provenance, integration, portability, and exit as linked records.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show procurement status, suppliers, evaluation, spend, contract, risks, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized approve, compare, award, hold, renew, remediate, and terminate controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Procurement charter; requirement and evaluation; supplier and conflict; contract and risk; delivery and acceptance; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify need, supplier, proposal, version, price, source, evaluator, method, time, result, conflict, and limitation. Proposal or certification proves only the supplier's bounded claim.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify requirements, supplier identity, conflicts, price, contract, security, privacy, IP, delivery, quality, support, continuity, acceptance, and exit. Test nonperformance and substitution.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Unapproved spend, sole-source without reason, conflict hidden, price-only decision, contract not linked to architecture, supplier claim as evidence, and exit impossible.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause purchase or use, preserve records, escalate conflict or nonperformance, apply remedy or substitute, protect data, and reassess dependency and cost. Reopen on supplier, product, contract, risk, or requirement change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Need, specification, authority, suppliers, evaluation, cost, contract, risks, acceptance, evidence, owners, exit, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending authorized approval.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve procurement, proposals, evaluations, contracts, suppliers, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Vendor, Supply Chain, Legal, Finance, Security, Architecture, and Operations.
