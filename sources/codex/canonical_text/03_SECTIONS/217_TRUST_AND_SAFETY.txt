SECTION 217 — Trust and Safety

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Govern the safety, integrity, fairness, reliability, and human impact of products, services, communities, and automated capabilities that people may rely on or be exposed to.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A trust-and-safety model with harms, affected parties, policies, controls, reporting, review, escalation, transparency, appeal, measurement, incident response, recovery, and accountable ownership.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Trust is earned through dependable behavior, truthful communication, protection from abuse, and meaningful recourse. Safety can fail through foreseeable misuse, opaque automation, inaccessible controls, weak moderation, or metrics that reward activity while hiding harm.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a product, service, platform, organization, AI capability, or decision process can affect people, public trust, rights, safety, dignity, access, or community conditions.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability only when the scope has no affected person, public interaction, consequential decision, abuse path, safety impact, or trust claim and an approved exact baseline documents the determination.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use user research, risk, privacy, security, abuse, fraud, legal, ethics, accessibility, incident, governance, and operations baselines. Identify vulnerable groups, harm owners, review authority, appeal, and emergency paths.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Users and affected non-users, intended and foreseeable use, harms, policies, reports, incidents, signals, models, controls, support, accessibility, impact measures, evidence, and unresolved concerns.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners set protected outcomes, unacceptable harm, policy, enforcement, transparency, appeal, emergency response, and risk acceptance. Humans review high-impact decisions, vulnerable-person impact, material policy changes, and claims made to the public.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may summarize authorized reports, identify patterns, draft policy options and tests, and surface uncertainty or conflicting evidence. It must preserve context, privacy, cultural and accessibility boundaries, and human recourse. It must not decide harm, deny rights, remove access, make public claims, or replace accountable review without delegation.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

User Experience Reviewer, Trust and Safety Reviewer, Researcher, Analyst, Ethics Reviewer, Privacy Reviewer, Security Auditor, Legal Advisor, Tester, Verifier, Governance Reviewer, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Identify affected people and harms; define policy and thresholds; design reporting, review, support, appeal, and transparency; assess foreseeable misuse and disparate impact; test controls; monitor outcomes; respond to incidents; communicate; recover; and review policy with evidence and lived experience.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show affected party, policy, context, evidence, uncertainty, action, reviewer, explanation, and recourse. Require human judgment for high-impact or irreversible action. Preserve accessible reporting, appeal, support, and emergency escalation.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate policy, detection, review, enforcement, appeal, support, measurement, incident, and audit. Design for regional context, accessibility, privacy, adversarial behavior, model drift, human workload, and safe degradation. Do not optimize trust metrics as a substitute for safety outcomes.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show policy version, affected populations, reports, harm signals, reviewer state, appeals, incidents, evidence freshness, and authorized pause, narrow, reverse, notify, escalate, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Trust-and-safety charter; harm and affected-party map; policy and control catalog; review, support, appeal, transparency, and incident design; impact assessment; test, decision, evidence, recovery, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify claim, affected party, source, context, policy, method, reviewer, result, uncertainty, impact, and limitation. User reports, ratings, model scores, and absence of complaints are bounded signals, not complete safety proof.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Test intended and foreseeable misuse, vulnerable-user journeys, reporting, review, appeal, accessibility, false positives, false negatives, evasion, human workload, incident response, reversal, and communication. Verify decisions can be explained and corrected.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Safety theater, opaque enforcement, no recourse, proxy discrimination, public claims without evidence, optimizing engagement over harm, and treating policy presence as lived safety.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Protect people, pause harmful behavior, preserve evidence, reverse unjustified action, communicate truthfully, provide support and appeal, and reassess controls and policy. Reopen on new harm, population change, model or policy drift, incident, or credible dissent.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Harms, affected parties, policies, controls, owners, evidence, review, recourse, incidents, recovery, tests, measures, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending independent verification and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve policy, impact, report, case, appeal, incident, evidence, decision, and exception history with privacy controls. Hand off to Abuse, Fraud, Privacy, Security, Legal, Ethics, Support, UX, Testing, and Governance.
