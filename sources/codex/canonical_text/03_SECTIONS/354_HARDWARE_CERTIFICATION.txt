SECTION 354 — Hardware Certification

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Establish whether a hardware product or component meets a declared certification, conformity, safety, electromagnetic, environmental, or sector requirement for its exact scope.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A certification model with authority, standard, product identity, configuration, evidence, test laboratory, samples, findings, conditions, validity, marking, surveillance, and change impact.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Certification is bounded by standard, version, sample, configuration, jurisdiction, and validity. A certificate can be misapplied to a changed product or treated as total safety or security.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a hardware product, device, component, facility, or process requires formal certification, conformity, approval, registration, or regulated evidence.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability only after qualified review of product, market, jurisdiction, use, standard, and change scope confirms no certification or conformity claim is affected.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use legal, regulatory, safety, hardware, manufacturing, testing, quality, supply, documentation, and change baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Product, model, revision, standard, jurisdiction, intended use, samples, configuration, tests, laboratory, evidence, findings, validity, and change process.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Qualified certification, legal, safety, quality, and engineering owners approve scope, laboratory, submission, findings, marking, change, surveillance, and release. Humans attest and communicate boundaries.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may organize authorized certification evidence, compare requirements, draft questions, and identify changed configuration. It must not certify, sign, select laboratory, conceal finding, or claim regulatory approval without qualified human authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Certification Engineer, Hardware Engineer, Safety Reviewer, Legal Advisor, Compliance Tester, Quality Assurance Reviewer, Researcher, Verifier, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Identify authority and standard; define product and configuration; select lab; prepare samples; test; address findings; submit; receive; mark and communicate; monitor validity; manage change; and renew or retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show product, revision, standard, jurisdiction, sample, lab, evidence, validity, finding, and limitation. Require qualified human review for submission, attestation, marking, public claim, and change impact.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Bind certification to model, configuration, components, firmware, manufacturing process, and intended use. Keep certificate, evidence, change, and release records linked.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show certification, scope, product, revision, jurisdiction, validity, findings, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized submit, hold, change, mark, renew, withdraw, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Certification charter; authority and standard; product and configuration; test and lab; findings and submission; certificate and change impact; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify authority, standard, version, product, configuration, sample, lab, method, time, result, validity, and limitation. Certificate proves only its declared scope and conditions.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify identity, configuration, standard, samples, laboratory, tests, findings, validity, marking, change notification, surveillance, and market scope. Test changed component or firmware impact.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Certificate applied to different revision, expired approval, unqualified lab, finding hidden, certification as total security, change without review, and marking beyond scope.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Hold or withdraw affected product, preserve evidence, notify authority and customers as required, assess field impact, remediate or recertify, and correct claims. Reopen on product, standard, jurisdiction, or change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Authority, standard, product, configuration, evidence, lab, validity, findings, owners, change impact, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending qualified acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve certificates, submissions, tests, labs, configurations, findings, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Manufacturing, Assembly, Safety, Legal, Compliance, Release, and Support.
