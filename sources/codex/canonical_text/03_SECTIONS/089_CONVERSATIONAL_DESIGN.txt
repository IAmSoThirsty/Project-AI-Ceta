SECTION 089 — Conversational Design

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define dialogue, turn-taking, intent, context, repair, escalation, and conversational content for systems that interact through language.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

 - Scoped participants, goals, channels, languages, authority, privacy, states, fallback, and lifecycle.
 - Dialogue models, content, evidence, tests, controls, and acceptance criteria.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Conversation can create false trust and hide system limits. A fluent response is not understanding, authorization, or a successful outcome.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to chat, assistants, agents, messaging, support, voice transcripts, and mixed human-machine dialogue.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No material dialogue, intent, disclosure, action, accessibility, privacy, or recovery effect; or an unchanged approved dialogue baseline with impact recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

User research, tasks, domain language, policies, content, models, tools, identity, permissions, privacy, safety, escalation, and acceptance authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

User research, tasks, domain language, policies, content, models, tools, identity, permissions, privacy, safety, escalation, and acceptance authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve goals, persona, disclosure, authority, tone, safety, privacy, escalation, and acceptance. They retain correction, stop, appeal, and human-support authority.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft dialogue and analyze transcripts. It must preserve context, uncertainty, consent, and provenance and must not invent policy, claim understanding, or execute beyond authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Conversational Designer, Voice Interface Designer, UX Reviewer, Researcher, Content Designer, Security and Privacy Auditors, Governance Reviewer, Tester, Verifier, Critic, Implementer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Model goals, turns, intents, context, confirmation, ambiguity, repair, error, handoff, privacy, language, accessibility, tool use, and recovery; prototype and test representative and adversarial conversations.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show speaker, context, intent, confidence, tool, authority, consequence, recording, and recovery. AI-generated responses remain provisional; users can correct, exit, and reach humans.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate dialogue state, policy, retrieval, generation, tool execution, identity, audit, and human handoff. Enforce authority outside language interpretation and preserve terminal state.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show conversation identity, state, speaker, tool calls, permissions, evidence, uncertainty, open failures, and controls to stop, clear, correct, escalate, or recover.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Dialogue map, intents, prompts, content rules, state model, escalation paths, privacy and authority model, test transcripts, evidence, decisions, and handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify speaker, context, language, model, prompt, tools, version, transcript, method, result, uncertainty, and limitation. A successful chat is bounded evidence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify ambiguity, refusal, prompt injection, privacy, identity, permissions, tool errors, long turns, context loss, accessibility, localization, handoff, and recovery.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Anthropomorphic claims; endless loops; hidden tool actions; no exit; invented sources or policy; confident ambiguity; recording without control; and treating engagement as resolution.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stop tool actions, preserve transcript and audit, revoke access, provide human or nonconversational fallback, and reassess affected users before resuming.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Goals, participants, states, authority, content, evidence, tests, fallback, recovery, open risks, and acceptance are explicit. Remain CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve dialogue versions, prompts, transcripts, tool and policy identities, decisions, evidence, incidents, and next action. Hand off to Voice, AI, Prompt, Security, Privacy, Content, Testing, and Operations.
