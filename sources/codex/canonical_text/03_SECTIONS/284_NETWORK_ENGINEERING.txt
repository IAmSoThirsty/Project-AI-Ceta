SECTION 284 — Network Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Design, secure, operate, observe, scale, troubleshoot, and recover networks that connect people, devices, services, data, and control boundaries.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A network model with topology, addressing, identity, segmentation, routes, protocols, trust boundaries, performance, availability, monitoring, change, and recovery ownership.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Networks carry authority and data across boundaries. Misrouting, flat trust, hidden dependencies, provider loss, and inaccurate telemetry can create broad exposure or make recovery impossible.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when network, communication, routing, connectivity, name resolution, load balancing, firewall, wireless, or physical link affects a system or service.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no network boundary, route, protocol, connectivity, trust, or operational behavior changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use architecture, identity, security, cloud, edge, server, database, storage, communications, configuration, monitoring, and operations baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Topology, assets, addresses, routes, protocols, identities, zones, policies, dependencies, capacity, latency, telemetry, provider, change, and recovery.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Network owners approve topology, trust, routes, access, provider, maintenance, outage, exceptions, and recovery. Humans review changes crossing protected boundaries.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze authorized topology, flow, metrics, configurations, and incidents; draft diagrams and tests; and propose diagnosis. It must not change routes, firewall, DNS, access, or capture content without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Network Engineer, Security Auditor, Architect, Cloud Engineer, Systems Administrator, Observer, Tester, Verifier, Analyst, Critic, and Operations Engineer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define topology and zones; allocate addressing; secure identity and protocols; configure routing and policy; monitor; capacity-plan; test failure; maintain; troubleshoot; recover; and retire links and devices.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show topology, source, route, zone, identity, action, evidence, privacy boundary, and limitation. Require human approval for firewall, routing, capture, production, and destructive changes.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use segmentation, least privilege, authenticated management, resilient routing, provider diversity, rate and fault controls, observability, encryption, and out-of-band recovery.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show topology, links, routes, zones, health, capacity, flows, incidents, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized isolate, reroute, restrict, restore, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Network charter and topology; addressing and trust model; routing and policy; capacity and monitoring; change, incident, recovery, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify link, device, route, zone, protocol, identity, configuration, method, time, result, actor, and limitation. Reachability proves neither authorization nor end-to-end service behavior.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify segmentation, routing, DNS, protocol, identity, encryption, capacity, latency, monitoring, failover, rollback, isolation, and recovery. Test provider, link, route, and control-plane loss.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Flat network, implicit trust, shared admin, unmonitored route, firewall change without review, packet capture without privacy authority, and connectivity treated as security or service success.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Isolate, preserve configuration and flow evidence, reroute or fail over, restore known-good policy, rotate credentials, communicate, and reassess data and user impact. Reopen on topology, provider, threat, or service change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Topology, zones, routes, identities, policies, capacity, evidence, recovery, owners, limits, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve diagrams, configs, changes, flows, incidents, backups, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Cloud, Edge, Servers, Database, Storage, Security, Communications, and Operations.
