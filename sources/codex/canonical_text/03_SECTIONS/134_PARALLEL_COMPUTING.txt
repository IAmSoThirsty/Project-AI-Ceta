SECTION 134 - Parallel Computing

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define governed engineering of computation divided across concurrent workers, threads, processes, devices, or nodes with explicit decomposition, synchronization, memory, correctness, and failure behavior.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A parallel-computing baseline with decomposition, data ownership, synchronization, race controls, scalability, reproducibility, resource limits, and recovery evidence.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Concurrency creates races, deadlocks, nondeterminism, contention, and numerical variation. Parallel speedup without correctness or diagnosability is an unsafe claim.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to multithreaded, vectorized, GPU, actor, task, map-reduce, distributed, and concurrent algorithms or services.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

May be non-applicable to sequential computation with no concurrency, parallelism, or scaling claim. Record scope, owner, evidence, and reopening trigger.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Requires workload, decomposition, memory and data ownership, synchronization, ordering, determinism, hardware, scheduler, correctness, performance, and failure requirements.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Provide parallel units, dependencies, shared state, synchronization, partitioning, scheduling, memory, precision, resource, performance, failure, and reproducibility constraints.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve determinism, correctness, resource allocation, performance tradeoffs, and acceptance. Humans decide whether nondeterminism or approximate results are permissible.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may propose decomposition, identify races, generate tests, and analyze profiles. It must not claim race freedom or alter synchronization and resource policy without review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Architect, Analyst, Implementer, Optimizer, Tester, Verifier, Observer, Security Auditor, Governance Reviewer, Critic, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, Coordinator, and Red Team. Existing role contracts remain controlling.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Define dependencies, ownership, ordering, determinism, workload, and correctness.
2. Select decomposition and synchronization; analyze races, deadlocks, contention, and failure.
3. Measure scaling and correctness under controlled and adversarial schedules.
4. Release, monitor, checkpoint, recover, and retire safely.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Expose worker, partition, synchronization, seed, hardware, result, evidence, and consequence. Human review is required for correctness and safety exceptions; status, cancel, and recovery paths remain available.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Make ownership, synchronization, memory visibility, ordering, cancellation, backpressure, checkpoint, and failure domains explicit. Capture schedules and versions sufficient for replay or bounded diagnosis.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show workers, queues, contention, lag, deadlocks, errors, nondeterminism, resource use, output validity, and controls to cancel, drain, checkpoint, isolate, or retry.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Parallel decomposition, ownership, synchronization design, correctness invariants, race and deadlock analysis, reproducible build, performance evidence, fault tests, and recovery runbook.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify code, runtime, hardware, schedule, seed, inputs, partition, configuration, method, result, and limits. Preserve race, deadlock, nondeterminism, and corrected-run evidence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify race, deadlock, ordering, cancellation, failure, replay, scaling, determinism, numerical tolerance, resource isolation, checkpoint, and recovery across schedules and worker counts.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Assuming sequential reasoning; shared mutable state; lock-free code without proof; unbounded queues; timing-based correctness; hiding nondeterminism; and accepting speedup with changed results.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stop affected workers, preserve schedules and partial output, restore a known-good synchronization path, validate results, compensate effects, and reopen the parallel correctness baseline.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Decomposition, ownership, synchronization, correctness, evidence, performance, recovery, and authority are explicit. Keep CONTENT-COMPLETE until independent verification and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve code, runtime, schedule, seed, hardware, traces, failures, decisions, evidence, and next safe action. Hand off to HPC, Distributed, Real-Time, Runtime, Testing, Reliability, and Operations.
