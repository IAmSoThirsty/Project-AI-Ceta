SECTION 387 — Compatibility Management

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Manage supported combinations, contracts, migrations, version coexistence, deprecation, fallback, and user or system impact across change.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A compatibility model with producer, consumer, version, platform, data, interface, support policy, test evidence, migration, fallback, owner, and retirement.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Compatibility is a relationship, not a property of one component. Silent breakage, indefinite support, or untested fallback creates hidden cost and user harm.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when versions, platforms, interfaces, formats, providers, devices, users, or consumers must coexist or transition.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no supported relationship, interface, consumer, platform, or migration behavior changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use architecture, interface, version, release, testing, dependency, support, data migration, and deprecation baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Producer, consumer, version, platform, contract, data, support, test, migration, fallback, owner, and retirement condition.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners define support and breaking-change policy; producers and consumers coordinate; humans approve deprecation, migration, exception, and customer communication.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze authorized compatibility matrices and changes, draft tests and migration, and identify risk. It must not declare compatibility, remove support, or change interface without human authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Compatibility Manager, Architect, Release Engineer, API Engineer, Data Engineer, Tester, Verifier, Support Reviewer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Inventory relationships; define support; identify breaking change; test; publish compatibility; provide migration and fallback; monitor; deprecate; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show producer, consumer, version, contract, platform, evidence, migration, support, and limitation. Require human approval for breaking change, deprecation, fallback, and communication.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use version negotiation, schema evolution, feature detection, adapter, backward compatibility, explicit failure, migration, fallback, and support metadata.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show matrix, support, versions, consumers, findings, evidence freshness, owner, migration, and authorized approve, restrict, deprecate, migrate, fallback, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Compatibility charter; producer and consumer matrix; contract and support; tests and migration; deprecation and fallback; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify producer, consumer, version, platform, contract, data, method, time, result, owner, and limitation. One compatible combination proves only that combination.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify contract, versions, data, platform, support, migration, fallback, error, security, performance, accessibility, and deprecation. Test old and new consumer.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Breaking change hidden, version support assumed, matrix ignored, fallback untested, indefinite support, migration one-way, and compatibility claim from one environment.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Restore compatibility or fallback, communicate, preserve evidence, migrate or roll back, and reassess users and consumers. Reopen on version, contract, platform, data, or policy change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Relationships, versions, support, contracts, evidence, migration, fallback, owners, deprecation, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve matrix, contracts, tests, migrations, communications, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Version, Deprecation, Release, Data, Support, Architecture, and Operations.
