SECTION 257 — Validation

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Determine whether the built capability solves the intended problem, supports real users and owners, produces acceptable outcomes, and remains suitable in its declared context.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A validation record connecting human objective, users, context, scenarios, outcomes, evidence, alternatives, harms, acceptance, and unresolved mismatch to a decision owner.

WHY THIS MATTERS

A system can conform perfectly to requirements that were wrong, incomplete, inaccessible, or detached from lived reality. Validation requires evidence from the people and conditions that give the work meaning.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a capability, product, service, policy, model, process, or decision must be shown useful, safe, acceptable, or fit for its intended purpose.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability only when no intended outcome, user, stakeholder, operational purpose, or material consequence is affected and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use research, requirements, user journeys, domain, safety, privacy, accessibility, quality, verification, acceptance, operations, and outcome evidence. Identify validation owner and affected parties.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Objective, users, affected non-users, context, scenarios, desired outcomes, requirements, artifact, evidence, alternatives, risks, harms, feedback, and acceptance authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans and representative users judge usefulness, fit, acceptability, harm, and tradeoffs. Owners decide whether evidence supports adoption; affected people must be able to report mismatch and withdraw or appeal where applicable.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may synthesize authorized research, compare outcomes, draft validation scenarios, and surface mismatch or uncertainty. It must not fabricate user evidence, speak for users, infer acceptance, or declare fitness from conformance or proxy metrics.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Validator, User Researcher, User Experience Reviewer, Researcher, Analyst, Domain Analyst, Safety Reviewer, Accessibility Reviewer, Verifier, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Restate intended outcome; identify users and context; test realistic scenarios; observe benefit, harm, comprehension, and recovery; compare alternatives; collect dissent; assess unintended effects; decide; monitor after use; and reopen when reality changes.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show objective, user, context, scenario, evidence, outcome, uncertainty, harm, alternative, and decision authority. Require direct human or user evidence where material and never present AI synthesis as participation.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Support user feedback, outcome instrumentation, accessibility, explainability, appeal, safety, privacy, rollback, support, and post-deployment learning. Separate proxy metrics from actual human outcomes.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show intended outcome, users, evidence, observed benefit and harm, dissent, owner, acceptance, limitations, and authorized pause, revise, compensate, reject, and reopen controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Validation charter; user and context map; scenario and outcome record; evidence and dissent ledger; harm and alternative assessment; decision, acceptance, monitoring, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify user or stakeholder, objective, context, scenario, method, time, observation, outcome, impact, uncertainty, and limitation. Conformance, satisfaction, usage, or model score alone does not prove fitness.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify representative users and context, intended outcome, actual use, accessibility, safety, privacy, support, harm, alternatives, dissent, and post-use monitoring. Reproduce material findings and document unresolved mismatch.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Validation by proxy metric, requirement compliance treated as usefulness, ideal-user testing, dissent erased, harm externalized, and adoption declared from launch or usage alone.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause or narrow use, protect affected people, preserve evidence, remedy harm, revise design or objective, and gather missing perspective. Reopen on user, context, outcome, population, policy, technology, or harm change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Objective, users, context, scenarios, outcomes, evidence, harms, alternatives, decision, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending affected-party and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve research, validation, user evidence, dissent, decisions, acceptance, monitoring, incidents, compensation, and open gaps. Hand off to Product, Requirements, UX, Quality, Operations, Support, Governance, and Review.
