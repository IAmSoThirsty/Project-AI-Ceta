SECTION 342 — Knowledge Management

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Create, organize, validate, retrieve, share, preserve, and retire knowledge needed for decisions, work, support, learning, governance, and continuity.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A knowledge model with sources, authority, topics, owners, version, context, access, search, freshness, reuse, feedback, retention, and unresolved questions.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Knowledge systems can spread stale, duplicated, decontextualized, or unauthorized claims. Retrieval speed is not truth, and a polished summary can conceal source gaps.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when information is curated for reuse in decisions, operations, support, training, education, community, AI, or organizational continuity.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no reusable knowledge, source, decision, support, or continuity claim changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use research, documentation, records, data, privacy, security, IP, legal, training, support, and governance baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Sources, claims, topics, authority, owner, version, context, access, audience, freshness, feedback, retention, and unresolved gaps.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Knowledge owners curate and verify; subject experts resolve meaning; humans approve authoritative status, access, retention, and retirement. Users can challenge and correct knowledge.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may retrieve authorized sources, summarize, classify, identify contradiction, and draft updates. It must preserve provenance and uncertainty and must not elevate, invent, expose, or retire knowledge without human authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Knowledge Manager, Researcher, Documentation Writer, Analyst, Domain Expert, Librarian, Verifier, Critic, Privacy Reviewer, and Memory Curator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Inventory and classify; establish authority; curate and contextualize; index; publish; review freshness; capture feedback and decisions; protect access; archive; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show source, authority, version, context, claim, audience, evidence, uncertainty, and limitation. Require human review for authoritative status, sensitive access, policy, and correction.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use provenance, metadata, search, source links, versioning, access, freshness, contradiction, feedback, retention, archive, and audit. Prevent summaries from replacing source where source matters.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show knowledge item, source, owner, authority, freshness, usage, contradiction, access, evidence, and authorized correct, annotate, restrict, promote, archive, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Knowledge charter; source and authority register; taxonomy and index; content and review; feedback, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify claim, source, version, context, authority, method, time, reviewer, result, and limitation. Search rank, reuse, or summary fluency proves neither truth nor authority.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify source, provenance, freshness, access, search, context, contradiction, correction, accessibility, retention, and user task. Test stale or conflicting knowledge.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Knowledge dumping, duplicate authority, stale runbook, source hidden, access leak, AI summary as truth, no correction, and usage metric as usefulness.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Mark stale or unsafe item, correct or withdraw, notify consumers, preserve history, and reassess decisions or operations based on it. Reopen on source, domain, policy, product, or audience change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Sources, authority, context, owners, version, access, freshness, evidence, feedback, retention, limits, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve sources, versions, reviews, corrections, usage, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Documentation, Training, Support, Education, AI Operations, Records, and Governance.
