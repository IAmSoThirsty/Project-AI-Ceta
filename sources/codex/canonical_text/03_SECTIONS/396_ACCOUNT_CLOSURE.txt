SECTION 396 — Account Closure

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Close a user, customer, service, tenant, supplier, or organizational account safely while revoking access, resolving obligations, protecting data, and preserving required records.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An account-closure model with authority, identity, entitlements, sessions, data, subscriptions, billing, dependencies, retention, export, deletion, support, appeal, and evidence.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Closing an account can strand data, leave credentials active, trigger financial or contractual error, or destroy evidence. Closure must balance user request, security, privacy, legal hold, and continuity.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when an account, identity, tenant, customer relationship, subscription, supplier access, or service account is suspended, closed, or terminated.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no account, identity, access, data, entitlement, billing, contract, or lifecycle state changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use IAM, privacy, records, retention, billing, payments, subscription, legal, support, security, data export, and service baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Account, owner, authority, sessions, access, entitlements, subscriptions, billing, data, export, holds, dependencies, support, and closure reason.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Account owners approve closure, access revocation, export, retention, deletion, billing, contract, legal hold, exception, and appeal. Humans handle disputed or high-impact closure.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may check authorized closure prerequisites and draft communications. It must not infer identity, close, revoke, delete, refund, expose data, or override hold without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

IAM Engineer, Privacy Reviewer, Records Manager, Legal Advisor, Billing Engineer, Customer Support, Security Auditor, Verifier, and Governance Reviewer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Authenticate request; assess authority and hold; inventory access, data, subscriptions, billing, and dependencies; export or retain; revoke; close; notify; verify; and dispose later.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show account, requester, authority, access, data, hold, obligations, action, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for closure, deletion, access revocation, refund, and disputed request.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use state machine, identity proof, session revocation, entitlement and billing linkage, export, hold, retention, deletion, audit, appeal, and recovery window.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show closure state, owner, access, sessions, data, subscriptions, billing, hold, evidence freshness, and authorized pause, export, revoke, close, restore, appeal, and delete controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Closure charter; authority and account; access and data; billing and subscription; export, revocation, communication, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify account, requester, authority, state, sessions, entitlements, data, hold, method, time, result, and limitation. Closure confirmation proves only recorded state and declared scope.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify identity, authority, sessions, access, subscriptions, billing, export, hold, retention, deletion, dependencies, communication, appeal, and recovery window.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Close without authentication, active credentials, data lost, billing continues, subscription orphaned, hold bypassed, no appeal, and closure used to erase evidence.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause closure, preserve state, restore or support account where authorized, revoke unsafe access, correct billing, communicate, and reassess rights and obligations. Reopen on dispute, hold, data, contract, or identity change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Account, authority, access, sessions, data, entitlement, billing, hold, export, retention, evidence, owners, appeal, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending qualified acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve closure, access, export, billing, subscriptions, communications, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to IAM, Privacy, Records, Billing, Payments, Support, Legal, Security, and Deletion.
