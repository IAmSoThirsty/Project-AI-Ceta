SECTION 139 - Graphics Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define governed engineering of visual computation, assets, pipelines, shaders, geometry, lighting, display, and interaction with explicit correctness, performance, accessibility, provenance, and platform boundaries.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A graphics baseline with scene, asset, shader, rendering, device, color, performance, accessibility, security, build, evidence, and recovery contracts.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Visual output can be wrong, inaccessible, misleading, or unsafe while appearing polished. GPU and asset failures also create portability, privacy, and resource risks.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to 2D or 3D graphics, real-time visualization, game graphics, UI rendering, GPU compute, asset pipelines, and display output.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

May be non-applicable to systems with no generated, rendered, displayed, or graphics-dependent output. Record scope, owner, evidence, and reopening trigger.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Requires visual purpose, target devices, GPU and API support, assets, color and accessibility rules, performance, security, licensing, build, testing, and release authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Provide scenes, geometry, materials, shaders, assets, color, resolution, input, frame and memory budgets, accessibility, privacy, and failure requirements.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve visual meaning, accessibility, asset rights, safety, performance tradeoffs, and release. Humans retain authority over public or consequential visual claims.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft shaders, assets, tests, and profiles. It must preserve provenance and licenses, avoid unsafe or deceptive output, and never ship generated content without review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Architect, Graphics Engineer, Designer, Researcher, Security Auditor, Accessibility specialist, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Governance Reviewer, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, and Red Team. Existing role contracts remain controlling.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Define visual purpose, targets, assets, APIs, color, accessibility, and budgets.
2. Build scene and asset pipelines with provenance, determinism, and fallback.
3. Test rendering, correctness, performance, compatibility, accessibility, and device loss.
4. Release, monitor, optimize, and retire assets and rendering paths.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show source or generated asset, shader, device, API, frame state, evidence, and confidence. Human review is required for rights, accessibility, and consequential visuals; fallback remains available when GPU or AI fails.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate assets, scene, materials, shader, renderer, display, input, GPU, cache, and content trust boundaries. Version pipelines and preserve software fallback and device-loss recovery.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show build, renderer, device, frame time, memory, visual errors, asset provenance, accessibility, incidents, and controls to disable effects, fall back, purge, or roll back.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Graphics charter, asset catalog and rights, render pipeline, shader records, device matrix, color and accessibility plan, performance evidence, build manifest, and recovery runbook.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify asset, shader, build, device, API, configuration, test method, result, date, and limits. Separate pixel correctness, visual interpretation, accessibility, performance, security, and provenance evidence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify rendering, color, scaling, device loss, shader failure, malformed assets, memory pressure, performance, accessibility, localization, screenshots, and software fallback.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Unlicensed assets; shader-only correctness; inaccessible contrast; device-specific assumptions; unbounded texture memory; hidden tracking in assets; and treating visual fidelity as semantic or user success.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Disable affected effects, preserve assets and logs, use fallback renderer, remove unsafe or unlicensed content, compensate external use, and reopen the graphics baseline.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Visual purpose, assets, rights, accessibility, performance, evidence, fallback, and lifecycle are explicit. Keep CONTENT-COMPLETE until independent verification and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve assets, licenses, shaders, build, device evidence, decisions, incidents, and next safe action. Hand off to Rendering, Animation, Game, Media, Accessibility, Testing, Build, and Operations.
