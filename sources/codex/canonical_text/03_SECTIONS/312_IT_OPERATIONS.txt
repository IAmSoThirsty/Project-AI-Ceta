SECTION 312 — IT Operations

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Operate organizational technology services, endpoints, accounts, infrastructure, support, access, change, security, and continuity for internal and external users.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An IT-operations model with service catalog, users, assets, support, identity, devices, applications, network, incidents, changes, suppliers, continuity, and retirement.

WHY THIS MATTERS

IT operations affects daily work and organizational control. Unmanaged endpoints, weak support, stale access, supplier failure, and undocumented workarounds create security, productivity, and continuity risk.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when organizational IT services, devices, applications, accounts, support, or infrastructure are operated or changed.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no IT service, user, asset, access, support, or continuity behavior changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use service management, IAM, endpoint, network, security, privacy, asset, supplier, support, backup, and continuity baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Services, users, assets, accounts, endpoints, applications, support, incidents, changes, suppliers, policies, evidence, continuity, and retirement.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

IT owners approve service, access, device, support, change, incident, supplier, continuity, exception, and retirement. Humans protect user agency and accessible support.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may classify authorized tickets, summarize health, draft knowledge, and identify patterns. It must not access user content unnecessarily, revoke access, change devices, close sensitive tickets, or make employment or user-impact decisions without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

IT Operations Engineer, Service Manager, Systems Administrator, Support Reviewer, Security Auditor, IAM Engineer, Network Engineer, Tester, Verifier, and Coordinator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Operate services and assets; provision and deprovision; support users; manage access, devices, applications, changes, incidents, suppliers, backups, continuity, and retirement; measure outcomes and improve.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show user, service, asset, ticket, access, action, evidence, privacy boundary, and limitation. Require human review for sensitive content, access, employment impact, device wipe, and closure.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use inventory, identity, endpoint management, least privilege, service catalog, support, knowledge, audit, privacy, backup, recovery, and accessible alternatives.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show IT service, asset, user impact, access, incidents, requests, suppliers, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized approve, support, restrict, recover, replace, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

IT operations charter; service and asset catalog; support and access; incident and change; supplier, continuity, recovery, decision, evidence, and retirement records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify service, user, asset, action, source, method, time, result, actor, privacy boundary, and limitation. Ticket status proves only workflow state.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify service, support, access, device, application, network, security, privacy, backup, restore, continuity, supplier, and user recovery. Test inaccessible support and lost device.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Ticket closure as resolution, shared account, unmanaged device, inaccessible support, wipe without authority, supplier hidden, and workaround without record.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Support affected users, preserve evidence, restore or replace, revoke or recover access, communicate, and reassess privacy and continuity. Reopen on user, service, asset, supplier, or policy change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Services, users, assets, access, support, incidents, changes, suppliers, recovery, evidence, owners, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve asset and service records, tickets, incidents, access, supplier, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Service Management, Security, IAM, Support, Operations, Records, and Governance.
