SECTION 261 — Package Management

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Govern the acquisition, storage, resolution, publication, promotion, integrity, vulnerability, licensing, and retirement of packages used to build or operate the system.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A package-management model with registries, repositories, provenance, resolution, lock or pin policy, access, signing, scanning, retention, promotion, rollback, and owners.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Packages are executable supply-chain inputs. A mutable registry, malicious artifact, dependency confusion, unsigned package, or unreviewed resolution can alter behavior before local source review.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when packages, modules, libraries, plugins, containers, firmware bundles, datasets, models, or provider components are fetched, built, cached, promoted, or consumed.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no package or registry input is used and an approved exact baseline covers the artifact.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use dependency, source, artifact, security, license, secrets, build, release, and network baselines. Identify registries, trust, ownership, retention, and emergency authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Package graph, registry, namespace, version, digest, provenance, license, advisory, policy, credentials, cache, lockfile, promotion, and retirement requirements.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners approve registries, namespaces, packages, trust, publishing, promotion, exceptions, retention, and withdrawal. Humans resolve identity and license ambiguity and accept supply-chain risk.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze authorized package metadata, graph, advisories, and lockfiles; draft updates and policies; and identify anomalies. It must not install, publish, promote, delete, or trust a package without authority and verification.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Package Manager, Supply Chain Analyst, Security Auditor, Dependency Manager, Build Engineer, Researcher, Tester, Verifier, Governance Reviewer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define approved sources; inventory and pin packages; verify provenance and integrity; scan license and vulnerability; isolate and cache; review and promote; monitor registry; withdraw or replace; retain evidence; and recover.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show package, registry, namespace, version, digest, provenance, policy, scan, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for install, publish, promotion, exception, and withdrawal.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use trusted registries, namespace controls, digest or version pinning, signed provenance, isolated builders, cache integrity, allowlists, SBOM, quarantine, and rollback.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show registry, package, version, digest, findings, provenance, consumers, evidence freshness, and authorized approve, quarantine, promote, withdraw, replace, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Package policy and inventory; registry and trust model; lock and provenance records; scan and promotion results; withdrawal and recovery; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify package, registry, namespace, version, digest, source, builder, scan, time, actor, result, and limitation. Name and version alone do not prove artifact identity or safety.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify source, namespace, integrity, provenance, license, vulnerability, resolution, cache, promotion, rollback, withdrawal, and consumer impact. Test unavailable, substituted, or malicious registry response.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Untrusted registry, dependency confusion, mutable tag, unsigned package, cache poisoning, package installed directly in production, and scan passed without exact digest.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Quarantine package and consumers, preserve provenance, rotate credentials, roll back or replace, notify owners, and assess downstream artifacts. Reopen on registry, package, advisory, policy, or build change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Packages, registries, trust, versions, digests, owners, scans, promotion, recovery, evidence, exceptions, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve package and digest history, registry policy, provenance, scans, promotions, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Dependency, Build, Artifact, Security, Release, and Platform.
