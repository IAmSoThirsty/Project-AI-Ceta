SECTION 232 — Hardware Testing

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Evaluate hardware behavior, interfaces, tolerances, safety, reliability, manufacturing variation, environmental response, and integration against declared requirements.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A hardware-test strategy with representative units, fixtures, instrumentation, conditions, limits, safety controls, data integrity, failures, coverage, acceptance, and lifecycle limitations.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Hardware behavior varies with component lot, temperature, power, wear, assembly, environment, and interaction. A single bench result cannot prove field reliability, safety, or behavior outside its configuration.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when physical components, boards, devices, sensors, actuators, enclosures, interfaces, or manufacturing variation affect a requirement or operational outcome.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability only when no physical behavior, interface, tolerance, safety, reliability, or manufacturing claim changes and an approved exact baseline applies.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use hardware, embedded, safety, quality, manufacturing, environmental, security, and requirements baselines. Define units, fixtures, calibration, hazards, test authority, and acceptance owner.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Requirements, drawings, revisions, bill of materials, tolerances, units, environments, loads, fixtures, instrumentation, hazards, prior failures, data plan, and acceptance criteria.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Hardware and safety owners approve conditions, hazards, destructive testing, sample size, deviations, and acceptance. Qualified testers protect people and equipment; owners decide whether a result represents the population.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may organize authorized specifications, test data, failure patterns, and calibration records; draft procedures and analysis; and propose bounded experiments. It must not control hazardous equipment, infer safety, alter data, or accept a unit without qualified human supervision.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Hardware Tester, Hardware Engineer, Safety Reviewer, Quality Engineer, Researcher, Analyst, Observer, Implementer, Verifier, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define requirements and risks; select representative units; verify fixtures and calibration; establish safe conditions; execute functional, boundary, environmental, durability, interface, and recovery tests; record failures; analyze variation; retest; and report limits.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show unit, revision, fixture, calibration, condition, load, instrument, method, result, hazard, and limitation. Require human approval for hazardous, destructive, field, or safety-relevant testing and acceptance.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Provide test points, instrumentation, safe isolation, fixture control, unit identity, environmental control, traceable data, and recovery from power, sensor, actuator, and communication failure.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show unit population, revision, condition, active test, hazard, calibration, failure, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized pause, stop, quarantine, accept, retest, and dispose controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Hardware test plan; unit and fixture record; calibration and condition record; results and failures; variation and coverage analysis; retest, acceptance, safety, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify unit, revision, lot, fixture, calibration, condition, load, instrument, method, time, result, operator, and limitation. A bench pass or certification proves only its declared conditions.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify fixtures, calibration, unit identity, safety, functional and boundary behavior, environmental limits, variation, repeatability, failure response, recovery, and data integrity. Reproduce material failures independently.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Uncalibrated instruments, nonrepresentative unit, unsafe setup, cherry-picked result, data alteration, tolerance ignored, destructive test without authority, and bench behavior treated as field proof.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stop unsafe testing, isolate unit and fixture, preserve data, investigate, repair or replace under authority, and reassess population and acceptance. Reopen on revision, lot, environment, requirement, or failure-pattern change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Units, conditions, fixtures, requirements, hazards, results, failures, coverage, evidence, acceptance, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending qualified review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve unit and fixture identity, calibration, data, failures, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Hardware, Embedded, Manufacturing, Safety, Quality, Release, Operations, and Disposal.
