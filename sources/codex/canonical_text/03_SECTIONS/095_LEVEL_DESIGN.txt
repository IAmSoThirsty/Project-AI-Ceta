SECTION 095 — Level Design

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define spaces, encounters, pacing, navigation, challenge, affordances, resources, and progression that support an intended player or participant experience.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

 - A scoped space, player, objective, mechanics, content, difficulty, accessibility, platform, and lifecycle model.
 - Level layouts and rules with evidence, tests, safety, recovery, and acceptance criteria.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Space teaches rules and shapes behavior. Poor pacing, navigation, difficulty, or accessibility can make the intended experience impossible or unfair.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to game levels, simulations, training spaces, virtual worlds, educational environments, and interactive physical or spatial scenes.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No spatial, navigation, challenge, content, accessibility, safety, or progression effect; or an unchanged approved level baseline with impact recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Game or simulation rules, player research, objectives, mechanics, narrative, platform, accessibility, content, performance, safety, and acceptance authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Game or simulation rules, player research, objectives, mechanics, narrative, platform, accessibility, content, performance, safety, and acceptance authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve objectives, difficulty, audience, representation, safety, accessibility, progression, and acceptance.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may generate layouts and analyze telemetry, but must disclose generation and preserve player agency. It must not alter live difficulty or target vulnerabilities without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Level Designer, Game Designer, Narrative Designer, UX Reviewer, Researcher, Accessibility specialist, Tester, Verifier, Critic, Governance Reviewer, Implementer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define goals, geometry, navigation, affordances, encounters, pacing, resources, difficulty, checkpoints, accessibility, hazards, feedback, and recovery; prototype and test representative play.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show objective, location, state, challenge, risk, automation, accessibility, and recovery. AI layouts remain proposals and player exit or assist controls remain visible.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Connect geometry, rules, navigation, triggers, state, assets, performance, checkpoints, telemetry, accessibility, and failure recovery. Avoid hidden progression dependencies.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show level version, player state, objective, difficulty, accessibility, performance, evidence, defects, and controls to pause, assist, skip, reset, or report.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Level brief, maps, encounter and pacing model, asset and trigger specification, accessibility notes, playtest evidence, decisions, defect register, and handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify player, level, platform, version, task, method, result, and limitation. Completion or average time is bounded evidence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify navigation, pacing, difficulty, checkpoints, exploits, accessibility, performance, ambiguity, safety, recovery, and representative player outcomes.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Unsignposted hazards; impossible navigation; difficulty spikes; inaccessible checkpoints; invisible dependencies; and telemetry optimized for friction rather than learning or enjoyment.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Offer assist, skip, reset, safe exit, and support; preserve progress and evidence; correct the level; and retest affected populations.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Goals, space, mechanics, players, difficulty, accessibility, evidence, tests, recovery, risks, and acceptance are explicit. Remain CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve maps, triggers, assets, versions, playtests, findings, decisions, evidence, and next action. Hand off to Game, Narrative, UX, Accessibility, Audio, Engineering, and Testing.
