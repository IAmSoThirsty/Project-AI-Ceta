SECTION 213 — Digital Forensics

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Preserve, acquire, examine, analyze, and report digital evidence in a lawful, controlled, reproducible, and privacy-aware manner.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A forensic plan and evidence package with authority, scope, preservation, acquisition, integrity, chain of custody, analysis, reporting, access control, retention, disclosure, and limitation records.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Digital evidence is volatile, duplicable, privacy-sensitive, and easy to alter. A technically plausible finding is not reliable if the source, acquisition method, time, integrity, analyst, or chain of custody cannot be reconstructed.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when system, device, cloud, application, network, identity, log, communication, or data artifacts may support incident response, legal action, safety review, fraud investigation, compliance, or learning.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no evidentiary question, preservation duty, incident, dispute, or consequential decision requires forensic handling and an approved exact baseline documents why ordinary operational records are sufficient.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Obtain authority, scope, legal and privacy guidance, preservation direction, evidence storage, trained personnel, time source, tooling, access, safety controls, and incident or records coordination.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Question, scope, systems, devices, accounts, time window, legal authority, preservation notice, source inventory, collection method, handling limits, hypotheses, known incidents, and reporting audience.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

The case owner and qualified forensic lead approve scope, collection, access, privacy handling, analysis, disclosure, retention, and release. Counsel or designated authority controls legal strategy. Humans decide material conclusions and whether evidence may be shared.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may index authorized copies, summarize records, identify patterns, draft timelines, compare artifacts, and propose follow-up questions. It must preserve source identity, integrity, chain of custody, uncertainty, and privacy boundaries. It must not collect, alter, delete, expose, attribute, or declare evidence authentic without authorized human-controlled procedures.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Forensic Analyst, Incident Responder, Security Auditor, Researcher, Observer, Analyst, Verifier, Governance Reviewer, Legal Advisor, Critic, Documentation Writer, and Memory Curator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define authority and question; preserve sources; document volatile state; acquire minimally and reproducibly; hash and seal copies; maintain custody; analyze with validated tools; separate observation from inference; review alternatives; report limitations; retain or dispose under authority.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show case, source, copy, hash, analyst, method, time, access authority, finding, confidence, and limitation. Require human approval for collection, privacy-sensitive review, attribution, disclosure, retention, and destruction. Never place raw sensitive evidence into an uncontrolled AI context.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate source systems, acquisition workspace, evidence store, working copies, analysis, reporting, access audit, legal hold, and disposal. Use immutable or tamper-evident records, least privilege, time synchronization, secure transfer, and reproducible tooling.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show case state, authority, scope, sources, custody, integrity, analysts, findings, privacy limits, disclosure state, and evidence freshness. Provide authorized preserve, restrict, release, escalate, correct, and close controls with durable audit.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Forensic charter; authority and preservation record; source and custody register; acquisition and integrity record; analysis notes; timeline; findings and alternative explanations; report; disclosure, retention, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify source, original state, acquisition method, copy, hash or integrity method, time basis, analyst, tool and version, access, observation, inference, confidence, and limitation. A screenshot, log export, or AI summary is bounded evidence only.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify authority, preservation, acquisition integrity, custody, time, tool behavior, reproducibility, privacy controls, independent review, report traceability, disclosure, retention, and disposal. Test corrupted copy, missing metadata, conflicting clocks, and tool failure.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Analyzing originals, undocumented access, broken custody, over-collection, unsupported attribution, altered timestamps, evidence in chat or tickets, and conclusions stated more strongly than the artifacts support.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Quarantine questionable evidence, preserve all known state, document the break, obtain independent review, reacquire where lawful, and downgrade or withdraw affected conclusions. Reassess privacy, legal, safety, and user impact before disclosure or closure.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Authority, scope, preservation, custody, integrity, analysis, findings, limitations, owners, disclosure, retention, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending independent verification and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve case identity, custody, hashes, methods, findings, decisions, disclosure, legal or privacy holds, and open gaps. Hand off to Incident Response, Legal, Records, Security Operations, Audit, Governance, and affected owners.
