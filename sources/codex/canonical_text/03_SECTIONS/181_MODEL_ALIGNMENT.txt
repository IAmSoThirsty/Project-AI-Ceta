SECTION 181 — Model Alignment

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define governed work to shape model behavior toward authorized objectives, policies, values, user needs, safety constraints, and truthful uncertainty without claiming that training preferences establish universal morality or authority.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

- Explicit behavior objectives, prohibited behaviors, value conflicts, feedback sources, and decision authority.
- Evidence of helpfulness, honesty, refusal, robustness, bias, manipulation resistance, and human-control behavior.
- Transparent limitations and residual disagreement.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Alignment targets are choices made under incomplete and conflicting evidence. Rewarding apparent compliance can produce sycophancy, concealment, over-refusal, or proxy gaming rather than trustworthy behavior.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to instruction tuning, preference optimization, constitutional or policy training, refusal behavior, value shaping, reward modeling, and behavior modification. Depth increases with autonomy, power, user impact, or contested values.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

May be non-applicable to unchanged model use or technical training with no behavior, policy, preference, or objective change. Record boundary, authority, and reopening trigger.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Requires explicit use, policies, values, affected parties, evaluation, safety, governance, data rights, and independent review. Depends on Sections 179, 180, 182, 183, 186, and 188.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Behavior objective; policies; values; prohibited actions; feedback; preference data; reward or rubric; disagreement; threat model; target users; evaluation; refusal and appeal; and owner authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans define and approve values, policies, tradeoffs, protected groups, refusal boundaries, exceptions, and residual risk. No alignment artifact can authorize a decision or substitute for legitimate governance.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft rubrics, scenarios, critiques, and behavior tests. It must preserve disagreement and cannot claim moral correctness, fabricate feedback, or infer that reward means alignment.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Researcher, Analyst, Safety Auditor, Governance Reviewer, Domain Expert, Critic, Red Team, Tester, Verifier, Privacy Reviewer, Security Auditor, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, and Decision Arbiter Advisory.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. State behaviors, values, policies, prohibited use, and decision authority.
2. Audit preference and feedback sources, representation, incentives, and conflicts.
3. Define reward, rubric, refusal, uncertainty, and appeal contracts.
4. Train or tune with reproducible data, configuration, and checkpoints.
5. Evaluate helpfulness, honesty, refusal, robustness, manipulation, bias, and safety.
6. Review failure cases and value disagreement with independent humans.
7. Record residual uncertainty, acceptance, monitoring, and reopening triggers.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Expose policy, rubric, feedback provenance, model version, refusal reason, uncertainty, and review. Users must challenge outputs and appeal decisions. Alignment must not remove correction, explanation, human service, or safe refusal.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Version objectives, policies, rubrics, preference data, reward models, training runs, checkpoints, evaluations, prompts, and deployment. Preserve behavior provenance and prevent policy changes from silently invalidating prior claims.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show alignment target, policy version, feedback, disagreements, evaluations, refusals, incidents, and authority. Allow authorized narrow, pause, revoke, compare, retrain, withdraw, compensate, and retire actions.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Alignment charter; policy and value register; feedback and preference provenance; rubric; training record; behavior and red-team evaluation; disagreement log; decision record; limitations; monitoring and handoff package.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Claims identify behavior target, policy, feedback, model, training, scenario, evaluator, result, disagreement, uncertainty, and limitation. Preference, reward, or refusal rate does not prove universal alignment or safe action.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Test sycophancy, deception, reward hacking, jailbreak, manipulation, over-refusal, under-refusal, ambiguity, subgroup behavior, policy conflict, prompt injection, and correction. Verify independent human review and reproducible results.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Reward equals values; majority feedback equals legitimacy; policy hidden in prompts; disagreement erased; obedience rewarded over truth; safety benchmark overreach; and aligned behavior treated as authorization.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Suspend affected behavior on unsafe drift, policy conflict, feedback poisoning, evaluator failure, or rights impact. Preserve artifacts and dissent, use prior qualified behavior or human fallback, review impacts, and reopen after value or policy change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Targets, values, policies, data, training, evaluation, disagreements, limits, recovery, evidence, and authority are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending reconciliation and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve behavior targets, policies, feedback, checkpoints, evaluations, failures, decisions, dissent, incidents, and gaps. Hand off to Training, Evaluation, Safety, Governance, Prompt Engineering, MLOps, Model Operations, and HITL.
