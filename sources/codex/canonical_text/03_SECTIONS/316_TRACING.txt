SECTION 316 — Tracing

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Follow a request, transaction, workflow, job, or decision across components and boundaries with sufficient context to understand latency, failure, dependency, and user outcome.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A tracing model with trace and span identity, propagation, boundaries, timing, attributes, sampling, privacy, storage, correlation, access, cost, and diagnostic use.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Without cross-boundary context, teams guess at causality. Traces can also leak payloads, create high cost, or imply complete causality when sampling and instrumentation are partial.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when distributed requests, jobs, events, workflows, tool calls, or decisions cross components and timing or causality matters.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no distributed path, dependency, diagnostic, or evidence claim changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use observability, logging, metrics, privacy, security, runtime, integration, incident, and data-retention baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Trace, span, propagation, component, timing, attributes, sampling, payload policy, storage, access, retention, and diagnostic questions.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners define diagnostic questions and context; privacy and security owners approve attributes and access; operators interpret traces; humans decide disclosure and action.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze authorized traces, identify dependency patterns, draft queries, and summarize latency or failure. It must not expose payloads, infer causality beyond evidence, alter sampling, or act on a trace without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Tracing Engineer, Observability Engineer, SRE, Application Engineer, Network Engineer, Privacy Reviewer, Analyst, Tester, Verifier, and Incident Responder.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define path and boundaries; instrument; propagate identity; capture timing and context; sample and protect; correlate with logs and metrics; query; validate gaps; retain; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show trace, span, component, timing, attributes, sample, query, evidence, privacy, and limitation. Require human approval for payload access, sensitive query, sampling, retention, and response.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use context propagation, clock handling, stable IDs, bounded attributes, redaction, sampling, resilient collectors, trace-to-log and metric correlation, and access control.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show trace health, coverage, latency, spans, dependencies, sampling, privacy, cost, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized query, restrict, preserve, adjust, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Tracing charter; path and span model; propagation and sampling; privacy and access; correlation and query; incident, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify trace, span, component, clock, sample, attribute, method, time, result, actor, and limitation. A trace shows observed spans, not every unseen path or causal explanation.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify propagation, identity, timing, sampling, privacy, access, correlation, collector loss, clock skew, dropped span, and diagnostic usefulness. Test known failure path.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Payload leakage, broken propagation, clock misread, sample treated as complete, high-cardinality cost, unowned traces, and causal claims from correlation alone.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Restore collection, preserve available traces, restrict payload access, adjust sampling, notify owners, and reassess diagnostic confidence. Reopen on path, component, privacy, sampling, or incident change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Paths, spans, identity, timing, sampling, privacy, access, correlation, evidence, cost, owners, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve trace schemas, propagation, sampling, queries, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Observability, Logging, Metrics, Application, Network, Privacy, SRE, and Operations.
