SECTION 283 — Systems Administration

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Operate and maintain systems, accounts, services, endpoints, configurations, jobs, users, and records so they remain secure, available, supportable, and recoverable.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A systems-administration model with inventory, ownership, access, routine work, change, patch, monitoring, support, incident, backup, recovery, and retirement procedures.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Routine administration is where drift and privilege accumulate. Unrecorded commands, shared accounts, stale jobs, and workarounds can become permanent control failures.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when people or automation administer servers, endpoints, services, accounts, configurations, jobs, or infrastructure.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no administrative action, account, service, configuration, or operational record changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use IAM, server, network, storage, database, configuration, patch, secrets, operations, incident, backup, and records baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Assets, accounts, services, jobs, runbooks, permissions, changes, patches, alerts, logs, support, backups, recovery, and retirement requirements.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Administrators operate within assigned privilege and record changes; owners approve access, maintenance, exceptions, emergency work, and retirement. Humans review high-impact and destructive actions.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze authorized logs and inventory, draft runbooks, suggest diagnosis, and identify drift. It must not execute privileged commands, access secrets, disable controls, or alter systems without explicit delegated authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Systems Administrator, Operations Engineer, Security Auditor, Platform Engineer, Server Engineer, Network Engineer, Database Administrator, Storage Administrator, Tester, and Verifier.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Inventory and access; perform routine maintenance; change and patch; monitor; back up; troubleshoot; support users; respond to incidents; recover; review privilege; document; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show asset, account, command or action, privilege, target, evidence, impact, and limitation. Require human approval for privileged, destructive, production, data, and emergency actions.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use just-in-time privilege, session audit, runbooks, automation with guardrails, configuration management, out-of-band recovery, logging, and separation of administration from approval.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show administrative activity, assets, access, changes, alerts, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized approve, execute, revoke, isolate, recover, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Administration charter; asset and account catalog; runbooks; access and change records; patch and incident; backup and recovery; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify asset, administrator, account, privilege, action, command reference, time, method, result, and limitation. A command log proves activity, not authorization or correctness.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify access, logging, runbook, change, patch, backup, restore, alert, support, recovery, and retirement. Test least privilege, emergency, and failed automation.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Shared admin, undocumented manual fix, privilege never reviewed, secrets in commands, alert ignored, routine job without owner, and recovery dependent on the same account or system.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stop unsafe action, preserve logs, revoke or rotate access, restore known-good state, communicate, and reassess downstream impact. Reopen on asset, admin, policy, tool, or environment change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Assets, accounts, privileges, procedures, changes, evidence, recovery, owners, exceptions, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve runbooks, inventories, sessions, changes, incidents, backups, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Server, Network, Database, Storage, Security, Platform, Support, and Operations.
