SECTION 092 — VR Design

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define virtual-reality experiences and interactions that create an artificial spatial environment while preserving orientation, consent, accessibility, safety, privacy, and exit.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

 - A scoped user, virtual environment, device, locomotion, embodiment, task, content, safety, privacy, and lifecycle model.
 - VR interaction, comfort, accessibility, evidence, tests, fallback, and acceptance records.

WHY THIS MATTERS

VR can affect balance, perception, identity, motion comfort, and awareness of real surroundings. Immersion must never remove informed control or safe exit.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to headsets, room-scale systems, simulation, training, games, therapy, design review, social VR, and virtual workspaces.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No immersive, locomotion, embodiment, physical, privacy, accessibility, or user-control effect; or an unchanged approved VR baseline with impact recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

User and environment research, device limits, virtual world model, locomotion, content, physical safety, accessibility, privacy, identity, performance, and acceptance authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

User and environment research, device limits, virtual world model, locomotion, content, physical safety, accessibility, privacy, identity, performance, and acceptance authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve purpose, content, age or population bounds, comfort, consent, safety, privacy, accessibility, and acceptance. They retain immediate exit and physical supervision authority.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may generate worlds, avatars, and scenarios, but must disclose generation and uncertainty and must not manipulate, surveil, or create consequential effects without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

VR Designer, Spatial Designer, UX and Interaction Designers, Researcher, Accessibility and Safety specialists, Privacy Auditor, Tester, Verifier, Critic, Implementer, Governance Reviewer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define world, embodiment, locomotion, interaction, comfort, boundary, content, consent, privacy, accessibility, interruption, safe exit, and recovery; prototype and test representative users and devices.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show virtual and physical state, user identity, tracking, comfort, content source, consequence, privacy, and exit. AI-generated scenes remain proposed and supervision remains available.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Define coordinate and boundary systems, tracking, latency, locomotion, haptics, embodiment, data, identity, moderation, performance, comfort settings, and safe exit.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show session, user, device, world, tracking, comfort, boundaries, incidents, evidence, and controls to pause, recenter, mute, exit, supervise, or recover.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

VR concept, world and interaction model, comfort and safety rules, content and avatar provenance, privacy and moderation model, tests, evidence, decisions, and handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify user, device, world, version, locomotion, environment, method, result, discomfort or safety signal, and limitation. A demo is bounded evidence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify tracking, latency, comfort, motion sickness, boundaries, safe exit, accessibility, privacy, harassment or abuse controls, content provenance, and device variation.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

No exit; forced locomotion; hidden recording; deceptive embodiment; inaccessible supervision; blurred real-world hazards; and immersive content treated as harmless by default.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause and exit, preserve session evidence, provide physical supervision and alternate mode, disable harmful content, and review affected users before resuming.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Purpose, world, users, devices, comfort, safety, privacy, accessibility, evidence, tests, recovery, open risks, and acceptance are explicit. Remain CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve world, content, avatars, configurations, sessions, findings, incidents, decisions, evidence, and next action. Hand off to Spatial, AR, XR, HCI, Safety, Privacy, Accessibility, and Testing.
