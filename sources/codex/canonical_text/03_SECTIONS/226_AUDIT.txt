SECTION 226 — Audit

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Provide independent, evidence-based examination of governance, risk, controls, operations, records, and claims, with findings that owners can act on.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An audit scope, criteria, independence statement, evidence plan, sampling method, procedures, findings, management response, remediation, follow-up, and residual-risk record.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Audit can expose gaps between policy, design, operation, and outcome, but it loses value when scope is vague, evidence is curated, independence is compromised, or a clean report is treated as permanent assurance.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when independent assurance is needed for material controls, obligations, risks, financial or operational claims, safety, security, privacy, quality, or governance.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when the consequence and scope do not warrant independent audit and a documented review or test provides sufficient bounded evidence. Name the alternative, owner, date, and reopening condition.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Define objective, criteria, scope, period, independence, access, confidentiality, sampling, evidence retention, reporting, remediation authority, and follow-up owner.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Criteria, policies, controls, risks, systems, records, population, period, prior findings, incidents, owners, evidence, sampling frame, access, and acceptance expectations.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Audit leadership protects independence and approves scope, methods, findings, report, and follow-up. Owners provide truthful access and responses; management decides remediation and accepts residual risk without suppressing findings.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may organize evidence, compare criteria, sample authorized records, identify anomalies, draft workpapers, and summarize responses. It must preserve source, sample, period, method, independence, and uncertainty. It must not fabricate evidence, alter samples, issue an opinion, suppress a finding, or sign an attestation.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Auditor, Researcher, Analyst, Verifier, Governance Reviewer, Security Auditor, Privacy Reviewer, Critic, Documentation Writer, and Memory Curator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Set criteria and scope; confirm independence; plan sampling; obtain evidence; perform procedures; document exceptions; validate with owners; rate findings; report; agree remediation; follow up; retain workpapers; and reassess when scope changes.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show criteria, population, sample, source, period, procedure, result, limitation, reviewer, and independence. Require human audit judgment for findings, opinion, severity, management response, and closure. Protect confidential evidence.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Link criteria, controls, systems, records, evidence, samples, findings, responses, and remediation. Preserve read-only or immutable workpapers, segregation of duties, access audit, and reproducible sampling.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show audit scope, criteria, period, independence, progress, evidence coverage, findings, owner response, due dates, residual risk, and authorized assign, challenge, accept, remediate, escalate, and close controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Audit charter; criteria and scope; independence statement; evidence and sampling plan; workpapers; findings and report; responses; remediation and follow-up; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify criterion, population, sample, source, period, procedure, auditor, result, exception, confidence, and limitation. A policy, screenshot, or management statement is bounded evidence and may require corroboration.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify scope, criteria, independence, sample reproducibility, evidence integrity, finding traceability, management response, remediation, follow-up, and report approval. Test contradictory or missing evidence and late changes.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Audit by checklist, management-selected evidence only, scope drift, compromised independence, unsupported clean opinion, findings without owners, and closure without remediation evidence.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Preserve workpapers, disclose scope or evidence limitations, reopen affected findings, obtain independent review, and correct the report through controlled amendment. Reassess when systems, criteria, period, risks, or evidence change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Scope, criteria, independence, evidence, procedures, findings, responses, remediation, follow-up, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending independent audit approval and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve charter, workpapers, evidence references, findings, responses, reports, follow-up, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Controls, Compliance, Legal, Records, Security, Privacy, Quality, and Governance.
