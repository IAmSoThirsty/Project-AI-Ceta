SECTION 140 - Rendering Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define governed engineering of pipelines that transform scene, geometry, data, or content into pixels or other visual output under declared device, timing, color, accessibility, and correctness constraints.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A rendering baseline with pipeline stages, resource ownership, output contract, device support, performance, fallback, evidence, and recovery.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Rendering failures include wrong ordering, stale resources, color errors, dropped frames, leaks, and nonreproducible GPU behavior. Output must be judged beyond appearance.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to raster, vector, 3D, offline, real-time, server-side, browser, GPU, display, and visualization renderers.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

May be non-applicable where no renderer or generated visual output exists. Record scope, owner, evidence, and reopening trigger.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Requires output purpose, renderer and device APIs, scene and asset contracts, color, frame, memory, accessibility, security, test, build, and fallback requirements.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Provide pipeline, resources, inputs, output, resolution, color, timing, memory, device, failure, determinism, accessibility, and comparison requirements.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve output meaning, quality, accessibility, budgets, device support, and release. Humans decide whether degraded rendering is acceptable.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inspect frames, generate tests, and suggest optimization. It must not claim visual correctness from similarity alone or ship rendering changes without review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Architect, Graphics Engineer, Analyst, Implementer, Optimizer, Tester, Verifier, Accessibility specialist, Security Auditor, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, and Red Team. Existing role contracts remain controlling.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Define output and pipeline stages, resources, targets, and budgets.
2. Implement deterministic, instrumented paths with fallback and device-loss handling.
3. Verify pixels, semantics, accessibility, performance, compatibility, and recovery.
4. Release and retire pipeline versions with evidence.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show pipeline, stage, resource, device, output, evidence, and degraded state. Human approval is required for material visual or accessibility changes; status and software fallback remain available.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate scene submission, scheduling, resource, shader, raster, display, cache, and capture boundaries. Version output contracts and isolate GPU faults.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show renderer, frame timing, memory, queue, device, errors, output quality, accessibility, and controls to lower fidelity, switch path, capture, stop, or roll back.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Renderer contract, pipeline model, device matrix, resource budget, visual and accessibility tests, performance evidence, capture fixtures, build record, and fallback runbook.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify renderer, stage, device, build, scene, assets, settings, method, result, and limits. Preserve captures, metrics, accessibility findings, and device-specific failures.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify stage ordering, resource lifetime, shader errors, color, scaling, frame and memory budgets, device loss, malformed assets, accessibility, and fallback.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Testing one GPU; visual snapshots without semantics; leaking resources; blocking the UI; unsafe shader input; and treating average frame rate as absence of deadline misses.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Switch to safe renderer, lower workload, preserve captures and logs, quarantine assets, restore prior pipeline, compensate visible effects, and reopen performance and correctness claims.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Pipeline, output, budgets, evidence, compatibility, fallback, and lifecycle are explicit. Keep CONTENT-COMPLETE until independent verification and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve renderer and shader revisions, captures, device results, decisions, incidents, evidence, and next safe action. Hand off to Graphics, Game, Browser, Accessibility, Testing, Build, and Operations.
