SECTION 138 - Game Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define governed engineering of interactive games and game-like simulations, including deterministic or networked state, input, rendering, audio, progression, safety, accessibility, integrity, distribution, and live operation.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A game-engineering baseline with authoritative state, client behavior, content and asset provenance, performance, accessibility, privacy, anti-abuse, release, telemetry, recovery, and retirement rules.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Players experience the runtime directly, and small state, timing, network, or accessibility errors can destroy fairness, safety, trust, or continuity.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to games, interactive simulations, game services, engines, clients, servers, multiplayer systems, tools, and live content platforms.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

May be non-applicable to noninteractive media or a static prototype with no gameplay, state, input, player, network, or distribution claim. Record scope, owner, evidence, and reopening trigger.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Requires game design, platform targets, player populations, authoritative state model, content rights, privacy, accessibility, safety, networking, moderation, monetization, performance, release, and support authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Provide mechanics, inputs, state, physics, timing, content, assets, progression, network, accounts, moderation, accessibility, telemetry, monetization, performance, and recovery requirements.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve player safety, content, fairness, privacy, monetization, moderation, release, and acceptance. Humans retain authority over bans, purchases, resets, and live-service commitments.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may generate or test content, diagnose performance, and suggest balancing. It must preserve provenance, avoid unsafe or discriminatory content, and never alter live economy, bans, or player state without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Architect, Game Designer, User Experience Reviewer, Accessibility specialist, Security Auditor, Analyst, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Governance Reviewer, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, Coordinator, and Red Team. Existing role contracts remain controlling.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Define player, platform, game state, input, content, network, authority, and lifecycle.
2. Implement deterministic or authoritative behavior, accessible interaction, secure accounts, and recoverable persistence.
3. Test mechanics, performance, compatibility, networking, abuse, content, economy, and recovery.
4. Operate live changes with review, telemetry, rollback, support, and retirement.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show player, client, server, state, latency, content source, AI involvement, evidence, and consequence. Human approval is required for live content, moderation, purchases, and player-state mutation; status and appeal remain available.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate authoritative state, client prediction, rendering, input, physics, assets, accounts, economy, moderation, telemetry, and platform services. Define replay, rollback, anti-cheat, rate limits, and compensation for interrupted effects.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show build, server state, session, latency, errors, content version, economy, moderation, incidents, accessibility, and controls to pause events, restore state, compensate, or retire content.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Game-engineering charter, state and network model, platform matrix, content and asset provenance, accessibility and safety records, security and abuse model, test evidence, live-ops runbook, and retirement plan.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify build, client, server, platform, session, inputs, content, configuration, test method, result, and limits. Separate frame rate, network health, mechanic correctness, player outcome, safety, and engagement evidence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify input, state authority, save and restore, reconnect, prediction, cheating, moderation, accessibility, content, performance, purchases, economy, migration, and safe shutdown.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Client-authoritative rewards; inaccessible controls; pay-to-fail recovery; untested netcode; opaque bans; unproven generated content; and treating engagement or frame rate as player wellbeing or fairness.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause affected systems, preserve sessions and ledgers, restore authoritative state, compensate valid effects, appeal moderation, communicate impact, and reopen live-change approval.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

State, content, authority, safety, accessibility, evidence, recovery, live change, and retirement are explicit. Keep CONTENT-COMPLETE until independent verification and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve build, content, asset rights, state, sessions, decisions, evidence, incidents, and next safe action. Hand off to Game Design, Graphics, Audio, Network, Security, Testing, Operations, Payments, and Retirement.
