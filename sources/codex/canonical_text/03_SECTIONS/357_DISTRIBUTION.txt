SECTION 357 — Distribution

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Deliver products, devices, packages, content, data, or services to authorized customers, channels, locations, or systems with identity, condition, entitlement, evidence, and support.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A distribution model with item or artifact, audience, channel, inventory, entitlement, route, condition, receipt, support, withdrawal, and reconciliation.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Distribution turns inventory into customer or operational state. Incorrect version, quantity, audience, location, or condition can create financial, legal, safety, and trust consequences.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when goods, software, firmware, data, content, or services are delivered to a customer, partner, site, device, or downstream system.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no delivery, audience, item, entitlement, channel, receipt, or customer outcome changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use logistics, packaging, release, licensing, commerce, customer, inventory, support, security, and legal baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Item or artifact, version, quantity, customer, entitlement, channel, route, condition, inventory, receipt, support, and withdrawal.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Distribution owners approve audience, entitlement, channel, route, condition, customer commitment, exception, withdrawal, and claim. Humans resolve disputes.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may validate authorized order and inventory metadata, draft allocation, and identify anomalies. It must not release, change quantity, widen audience, expose data, or certify receipt without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Distribution Manager, Logistics Manager, Release Engineer, Commerce Analyst, Customer Support, Security Auditor, Analyst, Verifier, and Coordinator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Validate order and entitlement; allocate; package; route; distribute; track; confirm receipt and condition; support; withdraw or replace; reconcile inventory and finance; and record.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show item, version, customer, entitlement, channel, route, condition, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for sensitive, regulated, high-value, disputed, or irreversible distribution.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use order-to-delivery identity, entitlement, inventory, provenance, tamper or integrity, receipt, withdrawal, support, and reconciliation controls.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show distribution state, item, customer, entitlement, route, receipt, condition, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized hold, release, reroute, withdraw, replace, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Distribution charter; order and entitlement; item and route; receipt and condition; support and withdrawal; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify item, version, customer, entitlement, channel, route, method, time, receipt, condition, actor, and limitation. Shipment or download proves only delivery event.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify identity, quantity, version, entitlement, route, condition, receipt, support, withdrawal, refund or replacement, inventory, and financial reconciliation.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Wrong customer or version, entitlement bypass, receipt assumed, inventory drift, withdrawal impossible, condition unverified, and delivery metric treated as customer success.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Hold or withdraw, preserve evidence, notify recipients, replace or refund, reconcile inventory and finance, and reassess impact. Reopen on item, customer, channel, route, or entitlement change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Item, customer, entitlement, route, condition, receipt, support, evidence, owners, recovery, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve distribution, logistics, orders, receipts, support, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Commerce, Billing, Customer Support, Logistics, Release, and Operations.
