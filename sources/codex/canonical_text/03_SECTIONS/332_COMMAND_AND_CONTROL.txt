SECTION 332 — Command and Control

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define who or what may observe, decide, direct, authorize, pause, stop, escalate, recover, and retire consequential operations across normal and emergency modes.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A command-and-control model with authority, identity, scope, mode, decision rights, delegation, communication, status, override, evidence, audit, safety, and recovery.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Systems fail when authority is ambiguous, duplicated, unavailable, or silently transferred to automation. Command must be visible, bounded, revocable, and accountable under pressure.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when people, automation, AI, services, devices, or organizations can direct consequential operation, resource, safety, security, user, or recovery action.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability only when no consequential decision, control, delegation, mode, escalation, or emergency action exists and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use authority, identity, operational, incident, continuity, safety, security, service, change, communication, and governance baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Actors, roles, authority, resources, modes, decisions, delegation, escalation, communication, status, evidence, override, stop, and recovery.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Accountable humans define authority, delegation, limits, escalation, emergency, stop, communication, and acceptance. Humans remain responsible for protected outcomes and can revoke automation.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may surface state, summarize options, and recommend bounded action. It must identify its authority and never self-escalate, impersonate, hide dissent, execute consequential action, or block human override.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Coordinator, Incident Commander, Governance Reviewer, Architect, Operations Engineer, Security Auditor, Verifier, Planner, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Map authority and actors; define modes and decisions; bind identity; establish delegation and expiry; provide command channel; communicate; record; exercise stop and override; recover; audit; and review.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show actor, authority, scope, mode, evidence, recommendation, action, consequence, revocation, and limitation. Require human confirmation for protected, irreversible, emergency, safety, rights, and external actions.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate observe, advise, decide, authorize, execute, audit, and recover. Use least privilege, dual control, expiry, quorum where needed, independent stop, resilient communication, and immutable decision record.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show current commander, mode, authority, active delegations, decisions, evidence freshness, actions, conflicts, and authorized pause, stop, revoke, escalate, override, recover, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Command charter; authority and delegation matrix; mode and decision model; communication and escalation; stop and override; audit, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify actor, identity, authority, scope, mode, decision, source, time, action, result, reviewer, and limitation. Command presence proves neither legitimacy nor correctness without evidence and scope.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify identity, authority, delegation, expiry, conflict, escalation, stop, override, communication, audit, unavailable commander, automation failure, and recovery. Test unauthorized and ambiguous command.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Shared command, hidden delegation, unrevocable automation, no stop path, authority by chat alone, multiple conflicting commanders, emergency exception permanent, and status without decision record.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Freeze unsafe action, revoke authority, establish accountable commander, preserve records, communicate, restore safe mode, and reassess decisions. Reopen on authority, actor, mode, system, incident, or policy change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Actors, authority, modes, decisions, delegation, communication, stop, override, evidence, audit, recovery, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve authority matrix, delegations, command timeline, decisions, communications, incidents, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Operations, Incident, Continuity, Safety, Security, Governance, and Retirement.
