SECTION 335 — Help Desk

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Provide the accessible first point of contact for requests, incidents, access, devices, applications, information, and routing to specialized support.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A help-desk model with intake, identity, categorization, priority, knowledge, communication, escalation, service level, privacy, accessibility, and closure verification.

WHY THIS MATTERS

The help desk shapes the first interpretation of a problem and can either restore work or send users through unsafe loops. A closed ticket is not proof that the user can work.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when users need a central channel for IT, service, access, device, application, or operational assistance.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no central request, incident, access, routing, or user-support workflow changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use IT operations, service management, IAM, technical support, knowledge, privacy, security, accessibility, and incident baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

User, identity, request, asset, issue, evidence, category, priority, knowledge, service level, escalation, and resolution.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Help-desk owners authenticate, protect data, route, communicate, and verify outcome. Humans approve access changes, device action, sensitive support, and disputed closure.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may classify requests, suggest approved knowledge, and draft responses. It must not request secrets, grant access, wipe devices, expose records, or close high-impact work without human review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Help Desk Analyst, IT Operations Engineer, Technical Support Engineer, IAM Engineer, User Experience Reviewer, Privacy Reviewer, Analyst, Verifier, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Intake; authenticate; categorize; prioritize; use knowledge; resolve or route; communicate; escalate; verify; record; identify recurring problems; and improve.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show user, request, identity, asset, category, action, evidence, privacy, and limitation. Require human approval for access, device, security, data, and closure actions.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use secure case records, identity, knowledge, routing, status, accessibility, audit, least privilege, redaction, and escalation integration.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show queue, category, priority, owner, SLA, escalation, evidence freshness, and authorized assign, escalate, communicate, resolve, reopen, and close controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Help-desk charter; intake and taxonomy; knowledge and routing; service-level and escalation; resolution, feedback, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify user, request, asset, identity, source, method, time, action, result, and limitation. Ticket status proves only workflow state.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify authentication, privacy, accessibility, categorization, routing, response, escalation, resolution, reopen, and user confirmation. Test urgent and inaccessible-channel cases.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Password or secret collection, ticket closure theater, no priority, inaccessible channel, wrong routing, unowned escalation, and user blamed for system failure.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Protect user and data, reopen, escalate, correct, communicate, and reassess. Reopen on repeated issue, security concern, user dispute, or service change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Users, requests, identity, routing, priority, knowledge, privacy, accessibility, evidence, owners, escalation, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve tickets, routing, knowledge, escalations, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Customer Support, Technical Support, IT Operations, IAM, Security, and Service Management.
