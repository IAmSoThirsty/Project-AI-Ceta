SECTION 196 — Endpoint Security

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Protect user, operator, server, mobile, virtual, and specialized endpoints through identity, configuration, software, data, telemetry, response, and lifecycle controls.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

Endpoint inventory, ownership, posture, access, software, patching, encryption, isolation, monitoring, response, recovery, and disposal are explicit and verifiable.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Endpoints are where people and physical systems meet untrusted content and credentials. Lost, compromised, unmanaged, or shared devices can bypass architecture and expose sensitive work.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to workstations, servers, mobile devices, virtual desktops, kiosks, field devices, privileged consoles, and endpoint management systems.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Only an approved exact endpoint population, operating system, management boundary, configuration, user group, and lifecycle change can justify non-applicability.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Sections 032-040, 045-046, 193-195, 197-206, 258-286, 301-318, and 398-400 provide application, architecture, security, network, endpoint, identity, engineering, operations, and disposal context.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Endpoint inventory, owner, user, location, operating system, software, configuration, identity, data, management, patch, vulnerability, telemetry, incident, backup, and disposal records.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Endpoint owners authorize enrollment, access, monitoring, isolation, wipe, recovery, exceptions, and retirement while respecting privacy, labor, safety, and legal authority.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may assess posture and anomalies and propose containment. It cannot inspect beyond authorization, wipe or isolate without delegated authority, infer user intent, expose personal data, or close findings from a score.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Endpoint Engineer, Security Auditor, Identity Engineer, Systems Administrator, Privacy Reviewer, Analyst, Observer, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Red Team, Governance Reviewer, and Operations Engineer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Inventory and classify endpoints, users, data, management, and trust.
2. Define enrollment, hardening, patch, software, identity, encryption, isolation, monitoring, backup, and disposal controls.
3. Test lost device, malware, privilege, offline, stale policy, remote wipe, restore, and evidence paths.
4. Review posture, exceptions, user burden, and lifecycle evidence.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show endpoint state, owner, user, posture, evidence freshness, privacy boundary, acting authority, and available quarantine, revoke, restore, or retire actions. Keep manual support and recovery available.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use managed enrollment, least privilege, secure boot where applicable, application control, encrypted storage, network segmentation, tamper-evident telemetry, offline limits, and independent recovery.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show population, posture, software, patch, identity, anomalies, exposure, evidence, exceptions, and authorized isolate, wipe, restore, replace, or retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Endpoint catalog, baseline, management and identity matrix, software inventory, patch and vulnerability records, privacy assessment, tests, response and recovery runbooks, exceptions, and handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify endpoint population, version, user context, configuration, method, time, coverage, result, owner, and limitations. Enrollment or compliance status proves only the measured condition.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Exercise malware, lost device, stale policy, local privilege, removable media, offline operation, management loss, remote action, restore, replacement, and evidence preservation.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Unmanaged devices, shared accounts, excessive monitoring, permanent admin, unpatched software, unenforced encryption, remote wipe without authority, and treating management enrollment as security.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Quarantine or revoke affected endpoints, preserve evidence, support affected users, restore or replace from approved state, validate data and identity impact, and reopen when population, platform, policy, or threat changes.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Population, owners, controls, privacy, evidence, tests, recovery, exceptions, and disposal are explicit; status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending qualified review and acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve endpoint identity, user, posture, configuration, decisions, evidence, incidents, dissent, and next action. Hand off to Mobile, Embedded, Identity, Application, Network, Security Operations, Incident, Support, Recovery, and Disposal.
