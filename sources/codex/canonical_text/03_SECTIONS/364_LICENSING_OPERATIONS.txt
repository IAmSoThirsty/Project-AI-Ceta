SECTION 364 — Licensing Operations

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Operate licenses, entitlements, seats, terms, renewals, usage, restrictions, notices, compliance, and termination across products, software, data, content, and services.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A licensing-operations model with asset, license, owner, customer, entitlement, term, use, usage evidence, renewal, restriction, notice, audit, and exit.

WHY THIS MATTERS

License rights can expire, narrow, or depend on use and distribution. A license record or purchase does not prove current entitlement, compliant use, or correct customer access.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when licensed material or entitlement is activated, measured, assigned, renewed, transferred, audited, restricted, or retired.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no licensed asset, entitlement, use, term, renewal, distribution, or restriction changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use IP, legal, procurement, commerce, subscription, package, dependency, asset, finance, audit, and records baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Asset, license, licensor, customer, entitlement, seats, term, usage, restriction, notice, renewal, audit, transfer, and termination.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

License owners and counsel approve use, entitlement, distribution, renewal, exception, audit response, termination, and customer restriction. Humans resolve ambiguous terms.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inspect authorized license metadata and usage, identify mismatch, and draft renewal or remediation. It must not interpret ambiguous terms conclusively, revoke access, disclose licensed content, or approve use without human authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

License Manager, Legal Advisor, IP Analyst, Procurement Analyst, Commerce Manager, Auditor, Researcher, Verifier, and Governance Reviewer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Inventory licenses; assign owners; map assets and customers; provision entitlement; measure use; monitor term and restrictions; renew; remediate; audit; revoke or transfer; and terminate.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show asset, license, customer, term, entitlement, use, restriction, evidence, and limitation. Require human or counsel review for ambiguous, high-impact, renewal, audit, and termination decisions.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Link asset, license, customer, entitlement, usage, billing, distribution, access, notice, audit, and termination. Prevent license state from silently diverging from service access.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show licenses, assets, terms, entitlements, use, expiry, customers, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized assign, renew, restrict, transfer, audit, and terminate controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Licensing-operations charter; asset and license; entitlement and usage; renewal and restriction; audit, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify asset, license, licensor, customer, term, entitlement, usage, source, method, time, result, and limitation. Entitlement record proves only its scope and time.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify license, asset, customer, entitlement, term, usage, restriction, billing, notice, renewal, transfer, audit, revocation, and termination. Test expiry and excess use.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Expired license, entitlement mismatch, usage unmeasured, restriction hidden, audit evidence missing, rights assumed from purchase, and revocation without customer process.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Restrict or cure use, notify owners and customers, renew or replace, preserve evidence, correct billing and access, and reassess downstream artifacts. Reopen on license, asset, customer, term, or law change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Assets, licenses, owners, customers, entitlements, terms, usage, evidence, restrictions, renewal, recovery, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending qualified acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve licenses, entitlements, usage, renewals, audits, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to IP, Legal, Procurement, Commerce, Subscriptions, Billing, Distribution, and Records.
