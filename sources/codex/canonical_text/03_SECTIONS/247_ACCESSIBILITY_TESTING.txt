SECTION 247 — Accessibility Testing

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Verify that people with varied abilities, devices, input methods, languages, and assistive technologies can perceive, understand, navigate, operate, and recover through the declared experience.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An accessibility-test model with users, standards, journeys, assistive technology, environments, criteria, defects, accommodations, evidence, and owner decision.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Automated checks find some structural issues but cannot establish actual comprehension, operability, dignity, or recovery. Inaccessible design can deny access even when the feature is technically functional.

APPLICABILITY

Apply to user interfaces, documentation, devices, services, communication, support, physical environments, data presentations, and AI interactions intended for or affecting people.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability only when no person-facing or human-control surface changes and an approved exact baseline covers the scope. Internal or “temporary” tools may still require accessibility when people rely on them.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use user research, accessibility requirements, design, content, platform, localization, privacy, security, support, and legal baselines. Include disabled users or qualified specialists and assistive technology where relevant.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Users, abilities, journeys, content, controls, platform, browser or device, assistive technology, standards, language, data, errors, recovery, accommodations, and acceptance criteria.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners fund and prioritize accessibility; qualified reviewers and users evaluate real experience; humans accept or reject exceptions and provide accommodation and remediation. Disabled participants must not be treated as test data without care and consent.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inspect authorized markup, content, interaction traces, and automated results; draft test cases and alternatives; and identify likely barriers. It must not claim accessibility from static inspection, speak for disabled people, expose personal data, or approve an exception without human and user-informed review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Accessibility Reviewer, User Experience Reviewer, Researcher, Tester, Verifier, Content Designer, Analyst, Critic, Governance Reviewer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define users and criteria; test keyboard, touch, voice, screen reader, magnification, contrast, motion, timing, language, cognitive load, error, support, and recovery; include disabled users where appropriate; record barriers; remediate; retest; and preserve limitations.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show user, assistive technology, platform, journey, content, method, result, and limitation. Require human review and lived-experience input for acceptance. Provide accessible owner and appeal controls.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Support semantic structure, keyboard and alternative input, focus, captions, text alternatives, reflow, contrast, timing control, error recovery, accessible authentication, localization, and non-visual owner control.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show accessibility scope, users, defects, severity, platform coverage, evidence freshness, accommodations, owner, and authorized prioritize, remediate, accept, provide alternative, and reopen controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Accessibility charter; user and technology matrix; journey and criteria; manual and automated results; participant and accommodation record; defect, exception, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify user or specialist, platform, assistive technology, version, journey, method, result, barrier, impact, and limitation. An automated scan or conformance claim proves only its rule set and scope.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify keyboard, non-visual, touch, voice, magnification, contrast, captions, semantics, timing, errors, forms, authentication, language, support, and recovery. Reproduce with representative technology and users.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Accessibility by scan only, disabled people consulted too late, inaccessible test or owner controls, color-only meaning, time limits without extension, and exception accepted without alternative or expiry.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Provide accessible alternative or support, preserve user impact, remediate, and retest with affected users. Reopen on content, platform, assistive technology, interaction, policy, or user-population change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Users, platforms, technologies, journeys, criteria, findings, accommodations, evidence, owners, exceptions, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending user-informed review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve criteria, participant care, platform results, defects, accommodations, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to UX, Engineering, Content, Localization, Support, Legal, Testing, and Operations.
