SECTION 337 — Community Management

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Cultivate and govern a community around a product, domain, mission, or practice through participation, norms, safety, support, learning, feedback, and accountable stewardship.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A community model with purpose, members, norms, moderation, accessibility, safety, privacy, participation, feedback, escalation, events, evidence, and continuity.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Communities create knowledge and trust but can also concentrate harm, silence dissent, expose people, or burden unpaid contributors. Activity is not belonging, health, or safety.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when people interact through a shared community, forum, event, network, repository, program, or practice with organizational stewardship.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no community, member, norm, moderation, safety, participation, or feedback behavior changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use trust and safety, privacy, accessibility, policy, user research, support, events, legal, ethics, and governance baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Purpose, members, norms, channels, moderators, reports, safety, accessibility, privacy, events, feedback, disputes, evidence, and escalation.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Community owners set purpose, norms, moderation, safety, appeals, privacy, and stewardship. Humans review high-impact enforcement, vulnerable-person concerns, and disputes.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may summarize authorized discussion, identify themes, draft moderation queues, and suggest resources. It must not expose members, infer intent, remove speech, punish, or represent community consensus without human review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Community Manager, Trust and Safety Reviewer, User Researcher, Moderator, Privacy Reviewer, Accessibility Reviewer, Analyst, Verifier, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define purpose and norms; onboard; moderate; protect privacy and safety; support accessibility; host events; gather feedback; handle conflict; appeal; measure health; and revise stewardship.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show member, context, norm, report, evidence, action, privacy, and limitation. Require human moderation for removal, sanctions, disclosure, and appeals. Do not treat volume or sentiment as consensus.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Provide reporting, moderation, evidence, privacy, identity, role, appeal, accessibility, status, and safe event controls. Protect volunteer and vulnerable participants.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show community health, reports, moderation, accessibility, participation, incidents, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized protect, restrict, appeal, communicate, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Community charter; membership and norms; moderation and appeal; safety and accessibility; event and feedback; decision, evidence, incident, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify member or segment, context, report, source, method, time, action, result, impact, and limitation. Activity, sentiment, or report count proves only a bounded signal.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify onboarding, norms, reporting, moderation, privacy, accessibility, appeal, events, conflict, safety, support, and member understanding. Test abuse and moderator failure.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Engagement as health, opaque moderation, reporter exposure, no appeal, volunteer exploitation, token participation, consensus theater, and safety policy without enforcement.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Protect members, preserve evidence, pause harmful behavior, reverse unjust action, provide support, communicate, and reassess norms or moderation. Reopen on harm, population, platform, policy, or event change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Purpose, members, norms, safety, privacy, accessibility, moderation, appeal, evidence, owners, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending community and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve charter, reports, moderation, appeals, events, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Trust and Safety, Support, Education, Product, Legal, Privacy, and Governance.
