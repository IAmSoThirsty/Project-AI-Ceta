SECTION 320 — Problem Management

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Identify and remove systemic causes of incidents, defects, risk, toil, and recurring service failure while preserving safe workarounds and evidence.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A problem model with symptoms, incidents, cause hypotheses, evidence, known error, workaround, corrective action, owner, risk, verification, and prevention.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Closing incidents restores service but may leave the cause. Root-cause language can also overclaim when evidence is incomplete or blame is substituted for system learning.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when incidents, defects, vulnerabilities, recurring workarounds, or systemic risk warrant investigation beyond immediate restoration.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no recurring or systemic issue exists and an incident closure is sufficient for the bounded event.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use incident, change, architecture, quality, vulnerability, reliability, data, support, and evidence baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Incidents, symptoms, timeline, systems, changes, dependencies, evidence, hypotheses, workarounds, risks, owners, and corrective-action criteria.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Problem owners coordinate investigation and action; domain experts challenge hypotheses; owners approve risk, workaround, change, and closure. Humans avoid assigning causal certainty without evidence.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may correlate authorized incidents, draft hypotheses, identify patterns, and suggest experiments. It must distinguish correlation from cause, preserve uncertainty, and not change systems or close problems without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Problem Manager, Incident Responder, Analyst, Researcher, Architect, SRE, Tester, Verifier, Critic, Governance Reviewer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Link incidents; define symptom and impact; preserve evidence; investigate hypotheses; establish workaround or known error; design corrective action; change safely; verify; communicate; and capture prevention.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show symptom, evidence, hypothesis, confidence, impact, workaround, action, owner, and limitation. Require human review for cause, risk, change, and closure.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Link incident, change, dependency, configuration, architecture, test, evidence, and corrective action. Support reproducible diagnosis, safe experiments, rollback, and knowledge retention.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show problem, linked incidents, cause status, evidence, workaround, owner, corrective action, risk, due date, and authorized assign, investigate, mitigate, remediate, reopen, and close controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Problem record; incident links; hypothesis and evidence; known error and workaround; corrective action; verification, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify symptom, incident, system, change, source, method, time, hypothesis, result, confidence, and limitation. A plausible narrative is not causal proof.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify evidence, reproducibility, workaround, corrective action, regression, user impact, change, monitoring, and recurrence. Independent challenge causal conclusion where material.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Root cause by assertion, blame, workaround as fix, problem backlog without owner, incident closure copied as solution, and corrective action without recurrence evidence.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Use safe workaround, preserve evidence, protect users, implement or roll back corrective change, and reassess recurrence. Reopen on repeat incident, new evidence, invalid cause, or changed system.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Symptom, impact, evidence, hypothesis, workaround, corrective action, owner, verification, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve problem, incidents, evidence, workarounds, changes, decisions, corrective action, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Incident, Change, Architecture, Quality, SRE, Security, and Operations.
