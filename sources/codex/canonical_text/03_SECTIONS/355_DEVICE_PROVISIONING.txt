SECTION 355 — Device Provisioning

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Initialize devices with identity, firmware, configuration, credentials, ownership, policy, data, connectivity, and support so they enter service in a known and recoverable state.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A device-provisioning model with asset identity, hardware and firmware, keys, owner, configuration, enrollment, security, data, validation, handoff, update, wipe, and retirement.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Provisioning establishes device trust and ownership. Shared credentials, wrong configuration, incomplete enrollment, exposed keys, or untracked handoff can compromise an entire fleet.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a device, embedded system, endpoint, gateway, or hardware unit is prepared, enrolled, assigned, re-provisioned, transferred, or recovered.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no device identity, configuration, ownership, credential, firmware, enrollment, or lifecycle behavior changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use hardware, embedded security, IAM, keys, secrets, configuration, manufacturing, inventory, update, privacy, support, and disposal baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Device, hardware, firmware, identity, keys, owner, configuration, policy, network, data, enrollment, validation, support, wipe, and retirement.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Device owners approve identity, assignment, policy, data, key, enrollment, exception, transfer, wipe, recovery, and retirement. Humans verify chain of custody and safety.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inspect authorized provisioning metadata and draft checklists or validation. It must not generate or expose keys, assign ownership, enroll, configure, wipe, or declare trust without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Device Provisioning Engineer, Embedded Engineer, IAM Engineer, Key Manager, Security Auditor, Manufacturing Engineer, Tester, Verifier, and Operations Engineer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Verify asset; provision identity and keys; install firmware; configure; enroll; validate; record owner and chain; hand off; update; recover; transfer; wipe; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show device, hardware, firmware, identity reference, owner, configuration, action, evidence, and limitation without exposing secret material. Require human approval for key, assignment, wipe, and retirement.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use secure provisioning station, unique identity, protected keys, signed firmware, least privilege, inventory, policy, enrollment, audit, recovery, and secure handoff.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show device identity, owner, version, posture, configuration, enrollment, evidence freshness, and authorized assign, restrict, update, recover, transfer, wipe, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Provisioning charter; device and chain-of-custody; identity and key metadata; firmware and configuration; validation, handoff, recovery, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify device, hardware, firmware, identity reference, station, operator, owner, method, time, result, and limitation. Enrollment proves only the recorded action, not complete trust.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify identity, keys, firmware, configuration, ownership, enrollment, network, security, privacy, support, update, recovery, transfer, wipe, and inventory.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Shared identity, key exposure, wrong owner, firmware mismatch, enrollment without validation, chain break, wipe without scope, and device status treated as security.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Quarantine, preserve evidence, revoke identity, rotate keys, restore known-good firmware, re-provision, notify owner, and reassess fleet or user impact. Reopen on hardware, firmware, owner, policy, or threat change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Device, owner, identity, firmware, keys, configuration, enrollment, validation, evidence, recovery, wipe, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending qualified acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve device metadata, provisioning, ownership, keys references, firmware, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Manufacturing, Embedded, Security, Mobile, Operations, Support, and Disposal.
