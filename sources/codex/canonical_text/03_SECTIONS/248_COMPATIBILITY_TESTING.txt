SECTION 248 — Compatibility Testing

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Verify that a capability behaves acceptably across supported versions, platforms, devices, browsers, providers, configurations, interfaces, data formats, and neighboring systems.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A compatibility matrix with supported combinations, assumptions, contracts, prioritized coverage, results, known incompatibilities, migration or fallback, and owner decision.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Compatibility failures are often conditional on version, configuration, locale, hardware, provider, or data. A successful test on one combination cannot justify a universal support claim.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when users, systems, devices, providers, formats, environments, or versions must interoperate or remain supported across change.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no supported combination, interface, version, or consumer behavior changes and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use support policy, architecture, contracts, version management, user population, platform, localization, data, release, and operations baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Supported matrix, versions, configurations, devices, providers, interfaces, data formats, users, priorities, known limits, test environments, and acceptance criteria.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners define support and deprecation; testers prioritize combinations; domain and support owners judge user impact. Humans approve unsupported behavior, migration, fallback, and communication.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze compatibility metadata, propose matrix coverage, generate cases, and summarize failures. It must not infer support from similarity, silently drop combinations, or make compatibility commitments without human review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Compatibility Tester, Architect, Release Engineer, Researcher, Analyst, Tester, Verifier, User Experience Reviewer, Support Reviewer, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define support; inventory combinations; prioritize risk and population; prepare environments and data; execute functional, interface, performance, accessibility, security, and migration cases; record incompatibility; provide fallback or communication; retest.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show combination, support status, version, configuration, user, data, method, result, and limitation. Require human approval for support, deprecation, fallback, and customer communication.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use explicit compatibility contracts, feature detection, version negotiation, migration, graceful fallback, observability, configuration capture, and support boundaries. Avoid silent behavior changes.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show matrix, tested combinations, failures, support status, evidence freshness, owner, fallback, deprecation, and authorized support, restrict, migrate, accept, and reopen controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Compatibility matrix; support and deprecation policy; environment record; results and incompatibility register; migration and fallback plan; decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify combination, version, configuration, device or provider, data, user, method, result, time, and limitation. A passing combination proves only that combination.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify prioritized coverage, interfaces, data, performance, security, accessibility, migration, fallback, documentation, and support. Reproduce material incompatibilities and confirm boundaries.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Support by assumption, matrix explosion without prioritization, silent drop of old users, provider behavior assumed identical, fallback untested, and compatibility claim from one platform.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Restrict unsupported combinations, provide fallback or migration, communicate, preserve failure evidence, and reassess user impact. Reopen on platform, provider, version, data, or support change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Support, combinations, cases, results, incompatibilities, fallback, owners, evidence, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve matrix and versions, results, support decisions, migrations, exceptions, evidence, and open gaps. Hand off to Release, Version Management, Deprecation, Support, Operations, UX, and Testing.
