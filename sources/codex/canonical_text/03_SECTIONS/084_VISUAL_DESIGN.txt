SECTION 084 — Visual Design

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define visual language, composition, typography, color, imagery, iconography, motion cues, and hierarchy in service of comprehension, identity, accessibility, and authorized outcomes.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

 - A scoped visual system with audience, medium, content, brand, accessibility, cultural, technical, and lifecycle constraints.
 - Reusable visual rules, assets, specifications, evidence, exceptions, and acceptance criteria.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Visual choices shape attention, interpretation, trust, and access. Aesthetic consistency that obscures status, hierarchy, or difference can create operational and exclusionary harm.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to digital interfaces, products, services, environments, campaigns, data displays, identities, and visual systems with material interpretation or access effects.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No material visual meaning, hierarchy, accessibility, identity, content, or interaction effect; or an unchanged approved visual baseline with impact recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Brand and domain rules, UI and UX models, content, audience research, accessibility, localization, device and medium constraints, legal rights, asset provenance, and acceptance authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Brand and domain rules, UI and UX models, content, audience research, accessibility, localization, device and medium constraints, legal rights, asset provenance, and acceptance authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve meaning, audience, representation, cultural and accessibility obligations, rights, identity, risk, and acceptance. They resolve conflicts and retain publication and correction authority.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may propose visual alternatives, generate drafts, and inspect consistency. It must disclose source and license limits, preserve accessibility and representation context, and not publish assets or infer taste as fact.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Visual Designer, UI Designer, UX Designer, Graphic Designer, Researcher, User Experience Reviewer, Accessibility specialist, Content Designer, Critic, Governance Reviewer, Tester, Verifier, Implementer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define visual principles, hierarchy, type, color, imagery, iconography, spacing, data display, responsive behavior, accessibility, localization, asset rights, variants, and usage rules; prototype and validate.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show hierarchy, status, contrast, actor, automation, consequence, accessibility, source, and alternative representation. AI-generated assets remain proposals and must not obscure controls or provenance.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use tokens, semantic color, readable typography, contrast, nonvisual cues, responsive constraints, asset metadata, localization, performance, and content integrity. Visual styling must not substitute for authorization or status truth.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show visual baseline, medium, audience, asset provenance, accessibility, content, contrast, open findings, and controls to approve, replace, annotate, withdraw, or restore.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Visual system and style guide, tokens, type and color rules, asset catalog and rights, compositions, accessibility annotations, responsive specifications, test evidence, decisions, and handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify asset, source, license, audience, medium, version, method, rendering context, result, and limitation. A mockup or brand review proves bounded appearance, not comprehension or access.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify contrast, scaling, reflow, non-color cues, typography, localization, content fit, asset rights, assistive use, low bandwidth, device variation, and implementation fidelity.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Color-only status; inaccessible type; decorative hierarchy that misleads; unlicensed assets; cultural stereotypes; brand rules over user need; and visual consistency treated as comprehension.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Withdraw harmful or infringing assets, preserve provenance and prior version, provide accessible alternate representation, notify owners, and retest. Reopen when audience, medium, brand, content, rights, or standards change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Visual purpose, audience, assets, rules, accessibility, provenance, evidence, tests, recovery, exceptions, open risks, and acceptance are explicit. Remain CONTENT-COMPLETE pending reconciliation, hostile review, and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve rules, tokens, assets, licenses, versions, decisions, findings, evidence, exceptions, and next action. Hand off to UI, Graphic, Motion, Content, Design Systems, Accessibility, Localization, and Frontend.
