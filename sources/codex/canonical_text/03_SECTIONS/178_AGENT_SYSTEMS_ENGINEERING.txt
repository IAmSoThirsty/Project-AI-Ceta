SECTION 178 — Agent Systems Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define engineering of systems that perceive context, plan, reason, call tools, coordinate, learn, or act toward goals while preserving explicit identity, authority, state, human oversight, evidence, and compensation.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

- A bounded agent contract for goal, capabilities, tools, data, permissions, state, planning, action, review, and termination.
- Reproducible evaluation of planning, tool use, delegation, refusal, recovery, security, safety, and user control.
- Immutable decisions and append-only evidence for consequential action.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Agents combine model uncertainty with state, tools, and external effects. A persuasive plan or completed tool call is not authorization, correctness, or safe recovery.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to autonomous, semi-autonomous, tool-using, multi-agent, workflow, planning, and long-running agents. Depth increases with external mutation, delegation, sensitive data, autonomy, persistence, or multi-party impact.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

May be non-applicable to stateless generation with no tool, state, delegation, or external effect. Record boundary, owner, evidence date, and reopening trigger.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Requires requirements, process, identity, authorization, data, tool contracts, policy evaluation, observability, evaluation, safety, security, and HITL authority. Depends on Sections 169, 170, 180-183, 185, 186, and 188.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Goal; user; agent identity; capability token; tools; data; policies; state; memory; delegation; approval; mutation class; stop conditions; trace; evaluation; compensation; and retirement rules.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans define goal, scope, capability, approval, protected outcomes, exceptions, risk, and final decisions. They may stop, revoke, narrow, compensate, or retire. Agents never derive authority from a goal or model confidence.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

Agents may plan, recommend, and invoke authorized tools within a complete governance binding. They must verify state and scope, expose uncertainty, and stop on expired capability, policy mismatch, missing quorum, or unresolved ambiguity.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Architect, Implementer, Planner, Coordinator, Governance Reviewer, Security Auditor, Safety Auditor, Researcher, Analyst, Tester, Verifier, Critic, Red Team, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, and Human-in-the-Loop Operator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Define goal, actor, resources, authority, mutation class, and prohibited action.
2. Model planning, state, memory, tools, delegation, approval, and termination.
3. Bind each mutation to trace, policy evaluation, capability, decision, and audit records.
4. Test prompt injection, tool misuse, delegation, replay, concurrency, failure, and recovery.
5. Validate human cockpit, stop, revoke, appeal, explanation, and compensation.
6. Monitor traces, policy outcomes, costs, drift, and incidents.
7. Baseline, hand off, and retire without deleting prior decisions.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show goal, plan, state, identity, capability, policy result, tool, data, mutation, decision, and trace. Require approval or quorum when applicable. Preserve terminal stream semantics, visible stop, correction, appeal, and compensation paths.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Represent RunRequest, ToolInvocationRequest, PolicyEvaluationRequest and Result, GovernanceEvaluationBinding, MutationGovernanceBinding, DecisionRecord, AuditDecision, trace, artifact, and compensation links as immutable or append-only records. Enforce default-deny and expiry at tool boundaries.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show active runs, goal, state, plan, pending actions, authority, capability scope, policy, decisions, traces, artifacts, cost, and incidents. Provide authorized pause, deny, revoke, quarantine, compensate, resume, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Agent charter; capability and tool catalog; state and delegation model; policy and governance binding; decision and audit records; evaluation suite; trace and artifact package; runbook; incident and compensation plan; and handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Every consequential action identifies trace, request, agent identity, capability, policy result, decision record, tool, resource, input, output, reviewer, and result. A plan, tool response, or successful run is not proof of authorization or outcome.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Expire or revoke capability; alter policy; remove quorum; inject conflicting instructions; replay requests; duplicate tools; corrupt state; lose provider; interrupt stream; and verify default-deny, audit, terminal event, compensation, and recovery.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Goal treated as permission; prompt as policy; missing capability binding; local-only policy check; hidden delegation; unbounded loops; silent tool mutation; lost terminal event; and destructive rollback.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stop affected execution on authority, trace, policy, state, tool, or safety failure. Preserve immutable records and artifacts, quarantine outputs, compensate only through linked transactions, review impact, and resume only after fresh evaluation and authority.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Goal, boundary, identity, capabilities, policy, state, tools, decisions, audit, trace, evaluation, stop, compensation, and owner authority are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending reconciliation and acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve run IDs, decisions, bindings, traces, tool calls, artifacts, state, incidents, dissent, and gaps. Hand off to AI Engineering, Governance, Safety, Security, MLOps, Model Operations, Prompt Engineering, and HITL Operations.
