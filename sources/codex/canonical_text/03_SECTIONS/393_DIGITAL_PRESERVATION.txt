SECTION 393 — Digital Preservation

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Preserve digital records, data, artifacts, evidence, context, provenance, integrity, accessibility, and meaning for as long as an authorized purpose requires.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A preservation model with selection, authority, metadata, fixity, format, context, storage, access, migration, privacy, hold, authenticity, review, and disposition.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Digital material can become unreadable, unverifiable, inaccessible, or meaningless even when bits remain. Preservation differs from backup: it protects future interpretation and trust as well as recovery.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when digital records, evidence, cultural or organizational memory, research, code, data, artifacts, or decisions must remain usable and trustworthy over time.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no long-term record, evidence, memory, authenticity, or future-use obligation exists and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use records, retention, archive, legal, privacy, security, storage, format, evidence, IP, and lifecycle baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Material, selection reason, authority, metadata, format, provenance, fixity, storage, access, privacy, migration, review, and disposition.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Archivists, records, legal, privacy, data, and domain owners approve selection, authenticity, access, migration, hold, retention, and disposition. Humans resolve cultural and evidentiary meaning.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inspect authorized metadata, identify format or integrity risk, draft descriptive metadata, and propose migration. It must not alter originals, infer authenticity, expose restricted material, or dispose of preserved content without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Digital Archivist, Records Manager, Data Engineer, Researcher, Legal Advisor, Privacy Reviewer, Security Auditor, Verifier, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Select and appraise; capture metadata and provenance; fixity-check; package; store; control access; monitor format and medium; migrate or emulate; validate; apply hold; and dispose when authorized.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show item, provenance, authority, format, fixity, access, action, evidence, and limitation. Require human review for authenticity, migration, disclosure, hold, and disposition.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use durable formats, checksums, preservation metadata, redundant storage, catalog, access control, encryption, migration, emulation, audit, and independence from operational systems.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show item, class, fixity, format, age, access, hold, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized retrieve, migrate, preserve, restrict, annotate, and dispose controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Preservation charter; selection and authority; package and metadata; fixity and format; migration and access; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify item, source, format, provenance, fixity, metadata, authority, method, time, result, owner, and limitation. Bit preservation proves neither readability nor meaning.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify fixity, metadata, provenance, format, access, retrieval, migration, privacy, hold, integrity, context, and disaster recovery. Retrieve and interpret representative material.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Bits without context, format obsolescence, fixity absent, archive inaccessible, preservation confused with backup, originals altered, and indefinite preservation without authority.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Restore alternate copy, recover metadata, migrate or emulate, restrict access, preserve evidence, notify owners, and reassess authenticity and rights. Reopen on format, medium, law, purpose, or integrity change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Selection, authority, provenance, metadata, fixity, format, access, migration, privacy, evidence, owners, disposition, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending qualified acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve preservation packages, catalogs, fixity, migrations, access, holds, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Archive, Records, Retention, Legal, Privacy, Storage, and Governance.
