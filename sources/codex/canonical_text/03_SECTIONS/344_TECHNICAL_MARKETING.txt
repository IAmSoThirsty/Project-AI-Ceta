SECTION 344 — Technical Marketing

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Explain technical capability, architecture, integration, performance, security, and operational value accurately to technical audiences and decision makers.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A technical-marketing model with audience, use case, architecture, claims, evidence, limitations, proof, content, review, enablement, and correction.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Technical audiences act on specifics. Vague or inflated claims about compatibility, security, scale, AI, or operations can cause unsafe design and unrealistic commitments.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when technical features, architecture, benchmarks, security, integration, deployment, or operational claims are communicated externally or internally.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no technical claim, audience, artifact, comparison, or customer expectation changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use architecture, performance, security, testing, product, legal, IP, support, documentation, and release baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Audience, use case, architecture, version, benchmark, security, compatibility, limits, source, content, reviewer, and correction path.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Technical and product owners verify claims; legal reviews required content; marketing communicates within approved boundaries. Humans approve benchmark, security, AI, and comparison claims.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft technical content from authorized sources and summarize evidence. It must not fabricate benchmark, customer, security, compatibility, or architecture claims or publish without qualified review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Technical Marketer, Architect, Product Manager, Engineer, Researcher, Analyst, Legal Advisor, Verifier, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define audience and use case; gather current technical evidence; draft; review with engineers and users; validate examples and benchmarks; publish; enable sales and support; monitor correction; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show claim, version, source, method, environment, audience, limitation, reviewer, and uncertainty. Require human technical, legal, security, and accessibility review.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Link claims to actual architecture, configurations, tests, artifacts, support, and version. Preserve benchmark conditions, terms, diagrams, and correction history.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show technical claim, source, version, evidence freshness, owner, risk, content, feedback, and authorized approve, correct, qualify, withdraw, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Technical-marketing charter; audience and claim register; evidence and benchmark; content and review; enablement, correction, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify claim, version, environment, method, source, result, date, reviewer, and limitation. Benchmark or diagram proves only its declared conditions.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify technical accuracy, version, benchmark, security, compatibility, architecture, operations, accessibility, legal, support, and correction. Test content against actual behavior.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Benchmark without workload, security badge as proof, architecture diagram as runtime truth, AI-generated proof, stale content, and technical detail used to hide limitations.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Correct, qualify, or withdraw content, notify users and teams, preserve evidence, and reassess commitments. Reopen on version, benchmark, architecture, threat, or audience change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Audience, claims, versions, sources, methods, limitations, reviewers, evidence, content, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending technical and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve claims, benchmarks, diagrams, sources, approvals, versions, corrections, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Product Marketing, Sales, Solutions, Documentation, Support, Legal, and Engineering.
