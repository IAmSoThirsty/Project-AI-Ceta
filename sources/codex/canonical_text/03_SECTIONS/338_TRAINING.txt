SECTION 338 — Training

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Develop and verify the knowledge, skills, judgment, practice, accessibility, and support people need to perform a defined role or use a capability safely and effectively.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A training model with audience, objectives, prerequisites, content, practice, assessment, accessibility, records, refresh, support, transfer, and outcome evidence.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Completion does not prove competence or safe performance. Training can also create harm when it teaches outdated, inaccessible, unsafe, or context-free procedures.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when people need structured learning to perform, operate, support, govern, or recover a system, process, role, or capability.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no skill, role, safety, compliance, operational, or user outcome changes and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use user research, role, requirements, safety, security, privacy, accessibility, documentation, operations, and legal baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Audience, role, objectives, prerequisites, content, practice, assessment, environment, accessibility, instructor, records, refresh, and success measure.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Training owners define competence and safety; subject experts validate content; humans assess learners and approve records. Learners can ask, fail safely, and request accommodation.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft exercises, explain approved material, personalize practice, and summarize feedback. It must not certify competence, invent policy, expose learner data, or replace instructor or assessor judgment.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Training Designer, Teacher, Subject Matter Expert, User Experience Reviewer, Accessibility Reviewer, Researcher, Tester, Verifier, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define outcome and audience; assess prerequisite; design content and practice; deliver accessibly; assess; record; support; refresh; measure transfer; and retire obsolete training.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show objective, source, learner, exercise, assessment, evidence, privacy, and limitation. Require human review for safety, policy, certification, and accommodation decisions.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Provide versioned content, sandbox practice, safe failure, identity and records, accessibility, privacy, assessment integrity, and support for offline or alternate formats.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show course, learner group, objective, completion, assessment, accessibility, content version, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized revise, accommodate, recertify, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Training charter; audience and competency; content and practice; assessment and accommodation; completion and transfer; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify learner or group, objective, content version, activity, assessment, method, time, result, assessor, and limitation. Completion proves participation, not competence or transfer.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify content accuracy, accessibility, practice, assessment, safety, privacy, records, learner understanding, transfer, refresh, and support. Test outdated content and accommodation.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Completion as competence, inaccessible course, outdated content, unsafe practice, assessment leakage, no refresh, and training used to transfer system responsibility to users.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Correct content, notify learners, suspend unsafe certification, provide support, reassess competence, and update controls. Reopen on role, policy, system, safety, or content change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Audience, objectives, content, practice, assessment, accessibility, evidence, owners, refresh, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending subject-matter and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve content versions, assessments, accommodations, records, feedback, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Education, Documentation, Support, Operations, Security, and Governance.
