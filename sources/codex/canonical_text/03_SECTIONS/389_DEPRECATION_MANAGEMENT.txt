SECTION 389 — Deprecation Management

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Announce, support, monitor, migrate, restrict, and retire a version, feature, interface, dependency, policy, product, or service that will no longer be preferred or supported.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A deprecation model with reason, scope, replacement, consumer, notice, support window, compatibility, migration, security, exception, deadline, and retirement.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Deprecation is a promise about future support. Without notice, replacement, migration, and owner, it creates stranded users and hidden operational risk; indefinite deprecation creates debt.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a capability, version, interface, asset, provider, policy, or service is marked for reduced support or eventual removal.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no support, consumer, interface, version, or lifecycle status changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use version, compatibility, release, customer, support, legal, security, data, migration, records, and sunset baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Target, reason, owner, consumers, replacement, date, support, notice, migration, exception, evidence, and retirement criteria.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners approve reason, notice, support, migration, exception, date, security, customer impact, and retirement. Humans resolve contractual or vulnerable-user impact.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inventory consumers, draft notices and migration, and identify stale use. It must not deprecate, withdraw support, contact customers, or retire capability without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Deprecation Manager, Product Manager, Version Manager, Support Reviewer, Architect, Researcher, Analyst, Legal Advisor, Verifier, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Identify reason and consumers; choose replacement; announce; support; migrate; monitor; manage exception; enforce deadline; verify no critical consumer remains; and retire or extend with decision.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show target, reason, consumers, replacement, support, date, evidence, authority, and limitation. Require human approval for public notice, breaking change, exception, and removal.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use compatibility, migration, alias, warning, telemetry, fallback, access and data transition, notice, deadline, and removal controls.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show deprecated item, consumers, replacement, dates, support, migration, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized notify, extend, restrict, migrate, remove, and reopen controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Deprecation charter; target and reason; consumer and replacement; notice and support; migration and exception; decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify item, version, consumer, source, reason, notice, date, method, result, migration, owner, and limitation. Notice or usage scan proves neither migration nor safe removal.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify consumers, notice, replacement, compatibility, migration, support, security, contract, data, exception expiry, and retirement. Test unresponsive and critical consumer.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Deprecation without replacement, silent breaking change, indefinite status, notice inaccessible, consumer inventory incomplete, exception permanent, and removal before migration.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause removal, restore support or compatibility, notify, preserve evidence, migrate, and reassess contract and user impact. Reopen on consumer, replacement, threat, law, or timeline change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Target, reason, consumers, replacement, support, notice, dates, migration, evidence, exceptions, owners, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve notices, consumer inventory, migrations, support, exceptions, decisions, evidence, and open gaps. Hand off to Version, Compatibility, Release, Support, Product, Legal, Data, and Sunset.
