SECTION 268 — Continuous Deployment

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Deploy validated artifacts through automated, observable, reversible, and authorized paths while protecting users, data, service continuity, and owner control.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A deployment model with trigger, artifact, target, identity, policy gates, strategy, configuration, migration, monitoring, rollback, communication, approval, and incident behavior.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Automation can change production faster than humans can see. Without strong gates, canary, rollback, observability, and authority, a small change can become a broad outage, exposure, or irreversible data event.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when artifacts are automatically or repeatedly deployed to an environment, device population, customer, service, or infrastructure target.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no deployment, target, configuration, data, or operational behavior changes and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use delivery, artifact, environment, configuration, identity, security, testing, operations, migration, backup, rollback, and incident baselines. Define deployment and stop authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Artifact, target, version, trigger, identity, gates, configuration, migration, strategy, health, telemetry, rollback, communication, and acceptance criteria.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners approve automation scope, target, gates, strategy, rollback, exceptions, and kill switch. Humans decide emergency deployment, data migration, service interruption, and recovery tradeoffs.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may assemble deployment evidence, draft manifests, analyze health, and recommend pause or rollback. It must not deploy, expand target, alter gates, suppress health, or declare success without explicit delegation and human control.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Deployment Engineer, Release Engineer, Platform Engineer, Operations Engineer, Tester, Verifier, Security Auditor, Coordinator, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Validate artifact and target; check gates and identity; deploy phased or canary; observe; pause or roll back on threshold; reconcile configuration and data; communicate; record; and improve.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show artifact, target, identity, gate, phase, health, evidence, user impact, rollback, and authority. Require human approval for production, sensitive target, migration, gate bypass, and recovery.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use progressive rollout, feature flags, immutable artifact, health checks, automatic safe pause, independent control path, migration checkpoint, rollback, audit, and target isolation.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show deployment phase, target population, artifact, health, incidents, evidence freshness, owner, rollback state, and authorized pause, stop, rollback, narrow, promote, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Deployment policy and manifest; target and identity record; rollout and health evidence; migration and rollback; incident and communication; decision, exception, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify artifact, target, phase, identity, configuration, health metric, threshold, method, time, result, impact, and limitation. Deployment completion proves execution, not service or user success.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify gates, target isolation, canary, health, pause, rollback, migration, audit, access, cleanup, and recovery. Test failed deployment, partial rollout, stale health, and control-plane loss.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Broad automatic rollout, no kill switch, health metric blind to user outcome, rollback that cannot reverse data, gate bypass, and deployment success equated with acceptance.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause or roll back, preserve state, communicate, restore or compensate, analyze impact, and reassess release. Reopen on target, artifact, health, configuration, migration, or dependency change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Artifact, target, gates, identity, rollout, health, rollback, owner, evidence, incidents, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve manifests, artifacts, rollout timeline, health, decisions, incidents, communications, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Operations, Release, Platform, Security, Support, and Incident Response.
