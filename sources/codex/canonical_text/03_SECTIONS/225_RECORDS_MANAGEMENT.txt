SECTION 225 — Records Management

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Identify, classify, create, capture, protect, retain, retrieve, preserve, transfer, and dispose of records that provide evidence of obligations, decisions, rights, operations, or history.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A records model with record classes, owners, authoritative copies, metadata, retention, legal hold, access, integrity, disposition, audit, continuity, and exception rules.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Records must remain usable and trustworthy for the period required, while unnecessary retention increases privacy, cost, and discovery risk. A backup, document, log, or database row is not automatically a record or an authoritative copy.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when information documents business, legal, regulatory, financial, safety, security, product, user, operational, or governance activity or must be preserved for accountability.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability only when no record, hold, retention, evidence, right, audit, or disposition obligation is affected and an approved exact baseline supports the conclusion.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use Legal, Regulatory, Privacy, Security, Data, Evidence, Audit, Backup, Archive, and Deletion baselines. Identify jurisdictions, owners, record classes, authoritative systems, holds, retention authority, and disposal approval.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Record classes, sources, metadata, owners, business and legal value, retention, access, integrity, holds, jurisdictions, disposition, transfers, systems, evidence, and exceptions.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Records owners and counsel approve classification, retention, hold, access, transfer, disposition, and exceptions. Humans resolve whether a record is authoritative, whether a hold applies, and whether destruction is lawful and safe.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may classify authorized metadata, identify duplicates, draft inventories, summarize records, and propose retention or disposition queues. It must preserve provenance and not delete, alter, reclassify, disclose, or declare a record non-authoritative without explicit human authority and hold checks.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Records Manager, Legal Advisor, Privacy Reviewer, Archivist, Researcher, Analyst, Governance Reviewer, Security Auditor, Verifier, Documentation Writer, and Memory Curator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define record classes and authority; inventory sources; capture metadata; establish retention and access; apply holds; preserve integrity; enable retrieval; transfer or archive; review and dispose; audit decisions; handle exceptions and continuity.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show record class, source, authority, owner, hold, retention, sensitivity, action, evidence, and uncertainty. Require human approval for hold, disclosure, reclassification, disposition, and destruction. Keep protected content out of unnecessary AI context.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate operational data, records, evidence, backups, archives, holds, indexes, access, and disposition. Preserve metadata and integrity across migration, provide legal hold override, and prevent retention automation from destroying held records.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show record class, owner, authoritative source, retention, hold, access, integrity, evidence freshness, disposition state, and authorized classify, preserve, search, restrict, transfer, and destroy controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Records schedule; classification and authority register; metadata and access model; hold and disposition procedures; transfer and archive plan; retrieval, audit, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify record, source, class, authority, version, metadata, integrity, owner, hold, retention, access, method, time, result, and limitation. A backup or export proves only its capture and declared completeness.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify capture, metadata, search, access, integrity, hold, retention, transfer, archive, disposition, deletion, recovery, and audit. Test duplicate sources, migration, clock, hold arrival, restricted access, and failed disposition.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Records without owner or metadata, uncontrolled copies, hold bypass, premature deletion, indefinite retention, backup confused with record, and AI summary treated as the authoritative record.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stop disposition, preserve affected copies, apply or extend holds, restore metadata, notify owners, and investigate loss or disclosure. Reassess legal, privacy, evidentiary, and continuity impact before resuming.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Classes, sources, authority, owners, metadata, access, holds, retention, disposition, evidence, tests, exceptions, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending qualified review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve schedules, records, holds, disposition decisions, transfers, evidence, incidents, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Legal, Compliance, Privacy, Audit, Data, Archive, Backup, Deletion, and Operations.
