SECTION 347 — Solutions Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Design and validate implementable customer solutions that connect business outcomes, product capability, architecture, integration, security, operations, delivery, and ownership.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A solution model with customer outcome, requirements, architecture, data, integration, security, deployment, operations, cost, risks, assumptions, evidence, and handoff.

WHY THIS MATTERS

A solution can satisfy a diagram while failing implementation, support, security, data, cost, or customer adoption. The handoff must preserve assumptions and unresolved work rather than turning proposal into fact.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a customer, partner, or internal stakeholder needs a composed technical and operational solution across products, teams, providers, or domains.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no composed solution, architecture, integration, delivery, customer outcome, or ownership claim changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use requirements, solution architecture, product, domain, data, security, privacy, integration, operations, cost, legal, licensing, delivery, and support baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Outcome, stakeholders, requirements, constraints, current state, products, architecture, data, integrations, security, deployment, operations, cost, risks, and acceptance.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Solution owners coordinate specialists and preserve customer truth. Accountable product, architecture, security, legal, delivery, operations, and customer owners approve commitments and tradeoffs.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may synthesize authorized requirements and sources, draft options and diagrams, identify gaps, and compare bounded designs. It must not claim feasibility, make commitments, expose customer data, or replace specialist or owner approval.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Solutions Engineer, Architect, Business Analyst, Researcher, Analyst, Security Auditor, Data Engineer, Operations Engineer, Verifier, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Discover outcome and constraints; inspect current state; model requirements and dependencies; design options; assess security, data, cost, delivery, operations, support, and lifecycle; validate; decide; and hand off.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show outcome, stakeholder, option, evidence, assumption, risk, owner, commitment, and limitation. Require human approval for architecture, security, data, legal, pricing, deployment, and customer claims.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Link outcome to capabilities, components, data, interfaces, identity, security, deployment, operation, support, recovery, cost, and retirement. Preserve alternative and rejected option rationale.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show solution state, requirements, options, risks, assumptions, evidence freshness, owners, decisions, and authorized approve, revise, qualify, escalate, reject, and handoff controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Solution charter; outcome and requirement map; architecture and integration; data and security; deployment and operations; cost, risk, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify outcome, requirement, source, product, version, environment, method, result, assumption, owner, and limitation. A diagram or demo proves neither feasibility nor production outcome.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify requirements, architecture, integration, data, identity, security, privacy, cost, delivery, operations, support, recovery, and customer understanding. Reproduce material assumptions.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Solution theater, product fit assumed, customer data exposed, unsupported architecture, handoff without owner, hidden operations, and proposal presented as committed implementation.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Mark uncertainty, correct customer and internal records, preserve decisions, revise or reject option, and reassess commitments and downstream work. Reopen on requirement, product, architecture, customer, risk, or provider change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Outcome, requirements, architecture, options, dependencies, evidence, risks, owners, commitments, operations, recovery, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending stakeholder acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve discovery, requirements, diagrams, assumptions, decisions, evidence, commitments, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Product, Architecture, Engineering, Delivery, Operations, Support, Security, Legal, and Customer Success.
