SECTION 307 — Data Operations

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Operate data pipelines, stores, quality, access, refresh, lineage, retention, deletion, recovery, and consumer services so data remains governed and useful.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A data-operations model with sources, pipelines, stores, owners, schedules, quality, freshness, access, incidents, correction, backup, recovery, retention, and consumer communication.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Data operations failures propagate silently into reports, models, decisions, and obligations. A pipeline can be green while data is late, duplicated, unauthorized, or semantically wrong.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when data is ingested, transformed, stored, published, refreshed, accessed, corrected, archived, deleted, or recovered in ongoing operation.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no data source, pipeline, store, consumer, quality, access, lifecycle, or recovery behavior changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use data architecture, quality, privacy, security, warehouse, lake, database, storage, records, AI, observability, and recovery baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Sources, contracts, schemas, schedules, lineage, stores, consumers, quality, access, freshness, incidents, retention, deletion, backup, and recovery.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Data owners approve meaning, access, refresh, correction, quarantine, retention, deletion, recovery, and consumer notice. Humans decide whether data is fit for consequential use.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inspect authorized metadata, quality, lineage, and bounded samples; summarize incidents; and draft runbooks or remediation. It must not expose raw data, alter authoritative data, widen access, delete, or declare fitness without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Data Operations Engineer, Data Engineer, Data Quality Analyst, Data Architect, Privacy Reviewer, Security Auditor, Analyst, Tester, Verifier, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Operate ingestion and transformation; monitor quality and freshness; manage access; reconcile; quarantine and correct; notify consumers; back up and restore; retain and delete; respond; and improve.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show source, schema, grain, time, quality, access, consumer, action, authority, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for correction, quarantine, access, deletion, recovery, and consequential use.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use contracts, lineage, quality gates, quarantine, versioned pipelines, access control, privacy, freshness, reconciliation, backup, deletion propagation, and consumer impact.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show pipeline, source, freshness, quality, lineage, access, consumer, incidents, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized pause, quarantine, correct, backfill, restore, restrict, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Data-operations charter; source and pipeline catalog; quality and freshness; access and lineage; incident, correction, backup, recovery, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify source, pipeline, schema, grain, population, window, method, result, actor, quality, and limitation. Pipeline success or row count proves only execution and declared slice.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify ingestion, schema, quality, freshness, lineage, access, privacy, reconciliation, correction, backfill, backup, restore, deletion, and consumer notification.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Green pipeline, stale data, hidden correction, unrestricted raw zone, consumer unaware, deletion not propagated, backup not restored, and quality score as truth.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Quarantine, preserve versions, notify consumers, correct or backfill, restore, reconcile, and reassess downstream decisions. Reopen on source, schema, quality, pipeline, consumer, or lifecycle change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Sources, pipelines, stores, owners, quality, access, freshness, lineage, recovery, retention, evidence, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve data state, pipeline versions, incidents, corrections, backups, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Data Engineering, Quality, Privacy, Security, Analytics, AI, Records, and Operations.
