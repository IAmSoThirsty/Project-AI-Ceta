SECTION 269 — DevOps

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Integrate development, delivery, operations, security, quality, and learning so teams can change systems quickly while preserving reliability, accountability, and user outcomes.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A DevOps operating model with shared ownership, flow, feedback, automation, environments, observability, incident learning, quality, security, and measurable improvement.

WHY THIS MATTERS

DevOps is not a tool list or a team merger. Without clear authority and protected outcomes, speed can shift work to operators, hide risk, increase fatigue, or optimize deployment frequency over service quality.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when development and operations jointly create, release, operate, secure, support, or improve a product or service.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no shared delivery, operating, support, security, or feedback workflow is affected and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use product, architecture, quality, security, delivery, operations, support, reliability, incident, and governance baselines. Identify team boundaries, service owners, and decision rights.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Value and user outcomes, team roles, workflow, source, build, deployment, operations, telemetry, incidents, support, risks, measures, and improvement constraints.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Service and product owners share outcomes but retain explicit authority. Humans set risk, staffing, on-call, change, incident, security, and user-impact decisions and protect sustainable workload.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may summarize flow and operational evidence, draft automation, identify bottlenecks, and propose improvements. It must not erase ownership, change production, suppress incidents, set policy, or optimize delivery speed at the expense of protected outcomes without human decision.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Architect, Planner, Implementer, Operations Engineer, Security Auditor, Tester, Verifier, Coordinator, Optimizer, User Experience Reviewer, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define outcomes and ownership; map flow; automate safe feedback; integrate testing and security; deploy progressively; observe; operate and support; respond; learn; improve; measure quality and workload; and review architecture and team boundaries.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show owner, outcome, change, evidence, risk, operational state, user impact, and authority. Require human control for production, security, incident, staffing, and service commitments. Preserve operator stop and appeal.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Support self-service with guardrails, immutable artifacts, policy gates, observability, ownership metadata, safe deployment, rollback, incident, knowledge, and feedback loops. Avoid central automation becoming a single control failure.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show flow, lead time, change, reliability, incidents, workload, cost, security, evidence freshness, owners, and authorized pause, rollback, remediate, escalate, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

DevOps charter; ownership and service map; flow and automation design; CI/CD and operations model; observability and incident learning; measures, decisions, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify outcome, service, change, environment, measure, period, source, method, actor, result, and limitation. Delivery frequency or automation count is not service quality.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify ownership, workflow, security, tests, deployment, observability, support, incident, recovery, workload, and user outcomes. Test team or provider failure and unsafe automation.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Tool worship, speed-only metrics, invisible operations work, shared accountability with no owner, automation without rollback, on-call burnout, and incidents treated as individual failure.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stabilize service, protect operators and users, preserve evidence, roll back or reduce change, address workload and systemic cause, and revisit measures. Reopen on outcome, organization, service, architecture, or risk change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Outcomes, owners, flow, automation, security, quality, operations, support, measures, evidence, workload, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve service maps, ownership, workflows, runbooks, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, workload assumptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Platform, DevSecOps, GitOps, SRE, Support, Product, and Governance.
