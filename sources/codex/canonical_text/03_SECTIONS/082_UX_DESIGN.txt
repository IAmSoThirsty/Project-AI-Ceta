SECTION 082 — UX Design

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define the design of usable, accessible, understandable, and recoverable experiences that meet authorized user and service outcomes.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

 - A declared user, task, context, journey, interaction, content, accessibility, risk, and outcome scope.
 - Tested designs and specifications with traceable evidence, decisions, limitations, and implementation handoff.

WHY THIS MATTERS

UX design translates evidence into behavior and presentation. It must account for the whole task, not only the attractive or nominal path.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to user, operator, admin, support, assistive, mobile, desktop, physical, conversational, and multimodal experiences.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No material user, task, state, content, accessibility, permission, or recovery effect; or an unchanged approved UX design with impact recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Research, requirements, journeys, information and interaction models, content, devices, accessibility, privacy, security, design system, implementation constraints, and acceptance authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Research, requirements, journeys, information and interaction models, content, devices, accessibility, privacy, security, design system, implementation constraints, and acceptance authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

User and product owners approve outcomes, populations, inclusion, burden, risk, content meaning, tradeoffs, and acceptance. They retain correction, appeal, and stop authority.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may generate alternatives, copy drafts, prototypes, and critique. It must preserve research context and uncertainty and must not claim usability, accessibility, consent, or user intent without evidence.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

UX Designer, User Experience Reviewer, Researcher, Interaction Designer, Visual Designer, Content Designer, Architect, Accessibility specialist, Tester, Verifier, Critic, Governance Reviewer, Implementer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Frame goals; model tasks and states; design flows, content, feedback, permissions, and recovery; prototype; test with representative and assistive users; resolve findings; specify and hand off.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show user, state, actor, automation, authority, consequence, uncertainty, and recovery. AI proposals remain drafts and must not bypass review, accessibility, privacy, or implementation checks.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Define behavior, structure, content, visual, accessibility, localization, data, identity, error, analytics, and lifecycle contracts. Ensure design intent survives implementation and degraded operation.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show design version, task, population, evidence, accessibility findings, open defects, implementation status, and controls to approve, correct, annotate, supersede, or recover.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

UX requirements, journey and task flows, wireframes or prototypes, interaction and content specs, accessibility annotations, research findings, test results, decisions, and implementation handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify task, user, context, device, design version, method, participant coverage, result, and limitation. A prototype, heuristic review, or AI critique is bounded evidence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify task success, comprehension, accessibility, error prevention, recovery, content, permissions, privacy, latency, localization, and implementation fidelity.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Designing the happy path only; inaccessible critical actions; ambiguous state; hidden automation; decorative hierarchy that obscures task; and calling a heuristic review user validation.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause affected release, preserve findings and user evidence, provide alternate or manual path, correct design, and retest. Reopen when requirements, population, technology, content, or evidence changes.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Goals, users, tasks, flows, states, content, accessibility, evidence, tests, risks, recovery, open findings, and acceptance authority are explicit. Remain CONTENT-COMPLETE pending reconciliation, hostile review, and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve design versions, prototypes, specifications, findings, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and next action. Hand off to UI, Visual, Content, Design Systems, Application, Accessibility, Testing, and Operations.
