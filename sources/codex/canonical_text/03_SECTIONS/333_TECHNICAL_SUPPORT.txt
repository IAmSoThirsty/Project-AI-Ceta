SECTION 333 — Technical Support

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Help users and operators diagnose, resolve, work around, and recover from technical problems while preserving security, privacy, evidence, accessibility, and learning.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A support model with intake, identity, severity, context, troubleshooting, escalation, communication, workaround, resolution, knowledge, privacy, and feedback ownership.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Support is part of the product's real behavior. Poor support can expose data, repeat unsafe advice, blame users, hide defects, and leave people without an accessible recovery path.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a person or team needs technical assistance to use, operate, integrate, recover, or understand a capability.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no user or operator support path, technical issue, commitment, or recovery need changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use service management, application and platform operations, privacy, security, identity, knowledge, accessibility, incident, and support baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

User, entitlement, issue, environment, version, steps, evidence, severity, impact, privacy, workaround, owner, escalation, and resolution criteria.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Support owners protect users, verify access, triage, escalate, communicate, and record. Humans approve sensitive diagnosis, account or data action, compensation, and closure.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may classify tickets, suggest known steps, summarize context, and draft responses from approved knowledge. It must not request secrets, expose private data, change user state, make unsupported claims, or close high-impact cases without human review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Technical Support Engineer, Support Reviewer, Application Engineer, Systems Administrator, Security Auditor, User Experience Reviewer, Analyst, Verifier, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Intake and authenticate; capture context; classify; troubleshoot safely; provide workaround; escalate; communicate; resolve; verify user outcome; document; identify defect; and improve knowledge.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show user, issue, version, evidence, action, source, privacy, confidence, and limitation. Require human review for sensitive, security, account, data, safety, and closure decisions.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Provide ticket identity, context, redaction, knowledge links, escalation, diagnostic access, audit, user-visible status, and secure support tools with least privilege.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show queue, severity, owner, user impact, evidence freshness, escalation, SLA, privacy, and authorized assign, escalate, communicate, resolve, reopen, and close controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Support charter; intake and severity; troubleshooting and escalation; response and workaround; resolution and user confirmation; knowledge, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify user, entitlement, issue, version, environment, source, method, time, action, result, and limitation. Ticket closure proves workflow state, not user success.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify authentication, privacy, diagnosis, safe advice, escalation, accessibility, communication, workaround, resolution, reopen, and knowledge capture. Test missing context and unavailable support tools.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Secrets requested, copy-paste diagnosis, blame, unsupported workaround, privacy exposure, no escalation, inaccessible channel, and closure without user outcome.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Protect user and data, correct advice, escalate, preserve evidence, communicate, restore or compensate, and reassess. Reopen on repeated issue, new evidence, security concern, or user dispute.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Users, issue, severity, evidence, authority, response, escalation, resolution, privacy, accessibility, owners, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve tickets, evidence, responses, workarounds, incidents, decisions, knowledge, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Customer Support, Help Desk, Engineering, Security, Service Management, and Operations.
