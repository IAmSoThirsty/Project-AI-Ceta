SECTION 349 — Vendor Management

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Govern supplier relationships, performance, risk, security, privacy, contracts, service levels, changes, incidents, continuity, and exit throughout the vendor lifecycle.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A vendor model with identity, service, contract, owner, performance, risk, data, access, evidence, review, issue, remediation, renewal, and exit.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Vendor dependency persists beyond purchase. A supplier may change ownership, subprocessor, control, price, support, region, or viability without a local code change.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when an external supplier provides goods, service, data, software, infrastructure, labor, support, logistics, or operational dependency.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no vendor, contract, service, data, dependency, risk, or relationship behavior changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use procurement, legal, privacy, security, architecture, supply chain, service-level, finance, continuity, and exit baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Vendor, service, contract, owner, contacts, data, access, SLA, controls, performance, incidents, financial health, changes, and exit.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Vendor owners monitor and communicate; legal, security, privacy, finance, and service owners review their concerns. Humans approve renewal, exception, remediation, notice, and termination.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may organize authorized vendor evidence, compare performance, draft questions, and identify change signals. It must not certify supplier, negotiate, disclose confidential information, accept risk, or terminate without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Vendor Manager, Procurement Analyst, Legal Advisor, Security Auditor, Privacy Reviewer, Service Manager, Financial Analyst, Researcher, Verifier, and Governance Reviewer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Inventory vendors; review contract and controls; monitor performance, incidents, data, and change; assess risk and financial health; remediate; renew; substitute; and exit.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show vendor, service, contract, evidence, performance, risk, data, change, owner, and limitation. Require human approval for renewal, exception, disclosure, escalation, and termination.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Link vendor to services, data, access, dependencies, SLAs, subvendors, regions, artifacts, continuity, and exit. Minimize lock-in and retain alternate path where consequence warrants.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show vendor health, service, contract, performance, incidents, evidence freshness, data, owner, and authorized remediate, restrict, renew, substitute, and terminate controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Vendor register; service and contract; risk and control; performance and incident; renewal and exit; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify vendor, service, contract, period, source, method, result, owner, data, incident, and limitation. Vendor attestation or dashboard proves only its stated scope.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify identity, contract, service, controls, data, access, SLA, incident, continuity, subvendor, change notice, financial viability, and exit. Test vendor outage and nonperformance.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

No owner, contract not operationalized, vendor claim as proof, data or subprocessor hidden, renewal by default, lock-in, and incident communication delayed.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Restrict access, preserve evidence, invoke remedy, substitute or fail over, notify stakeholders, protect data, and reassess relationship. Reopen on vendor, service, contract, control, or viability change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Vendor, service, contract, owner, data, access, performance, risk, evidence, remediation, renewal, exit, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve vendor records, contracts, assessments, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Procurement, Supply Chain, Legal, Security, Privacy, Service Management, and Operations.
