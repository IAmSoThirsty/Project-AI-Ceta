SECTION 356 — Logistics

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Move materials, products, devices, documents, data, and equipment safely, traceably, timely, securely, and cost-effectively across locations and custody boundaries.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A logistics model with item identity, packaging, custody, route, carrier, condition, timing, security, customs or jurisdiction, exception, receipt, damage, and recovery.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Movement introduces loss, tampering, delay, damage, misrouting, privacy, safety, and chain-of-custody risk. Shipment status is not proof of condition, receipt, or correct destination.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when physical or digital items are transported, staged, stored in transit, delivered, returned, or transferred between custodians.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no item, custody, route, delivery, storage-in-transit, or transport risk changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use supply chain, manufacturing, device, packaging, distribution, security, privacy, legal, customs, inventory, and customer baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Item, identity, quantity, condition, packaging, origin, destination, custodian, carrier, route, timing, security, receipt, and exception.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Logistics owners approve route, carrier, custody, security, condition, cost, exception, and loss response. Humans verify receipt and resolve damage or claims.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze authorized shipment metadata, forecast delay, draft routes, and identify anomalies. It must not expose customer data, change destination, certify condition, or accept delivery without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Logistics Manager, Supply Chain Manager, Device Provisioning Engineer, Security Auditor, Procurement Analyst, Analyst, Observer, Verifier, and Coordinator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Identify and package; assign custody; select route and carrier; ship; track; handle customs or jurisdiction; receive and inspect; reconcile inventory; address exception; return or dispose.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show item, custody, route, destination, condition, evidence, action, privacy, and limitation. Require human approval for high-value, sensitive, regulated, hazardous, or disputed shipment.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use serialization, chain of custody, tamper evidence, condition, tracking, access, privacy, route resilience, inventory reconciliation, and exception workflow.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show shipment, item, custody, location, condition, ETA, exception, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized hold, reroute, inspect, accept, return, and claim controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Logistics charter; item and custody; packaging and route; carrier and tracking; receipt and exception; decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify item, serial or version, quantity, custodian, origin, destination, carrier, method, time, condition, result, and limitation. Tracking event proves location signal, not condition or receipt.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify identity, quantity, packaging, custody, route, condition, tracking, receipt, security, privacy, customs, damage, return, and inventory reconciliation.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Untracked transfer, wrong destination, custody gap, tamper ignored, sensitive data exposed, receipt assumed, carrier single point, and delivery speed over safety or condition.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Hold, investigate, preserve custody evidence, reroute or replace, notify owners and recipients, reconcile inventory, and reassess security and customer impact. Reopen on route, carrier, item, condition, or jurisdiction change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Items, custody, routes, carriers, condition, evidence, owners, exceptions, receipt, recovery, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve shipment, custody, tracking, receipt, damage, claims, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Distribution, Manufacturing, Device Provisioning, Vendor, Support, Finance, and Operations.
