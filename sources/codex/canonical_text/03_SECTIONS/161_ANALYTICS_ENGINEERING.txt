SECTION 161 — Analytics Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Engineer trustworthy analytical models, metrics, transformations, tests, documentation, and delivery practices that turn governed data into reproducible decision support.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

Versioned analytical models with metric definitions, lineage, tests, owners, freshness, access, documentation, deployment, monitoring, and recovery.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Analytics can encode business rules as hidden software. A polished metric may be stale, duplicated, biased, or semantically wrong.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when reusable transformations, metrics, analytical models, semantic layers, or decision datasets are built or changed.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No material metric, transformation, lineage, consumer, access, quality, or lifecycle behavior changes and an exact analytical baseline covers the work.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Data contracts, business definitions, source lineage, consumers, tests, freshness, quality, access, deployment, ownership, and acceptance rules.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Models, metrics, SQL or code, dependencies, tests, samples, thresholds, reports, owners, environments, evidence, assumptions, and known gaps.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

The owner approves metric meaning, thresholds, access, publication, exceptions, risk, and acceptance; assigns data, domain, analytics, privacy, security, and verification authority.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inspect authorized model code, schemas, lineage, tests, metric catalogs, query plans, freshness signals, and bounded samples; draft transformations, tests, documentation, impact analyses, and explanations; and propose corrections or optimization experiments.

AI must preserve metric definitions, grain, population, filters, time window, source and model versions, transformation lineage, freshness, uncertainty, and known exclusions. It must distinguish a syntactically valid query from a semantically valid measure and a useful analysis from a decision.

AI must not redefine metrics, change business meaning, publish consequential results, modify production models, bypass review gates, infer authorization, or present correlation, forecast, or generated narrative as causation or verified fact without delegated authority and human acceptance.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Analyst, Data Engineer, Researcher, Architect, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Governance Reviewer, Security Auditor, Critic, Optimizer, Documentation Writer, and Memory Curator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define metrics and grain; build tested transformations; document lineage; validate freshness and quality; deploy controlled changes; monitor drift; notify consumers; preserve recovery.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

AI may draft transformations and tests from authorized sources. It must preserve metric definitions, disclose uncertainty, and never publish consequential analysis or infer business authority.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Treat analytical models as versioned software with contracts, tests, lineage, environments, dependencies, access, observability, rollback, and retirement semantics.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show model version, metric owner, source freshness, tests, consumers, deployment, incidents, evidence, and controls to approve, pause, revert, correct, restrict, and retire.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Metric catalog; analytical models; lineage; tests; deployment and monitoring design; quality and access records; decisions, evidence, limitations, and handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify source, model, metric, version, population, query, environment, method, result, and limitation. A chart or passing test proves only its bounded claim.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify metric definitions, grain, joins, freshness, tests, access, reproducibility, change impact, deployment, failure, correction, and consumer understanding.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Metric duplication, hidden filters, untested transformations, stale dashboards, circular validation, manual edits, and treating engagement or correlation as causation.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Withdraw affected outputs, preserve versions and evidence, notify consumers, and use prior model, corrected data, restricted publication, or controlled reprocessing.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Models, metrics, owners, tests, lineage, evidence, limitations, gaps, and review cadence are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending later gates.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Hand off to BI, Data Analysis, Data Science, Data Quality, Data Engineering, Testing, Governance, Security, Operations, and Retirement.
