SECTION 273 — Developer Experience

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Design the tools, workflows, documentation, environments, feedback, and support that let developers and technical contributors perform authorized work effectively, safely, accessibly, and sustainably.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A developer-experience model with user goals, journeys, setup, tooling, access, feedback, failure recovery, security, accessibility, support, measures, ownership, and retirement.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Poor developer experience creates hidden work, unsafe workarounds, onboarding delay, inconsistent environments, and operational burden. Speed metrics alone can reward shortcuts that increase defects and fatigue.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when contributors use a shared development workflow, platform, toolchain, repository, environment, documentation, or support path.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no contributor journey, tool, access, workflow, support, or quality outcome changes and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use user research, architecture, source, platform, security, identity, accessibility, quality, DevOps, support, and governance baselines. Identify contributor populations and product owner.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Contributor goals, journeys, tools, repositories, environments, access, documentation, onboarding, support, telemetry, friction, incidents, accessibility, and outcomes.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

DX owners prioritize contributor outcomes and accessibility; teams own truthful documentation and support; humans approve access, policy, tool changes, and workload tradeoffs.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may summarize authorized feedback, draft docs and setup, identify friction, and propose improvements. It must not infer user intent, expose source or credentials, grant access, or change shared workflows without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

User Experience Reviewer, Researcher, Developer Experience Engineer, Platform Engineer, Documentation Writer, Security Auditor, Analyst, Tester, Verifier, and Critic.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Research contributors; map journeys; simplify setup; secure access; provide examples and diagnostics; test tools and documentation; measure friction and outcomes; support recovery; review changes; and retire obsolete paths.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show contributor, journey, tool, version, access, evidence, uncertainty, and impact. Require human review for shared guidance, access, security, and policy changes. Preserve accessible and manual support.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Provide reproducible setup, self-service with guardrails, clear errors, diagnostics, documentation versioning, isolated environments, secure defaults, accessibility, and recovery.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show journey health, setup failures, tool versions, support demand, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized prioritize, change, rollback, restrict, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

DX charter; contributor and journey map; tool and environment catalog; setup and support paths; feedback and measure model; test, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify contributor, journey, tool, version, method, period, source, result, and limitation. Onboarding time or usage count proves only its measured slice.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify setup, access, documentation, tooling, accessibility, security, diagnostics, recovery, support, and representative contributor outcomes. Test clean machine, failure, and role variation.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Tool-first design, tribal knowledge, setup requiring hidden access, documentation drift, metrics without user evidence, unsafe workaround, and support burden shifted to individuals.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Restore prior path, provide support, protect credentials, preserve feedback, and correct tooling or documentation. Reopen on contributor, platform, policy, security, or workflow change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Users, journeys, tools, access, support, accessibility, evidence, outcomes, owners, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending contributor and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve journeys, tool versions, docs, feedback, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Tooling, Platform, DevOps, Security, Support, Documentation, and Governance.
