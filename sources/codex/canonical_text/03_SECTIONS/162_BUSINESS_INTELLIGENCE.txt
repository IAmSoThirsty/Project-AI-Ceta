SECTION 162 — Business Intelligence

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Design governed reporting and decision-support experiences that present timely, understandable, accessible, and traceable information without overstating certainty.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A BI model with audience, decision purpose, metrics, lineage, refresh, permissions, accessibility, explanation, alerts, owner, evidence, and correction paths.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Dashboards influence action while hiding definitions and uncertainty. Visual clarity and a green indicator are not proof of valid decisions.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when reports, dashboards, scorecards, alerts, self-service analytics, or executive views influence material decisions or operations.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No material metric, audience, decision, data, access, presentation, or alert behavior changes and an exact BI baseline covers the work.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Decision purpose, audiences, data, metrics, definitions, refresh, lineage, access, accessibility, thresholds, risk, and acceptance authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Reports, queries, metric definitions, filters, audiences, source windows, permissions, refresh results, user research, incidents, evidence, and limitations.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

The owner approves decisions, metrics, publication, access, thresholds, exceptions, risk, and acceptance; assigns business, data, privacy, security, accessibility, and reporting authority.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inspect authorized report definitions, semantic models, lineage, refresh status, accessibility metadata, bounded report data, and alert history; draft summaries, visual alternatives, test cases, and explanations; and identify stale, contradictory, or anomalous presentation signals.

AI must identify the report version, source window, filters, audience, freshness, metric definition, uncertainty, and limitations for every material summary. It must distinguish displayed values from interpretation and interpretation from a recommended decision.

AI must not change metric meaning, hide contrary evidence, suppress or trigger consequential alerts, infer a user's authority, publish or withdraw a report, expose restricted data, or make a business decision without explicit delegation, accessible human review, and recorded acceptance.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Analyst, Researcher, Architect, Data Engineer, User Experience Reviewer, Tester, Verifier, Governance Reviewer, Security Auditor, Critic, Documentation Writer, and Memory Curator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define decisions and audiences; establish semantic models; build reports and alerts; validate definitions, accessibility, refresh, permissions, uncertainty, failure, and correction; monitor use and drift.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

AI may summarize authorized reports and propose views, but must cite sources, disclose freshness and uncertainty, and never make the decision, hide contrary evidence, or publish without authority.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate source, metric, semantic model, visualization, alert, interpretation, and decision. Preserve lineage, filters, permissions, accessibility, snapshots, corrections, and audit.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show report version, audience, metric definitions, freshness, sources, permissions, alerts, uncertainty, incidents, evidence, and controls to restrict, correct, withdraw, and retire.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

BI charter; semantic model; metric and report catalog; audience and accessibility evidence; refresh and alert design; lineage, access, test, decision, evidence, and handoff records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify source, metric, filter, window, report version, audience, environment, method, result, and limitation. A screenshot proves only what it displayed at that time.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify metric meaning, filters, freshness, permissions, accessibility, alert thresholds, uncertainty, contradictory data, reproducibility, and correction or withdrawal.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Dashboard theater, hidden filters, stale refresh, inaccessible charts, vanity metrics, alert overload, false precision, and conclusions presented without source or limitations.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Withdraw or label affected reports, preserve snapshots and evidence, notify decision makers, and use prior validated views, manual review, corrected data, or restricted publication.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Purpose, audience, metrics, sources, access, accessibility, refresh, tests, evidence, gaps, and review authority are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Hand off to Analytics, Data Quality, Data Governance, Data Engineering, UX, Accessibility, Security, Testing, Operations, and Records.
