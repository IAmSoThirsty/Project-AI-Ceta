SECTION 401 — Environmental Disposal or Recycling

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Dispose of or recycle hardware, materials, chemicals, packaging, media, and equipment through lawful, safe, traceable, and environmentally responsible channels after data and security obligations are handled.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An environmental-disposition model with material, hazard, asset, data, method, vendor, jurisdiction, chain of custody, recycling or disposal result, certificate, and impact evidence.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Recycling is not automatically safe or secure. Hazardous material, data-bearing media, export, informal disposal, and unverified vendor handling can harm people, ecosystems, and trust.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when equipment, electronics, batteries, media, chemicals, packaging, or materials are recycled, disposed, returned, reused, or transferred to a waste channel.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no physical material, environmental obligation, data-bearing asset, hazardous condition, or disposal channel changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use hardware disposal, secure destruction, records, privacy, legal, procurement, vendor, logistics, safety, and environmental baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Material, asset, hazard, data, security method, owner, vendor, jurisdiction, route, certificate, recycling or disposal method, and evidence.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners, environmental, safety, security, legal, and procurement authorities approve method, vendor, custody, hazard, data treatment, and evidence. Humans resolve regulatory and ethical tradeoffs.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may classify authorized inventory and vendor evidence and draft disposition plans. It must not select hazardous method, expose data, transfer asset, or certify environmental or secure result without qualified human authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Environmental Reviewer, Hardware Engineer, Safety Reviewer, Security Auditor, Procurement Analyst, Logistics Manager, Records Manager, Verifier, and Governance Reviewer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Classify material and hazard; handle data and keys; select channel and vendor; maintain custody; transport; dispose or recycle; obtain certificate; verify; reconcile asset; and record environmental result.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show material, asset, hazard, data, method, vendor, jurisdiction, custody, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for hazardous, regulated, data-bearing, export, and irreversible action.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use asset identity, secure wipe or destruction, hazardous-material controls, approved vendor, chain of custody, environmental tracking, certificate, and audit.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show asset, material, hazard, data state, method, custody, vendor, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized quarantine, transfer, recycle, destroy, verify, and close controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Environmental-disposition charter; material and hazard; data and security; vendor and custody; method and certificate; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify material, asset, hazard, data, owner, vendor, method, jurisdiction, time, result, certificate, and limitation. Vendor claim or receipt proves only its stated scope.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify classification, data treatment, custody, vendor, route, method, safety, certificate, environmental outcome, inventory, and no active identity or access.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Recycle without data destruction, hazardous waste misclassified, vendor chain unclear, certificate vague, informal disposal, environmental claim without evidence, and cost-only selection.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stop transfer, recover material where possible, preserve evidence, notify authority, remediate exposure or hazard, and reassess environmental and security impact. Reopen on vendor, method, material, or regulation change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Material, hazard, asset, data, method, vendor, custody, certificate, environmental result, evidence, owners, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending qualified acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve disposition, custody, certificates, vendors, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Hardware Disposal, Secure Destruction, Procurement, Logistics, Safety, Security, Records, and System Retirement.
