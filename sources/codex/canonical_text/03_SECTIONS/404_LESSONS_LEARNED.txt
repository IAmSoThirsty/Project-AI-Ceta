SECTION 404 — Lessons Learned

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Convert verified experience, evidence, incidents, experiments, deliveries, and decisions into reusable learning, changed practice, and measurable improvement.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A lessons-learned model with event, evidence, insight, uncertainty, applicability, action, owner, adoption, measure, review, and knowledge handoff.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Lessons can become slogans or documents that never change behavior. A lesson must state where it applies, what evidence supports it, and how adoption will be checked.

APPLICABILITY

Apply after material work, incident, experiment, review, migration, release, failure, or success where experience can improve future decisions or practice.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no reusable insight, control, practice, decision, or continuity learning exists beyond the bounded record.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use postmortem, review, research, evidence, knowledge, policy, training, quality, risk, and roadmap baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Event, context, evidence, outcome, decision, participant, insight, uncertainty, applicability, action, owner, and measure.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans validate insight, respect participant context, decide action, assign ownership, fund adoption, and reject generalized lessons unsupported by evidence.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may synthesize authorized lessons, identify patterns, and draft changes to knowledge, process, or tests. It must not generalize from anecdotes, erase dissent, invent learning, or change policy without human review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Memory Curator, Researcher, Analyst, Teacher, Documentation Writer, Quality Manager, Planner, Critic, Verifier, and Governance Reviewer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Collect context and evidence; identify insight; state limits; test applicability; propose action; assign owner; adopt in docs, tests, policy, or design; measure; review; and retire stale lesson.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show source, context, evidence, insight, uncertainty, applicability, action, owner, and limitation. Require human validation before generalization or policy change.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Link lesson to incident, decision, requirement, architecture, test, policy, knowledge, and measure. Preserve provenance and prevent learning from being detached from its conditions.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show lesson, source, context, applicability, evidence freshness, owner, adoption, measure, and authorized validate, adopt, limit, revise, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Lessons charter; source and context; insight and limits; action and adoption; measure, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify event, source, context, method, participants, observation, insight, confidence, action, and limitation. Anecdote, success, or failure alone proves only a bounded experience.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify evidence, context, applicability, dissent, action, owner, adoption, measure, and change. Check whether the lesson remains valid in new context.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Slogan as lesson, blame, anecdote generalized, no owner, no adoption, stale guidance, and document publication treated as organizational learning.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Qualify or withdraw lesson, correct knowledge and policy, notify consumers, preserve evidence, and reassess affected decisions. Reopen on contrary evidence, context, or outcome change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Source, context, evidence, insight, limits, applicability, action, owner, adoption, measure, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve lessons, sources, decisions, actions, adoption, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Knowledge, Documentation, Training, Quality, Architecture, Risk, and Governance.
