SECTION 360 — Payments Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Authorize, initiate, authenticate, settle, reconcile, reverse, refund, and record payments securely, reliably, transparently, and within customer and regulatory boundaries.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A payments model with instrument, customer, authorization, provider, amount, currency, idempotency, status, fraud, settlement, dispute, refund, privacy, and reconciliation.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Payments combine money, identity, availability, fraud, privacy, and legal obligations. A provider response can be delayed, duplicated, reversed, or inconsistent with commerce and ledger state.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a system initiates, accepts, authorizes, settles, refunds, disputes, or reconciles monetary payment.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no payment, customer, amount, instrument, provider, settlement, refund, or financial record changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use commerce, billing, identity, fraud, privacy, security, legal, provider, ledger, records, and reconciliation baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Customer, amount, currency, product, instrument reference, provider, authorization, idempotency, status, settlement, refund, dispute, and evidence.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Payments owners approve provider, instrument, limits, fraud, refund, dispute, access, exception, and reconciliation. Humans handle high-risk or disputed financial decisions.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze authorized payment metadata, identify anomalies, and draft reconciliation or support explanations. It must not access instrument secrets, initiate or reverse payment, approve refund, or decide fraud without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Payments Engineer, Billing Engineer, Fraud Analyst, Security Auditor, Privacy Reviewer, Commerce Manager, Financial Analyst, Tester, Verifier, and Incident Responder.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define payment state; tokenize or reference; authorize; handle provider response; enforce idempotence; settle; reconcile; refund or dispute; monitor; secure; and recover.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show payment reference, customer, amount, currency, provider, status, action, authority, evidence, and limitation without exposing instrument data. Require human approval for refund, reversal, dispute, and exception.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use tokenization, least privilege, idempotency, state machine, provider abstraction, ledger, webhook validation, audit, privacy, fraud control, and retry without duplicate charge.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show payment state, provider, amount, currency, settlement, dispute, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized hold, retry, refund, reverse, reconcile, and restrict controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Payments charter; state and provider; instrument and security; authorization and settlement; reconciliation, refund, dispute, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify payment reference, customer, amount, currency, provider, state, method, time, actor, result, and limitation. Provider response proves only its recorded response and status.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify authorization, token, amount, currency, idempotence, provider, webhook, settlement, refund, dispute, ledger, privacy, fraud, and recovery. Test timeout, duplicate, reversal, and provider outage.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Instrument data in logs, duplicate charge, provider state as ledger truth, retry without idempotence, refund without authorization, webhook trust, and payment success treated as fulfillment.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Freeze or reconcile, preserve provider and ledger evidence, refund or reverse through authority, communicate, protect customer, and reassess. Reopen on provider, state, fraud, contract, or dispute change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Customer, amount, currency, provider, state, authorization, settlement, refund, dispute, evidence, owners, recovery, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve references, states, provider events, ledgers, disputes, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Billing, Commerce, Fraud, Finance, Legal, Support, Security, and Records.
