SECTION 228 — Quality Management

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Govern quality objectives, responsibilities, methods, evidence, defects, risks, improvement, and acceptance across the lifecycle.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A quality-management system with quality characteristics, owners, criteria, measures, reviews, nonconformance, corrective action, escalation, records, and continuous-improvement decisions.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Quality is broader than passing tests or meeting schedule. Without explicit characteristics and owners, teams optimize visible output while defects, user harm, maintainability, accessibility, reliability, and evidence quality accumulate.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a product, service, process, system, artifact, or decision has quality expectations, acceptance risk, repeatable work, or consequences from defect or variation.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability only for a bounded, reversible artifact with no material quality claim, consumer, safety, reliability, compliance, or lifecycle consequence and an approved exact baseline.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use requirements, architecture, risk, governance, quality characteristics, testing, operations, user research, support, and records baselines. Identify owner, consumers, acceptance authority, and improvement cadence.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Objectives, stakeholders, quality characteristics, requirements, risks, processes, controls, defects, measures, test results, incidents, feedback, resources, thresholds, and acceptance criteria.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Quality owners define criteria and measures; accountable humans approve tradeoffs, release or acceptance, exceptions, corrective action, and residual risk. Independent reviewers challenge claims and cannot be replaced by a green metric.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may summarize quality evidence, identify trends, draft checklists and tests, and propose improvements. It must preserve source, population, period, definition, and uncertainty. It must not redefine quality, suppress defects, approve release, or attest to conformance without human authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Quality Manager, Quality Engineer, Quality Assurance Reviewer, Researcher, Analyst, Architect, Tester, Verifier, Critic, Governance Reviewer, Documentation Writer, and Memory Curator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define quality and acceptance; map objectives and risks; assign owners; establish methods and measures; plan reviews and tests; record defects and nonconformance; correct and prevent recurrence; report trends; review changes; and improve the system.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show quality definition, source, population, period, measure, threshold, result, limitation, owner, and decision. Require human review for material tradeoffs, acceptance, exceptions, and corrective-action closure.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Link requirements, quality characteristics, controls, tests, defects, incidents, measures, decisions, and evidence. Preserve versioned baselines, independent review, traceability, and feedback loops that do not reward hiding defects.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show quality objectives, measures, findings, owners, evidence freshness, trends, risk, exceptions, corrective actions, and authorized accept, reject, escalate, remediate, reopen, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Quality charter; quality characteristics and criteria; measure catalog; review and test plan; defect and nonconformance register; corrective-action records; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify characteristic, definition, source, population, period, method, result, owner, reviewer, uncertainty, and limitation. A score, inspection, or passing test proves only its declared slice.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify quality definitions, measurement, sampling, traceability, defects, corrective action, independent review, acceptance, change impact, and feedback. Test missing evidence, metric drift, and conflicting signals.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Quality theater, vanity metrics, defect suppression, acceptance by schedule, unowned exceptions, measuring activity instead of outcome, and treating compliance paperwork as quality.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Mark unsupported claims, contain affected output, preserve evidence, correct defects, notify owners, and reassess acceptance. Reopen on changed requirements, users, risk, evidence, process, supplier, or lifecycle.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Objectives, characteristics, owners, criteria, measures, evidence, reviews, defects, corrective action, exceptions, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending independent assurance and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve quality baseline, measures, findings, decisions, evidence, corrective action, incidents, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Quality Engineering, QA, Testing, Operations, Governance, Support, and Retirement.
