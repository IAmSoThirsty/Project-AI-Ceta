SECTION 144 - Media Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define governed engineering of systems that create, transform, manage, deliver, and preserve coordinated text, image, audio, video, and interactive media.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A media baseline with asset identity, rights, provenance, synchronization, formats, accessibility, delivery, quality, security, evidence, and lifecycle.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Media pipelines multiply format, rights, synchronization, and preservation risks. A valid file can still be misleading, inaccessible, stale, or unlawfully used.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to media repositories, production, editing, publishing, digital experiences, asset pipelines, archives, and multi-format delivery.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

May be non-applicable to systems with no media asset, format, delivery, display, archive, or content-dependent outcome. Record scope, owner, evidence, and reopening trigger.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Requires asset purpose, owners, rights, consent, formats, metadata, provenance, synchronization, accessibility, privacy, security, quality, delivery, and retention policy.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Provide asset types, sources, versions, transformations, relationships, timing, captions, translations, delivery, access, retention, and failure requirements.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve rights, consent, publication, accessibility, privacy, retention, safety, and acceptance. Humans decide withdrawal and correction for consequential media.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may classify, transform, caption, translate, search, and generate media drafts. It must preserve source and rights, label transformation, and never publish or delete without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Architect, Researcher, Media Engineer, Designer, Privacy Reviewer, Security Auditor, Accessibility specialist, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Governance Reviewer, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, and Red Team. Existing role contracts remain controlling.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Inventory assets, owners, rights, sources, formats, relationships, and lifecycle.
2. Design ingest, transform, synchronize, deliver, search, archive, correction, and deletion paths.
3. Verify provenance, quality, accessibility, security, privacy, rights, and recovery.
4. Operate, publish, withdraw, preserve, and retire with traceable decisions.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show asset, source, transformation, rights, audience, provenance, accessibility, evidence, and state. Human review is required for publication, generation, licensing, and deletion.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate source, derivative, metadata, rights, transformation, delivery, cache, archive, and user presentation. Preserve lineage and format migration without overwriting source.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show asset graph, version, rights, delivery, quality, access, captions, incidents, evidence, and controls to approve, correct, restrict, withdraw, migrate, archive, or delete.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Media charter, asset catalog, rights and consent records, provenance graph, format policy, accessibility plan, pipeline and delivery design, test evidence, and preservation package.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify asset, source, derivative, rights, version, transformation, tool, configuration, method, result, and limits. Preserve failed, stale, superseded, and withdrawn evidence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify ingest, lineage, rights, format conversion, synchronization, captions, search, access, cache, corruption, restoration, migration, withdrawal, and deletion.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Overwriting masters; missing rights; unlabeled transformations; inaccessible media; format monoculture; unbounded storage; and treating popularity or fidelity as truth.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Quarantine affected assets, preserve source and lineage, restrict distribution, restore a prior derivative, compensate decisions, and reopen rights, provenance, and preservation review.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Assets, rights, lineage, accessibility, evidence, controls, delivery, preservation, and lifecycle are explicit. Keep CONTENT-COMPLETE until independent verification and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve asset graph, source, rights, transformations, decisions, evidence, incidents, and next safe action. Hand off to Content, Video, Audio, Streaming, Search, Privacy, Records, and Operations.
