SECTION 223 — Intellectual Property

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Identify, protect, attribute, license, use, create, transfer, and retire intellectual-property interests and restrictions affecting the work.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An IP register with works, inventions, marks, data, model or training material, owners, contributors, provenance, rights, restrictions, licenses, approvals, confidentiality, publication, enforcement, and evidence.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Ownership and permission can be unclear across employees, contractors, suppliers, open-source projects, generated material, data, and jurisdictions. A repository or citation is not proof of permission, ownership, originality, or compliant reuse.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when the work creates, uses, modifies, distributes, trains on, embeds, names, publishes, or derives value from potentially protected works, inventions, data, marks, or confidential material.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability only when qualified review confirms no protected or restricted material, contributor claim, license, confidentiality, publication, or transfer is affected.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use Legal, Licensing, Records, Security, Privacy, Procurement, employment, supplier, and source-control baselines. Identify contributors, employers, licensors, repositories, jurisdictions, confidentiality, and approval authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Assets, authors, contributors, source and provenance, licenses, contracts, data, dependencies, marks, inventions, confidentiality, publication plan, notices, restrictions, and unresolved ownership questions.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners and qualified counsel approve ownership, assignment, license, attribution, publication, disclosure, enforcement, and use of restricted material. Humans resolve contributor disputes and decide whether a source or generated artifact may be used.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inventory supplied sources, compare license text, track provenance, draft attribution and review questions, and identify possible conflicts. It must not assert originality, ownership, permission, authorship, or legal sufficiency; it must not ingest restricted material or publish, license, or disclose work without authorized human decision.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Legal Advisor, IP Analyst, Researcher, Governance Reviewer, Documentation Writer, Security Auditor, Privacy Reviewer, Verifier, Critic, and Memory Curator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Inventory and classify IP; capture provenance and contributors; review contracts and licenses; map obligations; protect confidential material; obtain assignments or permissions; preserve notices and attribution; approve publication; monitor use; handle claims and retirement.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show source, contributor, license, jurisdiction, confidence, restriction, and unresolved question. Keep confidential or restricted material in approved systems. Require human or counsel review for ownership, permission, attribution, infringement, publication, and enforcement.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Represent IP assets, provenance, license, contributor, restrictions, approvals, notices, and distribution as linked versioned records. Enforce repository, build, model, data, and publication gates and prevent unauthorized material from entering generated or shipped artifacts.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show asset, owner, contributor, source, license, restrictions, approval, publication state, evidence freshness, and authorized review, restrict, correct, attribute, withdraw, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

IP and provenance register; contributor and ownership record; license and restriction map; confidentiality and publication review; attribution and notice package; claim, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify asset, source, author, contributor, date, license or contract, jurisdiction, approval, version, method, result, and limitation. A URL, repository, generated output, or citation proves neither permission nor ownership.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify provenance, contributor rights, license compatibility, attribution, notices, confidentiality, publication, build inclusion, data or model restrictions, and withdrawal paths. Obtain qualified review for material uncertainty.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Untracked copied code or data, license mixing, missing attribution, confidential material in public artifacts, authorship assumed from tool output, and publication before rights review.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Restrict distribution, preserve provenance and claim evidence, notify owners and counsel, replace or remove affected material, correct notices, and assess downstream copies. Reopen on new source, contributor, license, jurisdiction, publication, or claim information.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Assets, sources, contributors, ownership, licenses, restrictions, approvals, notices, evidence, publication, recovery, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending qualified review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve provenance, licenses, contracts, approvals, attribution, claims, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Legal, Licensing, Procurement, Records, Security, Privacy, Engineering, and Release owners.
