SECTION 295 — Environment Promotion

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Move an identified artifact, configuration, data change, or capability between environments only after declared evidence, policy, ownership, and readiness conditions are met.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A promotion model with source and target environment, artifact, configuration, data, gates, approvals, compatibility, migration, health, rollback, and evidence.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Promotion can hide environment drift and turn test evidence into an unsupported production claim. Data, identity, provider, capacity, and operations can change the meaning of a result.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a candidate moves from development, test, staging, field, canary, or recovery into another environment or population.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no environment, artifact, configuration, data, or audience transition occurs and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use artifact, environment, configuration, testing, acceptance, release, change, data migration, identity, operations, and rollback baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Candidate, source and target, evidence, configuration, data, gates, compatibility, approvals, migration, health, support, and rollback.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners approve gate interpretation, target, data, migration, risk, exception, timing, and promotion. Humans decide whether evidence transfers honestly between environments.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may compare environment state, assemble evidence, identify gaps, and draft promotion plans. It must not approve, promote, alter gates, or claim parity without human authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Release Engineer, Environment Manager, Deployment Engineer, Tester, Verifier, Architect, Operations Engineer, Security Auditor, and Coordinator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Baseline source and target; compare configuration and dependencies; review evidence; validate data and identity; approve; promote; observe; pause or roll back; record; and update support.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show candidate, source, target, differences, gates, evidence, impact, authority, and rollback. Require human approval for production, sensitive data, migration, and exception.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use immutable artifacts, environment-specific config, policy gates, staged promotion, synthetic or controlled data, health, rollback, migration checkpoints, and audit.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show source and target, candidate, drift, gates, approvals, health, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized promote, pause, rollback, restrict, and reopen controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Promotion plan; environment comparison; readiness and approvals; migration and health; rollout and rollback; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify source, target, artifact, config, data, test, approval, method, time, result, impact, and limitation. Promotion proves movement, not target acceptance or user success.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify artifact, environment, config, data, identity, gates, compatibility, migration, health, rollback, audit, and partial promotion. Test target drift and failed gate.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Environment parity assumed, gate bypass, production data copied, target health stale, configuration overwritten, promotion treated as deployment success, and rollback without data plan.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause or roll back, preserve source and target state, restore data, communicate, and reassess evidence transfer and user impact. Reopen on environment, candidate, dependency, data, or gate change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Source, target, candidate, gates, approvals, config, data, health, rollback, evidence, owners, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve comparisons, approvals, promotions, health, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Deployment, Release, Configuration, Operations, Support, and Governance.
