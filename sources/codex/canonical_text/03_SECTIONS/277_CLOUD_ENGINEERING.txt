SECTION 277 — Cloud Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Design and operate cloud resources, services, accounts, networks, identities, data, automation, cost, security, resilience, and portability for declared outcomes.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A cloud model with provider, account or tenant, region, resources, identity, network, data, service limits, availability, cost, observability, governance, recovery, and exit.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Cloud speed and abstraction can hide shared failure, provider dependency, data residency, cost, identity, and recovery assumptions. A deployed resource is not automatically secure, resilient, or owned.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a public, private, hybrid, or managed cloud provides computing, storage, network, data, identity, platform, AI, or operations capability.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no cloud resource, provider, region, account, data, identity, cost, or operational behavior changes and an approved exact baseline applies.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use enterprise, cloud, infrastructure, identity, security, privacy, data, cost, resilience, backup, exit, and operations baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Provider, account, region, resource, topology, identity, data, workload, service limits, SLA, cost, telemetry, compliance, backup, restore, and exit requirements.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Cloud owners approve provider, region, data, identity, cost, architecture, service limits, exceptions, outage, recovery, and exit. Humans accept provider and shared-responsibility risk.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inspect authorized cloud metadata, plans, metrics, and cost; draft IaC and policies; and propose optimization. It must not access tenants, provision, change policy, delete resources, or claim cloud security without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Cloud Engineer, Architect, Infrastructure Engineer, Security Auditor, FinOps Analyst, Data Engineer, Platform Engineer, Tester, Verifier, and Operations Engineer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define provider and boundaries; design accounts, network, identity, resources, data, availability, cost, observability, backup, recovery, compliance, portability, and retirement; provision; validate; monitor; and improve.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show provider, account, region, resource, identity, data, cost, change, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for privileged, data, regional, destructive, cost, and provider changes.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use landing zones, tenancy, segmentation, least privilege, encryption, policy, resource limits, multi-zone design, backups, provider abstractions where useful, and tested exit or recovery.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show cloud topology, health, resource, cost, data region, identity, findings, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized scale, isolate, restore, restrict, migrate, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Cloud charter; account and region model; resource and dependency catalog; security, cost, observability, backup, recovery, exit, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify provider, account, region, resource, configuration, identity, data, method, time, result, cost, owner, and limitation. Provider certification or dashboard proves only its declared scope.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify identity, segmentation, encryption, policy, limits, availability, cost, telemetry, backup, restore, failover, regional behavior, portability, and deletion. Test provider and control-plane outage.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Shared responsibility misunderstood, admin credentials everywhere, region or data ignored, uncontrolled cost, provider lock-in without exit, backup untested, and cloud health equated with service outcome.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Restrict affected resources, preserve state and logs, fail over or restore, rotate credentials, communicate, and reassess data, cost, user, and provider impact. Reopen on provider, region, service, workload, or policy change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Provider, boundaries, resources, identity, data, cost, security, resilience, evidence, recovery, exit, owners, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve cloud inventory, IaC, accounts, policies, metrics, cost, incidents, backups, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Infrastructure, Platform, Security, FinOps, Data, and Operations.
