SECTION 230 — Quality Assurance

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Provide independent confidence that quality processes, controls, evidence, and outcomes satisfy their declared criteria within a bounded scope.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A quality-assurance plan with independence, criteria, reviews, test oversight, evidence, nonconformance, escalation, release or acceptance input, and follow-up.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Assurance is weakened when the people producing an artifact are the only people judging it, when evidence is selected to confirm a conclusion, or when a passing review is treated as permanent confidence.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when independent review or assurance is needed for quality, safety, security, privacy, compliance, user impact, release, or material operational claims.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability only when the consequence is low, the scope is reversible, and a documented peer or automated check provides sufficient bounded confidence.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Define criteria, scope, independence, evidence access, reviewers, confidentiality, sampling, decision authority, and escalation. Use quality management, engineering, testing, audit, and governance baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Requirements, quality criteria, risks, designs, processes, controls, test results, defects, incidents, user evidence, owners, environments, and acceptance expectations.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

QA owners protect independence, plan reviews, challenge evidence, report findings, and recommend acceptance. Accountable owners decide release, exception, remediation, or residual risk; reviewers do not silently accept on their behalf.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may compare evidence to criteria, identify omissions, draft review questions, and summarize bounded findings. It must preserve reviewer independence, source, scope, and uncertainty. It must not issue assurance, suppress findings, fabricate review, or sign approval.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Quality Assurance Reviewer, Quality Manager, Tester, Verifier, Auditor, Researcher, Analyst, Critic, Governance Reviewer, User Experience Reviewer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Set criteria and scope; confirm independence; review plans and artifacts; sample evidence; observe tests and operations; record nonconformance; communicate findings; verify corrective action; provide bounded assurance; and reopen on change.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show criterion, evidence, method, reviewer, scope, result, limitation, and decision authority. Require human judgment for findings, assurance, release, exception, and closure. Keep confidential evidence controlled.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Link requirements, controls, tests, observations, findings, decisions, and evidence. Preserve immutable review records, segregation, repeatable sampling, and traceable baselines.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show assurance scope, criteria, reviewer, evidence coverage, findings, exceptions, due dates, and authorized challenge, accept, remediate, escalate, reopen, and close controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

QA charter; criteria and review plan; evidence and sampling record; findings and nonconformance; corrective-action follow-up; assurance statement; decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify criterion, artifact, environment, sample, method, reviewer, time, result, limitation, and confidence. A review checklist or automated pipeline proves only its actual scope and operation.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify independence, criteria, scope, evidence, sampling, test observation, finding traceability, corrective action, release input, and closure. Test contradictory evidence, stale baselines, and reviewer conflict.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Assurance by checkbox, self-approval, undisclosed conflict, selective evidence, green pipeline as release proof, and findings closed by assertion.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Withdraw unsupported assurance, preserve workpapers, obtain independent review, correct findings, and reassess release or acceptance. Reopen on changed evidence, scope, requirements, risk, or operation.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Criteria, scope, independence, evidence, findings, decisions, limitations, follow-up, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending independent approval and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve reviews, evidence references, findings, decisions, exceptions, corrective actions, and open gaps. Hand off to Quality Management, Testing, Audit, Governance, Release, Operations, and Support.
