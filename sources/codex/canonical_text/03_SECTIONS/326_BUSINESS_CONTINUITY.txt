SECTION 326 — Business Continuity

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Preserve essential business, human, operational, legal, financial, and service outcomes through disruption, degradation, absence, dependency loss, and recovery.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A continuity model with essential functions, priorities, people, facilities, dependencies, minimum service, alternate process, communication, recovery objective, exercise, and decision authority.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Continuity is about what must continue and how people work, not only how systems restart. A plan can fail through staffing, suppliers, communications, inaccessible alternatives, or untested assumptions.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when disruption could materially affect essential operations, obligations, people, customers, safety, finance, data, or public trust.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability only when no essential outcome or dependency can be materially disrupted within scope and an approved exact baseline supports it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use business impact, operational, disaster recovery, service, people, supplier, facility, data, communication, safety, and legal baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Essential functions, priorities, users, staff, facilities, suppliers, systems, data, minimum service, dependencies, communication, recovery, and exercise evidence.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Continuity owners define essential outcomes, priorities, authority, staffing, alternate work, communication, and acceptance. Humans make life, safety, service, and resource tradeoffs.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may map dependencies, summarize plans, draft exercises, and identify gaps. It must not declare continuity, reprioritize essential outcomes, communicate emergency instruction, or act on behalf of authorities without human control.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Continuity Planner, Business Analyst, Operations Engineer, Coordinator, Risk Analyst, Researcher, Tester, Verifier, Safety Reviewer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Identify essential functions and impact; set priority and minimum service; map people and dependencies; design alternate process and facility; communicate; exercise; recover; review; and update.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show function, priority, dependency, people, minimum service, authority, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for emergency action, resource allocation, public communication, and return to normal.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Support alternate channels, manual operation, resilient communications, data access, staffing, supplier substitution, dependency independence, accessible procedures, and recovery evidence.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show continuity mode, essential functions, priorities, resources, staffing, dependencies, communications, evidence freshness, and authorized activate, reprioritize, communicate, recover, and close controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Continuity charter; impact and essential-function map; alternate process and resource; communication and exercise; recovery, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify function, disruption, priority, people, dependency, method, time, result, impact, recovery, and limitation. A plan or tabletop proves only the exercised assumptions.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Exercise staffing, facility, supplier, communication, manual work, data, accessibility, prioritization, alternate service, recovery, and return to normal. Record actual gaps.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

System-only plan, no priority, unrealistic staffing, inaccessible procedures, supplier assumption, communication failure, tabletop theater, and continuity that shifts harm without decision.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Activate alternate work, protect people, communicate, preserve evidence, recover essential service, and reassess priority and residual impact. Reopen on function, people, dependency, facility, or threat change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Essential functions, priorities, people, dependencies, alternatives, authority, exercises, evidence, recovery, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve plans, contacts, exercises, decisions, communications, gaps, recovery, exceptions, and open actions. Hand off to Disaster Recovery, Operations, HR, Facilities, Suppliers, Legal, and Governance.
