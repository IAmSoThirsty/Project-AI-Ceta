SECTION 363 — Financial Operations

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Record, control, reconcile, forecast, report, and protect financial activity, resources, obligations, approvals, and evidence across the lifecycle.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A financial-operations model with accounts, transactions, budgets, commitments, controls, approvals, reconciliation, reporting, close, audit, privacy, and correction.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Financial records drive decisions, obligations, trust, and legal reporting. A dashboard or ledger entry can be wrong through timing, classification, duplicate, omission, adjustment, or unsupported assumption.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when money, assets, commitments, budgets, costs, revenue, expenses, payments, forecasts, or financial reports are created, changed, or controlled.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no financial value, transaction, budget, obligation, control, or report changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use commerce, billing, payments, procurement, controls, audit, legal, tax, records, data, and policy baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Accounts, transactions, invoices, payments, budgets, commitments, prices, evidence, approvals, controls, reconciliations, periods, and reports.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Finance owners approve accounting meaning, budget, adjustment, payment, write-off, close, report, access, exception, and attestation. Humans preserve segregation of duties and resolve disputes.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze authorized financial metadata, identify anomalies, draft reconciliations, and summarize reports. It must not alter ledger, approve payment, set accounting treatment, disclose data, or attest without human authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Financial Analyst, Accountant, Auditor, Controls Analyst, Commerce Manager, Billing Engineer, Payments Engineer, Researcher, Verifier, and Governance Reviewer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define accounts and periods; record; approve; reconcile; budget; forecast; close; report; control access; investigate variance; correct; audit; and preserve or retire records.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show account, transaction, period, source, treatment, approval, evidence, variance, and limitation. Require human review for adjustment, close, report, attestation, payment, and exception.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use immutable ledger history, segregation, approval, reconciliation, period close, lineage, access, retention, correction, and audit. Separate operational estimates from authoritative records.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show period, balances, transactions, budget, variance, approvals, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized hold, adjust, reconcile, close, report, and reopen controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Financial-operations charter; account and control; transaction and reconciliation; budget and forecast; close, report, audit, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify account, transaction, period, source, treatment, method, time, actor, approval, result, and limitation. Report or ledger status proves only recorded state and scope.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify authorization, segregation, completeness, accuracy, cutoff, reconciliation, adjustment, period close, report, access, retention, and audit. Test duplicate, late, reversed, and corrected transaction.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Unreconciled ledger, unsupported adjustment, shared approval, period drift, forecast as fact, report without source, and financial control as paperwork only.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Freeze affected record or period, preserve evidence, correct or reverse, reconcile, communicate, and reassess legal and financial impact. Reopen on transaction, rule, period, source, or obligation change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Accounts, transactions, periods, controls, approvals, reconciliation, reports, evidence, owners, corrections, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending authorized acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve ledger, budgets, reports, reconciliations, approvals, adjustments, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Billing, Payments, Commerce, Revenue, Audit, Legal, Procurement, and Records.
