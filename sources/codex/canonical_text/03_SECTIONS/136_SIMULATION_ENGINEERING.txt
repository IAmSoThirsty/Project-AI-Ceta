SECTION 136 - Simulation Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define governed engineering of simulations that represent systems, environments, processes, or scenarios for analysis, training, prediction, design, or decision support without confusing simulated behavior with reality.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A simulation baseline with purpose, fidelity, boundaries, assumptions, parameters, scenarios, calibration, validation, uncertainty, controls, and decision-use limits.

WHY THIS MATTERS

A simulation may be internally consistent yet externally wrong. Overconfidence can transfer model error into safety, finance, policy, training, or operational decisions.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to discrete-event, continuous, agent-based, Monte Carlo, training, operational, design, and scenario simulations.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

May be non-applicable to a static illustration or explanatory example that makes no predictive, training, decision, performance, or system-behavior claim. Record scope, owner, evidence, and reopening trigger.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Requires simulation purpose, system boundary, fidelity, source data, assumptions, equations or rules, calibration, validation targets, uncertainty, runtime, and decision authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Provide scenarios, entities, events, time model, parameters, distributions, initial conditions, outputs, validation data, sensitivity, reproducibility, and use restrictions.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve fitness for use, fidelity, scenarios, assumptions, thresholds, and consequential decisions. Domain owners decide when simulation evidence is admissible.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may generate scenarios, explore sensitivity, and summarize runs. It must not invent calibration evidence, suppress outliers, or recommend real-world action beyond delegated scope.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Architect, Researcher, Analyst, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Critic, Governance Reviewer, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, Optimizer, Observer, and Red Team. Existing role contracts remain controlling.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Define purpose, boundary, fidelity, time, entities, scenarios, and decision limits.
2. Build model, calibrate against evidence, conduct sensitivity and uncertainty analysis.
3. Verify implementation, validate behavior, document mismatch, and preserve runs.
4. Review use, monitor drift, update under change control, and retire safely.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show simulation versus reality, model version, scenario, inputs, assumptions, uncertainty, and use status. Human approval is required before consequential use; AI cannot silently convert a run into an operational command.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate model, simulator, scenario, data, calibration, execution, output, visualization, and decision boundaries. Support deterministic replay, seeds, time controls, provenance, and safe co-simulation.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show model identity, scenario, progress, fidelity, calibration, uncertainty, validation state, output status, and controls to stop, branch, compare, quarantine, or withdraw a run.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Simulation charter, model and scenario catalog, calibration and validation records, experiment plan, uncertainty and sensitivity analysis, run manifest, results, limitations, and continuity package.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify model, simulator, version, scenario, data, seed, parameters, environment, method, output, uncertainty, and limitations. Simulation evidence remains distinct from observed-world evidence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify deterministic replay, conservation or invariants, calibration, validation cases, sensitivity, rare events, time-step effects, malformed inputs, model drift, and safe interpretation.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Treating a simulation as reality; validating only typical cases; hiding uncertainty; tuning to one dataset; untracked seeds; and using attractive visuals as evidence.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Mark outputs provisional, preserve runs, restrict affected decisions, recalibrate or select an approved prior model, compensate external decisions, and reopen fitness-for-use approval.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Purpose, fidelity, scenarios, evidence, uncertainty, limits, authority, and recovery are explicit. Keep CONTENT-COMPLETE until independent verification and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve models, scenarios, seeds, runs, evidence, decisions, mismatches, and next safe action. Hand off to Scientific, Digital Twin, Game, HPC, Testing, Data, and Operations sections.
