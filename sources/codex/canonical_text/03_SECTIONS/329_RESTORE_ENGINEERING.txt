SECTION 329 — Restore Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Restore data, configuration, artifacts, services, or infrastructure to a known, authorized, coherent, and usable state after loss, corruption, migration, or recovery action.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A restore model with source, point in time, target, authority, integrity, dependency, sequencing, validation, reconciliation, rollback, communication, and evidence.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Restore can overwrite good state, reintroduce vulnerabilities, violate retention, or produce a technically available but semantically inconsistent system. A successful copy is not a restored outcome.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when backup, archive, snapshot, replica, prior artifact, or known-good state is used to return a system, data, or service to operation.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no state, data, service, or recovery path requires restoration and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use backup, storage, database, data, identity, security, continuity, DR, failover, change, validation, and operations baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Source copy, point in time, target, authority, keys, dependencies, sequence, integrity, validation, reconciliation, rollback, and communication.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Recovery owners approve source, target, data loss, overwrite, access, sequence, communication, and return to service. Humans judge semantic and user impact.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inspect authorized restore metadata, compare state, draft runbooks, and analyze results. It must not restore, overwrite, delete, expose data, or declare success without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Restore Engineer, Data Engineer, Database Administrator, Storage Administrator, DR Planner, Security Auditor, Tester, Verifier, and Incident Commander.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define recovery objective; select and verify source; prepare target; protect current state; restore in order; validate integrity and semantics; reconcile; communicate; monitor; roll back or retry; and record.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show source, point in time, target, authority, data, keys, sequence, evidence, impact, and limitation. Require human approval for restore, overwrite, deletion, data exposure, and return to service.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use isolated restore, immutable source, key availability, dependency ordering, checkpoint, reconciliation, transaction, validation, rollback, and audit.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show restore phase, source, target, data point, integrity, dependencies, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized start, pause, rollback, reconcile, communicate, and accept controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Restore charter; source and target record; execution and integrity; validation and reconciliation; rollback and communication; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify source, version, point in time, target, method, authority, time, result, integrity, reconciliation, actor, and limitation. Copy completion proves neither usable state nor correctness.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify source, integrity, keys, target isolation, sequence, dependencies, data semantics, permissions, reconciliation, rollback, RTO/RPO, and user outcome.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Restore over current state, wrong point in time, key unavailable, dependency omitted, data integrity assumed, backup copy accepted without test, and return before validation.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stop, preserve both states, roll back or choose alternate source, reconcile, communicate, and reassess data and user impact. Reopen on source, target, key, dependency, or validation change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Source, point in time, target, authority, integrity, sequence, validation, reconciliation, evidence, owners, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve restore logs, source, target, checks, reconciliations, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Backup, DR, Failover, Data, Database, Storage, Security, and Operations.
