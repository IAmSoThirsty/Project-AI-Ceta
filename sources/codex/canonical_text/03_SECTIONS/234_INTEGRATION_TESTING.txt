SECTION 234 — Integration Testing

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Verify that connected components exchange data, control, identity, errors, timing, and recovery behavior according to their contracts.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An integration-test model with participating versions, interfaces, fixtures, environments, data, dependency behavior, observability, failure paths, results, and limitations.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Components may pass isolated tests while disagreeing on schema, timing, identity, retries, errors, versions, or ownership. Integration evidence is bounded by the participants and conditions actually exercised.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when two or more components, services, devices, teams, providers, data stores, or processes interact through a contract.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no interaction, interface, dependency, data exchange, or control boundary changes and an approved exact baseline covers the work.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use interface contracts, versions, data schemas, identity, environments, provider stubs or sandboxes, observability, recovery, and test-data controls.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Participants, interface, schema, versions, identities, data, timing, retries, failure behavior, environment, observability, prior defects, and acceptance criteria.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Interface owners define contracts and compatibility; testers control environments and data; humans judge semantic behavior, provider risk, exceptions, and acceptance.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft contract cases, fixtures, dependency matrices, and failure analysis from authorized artifacts. It must not claim external execution, alter provider or production state, expose data, or infer compatibility from syntax alone.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Tester, Integration Engineer, Architect, Analyst, Implementer, Verifier, Quality Engineer, Security Auditor, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Map participants and contracts; choose representative versions and data; exercise normal, boundary, timeout, retry, duplicate, ordering, schema, identity, and failure paths; observe; diagnose; retest; and record compatibility limits.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show participants, versions, contract, identity, data, environment, execution, result, and limitation. Require human approval for external or production dependency testing, data access, and acceptance.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Support contract versioning, test doubles with declared fidelity, isolated environments, correlation, deterministic cleanup, fault injection, retries, idempotency, timeout, and rollback.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show integration pair, versions, contract, status, failures, evidence freshness, owner, dependency health, and authorized run, pause, substitute, rollback, accept, and reopen controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Integration matrix; contract and fixture record; environment and dependency record; results and failure register; compatibility and coverage report; decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify participants, versions, interface, data, environment, method, execution, result, error, time, actor, and limitation. A schema match or mock pass proves only the exercised contract slice.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify serialization, semantics, identity, authorization, ordering, retries, timeout, duplicate, version skew, provider loss, rollback, observability, and cleanup. Reproduce material failures independently.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Testing only happy interactions, mocks with false fidelity, version skew ignored, data semantics unverified, retry storms, and integration pass treated as end-to-end acceptance.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Preserve traces, isolate failing participant, use approved fallback, correct contract or component, and rerun. Reopen on interface, schema, version, provider, data, or operational change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Participants, contracts, versions, cases, data, results, failures, coverage, limits, owners, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve integration and dependency versions, fixtures, traces, results, decisions, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to System, E2E, API, Event, Release, Operations, and Quality owners.
