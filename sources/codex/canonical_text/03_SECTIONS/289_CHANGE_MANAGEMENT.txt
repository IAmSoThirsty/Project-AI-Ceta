SECTION 289 — Change Management

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Control proposed, planned, emergency, rejected, implemented, failed, and retired change with proportionate impact analysis, authorization, evidence, and recovery.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A change model with request, scope, reason, impact, risk, dependencies, approval, implementation, validation, communication, rollback, closure, and learning.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Change introduces uncertainty into a working system. Process can become either bureaucracy that teams bypass or a thin approval ritual that does not examine impact, evidence, or recovery.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a change can affect behavior, users, data, security, safety, cost, service, architecture, obligations, or continuity.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when impact analysis shows no material behavior, dependency, risk, or operating effect and an approved exact baseline covers the change class.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use configuration, source, release, deployment, testing, architecture, risk, security, operations, communication, and rollback baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Request, owner, reason, scope, baseline, impact, risk, dependencies, schedule, approvals, implementation, tests, communication, rollback, and closure evidence.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Change owners assess and implement; approvers decide risk, timing, exception, and rollback; operators can stop unsafe change. Emergency changes require later review, not absence of accountability.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may summarize diffs, map dependencies, draft impact and test plans, and identify missing evidence. It must not classify risk conclusively, approve, implement, bypass, or close a change without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Change Manager, Architect, Planner, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Operations Engineer, Security Auditor, Governance Reviewer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Capture request; baseline; assess impact and risk; plan test, communication, and rollback; approve; implement; observe; validate; close or recover; review emergency; and update dependencies and records.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show change, baseline, risk, owner, authority, dependencies, evidence, implementation state, and rollback. Require human approval for protected, production, data, security, safety, and emergency changes.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use traceable change records, dependency graph, policy gates, staged execution, immutable artifacts, feature flags, rollback, audit, and change-aware evidence invalidation.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show change state, impact, risk, approvals, dependencies, evidence freshness, implementation, incidents, rollback, and authorized approve, reject, schedule, pause, roll back, and close controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Change record; impact and risk assessment; implementation and test plan; approvals; communication; validation and rollback; emergency review; decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify change, baseline, owner, approver, scope, environment, method, time, result, impact, and limitation. Approval proves authorization, not successful or safe outcome.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify impact, approvals, tests, communication, implementation, monitoring, rollback, emergency review, closure, and traceability. Test failed, partial, conflicting, and out-of-window change.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Approval without impact, change without baseline, emergency forever, hidden manual action, rollback untested, ticket closed by deployment, and process bypass caused by excessive friction.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause or roll back, preserve state and evidence, communicate, recover, and reassess change and downstream impact. Reopen on incident, failed validation, new dependency, or invalidated evidence.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Request, scope, impact, risk, authority, implementation, validation, recovery, evidence, communication, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve change history, approvals, implementations, incidents, rollbacks, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Release, Deployment, Configuration, Operations, Security, Testing, and Governance.
