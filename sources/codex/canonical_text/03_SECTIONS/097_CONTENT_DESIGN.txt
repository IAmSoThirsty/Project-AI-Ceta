SECTION 097 — Content Design

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define content that helps a declared audience understand, decide, act, recover, and maintain an accurate mental model of a product, service, or system.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

 - Scoped audience, purpose, context, channel, language, content type, accessibility, and lifecycle.
 - Content models, terminology, drafts, evidence, review, publication, correction, and retirement controls.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Content is part of behavior and can create legal, safety, privacy, and trust consequences. Clear prose is not automatically accurate or inclusive.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to interface, help, instructional, transactional, error, notification, onboarding, support, and operational content.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No material meaning, instruction, disclosure, action, accessibility, localization, or recovery effect; or an unchanged approved content baseline with impact recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Information architecture, user research, requirements, domain terms, policies, product behavior, accessibility, localization, legal review, source evidence, and authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Information architecture, user research, requirements, domain terms, policies, product behavior, accessibility, localization, legal review, source evidence, and authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve meaning, claims, audience, tone, disclosure, rights, safety, accessibility, and publication. They retain correction, withdrawal, and acceptance authority.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft, transform, translate, and critique content. It must preserve source, context, uncertainty, and rights and must not invent facts, cite nonexistent sources, or publish consequential text.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Content Designer, Technical Writer, Copywriter, UX Reviewer, Researcher, Domain Expert, Accessibility specialist, Legal and Governance Reviewers, Tester, Verifier, Implementer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define audience and task; gather sources; model terms and states; draft; check accuracy, accessibility, tone, localization, rights, and behavior; test comprehension; publish, monitor, correct, and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show source, status, audience, actor, state, consequence, uncertainty, and correction route. AI content remains draft until reviewed and users retain non-AI support.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Connect content to product states, terminology, permissions, data, localization, versioning, search, accessibility, audit, and retirement. Keep content changes traceable to behavior changes.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show content version, owner, source, audience, locale, status, evidence, approvals, findings, and controls to edit, withdraw, supersede, or restore.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Content model, glossary, drafts, source and rights record, state and message inventory, accessibility and localization notes, reviews, tests, evidence, decisions, and handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify source, claim, audience, context, version, reviewer, method, result, and limitation. Readability or engagement is bounded evidence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify accuracy, task comprehension, state alignment, accessibility, localization, legal and privacy disclosure, error recovery, source traceability, and implementation fidelity.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Invented claims; vague errors; manipulative consent; untranslated critical content; inaccessible tables; stale help; and content that promises behavior the system does not perform.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Withdraw or correct content, preserve prior evidence, notify affected users, provide human support, and retest behavior and locales before republishing.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Audience, purpose, claims, sources, states, accessibility, evidence, tests, correction, recovery, open risks, and acceptance are explicit. Remain CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve content IDs, sources, versions, locales, reviews, decisions, evidence, corrections, and next action. Hand off to Content Strategy, UX, UI, Technical Writing, Localization, Legal, and Testing.
