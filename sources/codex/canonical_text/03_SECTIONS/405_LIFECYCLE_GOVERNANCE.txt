SECTION 405 — Lifecycle Governance

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Govern the full lifecycle of systems, services, products, data, assets, decisions, and relationships through authority, evidence, gates, ownership, change, continuity, and retirement.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A lifecycle-governance model with states, entry and exit criteria, owners, decision rights, evidence, risk, dependencies, review, exceptions, handoff, retention, and retirement.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Lifecycle gaps create abandoned systems, stale evidence, orphaned access, unowned debt, unsupported users, and unsafe retirement. Governance must operate across the entire life, not only at approval points.

APPLICABILITY

Apply to any system, product, service, capability, data set, asset, policy, or relationship that moves through defined states over time.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no lifecycle state, owner, gate, evidence, risk, change, continuity, or retirement obligation exists and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use authority, evidence, requirements, architecture, risk, quality, operations, security, privacy, records, support, maintenance, and retirement baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Object, lifecycle states, owners, authority, criteria, artifacts, dependencies, risks, measures, records, exceptions, continuity, and retirement.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Accountable owners define gates, state, authority, risk, exceptions, review, handoff, and retirement. Humans make consequential decisions and ensure no state becomes ownerless.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may track lifecycle metadata, identify missing gates or stale owners, and draft reviews. It must not advance state, accept risk, revoke control, destroy records, or retire an object without human authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Governance Reviewer, Architect, Planner, Memory Curator, Auditor, Verifier, Researcher, Analyst, Coordinator, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define lifecycle and states; assign owners; set gates and evidence; review; manage change, support, security, quality, debt, continuity, retention, deprecation, disposal, and retirement; and audit gaps.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show object, state, owner, gate, authority, evidence, risk, dependency, action, and limitation. Require human approval for state transition, exception, risk, destruction, and retirement.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Represent state, criteria, artifacts, evidence, owner, authority, dependencies, decisions, access, records, and retirement as durable linked records with expiry and audit.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show lifecycle state, owner, gate, evidence freshness, risk, debt, dependencies, support, retention, and authorized advance, hold, remediate, deprecate, preserve, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Lifecycle charter; state and gate model; owner and authority; evidence and review; risk, exception, handoff, retention, retirement, decision, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify object, state, version, owner, gate, source, method, time, result, authority, and limitation. State label proves neither criteria satisfaction nor owner acceptance.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify every state has owner, entry, exit, evidence, authority, support, recovery, handoff, retention, and retirement. Test orphaned, stale, disputed, and failed transitions.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Ownerless state, gate theater, approval without evidence, lifecycle stops at deployment, access survives retirement, debt ignored, and records destroyed before governance review.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Freeze unsafe transition, restore known-good state, assign owner, preserve evidence, remediate, communicate, and reassess lifecycle. Reopen on object, state, owner, law, risk, evidence, or dependency change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

States, owners, authority, gates, evidence, risk, dependencies, handoff, retention, support, retirement, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve lifecycle history, decisions, evidence, exceptions, owners, handoffs, incidents, and open gaps. Hand off to all applicable section owners and the final retirement authority.
