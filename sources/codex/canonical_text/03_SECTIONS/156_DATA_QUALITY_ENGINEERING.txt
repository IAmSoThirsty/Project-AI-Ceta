SECTION 156 — Data Quality Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Engineer measurable, observable, explainable, and recoverable data quality across source, transformation, storage, use, and lifecycle boundaries.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

Quality dimensions, rules, thresholds, sampling, monitoring, ownership, quarantine, correction, reconciliation, evidence, and impact handling for each material data product.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Plausible data can be incomplete, biased, stale, duplicated, or misclassified. A score without population, rule, threshold, and consequence is not a quality claim.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when data informs decisions, automation, reporting, models, records, compliance, safety, service, or material operations.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No material quality rule, threshold, source, transformation, consumer, or lifecycle behavior changes and an exact approved baseline covers the work; record the decision.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Purpose, data contract, population, dimensions, business rules, tolerances, source lineage, consumers, sampling, incidents, risk, and correction authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Datasets, profiles, rules, thresholds, samples, missingness, drift, duplicates, reconciliations, alerts, defects, impacts, evidence, and assumptions.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

The owner approves quality dimensions, thresholds, exceptions, risk acceptance, correction, quarantine, consumer notification, and acceptance; assigns domain, steward, and verification authority.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may profile authorized data, propose quality dimensions and rules, detect anomalies or drift, explain observed defects, and draft monitoring, quarantine, correction, reconciliation, and evidence artifacts. It must state population, sampling, method, uncertainty, limitations, and possible bias, protect sensitive values, and keep recommendations traceable to source and rule versions. It must not declare data fit for consequential use, silently impute or correct records, release quarantined data, change thresholds, suppress alerts, or notify consumers without authorized review. Any quality-control mutation or acceptance decision requires the applicable policy evaluation, capability scope, immutable decision record, human authority, and append-only audit linkage.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Analyst, Researcher, Observer, Architect, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Governance Reviewer, Security Auditor, Critic, Optimizer, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, and Red Team.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Define quality dimensions, rules, populations, thresholds, validity, and owners.
2. Profile and monitor data; detect missingness, drift, duplication, bias, corruption, and schema change.
3. Route failures to quarantine, correction, reconciliation, consumer notification, and evidence preservation.
4. Test representative and adversarial cases and review threshold fitness over time.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

AI may profile authorized data and propose rules or anomaly explanations. It must disclose sampling and uncertainty, protect values, and never auto-correct consequential data or declare fitness without authority.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Represent quality rules, results, populations, samples, thresholds, lineage, alerts, quarantines, corrections, and invalidation as versioned records. Separate data quality from model confidence and service uptime.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show dataset, rule version, population, freshness, score, threshold, uncertainty, impact, owner, alert, quarantine, correction, and evidence actions.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Quality charter; rule catalog; profiles; monitoring and alert definitions; defect, quarantine, correction, reconciliation, impact, test, decision, evidence, and handoff records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify population, sampling, dimensions, rule, threshold, source, version, method, time, result, uncertainty, and limitation. A sample never proves all records.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify rule correctness, representative coverage, threshold behavior, false positives and negatives, drift, access, correction, quarantine, consumer notification, and reproduction.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Single metric quality, biased samples, threshold gaming, silent imputation, destructive correction, alert fatigue, stale profiles, and quality gates that bypass owner decisions.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Quarantine affected data, preserve prior versions and evidence, notify consumers, and use approved correction, reconciliation, reprocessing, or restricted-use paths. Reassess decisions and models already using it.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Rules, thresholds, populations, owners, monitoring, response, tests, evidence, limitations, and review cadence are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending later gates.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Hand off quality contracts and alerts to Data Engineering, Data Governance, Integration, Warehousing, Analytics, ML, Testing, Operations, Incident, and Migration work.
