SECTION 384 — Modernization

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Improve an aging, constrained, risky, or obsolete system, process, product, or platform while preserving required outcomes, evidence, continuity, and realistic transition.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A modernization model with current state, drivers, outcomes, options, constraints, target state, increments, dependencies, migration, risk, cost, evidence, and exit criteria.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Modernization can reduce debt or simply replace known complexity with new complexity. New technology does not automatically improve reliability, security, usability, cost, or organizational capability.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a system, platform, process, architecture, product, or operational capability is substantially improved, migrated, replatformed, or replaced.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no structural, lifecycle, capability, risk, or operating condition changes and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use current-state research, architecture, requirements, debt, risk, cost, product, security, operations, migration, and retirement baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Current state, drivers, outcomes, users, constraints, debt, options, target, dependencies, migration, cost, risk, capacity, and exit.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners choose outcome and investment; architects and specialists assess options; humans approve target, tradeoffs, migration, deprecation, cost, and residual risk.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze authorized current state, identify debt, draft options and plans, and compare evidence. It must not choose architecture, promise benefit, hide transition risk, or change systems without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Architect, Planner, Refactorer, Researcher, Analyst, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Optimizer, Security Auditor, and Governance Reviewer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Baseline current state; define outcome and constraints; identify debt and risk; develop options; select target; sequence increments; migrate; validate; communicate; operate in transition; and retire old state.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show current state, target, option, evidence, dependency, cost, risk, user impact, and limitation. Require human approval for architecture, funding, migration, deprecation, and retirement.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use strangler or staged transition where useful, compatibility, parallel state, data reconciliation, feature flags, rollback, observability, support, and explicit end state.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show modernization phase, current and target, outcomes, debt, risk, cost, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized approve, sequence, pause, rollback, deprecate, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Modernization charter; current and target state; options and decision; roadmap and migration; debt and risk; validation, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify current state, target, option, source, method, cost, risk, result, owner, assumption, and limitation. New technology or benchmark proves neither outcome nor transition safety.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify current state, target, requirements, architecture, data, security, performance, cost, support, migration, rollback, user outcome, and retirement. Test transition and fallback.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Modernization as fashion, target before problem, debt hidden, migration big bang, benefit assumed, old system never retired, and transition work unowned.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause increment, preserve both states, roll back or stabilize, communicate, reassess outcome and cost, and revise target or sequence. Reopen on new evidence, constraint, technology, risk, or user need.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Current, target, outcomes, options, migration, dependencies, cost, risk, evidence, owners, exit, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve architecture, decisions, migrations, tests, incidents, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Refactoring, Debt, Migration, Version, Deprecation, Operations, and Product.
