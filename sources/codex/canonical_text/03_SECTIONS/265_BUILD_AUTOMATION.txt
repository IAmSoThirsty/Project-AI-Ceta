SECTION 265 — Build Automation

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Automate repeatable build orchestration, validation, artifact production, evidence capture, and safe failure handling without hiding authority or supply-chain risk.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A build-automation model with triggers, inputs, runners, steps, credentials, caches, outputs, logs, retries, cancellation, ownership, provenance, and maintenance.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Automation can scale errors and privilege. A script that “usually works” may silently fetch mutable inputs, leak secrets, retry unsafe actions, or generate artifacts with no trustworthy record.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when build or artifact creation is scripted, scheduled, event-driven, or run by CI, a platform, or an agent.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no build automation, artifact, privileged step, or evidence claim changes and an approved exact baseline applies.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use build, source, dependency, package, artifact, runner, secret, security, testing, and release baselines. Identify automation owner and stop authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Workflow, trigger, source, dependencies, runner, credentials, cache, steps, outputs, logs, retry, cancellation, gates, and acceptance criteria.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners approve triggers, privilege, credentials, gates, retries, release linkage, and workflow changes. Humans retain control over production or destructive steps.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft workflows, analyze logs, suggest tests, and identify unsafe permissions. It must not activate, expand, or modify privileged automation or approve output without explicit human control.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Build Automation Engineer, Build Engineer, Platform Engineer, Security Auditor, Tester, Verifier, Implementer, Analyst, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define workflow and trust; isolate runner; validate input; execute; capture logs and provenance; handle retry and cancel; scan; publish or quarantine; test failure; rotate credentials; maintain and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show workflow, trigger, identity, privilege, source, runner, step, output, log, and limitation. Require human approval for privileged or externally visible actions and gate changes.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use least privilege, ephemeral runners, secret injection, immutable artifacts, idempotent steps, bounded retries, cancellation, audit, concurrency controls, and failure isolation.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show workflow status, actor, source, runner, active step, output, findings, evidence freshness, and authorized run, cancel, retry, approve, quarantine, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Workflow definition; permissions and runner record; execution log; artifact and provenance; retry and failure record; test, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify workflow version, trigger, actor, runner, source, step, time, output, result, and limitation. A green workflow proves only that execution and declared coverage.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify permissions, trigger, input validation, secret masking, isolation, retries, cancellation, artifact identity, logs, cleanup, and failure recovery. Test malicious input and runner loss.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Overprivileged runner, secrets in output, unbounded retry, trigger surprise, mutable action, skipped gate, non-idempotent step, and workflow ownership without maintenance.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Cancel, isolate runner, preserve logs, rotate credentials, quarantine output, restore trusted workflow, and reassess downstream impact. Reopen on workflow, trigger, permission, tool, or artifact change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Workflow, trigger, permissions, runners, steps, artifacts, evidence, failure, owners, maintenance, exceptions, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve workflow versions, permissions, runs, logs, artifacts, findings, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to CI, CD, Build, Security, Platform, Release, and Operations.
