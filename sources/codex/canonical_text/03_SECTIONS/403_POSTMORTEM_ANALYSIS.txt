SECTION 403 — Postmortem Analysis

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Analyze a significant incident, failure, near miss, or harmful outcome to reconstruct what happened, why defenses failed, what was learned, and which actions reduce recurrence.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A blameless postmortem with scope, timeline, impact, detection, response, contributing conditions, uncertainty, evidence, actions, owners, due dates, and follow-up.

WHY THIS MATTERS

A postmortem can improve systems or become blame theater. It must distinguish proximate event from systemic conditions and preserve uncertainty rather than inventing a neat story.

APPLICABILITY

Apply to material incident, outage, safety event, security event, privacy event, near miss, failed recovery, or recurring problem where learning and action matter.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when the event has no material impact, recurrence, learning, or control consequence and a bounded incident record is sufficient.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use incident, problem, forensic, observability, evidence, safety, privacy, legal, quality, continuity, and change records.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Event, timeline, impact, detection, response, systems, changes, dependencies, evidence, decisions, communications, user reports, and actions.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Facilitators protect psychological safety and evidence integrity; owners accept actions and resources; humans resolve accountability, legal, safety, and communication boundaries.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may organize authorized timeline and evidence, identify patterns and questions, and draft action candidates. It must not assign blame, infer causality, expose private evidence, or close action without human review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Postmortem Facilitator, Incident Responder, Problem Manager, Researcher, Analyst, Forensic Analyst, Critic, Verifier, Governance Reviewer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Set scope and participants; preserve evidence; reconstruct timeline; quantify impact; examine detection and response; identify contributing conditions; record dissent; assign actions; review; and follow up.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show evidence, fact, inference, uncertainty, decision, impact, participant, and limitation. Require human validation of timeline, cause, harm, action, and external communication.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Link incident, change, configuration, dependency, alert, log, trace, metric, user, action, and control. Preserve immutable evidence and action tracking.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show event, impact, timeline, evidence freshness, contributing factors, actions, owners, due dates, and authorized challenge, assign, remediate, escalate, and reopen controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Postmortem charter; timeline and impact; evidence and uncertainty; contributing conditions; action register; decision, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify event, source, time basis, action, actor, impact, hypothesis, confidence, and limitation. A plausible root-cause narrative is not proof.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify timeline, impact, evidence, detection, response, contributing conditions, action ownership, due dates, recurrence measures, privacy, and blameless review.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Blame, hindsight, single root cause, action without owner, evidence discarded, impact minimized, postmortem delayed until memory fades, and closure by document publication.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Correct the record, protect participants, update actions, preserve evidence, communicate, and reopen when new evidence or recurrence appears. Do not erase uncertainty to close.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Scope, impact, timeline, evidence, uncertainty, contributing conditions, actions, owners, follow-up, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve postmortem, evidence, actions, decisions, communications, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Problem, Lessons Learned, SRE, Security, Safety, Quality, Governance, and Operations.
