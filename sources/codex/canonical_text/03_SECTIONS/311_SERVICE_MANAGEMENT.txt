SECTION 311 — Service Management

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Govern the design, delivery, support, change, performance, continuity, and improvement of services from a user's and owner's outcome perspective.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A service-management model with catalog, owner, consumer, level, request, incident, problem, change, knowledge, supplier, measure, continuity, and improvement.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Service work crosses technical and organizational boundaries. Without explicit service, consumer, support, and outcome contracts, teams measure components while users experience unresolved failure.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a capability is provided as an ongoing service with consumers, support, expectations, incidents, requests, changes, or service commitments.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no ongoing service, consumer, support, commitment, or operational outcome exists and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use operational architecture, service levels, product, support, incident, change, problem, capacity, security, privacy, supplier, and continuity baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Service, consumers, outcomes, catalog, level, support, requests, incidents, changes, suppliers, measures, costs, risks, and recovery.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Service owners define outcomes, commitments, support, priority, change, incident, supplier, cost, and risk. Humans approve service levels, exceptions, communication, and retirement.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may summarize service records, classify requests, identify trends, and draft knowledge or improvement. It must not make commitments, prioritize harm, close incidents, deny support, or change service without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Service Manager, Product Manager, Operations Engineer, Support Reviewer, Incident Responder, Problem Analyst, Researcher, Analyst, Verifier, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define service and catalog; identify consumers; set levels and support; operate request, incident, problem, change, supplier, knowledge, continuity, measure, and improvement processes; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show service, consumer, outcome, level, request, incident, evidence, authority, and limitation. Require human review for priority, commitment, user impact, escalation, and closure.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Link service, component, owner, dependency, contract, request, incident, problem, change, evidence, and level. Provide accessible support, status, communication, and recovery.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show service health, level, consumer impact, requests, incidents, changes, suppliers, cost, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized prioritize, communicate, recover, accept, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Service charter and catalog; consumer and support model; service levels; request, incident, problem, change, supplier, knowledge, continuity, decision, evidence, and improvement records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify service, consumer, outcome, level, period, source, method, result, actor, and limitation. Ticket closure or SLA percentage proves neither user resolution nor service quality.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify catalog, ownership, support, request, incident, problem, change, communication, service level, supplier, accessibility, continuity, and user outcome.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Component metric as service, ticket closure theater, SLA gaming, no consumer voice, unowned supplier, support inaccessible, and incident without problem learning.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Protect users, communicate, restore service, correct records, escalate, and reassess commitment and residual impact. Reopen on user, service, supplier, level, or operational change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Service, consumers, outcomes, ownership, support, levels, records, measures, evidence, recovery, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner and consumer acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve catalog, contracts, tickets, incidents, changes, supplier records, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Operations, Support, SRE, Product, Security, Governance, and Retirement.
