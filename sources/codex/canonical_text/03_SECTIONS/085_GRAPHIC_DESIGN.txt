SECTION 085 — Graphic Design

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define the design of visual compositions, symbols, illustrations, diagrams, and graphic assets that communicate an authorized message or support a task.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

 - Scoped audience, message, medium, cultural context, accessibility, asset rights, production, and lifecycle.
 - Approved or proposed graphic rules, assets, provenance, tests, evidence, and handoff.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Graphics can clarify or distort meaning, exclude audiences, or create rights and safety risk. Production quality is not comprehension.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to print, digital, instructional, brand, editorial, data, packaging, environmental, and product graphics.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No material message, representation, accessibility, asset, rights, or task effect; or an unchanged approved graphic baseline with impact recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Message, audience, content, domain, visual system, research, accessibility, localization, asset provenance, rights, production constraints, and acceptance authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Message, audience, content, domain, visual system, research, accessibility, localization, asset provenance, rights, production constraints, and acceptance authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve meaning, representation, rights, audience, cultural context, accessibility, publication, and acceptance.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft layouts and assets, but must preserve provenance, licensing, context, and uncertainty. It must not publish or claim audience comprehension without review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Graphic Designer, Visual Designer, UX Designer, Content Designer, Researcher, Accessibility specialist, Governance Reviewer, Critic, Tester, Verifier, Implementer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define message and audience; gather references; create alternatives; check hierarchy, representation, accessibility, rights, production, localization, and use context; test; document; hand off.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show message, source, audience, actor, status, accessible alternative, and consequence. AI drafts remain proposed and must not obscure warnings or controls.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Control asset identity, vector or raster constraints, color, type, composition, export, metadata, localization, accessibility, rights, versioning, and archival.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show graphic baseline, asset source, license, audience, medium, accessibility, evidence, findings, and controls to approve, replace, withdraw, or restore.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Graphic brief, concepts, final or proposed assets, source and rights register, specifications, accessibility review, research, tests, decisions, and handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify asset, source, license, audience, medium, version, method, result, and limitation. A polished composition proves bounded appearance only.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify message comprehension, contrast, scaling, nonvisual alternative, localization, rights, production fidelity, and representative audience response.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Unlicensed assets; stereotypes; decorative overload; inaccessible text in images; misleading scale or cropping; and publication without provenance.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Withdraw harmful or infringing work, preserve source and decision history, issue an accessible replacement, and retest affected contexts.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Message, audience, assets, rights, accessibility, evidence, tests, recovery, open issues, and acceptance authority are explicit. Remain CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve asset IDs, sources, rights, versions, findings, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and next action. Hand off to Visual, UI, Content, Localization, Accessibility, and Publishing.
