SECTION 100 — Copywriting

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define persuasive, clear, and responsible language for an approved audience, offer, identity, or action without misleading, coercing, or hiding material conditions.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

 - A declared audience, objective, claim, channel, tone, evidence, rights, accessibility, localization, and lifecycle scope.
 - Copy briefs, drafts, approvals, source records, tests, corrections, and publication controls.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Copy can create commitments, shape consent, and influence vulnerable audiences. Persuasion must not substitute for truth, comprehension, or fair choice.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to marketing, product, campaign, transactional, onboarding, notification, fundraising, public, and brand copy.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No material claim, offer, disclosure, consent, audience, accessibility, localization, or action effect; or an unchanged approved copy baseline with impact recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Objective, audience research, offer, product behavior, substantiation, legal and policy constraints, brand, accessibility, localization, privacy, and approval authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Objective, audience research, offer, product behavior, substantiation, legal and policy constraints, brand, accessibility, localization, privacy, and approval authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve claims, audience, fairness, disclosure, consent, rights, risk, publication, and acceptance. They retain correction, withdrawal, and complaint authority.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may generate variants and analyze performance. It must preserve substantiation, audience context, and uncertainty and must not invent testimonials, scarcity, guarantees, or targeting rationale.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Copywriter, Content Designer, UX Writer, Researcher, Product Owner, Legal and Governance Reviewers, Accessibility specialist, Localization, Tester, Verifier, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define objective and audience; gather substantiation; draft and review claims; check accessibility, localization, fairness, consent, rights, and behavior; test comprehension; publish, monitor, correct, and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show claim source, audience, actor, offer, material condition, consent, automation, and correction. AI variants remain drafts and critical disclosures cannot be hidden.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Connect copy to product state, pricing, policy, identity, localization, accessibility, analytics, records, and legal approval. Separate persuasion from authorization.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show copy version, claim source, audience, locale, approval, evidence, performance limits, complaints, and controls to approve, correct, withdraw, or restore.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Copy brief, claims and substantiation register, variants, content and legal review, accessibility and localization review, test evidence, decisions, and handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify claim, source, audience, channel, version, method, result, limitation, and reviewer. Conversion or engagement is bounded evidence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify factual claims, material conditions, comprehension, accessibility, localization, consent, fairness, behavior alignment, rights, and implementation fidelity.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Dark patterns; false urgency; hidden fees; fake social proof; inaccessible disclosures; unsubstantiated superiority; and optimizing conversion over informed choice.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause publication, correct or withdraw claims, notify affected audiences, preserve evidence, offer remedy where authorized, and retest before reuse.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Audience, objective, claims, substantiation, disclosures, evidence, tests, recovery, risks, and acceptance are explicit. Remain CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve briefs, claims, sources, variants, approvals, locales, decisions, complaints, evidence, and next action. Hand off to Content, Product, Legal, Localization, Accessibility, and Marketing.
