SECTION 221 — Policy

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Create, approve, communicate, implement, monitor, interpret, review, and retire governing statements that direct behavior, authority, risk, rights, and controls.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A policy lifecycle with purpose, scope, authority, definitions, obligations, exceptions, enforcement, implementation owners, communication, evidence, review, appeal, change, and retirement.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Policies can be ambiguous, contradictory, inaccessible, unenforced, or detached from actual work. A signed document is not proof that people understood it, systems implemented it, or exceptions were governed.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a rule, standard, principle, commitment, restriction, or decision framework governs people, systems, data, products, operations, suppliers, or agents.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no governing behavior, authority, protected outcome, or control is affected and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Identify authority, affected parties, purpose, legal and regulatory sources, existing policies, processes, controls, enforcement, accessibility, communication, and review owner.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Objective, scope, authority, definitions, principles, requirements, exceptions, stakeholders, risks, evidence, implementation, communication, appeals, review cadence, and source conflicts.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Policy owners draft and maintain policy; authorized humans approve, interpret material ambiguity, grant exceptions, resolve conflicts, communicate changes, and retire obsolete rules. Affected people must have a route to question, appeal, or report harm.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may compare sources, identify contradictions, draft language, map policy to controls, and summarize feedback. It must preserve authority, source, version, scope, and uncertainty. It must not silently change meaning, create obligations, interpret law conclusively, approve, enforce, or hide dissent without human authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Policy Analyst, Legal Advisor, Governance Reviewer, Researcher, Analyst, User Experience Reviewer, Accessibility Reviewer, Critic, Verifier, Documentation Writer, and Memory Curator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define need and authority; inventory sources; draft and review; resolve conflicts; assess impact and accessibility; approve and publish; map to controls and procedures; train and communicate; monitor exceptions and outcomes; review and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show source, authority, version, scope, definition, interpretation, affected party, and uncertainty. Require human approval for policy, exception, enforcement, legal meaning, and material communication. Present changes and impacts clearly and accessibly.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Represent policy, version, authority, scope, definitions, controls, exceptions, decisions, acknowledgments, and evidence as linked records. Ensure enforcement systems reference an approved version and fail safely when policy is unavailable or conflicting.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show policy status, owner, authority, scope, version, effective date, controls, exceptions, acknowledgments, findings, evidence freshness, and authorized approve, communicate, interpret, exempt, review, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Policy charter and text; authority and source register; impact and accessibility review; control mapping; exception and appeal procedure; communication and training; evidence, decision, review, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify policy, authority, version, scope, effective date, source, reviewer, control, implementation, communication, result, and limitation. Publication or acknowledgment proves neither comprehension nor operation.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify authority, definitions, conflicts, scope, accessibility, implementation, communication, control behavior, exceptions, appeal, review, and retirement. Sample actual operations and user understanding rather than relying only on signatures.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Policy sprawl, contradictory rules, undefined terms, secret policy, inaccessible text, no owner, exceptions without expiry, and enforcement that differs from the approved policy.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause or narrow affected enforcement, preserve versions and decisions, notify owners, correct communication, remediate harm, and use the prior valid policy where authorized. Reopen on legal, operational, technological, user, or risk change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Purpose, authority, scope, sources, definitions, controls, exceptions, communication, evidence, review, appeal, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending authorized approval and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve policy versions, approvals, interpretations, communications, acknowledgments, exceptions, evidence, findings, and open gaps. Hand off to Legal, Compliance, Governance, Operations, Security, Privacy, Support, and Documentation.
