SECTION 317 — Metrics Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define, instrument, calculate, validate, govern, and retire measures that describe system, service, user, business, security, quality, cost, and operational outcomes.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A metric model with definition, owner, unit, population, dimension, source, calculation, window, freshness, quality, threshold, action, lineage, and limitation.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Metrics create incentives and decisions. A precise-looking number can be semantically wrong, gamed, stale, biased, or disconnected from the outcome it claims to represent.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a measure informs operation, quality, performance, service level, governance, product, security, safety, cost, or human decision.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no metric, decision, threshold, outcome, or evidence claim changes and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use data, analytics, observability, service-level, quality, user research, privacy, security, and governance baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Question, outcome, definition, source, population, unit, dimensions, window, calculation, owner, threshold, action, freshness, and limitation.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Metric owners define meaning, use, threshold, and decision authority; domain owners validate semantics; humans approve consequential interpretation and resist metric gaming.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft metric definitions, inspect authorized data and telemetry, identify anomalies, and compare trends. It must not redefine meaning, infer causality, hide contrary measures, or make decisions from a metric without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Metrics Engineer, Data Analyst, Data Engineer, SRE, Product Analyst, Researcher, Privacy Reviewer, Verifier, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define decision and outcome; establish metric contract; instrument source; calculate; validate population and lineage; set threshold and action; monitor quality and freshness; review incentives; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show definition, source, population, window, calculation, owner, threshold, evidence, uncertainty, and limitation. Require human review for semantic, causal, high-impact, and target-setting decisions.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use metric catalog, semantic layer, lineage, versioning, quality checks, time and identity, dimensions, privacy, retention, alert, and reproducible calculation.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show metric definition, source, freshness, quality, trend, threshold, owner, consumers, evidence, and authorized correct, restrict, annotate, retire, and reopen controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Metric charter and catalog; source and lineage; calculation and quality; threshold and action; decision, evidence, limitation, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify metric, definition, source, population, window, version, method, time, result, owner, uncertainty, and limitation. A chart or number proves only its declared calculation and slice.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify meaning, grain, source, lineage, calculation, freshness, dimensions, privacy, threshold, alert, reproducibility, and relationship to outcome. Test missing and contradictory data.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Vanity metric, denominator drift, hidden filter, stale value, target gaming, causation from correlation, aggregate hiding harm, and metric as truth without source.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Annotate or withdraw affected metric, preserve versions, notify consumers, correct source or calculation, and reassess decisions. Reopen on definition, source, population, window, policy, or outcome change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Question, outcome, definition, source, population, calculation, owner, evidence, threshold, action, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve metric versions, definitions, lineage, data, dashboards, decisions, incidents, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Analytics, Data, BI, SRE, Service Management, Product, and Governance.
