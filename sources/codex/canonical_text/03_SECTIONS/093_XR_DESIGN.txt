SECTION 093 — XR Design

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define extended-reality experiences spanning augmented, virtual, mixed, and emerging spatial modalities while preserving user agency, safety, accessibility, privacy, and continuity.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

 - A bounded XR population, environment, device, modality, content, interaction, tracking, safety, privacy, and lifecycle scope.
 - Cross-modal behavior, evidence, tests, fallback, controls, and acceptance records.

WHY THIS MATTERS

XR combines physical and synthetic context and can fail through tracking, embodiment, attention, identity, or environment mismatch. A compelling experience is not proof of safe or inclusive use.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to systems combining AR, VR, spatial, embodied, immersive, wearable, or collaborative interfaces.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No modality, spatial, physical, privacy, accessibility, or user-control effect; or an unchanged approved XR baseline with impact recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Modalities, users, environments, devices, spatial models, content, interaction, research, physical safety, privacy, identity, accessibility, performance, and authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Modalities, users, environments, devices, spatial models, content, interaction, research, physical safety, privacy, identity, accessibility, performance, and authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve modality, content, safety, consent, privacy, accessibility, representation, and acceptance and retain physical stop and exit authority.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may generate or adapt XR content and analyze context. It must disclose uncertainty and generation and must not manipulate users or act physically without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

XR Designer, Spatial, AR, VR, UX, Interaction, Research, Safety, Privacy, Accessibility, Security, Testing, Verification, Governance, Implementation, and Documentation roles.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define modalities, transitions, environment, tracking, content, embodiment, input, accessibility, privacy, safety, performance, moderation, fallback, and recovery; prototype and test cross-device and cross-modal paths.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show modality, physical and virtual state, tracking, actor, authority, content source, uncertainty, hazards, and exit. AI adaptations remain proposed and nonimmersive fallback remains available.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Define modality contracts, coordinate systems, identity, data, rendering, latency, calibration, boundaries, shared space, accessibility, and graceful degradation.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show XR session, modality, user, device, environment, hazards, evidence, and controls to pause, switch mode, exit, supervise, or recover.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

XR architecture, modality and transition model, spatial and content specifications, safety, privacy, accessibility, moderation, tests, evidence, decisions, and handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify user, modality, device, environment, content, version, method, result, uncertainty, and limitation. A demo proves bounded conditions only.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify transitions, tracking, latency, comfort, accessibility, privacy, safety, shared space, content provenance, moderation, fallback, and exit.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Treating immersion as safety; hiding mode changes; no physical awareness; inaccessible transitions; unbounded generated content; and one-device evidence.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause, exit, switch modality, preserve evidence, provide supervision, and isolate harmful content or equipment before resuming.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Modalities, users, devices, safety, privacy, accessibility, evidence, tests, recovery, risks, and acceptance are explicit. Remain CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve session and content identity, device and calibration, findings, incidents, decisions, evidence, and next action. Hand off to AR, VR, Spatial, HCI, Safety, Privacy, Accessibility, and Testing.
