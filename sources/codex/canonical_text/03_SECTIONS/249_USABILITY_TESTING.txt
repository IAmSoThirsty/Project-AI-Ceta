SECTION 249 — Usability Testing

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Learn whether representative people can understand, operate, recover from, and derive intended value from a capability in realistic context.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A usability-test model with users, tasks, context, prototype or product, observation method, consent, accessibility, findings, severity, design response, and limitations.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Usability is contextual and cannot be inferred from interface polish, completion analytics, or expert opinion alone. A task can be completed while users misunderstand risk, lose agency, or need hidden support.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a person must understand, decide, operate, recover, learn, or trust a user-facing or operator-facing capability.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no human-facing behavior, task, decision, support, or control changes and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use user research, requirements, design, accessibility, privacy, safety, security, localization, support, and acceptance baselines. Obtain informed participation and protect participant data.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Users, tasks, context, goals, content, prototype or product, hypotheses, success criteria, accessibility, consent, data handling, observer, and acceptance questions.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Researchers recruit and protect participants; owners accept findings and prioritize change; humans interpret lived experience and decide tradeoffs. Participants may stop, withdraw, or report harm.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft tasks, analyze authorized notes, identify patterns, and propose design questions. It must not fabricate users, infer intent from sparse behavior, expose participant data, or decide usability or release without human interpretation.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

User Researcher, User Experience Reviewer, Accessibility Reviewer, Researcher, Analyst, Observer, Tester, Verifier, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define question and users; recruit ethically; prepare realistic task and context; observe comprehension, behavior, errors, confidence, and recovery; analyze; share findings; design changes; retest; and preserve participant care and limitations.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show participant context, task, observation, interpretation, evidence, uncertainty, and privacy handling. Require human review and do not represent AI synthesis as direct participant voice.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Support accessible interaction, explainability, undo, error recovery, support, privacy, instrumentation, safe prototypes, and separation of participant data from production records.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show research question, users, tasks, findings, severity, evidence freshness, participant care, owner, and authorized prioritize, redesign, accommodate, accept, and reopen controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Usability charter; participant and consent record; task and context set; observation and finding ledger; design response; test, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify participant or segment, task, context, method, observer, time, observation, interpretation, impact, confidence, and limitation. Completion rate or quote alone proves only its bounded claim.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify participant fit, consent, task realism, accessibility, comprehension, error recovery, privacy, analysis traceability, design response, and repeatability. Include people who use support or alternative methods.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Testing friends or ideal users only, leading tasks, treating analytics as understanding, ignoring failure and recovery, exposing participant data, and optimizing speed over comprehension or safety.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Protect participants, stop harmful study, correct design, preserve findings, and retest with affected users. Reopen on population, context, task, interface, content, or risk change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Question, users, tasks, context, evidence, findings, limitations, design response, owners, participant care, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending user-informed review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve consent, participant protections, findings, design decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Product, UX, Accessibility, Engineering, Support, Acceptance, Privacy, and Operations.
