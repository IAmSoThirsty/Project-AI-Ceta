SECTION 383 — Platform Migration

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Move workloads, services, infrastructure, identities, data, configuration, and operations from one platform to another while preserving outcomes, security, continuity, and exit evidence.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A platform-migration model with source and target, inventory, equivalence, dependencies, data, identity, compatibility, rehearsal, cutover, rollback, support, cost, and retirement.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Platform migration changes hidden assumptions about runtime, identity, networking, storage, provider, performance, cost, and support. A workload running on the target is not proof of equivalent or acceptable service.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when workloads, applications, data, infrastructure, tooling, or operations move between providers, platforms, runtimes, regions, or architectural foundations.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no platform, workload, dependency, identity, data, cost, or operational behavior changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use architecture, cloud, infrastructure, data, identity, security, cost, compatibility, testing, release, operations, continuity, and exit baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Source, target, workloads, dependencies, identity, data, configuration, performance, cost, support, rehearsal, cutover, rollback, and retirement.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Migration owners approve scope, equivalence, downtime, data, security, cost, cutover, rollback, communication, and retirement. Humans accept provider and operational tradeoffs.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inventory authorized platform metadata, compare options, draft mapping and tests, and identify gaps. It must not migrate, delete source, change access, expose data, or declare equivalence without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Migration Engineer, Architect, Cloud Engineer, Platform Engineer, Data Engineer, Security Auditor, Tester, Verifier, Operations Engineer, and Planner.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Inventory and baseline; define equivalence; map dependencies and identity; plan data and config; rehearse; test; cut over; observe; roll back or complete; retire source; and document.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show source, target, workload, dependency, identity, data, cost, evidence, action, owner, and limitation. Require human approval for cutover, deletion, production, data, security, and exit.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use parallel or staged migration, compatibility, identity mapping, data replication, configuration translation, observability, rollback, provider exit, and support readiness.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show migration phase, source and target, workloads, health, data, cost, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized pause, test, cut over, rollback, reconcile, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Migration charter; source and target; equivalence and dependency; rehearsal and cutover; data, identity, cost, support, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify source, target, workload, version, dependency, data, method, time, result, cost, user outcome, and limitation. Successful target deployment proves neither equivalence nor exit.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify workload, identity, data, config, network, storage, performance, security, cost, support, recovery, rollback, cutover, and source retirement. Test provider loss and partial migration.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Lift-and-shift assumption, source deleted early, identity mismatch, cost surprise, compatibility hidden, data split, no rollback, and provider exit ignored.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause, preserve both platforms, roll back or restore, reconcile data, communicate, and reassess outcome and cost. Reopen on platform, workload, provider, data, or requirement change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Source, target, workloads, dependencies, identity, data, equivalence, cutover, rollback, cost, evidence, owners, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve inventory, mappings, rehearsals, cutovers, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Cloud, Platform, Data, Security, Operations, Support, Finance, and Retirement.
