SECTION 222 — Ethics

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Make value conflicts, foreseeable harms, affected parties, power differences, duties, tradeoffs, and accountability explicit in decisions that are not settled by technical or legal analysis alone.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An ethics assessment with human purpose, affected parties, values, options, harms, benefits, uncertainty, dissent, safeguards, decision authority, monitoring, appeal, and reconsideration.

WHY THIS MATTERS

A technically feasible or legally permitted action can still be harmful, unfair, exploitative, deceptive, or inconsistent with the human purpose. Ethics work must not become a decorative approval after the decision is already fixed.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when choices affect people, rights, dignity, safety, opportunity, privacy, communities, environment, future users, or power relationships, especially when automation or AI influences outcomes.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability only when the change has no affected person, value conflict, material externality, or consequential choice and an approved exact baseline supports it. Technical simplicity is not a sufficient reason.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use user research, risk, privacy, safety, security, legal, accessibility, policy, impact, and governance evidence. Identify affected and absent voices, decision owner, escalation, and review authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Objective, options, stakeholders, affected non-users, values, constraints, benefits, harms, uncertainty, evidence, alternatives, power differences, safeguards, monitoring, and unresolved dissent.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans identify values, listen to affected people, resolve moral tradeoffs, set unacceptable harm, approve safeguards, and accept or reject residual impact. No agent may transfer that responsibility to a score, majority, policy, or technical metric.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may surface affected parties, compare options, identify value conflicts, summarize supplied perspectives, and challenge assumptions. It must not claim moral authority, manufacture consensus, erase dissent, infer values from proxy data, or decide human worth, rights, harm, or acceptable tradeoff without accountable human deliberation.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Ethics Reviewer, Researcher, User Experience Reviewer, Analyst, Critic, Red Team, Privacy Reviewer, Legal Advisor, Safety Reviewer, Governance Reviewer, Verifier, Documentation Writer, and Memory Curator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Frame the decision; identify affected and missing voices; articulate values and conflicts; generate alternatives; assess harms and benefits; test assumptions; design safeguards and recourse; document dissent; obtain accountable decision; monitor outcomes; and set reopening triggers.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show whose interests, evidence, values, uncertainty, assumptions, and tradeoffs are represented. Require human deliberation for material impact. Make participation accessible and do not present AI-generated balance as actual consultation.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Represent purpose, affected parties, values, harms, safeguards, decisions, dissent, monitoring, appeal, and evidence as durable records. Build in human override, transparency, privacy, accessibility, and recovery for systems that operationalize ethical commitments.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show decision, affected parties, values, options, harms, safeguards, dissent, owner, evidence, unresolved questions, and authorized pause, reject, revise, escalate, appeal, and reopen controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Ethics charter; affected-party and value map; options and harm assessment; safeguard and recourse design; dissent record; decision, monitoring, review, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify claim, affected party, source, perspective, method, date, assumption, uncertainty, decision, safeguard, and limitation. A principle, survey, model output, or legal permission is not complete ethical evidence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify affected-party coverage, alternatives, harm analysis, safeguard operation, accessibility, recourse, dissent preservation, outcome monitoring, and decision authority. Revisit with new evidence and lived experience.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Ethics washing, token consultation, consensus theater, value substitution by metrics, absent affected parties, harm externalized to powerless people, and “legal means ethical” reasoning.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause or narrow harmful action, protect affected people, preserve evidence, seek missing perspectives, remedy where possible, and revisit the decision with accountable owners. Reopen on new harm, population, purpose, evidence, technology, or value change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Purpose, affected parties, values, options, harms, safeguards, authority, evidence, dissent, recourse, monitoring, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending independent challenge and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve assessment, perspectives, dissent, decision, safeguards, incidents, monitoring, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Legal, Privacy, Safety, Trust and Safety, Policy, Governance, UX, Testing, and Operations.
