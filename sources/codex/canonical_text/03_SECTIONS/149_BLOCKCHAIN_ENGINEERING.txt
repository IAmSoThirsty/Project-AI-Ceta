SECTION 149 - Blockchain Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define governed engineering of shared ledgers, blockchain networks, wallets, nodes, consensus, transactions, cryptographic identity, data publication, and lifecycle without confusing immutability with correctness.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A blockchain baseline with network purpose, consensus, assets, keys, transaction semantics, privacy, governance, observability, recovery, upgrade, and retirement controls.

WHY THIS MATTERS

An immutable record can preserve an error forever. Key loss, consensus failure, opaque fees, privacy leakage, and irreversible transactions require explicit authority and compensation planning.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to permissioned or public ledgers, blockchain infrastructure, wallets, nodes, tokens, proofs, on-chain records, and ledger-connected applications.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

May be non-applicable where a conventional authoritative store satisfies the outcome and no consensus, shared ledger, token, or on-chain integrity claim exists. Record scope, owner, evidence, and reopening trigger.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Requires ledger purpose, participants, consensus, threat model, key authority, asset and data model, privacy, fees, finality, governance, legal, recovery, and retirement authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Provide transactions, identities, keys, assets, state, consensus, finality, fees, privacy, validators, off-chain links, upgrade, incident, and compensation requirements.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve network purpose, validators, keys, assets, legal and privacy use, upgrades, fees, risk, and acceptance. Humans retain authority over irreversible transactions and governance changes.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inspect transactions, model fees, draft code, and detect anomalies. It must not hold or request secrets, sign transactions, alter consensus, or declare finality without delegated authority and evidence.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Architect, Cryptography Engineer, Security Auditor, Privacy Reviewer, Analyst, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Governance Reviewer, Observer, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, Coordinator, and Red Team. Existing role contracts remain controlling.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Define participants, ledger, assets, keys, consensus, finality, privacy, and authority.
2. Design transaction, node, wallet, key recovery, monitoring, upgrade, and off-chain reconciliation paths.
3. Test adversarial consensus, replay, key compromise, reorganization, fees, privacy, and recovery.
4. Operate, govern, upgrade, compensate, and retire under explicit decisions.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show network, transaction, signer, key scope, fee, finality, privacy, evidence, and consequence. Human approval is required for signing, upgrades, and material transfers; no conversational path bypasses wallet policy.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate wallet, key, transaction, mempool, consensus, ledger, oracle, off-chain store, and application boundaries. Define nonce, replay, finality, reorg, fee, privacy, audit, and compensation semantics.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show chain, height, node health, transaction, signer, finality, fees, alerts, evidence, and controls to pause signing, revoke keys, isolate nodes, reconcile, or migrate.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Ledger charter, participant and node catalog, asset and transaction model, key and wallet policy, threat and privacy assessment, contract and upgrade records, test evidence, runbook, and retirement plan.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify chain, block, transaction, signer, key reference, code, node, input, result, finality, method, date, and limits. Preserve failed, replaced, reverted, and compensated evidence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify signatures, permissions, replay, nonce, consensus, reorg, finality, key rotation, node loss, privacy, oracle failure, fee exhaustion, upgrade, and compensation.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Treating immutability as truth; exposed private data; unaudited contracts; hot keys; unclear finality; ignoring reorgs; and assuming transactions can be destructively undone.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause signing, revoke or rotate keys, isolate contracts or nodes, preserve ledger evidence, compensate through an authorized transaction, communicate limits, and reopen governance and security review.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Purpose, authority, keys, consensus, evidence, privacy, upgrades, compensation, and retirement are explicit. Keep CONTENT-COMPLETE until independent verification and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve chain, block, transaction, key references, code, decisions, incidents, evidence, and next safe action. Hand off to Blockchain Architecture, Cryptography, Security, Privacy, Smart Contracts, Testing, and Operations.
