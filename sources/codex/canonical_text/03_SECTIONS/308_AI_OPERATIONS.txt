SECTION 308 — AI Operations

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Operate AI models, agents, prompts, tools, data, providers, policies, evaluations, human review, cost, safety, and recovery within explicit authority and use boundaries.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An AI-operations model with identity, model and prompt versions, input and output data, tools, providers, policy, evaluation, monitoring, escalation, human control, rollback, and retirement.

WHY THIS MATTERS

AI behavior can drift with data, prompt, model, provider, tool, context, or policy changes. A successful request does not prove truth, safety, fairness, privacy, or suitability for a consequential decision.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when an AI model, agent, workflow, evaluator, retrieval system, tool call, or generated output operates for users, staff, systems, or decisions.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no AI model, agent, data, provider, prompt, tool, output, or human-impacting behavior is operated or changed and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use AI architecture, model testing, privacy, security, data, identity, policy, evaluation, incident, cost, platform, and operations baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Model, provider, prompt, tool, data, policy, identity, use case, user, output, evaluation, latency, cost, safety, telemetry, fallback, and owner.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

AI owners approve intended use, model, provider, data, tools, authority, human review, thresholds, cost, incident, rollback, and retirement. Humans remain accountable for consequential decisions and user recourse.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may monitor authorized AI runs, identify drift or failures, summarize traces, and recommend bounded fallback. It must label generated content, preserve provenance, not self-authorize, access restricted data, conceal errors, or change model, prompt, policy, tool, or user authority without human control.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

AI Operations Engineer, AI Architect, Model Evaluator, Security Auditor, Privacy Reviewer, Data Engineer, SRE, Incident Responder, Tester, Verifier, and Governance Reviewer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Inventory model and provider; manage versions and prompts; secure data and tools; evaluate; deploy; monitor quality, safety, cost, and drift; review human escalation; respond; fallback; record; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show model, provider, prompt, data, tool, identity, output, confidence, human review, cost, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for high-impact output, tool action, data access, policy, rollback, and closure.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Trace model, prompt, retrieval, tool, policy, input, output, reviewer, and action. Provide guardrails, sandbox, rate limits, redaction, fallback, provider substitution, evaluation, kill switch, and durable audit.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show AI service, model, provider, version, health, drift, safety, cost, human-review rate, incidents, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized pause, restrict, fallback, rollback, revoke, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

AI operations charter; model and provider registry; prompt and tool records; evaluation and monitoring; incident and fallback; human-review, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify model, provider, prompt, input, data, tool, policy, output, reviewer, method, time, result, and limitation. Fluency, confidence, or request success proves neither truth nor safety.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify model and prompt version, data, privacy, security, tool authorization, output quality, safety, drift, cost, latency, human review, fallback, rollback, and incident handling.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

AI output as fact, prompt drift, hidden provider change, tool overreach, no human escalation, sensitive data leakage, evaluation on narrow examples, and cost or latency unbounded.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause or narrow AI use, preserve traces, route to human or fallback, revoke tools, switch provider or model, correct affected decisions, notify owners, and reassess harm. Reopen on model, data, prompt, provider, policy, tool, or population change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Use, model, provider, prompt, tools, data, authority, evaluation, monitoring, human control, recovery, evidence, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve model and prompt versions, traces, evaluations, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, cost, and open gaps. Hand off to AI Engineering, Data, Security, Privacy, SRE, Support, Governance, and Retirement.
