SECTION 293 — Installation Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Install, upgrade, configure, verify, roll back, uninstall, and recover a package or capability on its target while protecting data, users, service, and evidence.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An installation model with prerequisites, target, identity, package, state transition, configuration, migration, permissions, validation, rollback, support, and cleanup.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Installation crosses the boundary from artifact to live state. Partial installation, incompatible prerequisites, hidden migration, privilege, or cleanup failure can leave a system unusable or insecure.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a package, update, service, configuration, firmware, model, or infrastructure component is installed, upgraded, removed, or restored.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no target state, package, configuration, data, or installation behavior changes and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use packaging, provisioning, configuration, identity, data migration, compatibility, deployment, backup, rollback, support, and operations baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Package, target, prerequisites, identity, permissions, configuration, data, migration, state, validation, rollback, support, and acceptance.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners approve target, privilege, migration, downtime, rollback, support, exception, and completion. Humans decide whether data and service can be changed safely.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft installation steps, check prerequisites, analyze logs, and suggest recovery. It must not install, grant privilege, alter data, or claim completion without delegated authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Installation Engineer, Release Engineer, Deployment Engineer, Systems Administrator, Tester, Verifier, Security Auditor, Support Reviewer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Validate package and target; check prerequisites and authority; back up; install or upgrade; migrate; validate; record; roll back or repair; clean up; support; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show package, target, prerequisites, privilege, state, data, action, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for production, data, privileged, destructive, and recovery actions.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use transactional or staged install, prerequisite checks, state checkpoints, least privilege, atomic migration, rollback, idempotence, logs, and safe uninstall.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show target, package, phase, prerequisites, data state, health, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized start, pause, rollback, repair, verify, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Installation specification; target and prerequisite record; execution and state; migration and validation; rollback and support; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify target, package, version, identity, prerequisite, action, state, method, time, result, actor, and limitation. Installer completion proves only its reported steps.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify prerequisite, permission, package, data migration, service, configuration, rollback, uninstall, restart, recovery, logging, and support. Test partial and interrupted installation.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Install without backup, hidden privilege, partial state ignored, migration irreversible, uninstall leaves access, reboot surprise, and installer success treated as operational readiness.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stop, preserve state and logs, roll back or restore, repair data, communicate, and reassess target and users. Reopen on package, target, dependency, data, or prerequisite change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Package, target, prerequisites, permissions, state, data, validation, rollback, support, evidence, owners, limits, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve installation logs, state, package, migrations, failures, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Provisioning, Deployment, Operations, Support, Release, and Security.
