SECTION 096 — Narrative Design

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define story, character, world, theme, dialogue, sequence, choice, consequence, and continuity in support of an intended experience or communication outcome.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

 - A scoped audience, purpose, world, characters, content, medium, choice model, representation, sensitivity, and lifecycle.
 - Coherent narrative structures with provenance, review, evidence, safety, and acceptance records.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Narrative shapes interpretation, identity, empathy, and behavior. Coherence or emotional impact does not excuse deception, exclusion, or unreviewed harm.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to games, interactive media, products, education, simulations, marketing, immersive environments, and conversational or branching narratives.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No story, character, representation, choice, emotional, cultural, safety, or content effect; or an unchanged approved narrative baseline with impact recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Purpose, audience research, world and domain context, character and content rules, medium, localization, sensitivity, accessibility, rights, and acceptance authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Purpose, audience research, world and domain context, character and content rules, medium, localization, sensitivity, accessibility, rights, and acceptance authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve theme, representation, audience, content boundaries, rights, sensitivity, and acceptance. They resolve value conflicts and retain publication and correction authority.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may brainstorm, draft, translate, and check continuity. It must disclose generated content and preserve source, cultural context, and uncertainty; it must not invent lived experience or publish sensitive content without review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Narrative Designer, Game and Level Designers, Content Designer, Researcher, Cultural or domain reviewers, UX Reviewer, Accessibility specialist, Critic, Governance Reviewer, Tester, Verifier, Implementer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define premise, audience, world, characters, arcs, dialogue, branching, consequence, pacing, representation, content warnings, accessibility, localization, rights, and review; test and revise.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show source, author, character, fiction status, choice, consequence, sensitivity, and exit. AI-generated narrative remains proposed and users can skip, report, or reach support.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Maintain story graph, canon, state, branching, content metadata, provenance, localization, moderation, accessibility, and safe interruption. Separate fiction from factual claims.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show narrative version, audience, state, content warnings, representation review, evidence, open issues, and controls to approve, edit, withdraw, skip, or report.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Narrative bible, story graph, character and world records, dialogue, content warnings, rights and provenance, sensitivity and accessibility review, tests, decisions, and handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify source, author, audience, medium, version, cultural context, review method, result, and limitation. Emotional response or continuity score is bounded evidence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify continuity, choice consequences, representation, content warnings, accessibility, localization, age suitability, rights, moderation, and player or audience comprehension.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Stereotype; cultural appropriation; unmarked generation; coercive emotional manipulation; false factual framing; inaccessible dialogue; and branching that invalidates user choice.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Withdraw or revise harmful content, preserve source and decisions, provide warnings and alternate path, notify owners, and retest affected audiences.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Purpose, audience, world, content, representation, rights, evidence, tests, recovery, risks, and acceptance are explicit. Remain CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve story graph, canon, sources, versions, reviews, decisions, evidence, warnings, and next action. Hand off to Game, Level, Content, Localization, UX, Accessibility, Legal, and Testing.
