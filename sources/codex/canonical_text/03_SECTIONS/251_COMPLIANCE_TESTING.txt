SECTION 251 — Compliance Testing

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Test whether declared controls and operating practices satisfy applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, policy, or standard-based criteria for their exact scope and period.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A compliance-test plan with authority, criteria, scope, sample, evidence, test method, exceptions, findings, remediation, attestation boundaries, and qualified owner decision.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Compliance is contextual and time-bound. A control can be designed but not operating, a certificate can be stale, and an automated check can miss the obligation's meaning or population.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a claim of compliance, conformity, certification, attestation, contractual adherence, or regulatory control is required or material.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability only after qualified review of authority, jurisdiction, entity, activity, period, and obligation, with the alternative evidence and reopening condition recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use legal, regulatory, policy, records, control, audit, privacy, security, quality, and evidence baselines. Define qualified reviewer, criteria, and attestation authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Authority, criteria, scope, obligations, controls, population, period, evidence, samples, owners, exceptions, incidents, prior findings, and acceptance criteria.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Qualified compliance, legal, audit, or domain owners approve criteria, interpretation, samples, findings, reports, exceptions, and attestations. Humans never sign beyond evidence or delegate legal judgment to AI.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may organize criteria, map controls, sample authorized evidence, identify omissions, and draft workpapers. It must preserve source, authority, period, sample, and uncertainty; it must not declare compliance, sign, conceal findings, or communicate externally without human approval.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Compliance Tester, Legal Advisor, Auditor, Governance Reviewer, Security Auditor, Privacy Reviewer, Researcher, Analyst, Verifier, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Confirm authority and scope; define criteria and samples; collect evidence; test design and operation; record exceptions; validate with owners; report; remediate; follow up; preserve workpapers; and monitor change.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show authority, criterion, scope, period, sample, control, evidence, method, result, limitation, and reviewer. Require qualified human acceptance for conclusion, exception, attestation, and external report.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Link authority, obligation, control, population, evidence, finding, response, and report. Preserve immutable workpapers, access restrictions, legal holds, segregation, and source versions.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show authority, scope, criteria, evidence coverage, findings, exceptions, due dates, reviewer, attestation status, and authorized assign, challenge, remediate, attest, and reopen controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Compliance-test charter; criteria and sample; workpapers; evidence register; findings and exception record; remediation and follow-up; attestation boundary; decision and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify authority, criterion, scope, period, sample, control, source, method, result, reviewer, and limitation. A clean test proves only its criteria, sample, period, and method.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify applicability, source currency, sample integrity, control operation, evidence, exceptions, remediation, report, attestation, and change monitoring. Obtain qualified sign-off.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Checklist compliance, unsupported attestation, stale criteria, sample manipulation, control description as evidence, and AI-generated report treated as qualified assurance.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Withdraw unsupported result, preserve workpapers, notify authority, remediate, amend report, and reassess deadline or disclosure. Reopen on obligation, scope, period, control, or evidence change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Authority, criteria, scope, sample, evidence, results, findings, remediation, qualification, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending qualified acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve criteria, workpapers, evidence, findings, responses, attestations, decisions, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Legal, Compliance, Audit, Records, Controls, Security, Privacy, Quality, and Governance.
