SECTION 377 — Adaptive Maintenance

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Modify a system, product, service, process, or maintenance approach to remain fit as users, environment, dependencies, technology, threats, regulation, or operating conditions change.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An adaptive-maintenance model with change trigger, current baseline, intended adaptation, impact, compatibility, test, migration, rollout, rollback, evidence, and owner decision.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Adaptation can preserve relevance or introduce uncontrolled redesign. A change motivated by new conditions still requires traceability, safety, compatibility, support, and recovery.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when maintenance changes behavior, interfaces, platform, data, security, performance, compliance, usability, or environment in response to changed conditions.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no maintenance change or changed operating condition affects behavior, support, compatibility, or lifecycle and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use change, requirements, architecture, compatibility, security, privacy, quality, release, operations, and retirement baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Trigger, current baseline, changed condition, objective, impact, users, dependencies, design, test, migration, support, rollback, and acceptance.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners approve adaptation scope, risk, compatibility, migration, support, communication, exception, and retirement. Humans decide whether preserving old behavior remains required.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze change drivers, dependencies, evidence, and options; draft adaptations and tests; and identify gaps. It must not modify production, infer acceptance, remove compatibility, or retire old path without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Adaptive Maintenance Engineer, Architect, Product Manager, Researcher, Analyst, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Security Auditor, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Identify trigger; baseline; assess impact and users; design adaptation; validate security, privacy, compatibility, quality, and operations; migrate; release; monitor; rollback or retire; and update documentation.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show trigger, baseline, changed condition, adaptation, evidence, impact, compatibility, owner, and limitation. Require human approval for behavior, data, security, user, migration, and retirement decisions.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use versioning, compatibility, feature flags, migration, fallback, rollback, data conversion, support, observability, and explicit deprecation and exit path.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show adaptation, trigger, versions, affected users, dependencies, evidence freshness, owner, migration, and authorized approve, phase, rollback, support, deprecate, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Adaptive-maintenance charter; trigger and baseline; impact and design; compatibility and migration; test and rollout; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify trigger, baseline, version, change, user, source, method, time, result, compatibility, owner, and limitation. Adaptation success proves only its tested conditions and outcome.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify trigger, requirements, compatibility, security, privacy, data, quality, migration, support, rollback, user outcome, and retirement. Test old and new paths and changed environment.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Adaptation without baseline, breaking change hidden, old users ignored, migration one-way, support unprepared, dependency drift, and maintenance used as ungoverned redesign.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause or roll back, preserve state and evidence, support affected users, restore compatibility, communicate, and reassess adaptation need. Reopen on condition, user, dependency, threat, law, or design change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Trigger, baseline, adaptation, impact, compatibility, migration, evidence, owners, support, recovery, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve baselines, versions, migrations, tests, support, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Corrective, Preventive, Version, Deprecation, Release, Operations, and Product.
