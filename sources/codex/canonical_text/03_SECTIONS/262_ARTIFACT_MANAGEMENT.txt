SECTION 262 — Artifact Management

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Preserve the identity, provenance, integrity, storage, promotion, distribution, retention, and retirement of artifacts produced or consumed across the lifecycle.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An artifact model with type, version, digest, source, build, dependencies, environment, owner, status, approval, consumers, retention, evidence, rollback, and disposal.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Artifacts are what systems actually deploy, distribute, analyze, or archive. A source commit or successful build does not prove that the shipped artifact is the same, complete, authorized, or safe.

APPLICABILITY

Apply to binaries, packages, containers, firmware, models, datasets, reports, documentation, infrastructure plans, test evidence, and other lifecycle outputs.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no artifact is retained, promoted, consumed, or used as evidence and an approved exact baseline covers the work.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use source, package, dependency, build, security, license, release, environment, records, retention, and recovery baselines. Identify repository, registry, owner, and promotion authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Artifact type, source, version, digest, build, dependencies, metadata, owner, environment, approval, scan, consumers, retention, distribution, and recovery.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners approve artifact status, promotion, distribution, retention, withdrawal, exception, and deletion. Humans verify provenance and consequences before release or use.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inspect authorized metadata, compare artifacts, draft manifests and checks, and identify anomalies. It must not fabricate provenance, promote, alter, replace, publish, or delete artifacts without explicit authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Artifact Manager, Build Engineer, Release Engineer, Security Auditor, Dependency Manager, Tester, Verifier, Governance Reviewer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define artifact classes; capture metadata and provenance; hash and sign where required; scan; store; approve; promote; distribute; monitor; revoke or replace; back up; retain; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show artifact, digest, source, build, dependency, environment, status, owner, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for promotion, distribution, withdrawal, retention exception, and deletion.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use immutable storage, content addressing, provenance, SBOM, access control, quarantine, promotion stages, retention, replication, rollback, and consumer impact tracking.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show artifact identity, status, provenance, consumers, findings, evidence freshness, retention, owner, and authorized approve, promote, quarantine, withdraw, restore, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Artifact inventory and schema; provenance and integrity record; storage and promotion model; scan and approval; distribution and rollback; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify artifact, digest, source, build, dependencies, environment, signer, method, time, result, actor, and limitation. Filename, tag, or registry path alone proves neither identity nor integrity.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify identity, provenance, digest, signature, metadata, scan, access, promotion, distribution, rollback, withdrawal, retention, and recovery. Test corruption, replacement, provider loss, and consumer mismatch.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Mutable artifact, missing digest, build output not retained, artifact not linked to source, unscanned release, broad registry access, and retirement without consumer impact analysis.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Quarantine or withdraw, preserve evidence, restore known-good artifact, notify consumers, rotate trust if needed, and reassess releases and records. Reopen on source, build, dependency, policy, or consumer change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Artifacts, identity, provenance, owners, status, consumers, storage, promotion, evidence, recovery, retention, exceptions, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve artifact and digest history, build links, consumers, promotions, findings, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Build, Release, Package, Security, Records, Platform, and Operations.
