SECTION 113 — Operating Systems Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define the engineering of operating-system services that manage hardware, processes, memory, files, devices, identity, isolation, scheduling, and system calls.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A versioned OS contract, kernel and user-space boundaries, security model, driver and hardware assumptions, compatibility, test evidence, update, recovery, and retirement plan.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Operating systems mediate nearly every resource and authority. A defect can compromise confidentiality, integrity, availability, safety, or recoverability across all applications.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to kernels, system services, distributions, boot systems, filesystems, process models, security subsystems, device support, and OS-level configuration or update.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Using an unchanged supported OS without altering OS behavior, configuration baseline, trust boundary, or support claim is not OS engineering; record the baseline.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Requires hardware and platform model, threat model, privilege model, resource goals, compatibility, update, backup, recovery, testing, support, and acceptance authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

System calls, processes, memory, storage, scheduling, identity, permissions, boot, update, logging, hardware, drivers, failure, recovery, target platforms, and lifecycle obligations.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve privilege, security, safety, compatibility, update, release, incident, and retirement decisions. They retain authority to isolate, stop, restore, and accept residual risk.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze source and telemetry, draft tests and documentation, and propose fixes. It must not alter kernel, privilege, boot, update, or recovery controls without explicit authority and independent review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Architect, Analyst, Researcher, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Security Auditor, Red Team, Critic, Governance Reviewer, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, and Optimizer. Existing role contracts remain controlling.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Establish platform, hardware, scope, privilege, trust, lifecycle, and non-goals.
2. Specify kernel, service, device, storage, process, identity, update, telemetry, and recovery contracts.
3. Implement with least privilege, isolation, safe defaults, diagnostics, and reproducible builds.
4. Test faults, hostile inputs, upgrades, hardware loss, corruption, recovery, and compatibility.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

AI-assisted OS changes remain proposed until human review. Show toolchain, source, target, authority, evidence, and affected assets. Keep offline administration, safe boot, recovery, and evidence access available.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate kernel, services, drivers, firmware, configuration, secrets, hardware, boot artifacts, and logs. Enforce privilege separation, secure update, rollback or recovery, integrity measurement, and compatibility.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show platform, version, boot state, privilege, patch, driver, resource, security, evidence, and recovery state with authorized isolate, restore, update, and rollback controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

OS architecture and threat model, source, build and signing records, configuration baseline, compatibility matrix, driver inventory, tests, incident and recovery runbooks, update, support, and retirement records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify hardware, OS, configuration, privilege, test, environment, result, reviewer, and limitations. A booted host or patch result does not prove secure or reliable operation.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Test privilege boundaries, system calls, memory, storage, scheduling, drivers, boot, update, corruption, outage, hostile input, isolation, logging, restore, and hardware compatibility.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Running as administrator, unsigned updates, shared secrets, hidden driver assumptions, missing recovery media, suppressed kernel errors, unbounded resource use, and destructive rollback that erases evidence.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Isolate affected hosts, preserve forensic and operational evidence, use safe boot or approved prior image, restore or replace hardware, compensate external effects, and reopen after independent review.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

The OS behavior, privilege, platform, security, update, compatibility, recovery, evidence, ownership, support, and retirement boundaries are explicit and verified.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Hand off platform, source, signed artifacts, configuration, hardware assumptions, test evidence, incidents, decisions, recovery, and open gaps to Kernel, Driver, Infrastructure, Security, Operations, and Maintenance sections.
