SECTION 256 — Verification

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Determine, with traceable evidence, whether the specified artifact, process, or system was built and operates according to approved requirements, design, contracts, and constraints.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A verification record linking requirement or constraint to method, artifact, configuration, test or inspection, result, reviewer, exception, and conclusion within a bounded scope.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Verification answers whether the work conforms to its specification; it does not by itself prove that the specification solves the human problem. Confusing verification with validation creates polished failure.

APPLICABILITY

Apply whenever conformance to requirements, design, contract, standard, configuration, or implementation claim matters.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no conformance claim is made or an approved exact baseline and lower-risk evidence are sufficient for the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use requirements, architecture, contracts, baselines, configuration, tests, inspection, quality, evidence, and acceptance authority. Define independent reviewer where consequence warrants.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Requirement, source, version, artifact, configuration, method, test, inspection, evidence, result, exception, reviewer, and acceptance criteria.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners define conformance and exceptions; verifiers assess evidence; humans decide material deviation, waiver, and acceptance. The verifier must not claim more than the method demonstrates.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may trace requirements, inspect authorized artifacts, draft checks, and summarize results. It must not fabricate execution, infer conformance from similarity, alter evidence, or sign verification without human review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Verifier, Tester, Quality Assurance Reviewer, Requirements Engineer, Architect, Analyst, Researcher, Critic, Governance Reviewer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define conformance; baseline sources and versions; select method; inspect, analyze, or test; record evidence and deviations; review independently; issue bounded conclusion; track correction; and reopen on change.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show requirement, source, artifact, version, method, evidence, result, exception, reviewer, and limitation. Require human judgment for semantic interpretation, waiver, and conclusion.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Maintain traceability, immutable baselines, configuration identity, test reproducibility, independent evidence, and change impact. Keep verification records separate from unverified claims.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show requirement, conformance state, evidence freshness, deviation, owner, reviewer, and authorized verify, reject, waive, remediate, reopen, and accept controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Verification plan; traceability matrix; method and evidence record; results and deviations; reviewer conclusion; remediation, exception, decision, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify requirement, version, artifact, environment, method, execution or inspection, result, reviewer, time, and limitation. A test result proves only the tested requirement and conditions.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify source authority, version, traceability, method validity, evidence integrity, result reproducibility, deviation handling, independence, and conclusion boundary.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Verification by assertion, stale requirement, untraceable test, conformance inferred from intent, waiver without authority, and verification presented as user validation.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Mark conclusion uncertain, preserve evidence, correct artifact or requirement trace, obtain review, and reverify. Reopen on requirement, design, implementation, configuration, environment, or evidence change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Requirement, artifact, method, evidence, result, deviations, reviewer, conclusion, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending independent review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve baselines, traceability, evidence, results, deviations, decisions, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Validation, Acceptance, Quality, Release, Operations, Audit, and Governance.
