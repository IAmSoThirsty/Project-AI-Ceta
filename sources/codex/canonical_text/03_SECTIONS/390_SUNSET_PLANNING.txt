SECTION 390 — Sunset Planning

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Plan the intentional end of a product, service, version, feature, interface, asset, relationship, or capability while protecting people, data, obligations, continuity, and evidence.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A sunset plan with reason, scope, owners, consumers, dates, migration, communication, support, data, contract, financial, security, disposal, and completion criteria.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Ending something is an active operational change. Without an inventory, migration, notice, retention, support, and proof of completion, users and data can be stranded or exposed.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when an offering, system, service, version, feature, dependency, contract, or capability will stop being available, supported, or used.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no capability, consumer, data, contract, support, or lifecycle state ends and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use deprecation, version, compatibility, product, customer, data, legal, records, security, finance, support, migration, and decommissioning baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Target, reason, consumers, assets, data, contracts, licenses, support, migration, communication, dates, resources, disposal, and acceptance.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners approve sunset, customer and user impact, notice, migration, support, data retention, contract, financial close, security, and final completion. Humans resolve exceptional cases.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inventory consumers and dependencies, draft plan and communication, and identify incomplete migration. It must not announce, disable, delete, terminate, or declare complete without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Sunset Planner, Product Manager, Architect, Migration Engineer, Data Engineer, Support Reviewer, Legal Advisor, Security Auditor, Records Manager, and Verifier.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define reason and scope; inventory users and assets; choose successor; notify; migrate; support; close contracts and billing; retain or delete data; disable; dispose; verify; and archive evidence.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show target, reason, consumers, assets, data, dates, migration, support, authority, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for notice, disablement, deletion, contract, customer, and final closure.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use migration, compatibility, data export, access revocation, billing closure, retention, deletion, certificate and DNS cleanup, artifact withdrawal, audit, and rollback or contingency.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show sunset phase, consumers, migration, data, contracts, support, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized notify, extend, migrate, disable, preserve, delete, and close controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Sunset charter; scope and reason; consumer and asset; migration and communication; contract, data, support, disposal, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify capability, consumer, asset, version, source, notice, migration, method, time, result, disposal, owner, and limitation. Disablement proves neither user migration nor data disposition.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify consumer inventory, notice, successor, migration, support, contracts, billing, access, data retention and deletion, DNS and certificates, artifacts, disposal, and final user outcome.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Silent sunset, consumer unknown, data deleted too early, contract ignored, support withdrawn, DNS or certificate left active, and completion declared from shutdown command.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause shutdown, restore or extend support, protect data, communicate, recover successor or service, and reassess obligations. Reopen on consumer, contract, data, migration, legal, or incident change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Reason, scope, consumers, assets, migration, support, data, contracts, dates, disposal, evidence, owners, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve sunset plan, notices, migrations, contracts, data decisions, support, incidents, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Deprecation, Migration, Records, Legal, Finance, Support, Security, Operations, and Retirement.
