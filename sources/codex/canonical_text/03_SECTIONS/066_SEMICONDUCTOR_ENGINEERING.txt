SECTION 066 — Semiconductor Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define the governed engineering of semiconductor devices, process technology, fabrication assumptions, packaging, yield, reliability, security, and lifecycle evidence.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A traceable device or process baseline with device behavior, process limits, masks or equivalent design artifacts, packaging, test, yield, qualification, supply, and retirement controls.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Small process variation, aging, contamination, defect, or supply change can alter safety, timing, leakage, reliability, and trust while remaining invisible to nominal functional tests.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to integrated circuits, dies, process nodes, memory, sensors, power devices, accelerators, packaging, wafer or device test, and semiconductor supply decisions.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability only when no device, process, packaging, yield, reliability, supply, security, test, or lifecycle property is affected, with baseline and reopening trigger.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Functional and electrical requirements, process design rules, device models, packaging, thermal and reliability targets, fab and supplier constraints, security, qualification, and lifecycle plans.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Device intent, masks or layout, process assumptions, libraries, models, tolerances, operating envelope, test structures, packaging, environmental profile, yield targets, provenance, and evidence requirements.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Qualified human engineers approve process assumptions, critical device behavior, foundry or supplier choices, substitutions, qualification, security, export or compliance constraints, and release. Human owners accept residual risk.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze models, layouts, yield data, defects, and test results; propose checks; and identify anomalies. It must preserve provenance and units and must not certify silicon, infer yield from small samples, or approve release.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Architect, Researcher, Analyst, Critic, Security Auditor, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Red Team, Optimizer, Planner, Coordinator, Observer, Governance Reviewer, Documentation Writer, and Memory Curator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Define device, process, package, environment, criticality, and lifecycle. 2. Capture design rules, models, masks, libraries, and dependencies. 3. Analyze variation, power, timing, thermal, aging, defect, yield, and security cases. 4. Define wafer, package, production, incoming, field, and failure tests. 5. Qualify process and supply changes. 6. Baseline evidence and gaps.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

AI suggestions expose source model, process revision, sample, uncertainty, and alternatives. Human review is required for tape-out, qualification, security, supplier change, and release. Non-AI diagnostic and safe fallback paths remain available.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Control device identity, process revision, package, masks, models, libraries, test data, provenance, configuration, and lifecycle. Changes trigger electrical, computer, firmware, thermal, security, supply, and reliability impact analysis.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show device and process revision, lot, package, qualification, yield, defect, reliability, supply, security, evidence, and authorized quarantine, substitution, recall, or retire actions.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Device specification; process and design-rule record; models and libraries; layout or mask set; packaging record; test and yield plan; qualification report; supplier and change register; failure and evidence register.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Bind results to lot, wafer or device, process, package, test setup, calibration, workload, environment, sample, method, reviewer, and limitations. Simulation, characterization, qualification, and field evidence remain distinct.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify design rules, electrical behavior, timing, power, thermal, variation, aging, packaging, defect screening, yield, security, environmental qualification, traceability, and controlled change response.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Uncontrolled process change; stale models; insufficient sample; hidden lot dependence; yield-only acceptance; counterfeit or opaque supply; ignored aging; and treating simulation or vendor data as field qualification.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Quarantine affected lots or devices, preserve traceability, identify safe scope, and use approved re-test, binning, substitution, recall, redesign, or withdrawal. Reopen after process, foundry, package, defect, security, or evidence change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Device, process, package, tests, yield, qualification, supply, reliability, security, evidence, owner, and open gaps are explicit for the declared population and environment.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve masks, models, libraries, lots, package, tests, qualification, deviations, incidents, decisions, evidence, and next action. Hand off to Electronics, Computer, PCB, Hardware Testing, Manufacturing, Security, Supply Chain, and Retirement.
