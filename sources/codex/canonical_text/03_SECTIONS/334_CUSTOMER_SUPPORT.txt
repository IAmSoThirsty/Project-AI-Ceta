SECTION 334 — Customer Support

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Provide accessible, respectful, secure, and accountable assistance that helps customers achieve intended outcomes, recover from failure, and influence product improvement.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A customer-support model with customer identity, entitlement, channel, issue, priority, communication, resolution, escalation, privacy, accessibility, service level, and feedback.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Customers experience the whole service, including support. A fast but inaccurate answer, inaccessible channel, or unowned escalation can increase harm and erode trust.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when customers need help using, integrating, billing, securing, recovering, or understanding a product or service.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no customer, support, entitlement, service commitment, or recovery path changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use service management, technical support, customer success, privacy, security, billing, legal, accessibility, knowledge, and incident baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Customer, entitlement, issue, context, version, evidence, priority, impact, channel, service level, privacy, escalation, resolution, and feedback.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Support owners protect customer data, set priority, communicate, escalate, and verify outcome. Humans approve refunds, access, security, legal, safety, and high-impact closure.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may classify and summarize authorized cases and draft approved responses. It must not impersonate authority, request secrets, expose data, promise remedy, change account or billing, or close disputed cases without human review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Customer Support Specialist, Technical Support Engineer, Customer Success Manager, User Experience Reviewer, Privacy Reviewer, Security Auditor, Analyst, Verifier, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Receive; authenticate; understand outcome; classify; respond; troubleshoot; escalate; communicate; resolve; verify; support appeal; record feedback; and improve product or knowledge.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show customer, entitlement, issue, evidence, source, action, impact, privacy, and limitation. Require human review for account, billing, security, legal, safety, compensation, and disputed decisions.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Provide secure case identity, entitlement, redaction, consent, knowledge, routing, escalation, status, accessibility, audit, and customer-visible history.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show queue, customer impact, priority, owner, SLA, evidence freshness, escalation, sentiment or feedback without treating it as truth, and authorized assign, communicate, resolve, reopen, and compensate controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Customer-support charter; channel and entitlement; case and resolution; escalation and accessibility; feedback, incident, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify customer, entitlement, case, source, version, method, time, action, result, and limitation. Customer rating or closure proves only that interaction and response.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify identity, privacy, channel, accessibility, response, escalation, knowledge, resolution, reopen, communication, and feedback capture. Test vulnerable customer and unavailable channel.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Script over truth, customer data exposure, inaccessible support, no escalation, unsupported promise, metric gaming, and customer complaint dismissed as noise.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Protect customer, correct response, escalate, communicate, remediate, and reassess harm and commitment. Reopen on dispute, repeated failure, new evidence, or product change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Customers, outcomes, channels, entitlement, support, privacy, accessibility, evidence, owners, escalation, resolution, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve cases, responses, escalations, feedback, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Technical Support, Help Desk, Customer Success, Product, Legal, Security, and Operations.

SOURCE BASIS AND UNRESOLVED SOURCE GAPS
