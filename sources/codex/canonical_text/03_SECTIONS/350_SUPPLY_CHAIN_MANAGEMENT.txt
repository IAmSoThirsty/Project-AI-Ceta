SECTION 350 — Supply Chain Management

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Plan, source, qualify, monitor, protect, deliver, and recover the flow of materials, components, software, data, services, and suppliers across the lifecycle.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A supply-chain model with tiers, provenance, demand, inventory, criticality, suppliers, controls, logistics, quality, disruption, substitution, evidence, and retirement.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Supply chains are distributed trust systems. Counterfeit, compromised, delayed, substituted, single-sourced, or untraceable inputs can affect safety, security, quality, cost, and continuity.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a product, service, system, or operation depends on multi-tier materials, components, software, data, logistics, suppliers, or external capacity.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no supply input, tier, supplier, inventory, delivery, quality, or continuity behavior changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use procurement, vendor, hardware, software supply-chain, quality, security, logistics, manufacturing, finance, continuity, and risk baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Demand, materials, components, software, suppliers, tiers, provenance, inventory, lead time, quality, risk, logistics, alternatives, and disruption plans.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Supply-chain owners approve sources, substitutions, criticality, inventory, ethical and safety requirements, disruption response, cost, and risk. Humans resolve supplier conflicts and acceptance.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze authorized supply data, identify concentration or delay risk, and draft alternatives. It must not select or substitute critical input, expose supplier data, make safety claims, or alter orders without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Supply Chain Manager, Procurement Analyst, Vendor Manager, Hardware Engineer, Security Auditor, Quality Engineer, Researcher, Analyst, Verifier, and Planner.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Map tiers and critical inputs; qualify sources; capture provenance; plan demand and inventory; monitor quality and risk; manage logistics; prepare alternatives; respond to disruption; and retire sources.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show input, tier, supplier, provenance, criticality, inventory, evidence, risk, alternative, and limitation. Require human approval for substitution, safety, security, cost, and disruption action.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use traceability, approved source, component and package identity, dual source where warranted, inventory, quality gate, secure logistics, substitution, and continuity.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show supply graph, critical items, source, inventory, lead time, quality, risk, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized qualify, substitute, expedite, quarantine, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Supply-chain charter; tier and provenance map; demand and inventory; supplier and quality; disruption and alternative; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify input, tier, source, supplier, lot or version, method, time, result, owner, and limitation. Supplier declaration or inventory count proves only its bounded claim.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify provenance, source, quality, inventory, lead time, substitution, security, safety, logistics, supplier, continuity, and acceptance. Test counterfeit, delay, shortage, and supplier loss.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Single source, hidden tier, untraceable component, substitution without review, inventory as quality, supplier concentration ignored, and cost-only choice.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Quarantine affected input, preserve provenance, use approved alternative, communicate, reassess safety and quality, and update design or inventory. Reopen on source, tier, supplier, demand, or threat change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Inputs, tiers, suppliers, provenance, inventory, quality, risks, alternatives, evidence, owners, recovery, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve supply graph, sources, substitutions, quality, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Procurement, Vendor, Manufacturing, Logistics, Security, Quality, and Operations.
