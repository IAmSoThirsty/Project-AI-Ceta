SECTION 353 — Assembly

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Combine approved components, software, firmware, materials, and instructions into a traceable, safe, tested, and accepted assembly.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An assembly model with component identity, revision, sequence, tools, operators, interfaces, configuration, inspection, quality, safety, test, rework, and release.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Assembly errors can be invisible until field use. A component can be correct in isolation while orientation, connection, version, torque, firmware, or configuration makes the whole unsafe.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when parts, modules, software, firmware, or services are combined into a product, device, system, kit, or deployable unit.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no assembly, integration, component traceability, quality, safety, or output behavior changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use manufacturing, hardware, embedded, supply, quality, safety, testing, provisioning, and logistics baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Assembly definition, components, revisions, tools, sequence, operator, configuration, software, firmware, inspection, test, safety, and acceptance.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Assembly and quality owners approve parts, sequence, substitution, inspection, rework, safety, and release. Workers can stop unsafe or suspect assembly.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inspect authorized assembly records, identify mismatch, draft checklists, and analyze quality. It must not control equipment, approve lot, infer worker fault, or change sequence without qualified human authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Assembly Engineer, Manufacturing Engineer, Hardware Engineer, Embedded Engineer, Quality Engineer, Safety Reviewer, Tester, Verifier, and Observer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Verify parts and revision; prepare tools; assemble; configure; inspect; test; trace; quarantine; rework; release; package; and capture lessons.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show unit, part, revision, sequence, tool, operator role, configuration, result, safety, and limitation. Require human approval for substitution, rework, release, and safety.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use work instructions, serialization, component traceability, poka-yoke or equivalent controls, configuration binding, calibration, inspection, test, and rework segregation.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show unit, parts, revisions, progress, quality, safety, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized hold, inspect, rework, quarantine, release, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Assembly charter; unit and component record; work instruction; inspection and test; rework and release; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify unit, part, revision, sequence, tool, configuration, method, time, result, inspector, and limitation. Assembly completion proves neither correct function nor safety.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify component, orientation, connection, software, firmware, configuration, inspection, quality, safety, function, rework, traceability, and release. Test variation and misassembly.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Wrong revision, untraceable unit, skipped inspection, unsafe rework, tool calibration ignored, assembly count as quality, and component pass treated as system pass.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Quarantine unit and related lot, preserve evidence, inspect or rework, notify owners, retest, and reassess downstream units. Reopen on component, instruction, tool, design, or quality change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Unit, parts, revisions, sequence, tools, quality, safety, tests, evidence, owners, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending qualified acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve assembly records, units, parts, tests, rework, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Manufacturing, Certification, Provisioning, Logistics, Quality, and Operations.
