SECTION 264 — Build Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Design reproducible, secure, observable, and maintainable processes that transform controlled source, dependencies, configuration, and tools into identified artifacts.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A build model with inputs, toolchain, environment, dependency resolution, provenance, reproducibility, security, caching, outputs, failure, evidence, and owner control.

WHY THIS MATTERS

The build is a supply-chain boundary. Nonreproducible tools, mutable inputs, secret leakage, hidden network access, or untracked generated files can make an artifact impossible to trust or recover.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when source, packages, configuration, models, data, firmware, infrastructure, or other inputs are transformed into a distributable or deployable artifact.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no transformation, artifact, dependency, toolchain, or provenance claim is affected and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use source, dependency, package, artifact, environment, secrets, security, license, testing, and release baselines. Identify build owner, trust, runner, and output authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Source ref, dependencies, packages, toolchain, configuration, environment, secrets references, scripts, inputs, outputs, cache, provenance, and acceptance criteria.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Build owners approve toolchain, inputs, runners, network, secrets, output, reproducibility, exceptions, and release linkage. Humans decide whether a nonreproducible or emergency build is usable.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inspect authorized build definitions and logs, draft improvements, analyze failures, and propose hardening. It must not run untrusted code with privileged access, expose secrets, modify build gates, or claim provenance without verification.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Build Engineer, Supply Chain Analyst, Security Auditor, Dependency Manager, Platform Engineer, Tester, Verifier, Implementer, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define inputs and toolchain; pin dependencies; isolate runner; secure credentials; build; capture provenance and artifacts; verify reproducibility; scan; manage cache; handle failure; promote or reject; and retire toolchain.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show source, toolchain, runner, dependency, configuration, network, secret boundary, artifact, result, and limitation. Require human approval for privileged build, gate change, emergency output, and promotion.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use isolated and ephemeral runners, hermetic or declared network, provenance, SBOM, cache integrity, reproducible inputs, least privilege, artifact signing, and rollback.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show build, source, toolchain, runner, dependencies, findings, provenance, artifact, evidence freshness, and authorized start, cancel, retry, quarantine, approve, promote, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Build specification; toolchain and runner record; input and provenance manifest; logs; artifact and scan; reproducibility result; exception, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify source, toolchain, runner, dependencies, configuration, time, actor, method, artifact digest, result, and limitation. A successful build proves execution, not safe or reproducible inputs.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify isolation, dependencies, secrets, network, reproducibility, provenance, artifact identity, scans, cache, failure, cleanup, and rollback. Repeat from a clean environment.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Mutable toolchain, hidden network fetch, privileged runner, secrets in logs, untracked generated output, cache trust, nonreproducible build, and green build treated as release approval.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Quarantine output, preserve logs, restore trusted toolchain, rotate exposed secrets, rebuild, compare artifacts, and reassess consumers. Reopen on source, dependency, toolchain, runner, or configuration change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Inputs, toolchain, runner, dependencies, outputs, provenance, reproducibility, security, evidence, owners, exceptions, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve build definitions, toolchains, runners, logs, artifacts, provenance, findings, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Automation, CI/CD, Artifact, Security, Release, Platform, and Operations.
