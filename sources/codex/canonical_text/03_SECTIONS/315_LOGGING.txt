SECTION 315 — Logging

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Record meaningful, structured, attributable, time-bound, privacy-aware events for operation, diagnosis, security, audit, compliance, and learning.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A logging model with event taxonomy, fields, identity, time, correlation, severity, redaction, access, retention, integrity, transport, cost, and use.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Logs can be missing, misleading, excessive, sensitive, mutable, or impossible to correlate. More logging can increase privacy and cost while still failing to answer the operational question.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when event records support operation, security, audit, debugging, compliance, safety, user support, or forensic evidence.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no event evidence, operational question, audit, security, or user-support need changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use observability, privacy, security, records, incident, trace, metric, data, and operations baselines. Define log owner and retention authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Events, fields, identity, time, correlation, severity, source, data classification, redaction, destination, access, retention, integrity, and cost.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners define required evidence and collection; privacy and security owners approve content and access; operators interpret logs; humans decide disclosure and retention.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may summarize authorized logs, identify patterns, draft schemas and queries, and propose redaction. It must not expose sensitive content, delete evidence, infer certainty from silence, or alter retention without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Logging Engineer, Observability Engineer, Security Auditor, Privacy Reviewer, SRE, Data Engineer, Analyst, Tester, Verifier, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define events and fields; instrument; normalize; timestamp; correlate; redact; transport and store; protect access and integrity; monitor collection; query; retain; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show log source, event, identity, time, data class, query, evidence, privacy, and limitation. Require human approval for sensitive query, disclosure, retention, and deletion.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use structured schema, time sync, correlation ID, resilient transport, encryption, access control, tamper evidence, redaction, sampling, retention tier, and independent evidence store.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show log coverage, source health, schema, privacy, retention, cost, incidents, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized query, restrict, preserve, redact, export, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Logging charter; event and schema catalog; redaction and access; pipeline and retention; integrity and query; incident, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify source, event, schema, identity, time basis, correlation, method, query, result, actor, and limitation. A log line proves only its recorded event and integrity context.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify event coverage, fields, identity, time, correlation, redaction, access, retention, integrity, transport, query, collector loss, and evidence recovery.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Secrets in logs, personal data overcollection, no timestamp, uncorrelated events, mutable storage, log volume as observability, and log absence treated as no event.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Restore or substitute collection, preserve available records, rotate exposed secrets, restrict access, notify owners, and reassess blind period. Reopen on event, schema, privacy, incident, or retention change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Events, fields, source, identity, time, correlation, privacy, access, retention, integrity, evidence, owners, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve schemas, configs, retention, queries, incidents, evidence, decisions, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Observability, Security, Privacy, Tracing, Metrics, SRE, Records, and Operations.
