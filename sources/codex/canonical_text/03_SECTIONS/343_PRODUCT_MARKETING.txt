SECTION 343 — Product Marketing

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Translate a real product capability and customer outcome into truthful positioning, audience understanding, launch, adoption, and feedback without overclaiming.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A product-marketing model with audience, problem, value, evidence, positioning, claims, alternatives, content, launch, enablement, feedback, and correction.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Marketing shapes expectations and decisions. Claims that outrun product behavior create harm, support load, legal exposure, and loss of trust even when the product itself works.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a product, service, capability, launch, offer, or customer outcome is positioned, promoted, explained, or compared.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no audience, product claim, launch, adoption, customer expectation, or public content changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use product, user research, market, competitive, legal, IP, privacy, technical, support, accessibility, and roadmap baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Product, audience, problem, outcome, evidence, alternatives, limitations, claims, content, launch, support, feedback, and approval authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Marketing owners ensure claims are truthful and reviewed by product, technical, legal, and accessibility authorities. Humans approve public positioning, comparison, pricing, and correction.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft copy, segment authorized research, summarize evidence, and identify claim gaps. It must not invent capability, fabricate customer proof, conceal limitation, impersonate customer voice, or publish without human approval.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Product Marketer, Product Manager, Researcher, Analyst, Technical Marketer, Legal Advisor, Documentation Writer, Verifier, Critic, and User Experience Reviewer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Understand audience and problem; define outcome; validate positioning and claims; compare alternatives fairly; create content; coordinate launch and support; monitor feedback; correct overclaim; and retire stale material.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show claim, source, product version, audience, evidence, limitation, reviewer, and uncertainty. Require human review for legal, technical, customer, competitor, safety, and public claims.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Link claims to product behavior, evidence, versions, terms, support, accessibility, pricing, and correction paths. Preserve content version and distribution control.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show claims, evidence, product version, audience, content, feedback, risks, owner, and authorized approve, correct, withdraw, communicate, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Marketing charter; audience and positioning; claim and evidence register; content and launch; enablement, correction, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify claim, product version, source, customer or study, method, date, result, reviewer, and limitation. Reach, testimonial, or conversion proves only the bounded claim.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify capability, evidence, audience, comparison, legal, accessibility, support, limitation, version, and correction. Test content against actual product and failure path.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Overclaim, cherry-picked proof, competitor misrepresentation, hidden limitation, synthetic testimonial, old product content, and launch metric treated as value.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Correct or withdraw content, notify affected audiences, support users, preserve evidence, and reassess claims and commitments. Reopen on product, audience, evidence, law, or market change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Audience, problem, outcome, claims, evidence, limitations, approvals, content, support, owners, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve claims, sources, versions, approvals, launches, feedback, corrections, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Technical Marketing, Sales, Solutions, Product, Legal, Support, and Documentation.
