SECTION 331 — Operational Readiness

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Determine whether a service, system, product, process, or change can be safely operated, supported, monitored, secured, recovered, and owned in its declared environment.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An operational-readiness record with owner, scope, modes, runbooks, staffing, access, observability, support, security, capacity, continuity, recovery, evidence, gaps, and explicit decision.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Deployment or acceptance can occur before operations can safely sustain the outcome. Readiness is a decision with limitations, not a checklist or a green dashboard.

APPLICABILITY

Apply before entering a new operational mode, production, field use, support commitment, or material transition.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no operational ownership, support, mode, risk, user, dependency, or recovery condition changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use operational architecture, acceptance, release, deployment, security, privacy, support, SRE, observability, capacity, continuity, recovery, and training baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Service, environment, owner, users, modes, runbooks, staffing, access, alerts, logs, traces, metrics, support, risks, capacity, backup, recovery, training, and approvals.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Accountable owners decide ready, conditionally ready, hold, or reject. Operators and support owners confirm capability; security, privacy, safety, accessibility, and continuity authorities approve affected conditions.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may assemble evidence, identify missing readiness items, draft runbooks and exercises, and summarize risk. It must not declare readiness, accept residual risk, or approve operation without human authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Operational Readiness Reviewer, Operations Engineer, SRE, Release Manager, Support Reviewer, Security Auditor, Tester, Verifier, Coordinator, and Governance Reviewer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define scope and modes; confirm owners and access; validate runbooks, monitoring, support, security, capacity, backup, recovery, training, documentation, and communication; exercise; record gaps; decide; and schedule review.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show readiness condition, evidence, owner, gap, risk, environment, support, authority, and decision. Require human sign-off and preserve conditional or rejected status.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Provide operational interfaces, health, logs, traces, metrics, access, safe mode, support, rollback, backup, restore, runbooks, and owner control before operation.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show readiness state, owner, environment, gaps, evidence freshness, incidents, support, training, recovery, and authorized approve, condition, hold, reject, remediate, and reopen controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Readiness charter; owner and mode; runbooks; support and training; security and privacy; capacity, observability, continuity, recovery, decision, evidence, and gap records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify condition, system, version, environment, method, result, owner, reviewer, date, validity, gap, and limitation. Checklist completion proves neither operational effectiveness nor adoption.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify ownership, access, runbook, monitoring, alert, logs, traces, metrics, support, security, capacity, backup, restore, failover, training, communication, and user recovery.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Readiness by checklist, owner missing, support unprepared, stale evidence, no recovery, undocumented exception, conditional ready without expiry, and deployment treated as readiness.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Hold or narrow operation, preserve evidence, remediate gaps, support users, and reconvene decision authority. Reopen on version, environment, owner, mode, dependency, incident, or evidence change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Scope, owner, modes, evidence, runbooks, support, security, observability, capacity, recovery, gaps, decision, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve readiness package, decisions, gaps, approvals, runbooks, training, evidence, exceptions, and open actions. Hand off to Operations, SRE, Support, Security, Continuity, Release, and Governance.
