SECTION 086 — Motion Design

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define purposeful movement, transition, timing, feedback, and animation that communicate state and support comprehension without causing harm or blocking control.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

 - Scoped motion purpose, audience, device, timing, accessibility, performance, reduced-motion, and lifecycle rules.
 - Motion specifications, assets, evidence, tests, controls, and implementation handoff.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Motion can explain state or trigger vestibular, cognitive, performance, and safety problems. Delight is not evidence of benefit.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to interface animation, product behavior, data visualization, identity, marketing, games, physical displays, and feedback cues.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No material motion, timing, state, accessibility, performance, or user-control effect; or an unchanged motion baseline with impact recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Interaction and UI states, content, device performance, accessibility, reduced-motion, timing, research, media rights, and acceptance authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Interaction and UI states, content, device performance, accessibility, reduced-motion, timing, research, media rights, and acceptance authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve motion purpose, safety, accessibility, timing, content, publication, and acceptance.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may propose transitions or timing and analyze performance. It must expose uncertainty and accessibility implications and must not enable motion that overrides user control.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Motion Designer, UI Designer, Interaction Designer, UX Reviewer, Accessibility specialist, Researcher, Tester, Verifier, Critic, Implementer, Governance Reviewer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define motion intent, triggers, duration, easing, hierarchy, feedback, interruption, reduced-motion, performance, fallback, rights, and tests; validate with representative and sensitive users.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show what moved, why, duration, actor, consequence, pause or skip control, and reduced-motion alternative. AI proposals remain drafts.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Specify state-linked transitions, timing budgets, interruptibility, focus, performance, reduced-motion, offline fallback, and no-motion status alternatives.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show motion baseline, trigger, state, audience, device, accessibility, performance, evidence, and controls to pause, reduce, disable, or restore.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Motion specification, storyboards, timing sheets, assets, reduced-motion rules, performance and accessibility tests, decisions, evidence, and handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify motion, trigger, device, audience, version, method, result, and limitation. A video or designer review proves bounded appearance.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify comprehension, timing, interruptibility, reduced-motion, assistive use, performance, focus, localization, loading, and degraded behavior.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Motion without purpose; seizure or vestibular risk; blocking transitions; no pause; hidden loading; motion as the only status; and performance measured on one device.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Disable or reduce motion, preserve state, provide static status, notify owners, and retest affected populations and devices before restoring.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Purpose, timing, states, accessibility, controls, evidence, tests, recovery, open risks, and acceptance are explicit. Remain CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve timing, assets, versions, findings, decisions, evidence, accessibility results, and next action. Hand off to UI, Interaction, Visual, Accessibility, Frontend, and Testing.
