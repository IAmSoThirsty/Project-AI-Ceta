SECTION 135 - Scientific Computing

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define governed engineering of computational methods used to represent, analyze, or reproduce scientific phenomena, with explicit assumptions, numerical validity, provenance, uncertainty, and reproducibility.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A scientific-computing baseline with model purpose, equations or algorithms, inputs, units, numerical methods, uncertainty, validation, reproducible environment, and decision limits.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Scientific code can produce precise-looking but invalid results when assumptions, units, discretization, uncertainty, or provenance are hidden.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to computational science, numerical analysis, research software, scientific workflows, experiment analysis, and domain simulations.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

May be non-applicable to ordinary business computation with no scientific model, numerical claim, experimental inference, or research reproducibility requirement. Record scope, owner, evidence, and reopening trigger.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Requires scientific objective, domain authority, model assumptions, data and units, numerical method, precision, validation data, uncertainty, compute environment, provenance, and publication authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Provide equations, algorithms, parameters, units, boundary conditions, data sources, tolerances, seeds, workload, validation cases, uncertainty, and intended decisions.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve scientific meaning, assumptions, data use, uncertainty, publication, and decision fitness. Domain experts retain authority over interpretation and limitations.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft code, summarize literature supplied by the user, propose experiments, and inspect results. It must not invent citations, hide uncertainty, or present a numerical result as scientific truth without review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Researcher, Analyst, Architect, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Critic, Governance Reviewer, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, Optimizer, Security Auditor, and Red Team. Existing role contracts remain controlling.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Define scientific question, scope, model, data, units, assumptions, and acceptance.
2. Implement and profile numerical methods with provenance and reproducibility.
3. Validate against analytical, experimental, observational, or benchmark evidence.
4. Quantify uncertainty, publish limits, preserve artifacts, and retire invalid methods.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show model, version, input data, units, method, uncertainty, evidence, and interpretation state. Human review is required for scientific claims and consequential use; AI output remains proposed until verified.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate model, solver, data, parameter, execution, result, visualization, and decision boundaries. Capture units, precision, seeds, environment, provenance, and uncertainty through the pipeline.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show run identity, model, inputs, parameters, code, environment, convergence, uncertainty, validation, result state, and controls to stop, reproduce, compare, or withdraw.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Scientific objective, model record, data and units catalog, code and environment manifest, experiment plan, validation and uncertainty evidence, results, limitations, and reproducibility package.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify model, code, data, units, parameters, solver, hardware, seed, method, result, uncertainty, and limitations. Separate numerical convergence from empirical validity and scientific interpretation.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify units, conservation or invariants, convergence, sensitivity, reproducibility, analytical cases, benchmark or experimental comparison, uncertainty, malformed data, and failure recovery.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

False precision; unit mismatch; cherry-picked runs; hidden parameter tuning; invalid extrapolation; unreproducible environments; and treating convergence or visualization as validation.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Preserve inputs and environment, mark results invalid or provisional, rerun with corrected assumptions, withdraw affected claims, compensate decisions where needed, and reopen the scientific baseline.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Question, model, data, assumptions, method, evidence, uncertainty, interpretation, provenance, and limits are explicit. Keep CONTENT-COMPLETE until independent verification and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve code, data, environment, seeds, runs, results, decisions, dissent, evidence, and next safe action. Hand off to Simulation, HPC, Data, Statistics, Research, Testing, and Records sections.
