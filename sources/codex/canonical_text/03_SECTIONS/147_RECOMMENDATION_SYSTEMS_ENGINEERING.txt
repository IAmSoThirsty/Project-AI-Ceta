SECTION 147 - Recommendation Systems Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define governed engineering of systems that select, rank, personalize, or suggest items, actions, content, or opportunities under explicit relevance, user-control, fairness, privacy, safety, and evidence constraints.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A recommendation baseline with objectives, candidates, ranking, feedback, permissions, explanations, evaluation, controls, drift, and lifecycle ownership.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Recommendations allocate attention and opportunity. Optimizing clicks or conversions can create manipulation, exclusion, unsafe exposure, feedback loops, and opaque consequential decisions.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to content, products, people, routes, actions, alerts, jobs, treatments, learning, personalization, and automated prioritization systems.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

May be non-applicable to explicit user lookup or deterministic navigation with no ranking, personalization, suggestion, feedback, or allocation effect. Record scope, owner, evidence, and reopening trigger.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Requires purpose, users, protected outcomes, candidate sources, permissions, ranking objective, feedback, privacy, safety, fairness, explanation, evaluation, and override authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Provide candidates, context, features, constraints, ranking, exploration, feedback, exposure, opt-out, fairness, safety, latency, and lifecycle requirements.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve objectives, protected groups, data use, safety rules, explanations, overrides, experimentation, and acceptance. Humans retain authority over high-impact recommendations and appeals.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze candidates and draft ranking changes. It must expose proxy risks, uncertainty, feedback loops, and exclusions; it must not silently personalize consequential choices or suppress appeal.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Researcher, Analyst, Architect, User Experience Reviewer, Privacy Reviewer, Security Auditor, Governance Reviewer, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Critic, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, and Red Team. Existing role contracts remain controlling.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Define purpose, audience, candidates, permissions, constraints, and protected outcomes.
2. Design retrieval, ranking, exploration, feedback, explanation, opt-out, override, and safety controls.
3. Evaluate task success, relevance, diversity, fairness, calibration, harm, privacy, and drift.
4. Monitor, audit, correct, and retire models and policies under approval.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show why an item was shown, source, user controls, context, model or policy, uncertainty, and consequence. Human review is required for high-impact use; nonpersonalized and appeal paths remain available.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate candidate authority, features, ranker, policy filter, personalization, feedback, experiment, explanation, cache, and outcome. Enforce permission and safety before ranking and preserve opt-out.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show model and policy version, audience, exposure, explanation, metrics, drift, protected-group impact, incidents, and controls to pause, narrow, reset, or roll back recommendations.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Recommendation charter, objective and constraint record, candidate and feature catalog, privacy and fairness assessment, evaluation set, experiment record, explanation and appeal design, monitoring, and retirement package.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify model, policy, data, context, candidate set, audience, exposure, method, result, uncertainty, and limits. Separate ranking metrics from user outcome, fairness, safety, and long-term effects.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify permissions, opt-out, explanation, adversarial inputs, feedback loops, cold start, drift, protected-group outcomes, unsafe content, exploration, caching, and human override.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Click-only objectives; hidden personalization; proxy discrimination; filter bubbles; unsafe exploration; no opt-out; experimentation without consent; and treating engagement as benefit.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause or narrow ranking, restore prior policy, remove unsafe candidates, preserve exposure evidence, notify affected users, provide appeal or manual review, compensate decisions, and reopen evaluation.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Objective, constraints, controls, evidence, user choice, fairness, safety, recovery, and lifecycle are explicit. Keep CONTENT-COMPLETE until independent verification and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve model, policy, features, candidate sources, exposures, decisions, evidence, incidents, dissent, and next safe action. Hand off to Search, AI, Data, Privacy, Safety, Testing, and Operations.
