SECTION 379 — Firmware Updates

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Update firmware on devices and embedded systems securely, safely, recoverably, and with exact hardware, boot, identity, field, and lifecycle awareness.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A firmware-update model with image provenance, hardware compatibility, signing, boot trust, target, power and connectivity, staged rollout, rollback, safe state, telemetry, and recovery.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Firmware can brick devices, change physical behavior, invalidate certification, expose keys, or remove recovery paths. Field conditions make interruption and partial rollout material.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when firmware, bootloader, device image, microcode, or embedded update mechanism changes on deployed or production hardware.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no firmware, hardware behavior, trust root, device population, or update path changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use embedded, hardware, security, key, certificate, device provisioning, manufacturing, safety, testing, logistics, and field operations baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Device and hardware, current and target firmware, image, signature, keys, compatibility, power, connectivity, target population, rollout, rollback, and safety.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners approve image, hardware, rollout, safety, field window, rollback, certification, communications, exception, and retirement. Field operators retain safe local control.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inspect authorized firmware metadata, compatibility, rollout, and telemetry; draft plans; and identify failures. It must not flash, change boot trust, widen fleet, extract keys, or declare recovery without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Firmware Engineer, Embedded Engineer, Hardware Security Auditor, Device Provisioning Engineer, Safety Reviewer, Tester, Verifier, Field Operations Engineer, and Coordinator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Verify image and signing; inventory hardware; test; stage; back up or preserve recovery; update; monitor; pause or roll back; reconcile fleet; communicate; and retire old firmware.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show device, hardware, current and target image, signature reference, field state, authority, evidence, safety, and limitation. Require human approval for fleet, safety, boot, key, and rollback action.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use signed image, anti-rollback, A/B slots or equivalent, power-fail safety, local recovery, staged fleet, hardware compatibility, telemetry, and secure provisioning.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show fleet, device, hardware, firmware, rollout, health, evidence freshness, safety, owner, and authorized pause, isolate, update, rollback, recover, replace, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Firmware-update charter; image and compatibility; signing and rollout; device and telemetry; recovery and field communication; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify device, hardware, current and target image, signature, method, time, power, connectivity, result, operator, and limitation. Successful boot proves neither fleet safety nor field recovery.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify signing, hardware, boot, anti-rollback, power loss, connectivity, safe state, local recovery, rollout, telemetry, rollback, certification, and fleet reconciliation.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Unsigned image, wrong hardware, bricked device, fleet-wide rollout, recovery dependent on network, key exposure, certification ignored, and boot success treated as safe operation.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stop rollout, isolate, use known-good slot or replacement, preserve evidence, reconcile fleet, notify operators, and reassess safety and certification. Reopen on image, hardware, threat, field, or policy change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Image, hardware, signing, targets, safety, rollout, recovery, evidence, owners, certification, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending qualified acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve images, signatures, device inventory, rollout, failures, recovery, field communication, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Embedded, Hardware, Provisioning, Security, Safety, Field Operations, and Disposal.
