SECTION 266 — Continuous Integration

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Integrate controlled changes frequently and provide fast, trustworthy feedback about build, test, quality, security, dependency, and contract health.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A CI model with trigger, source, isolation, build, test, scan, artifact, evidence, feedback, gate, ownership, failure, and recovery behavior.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Fast feedback is valuable only when it is truthful, reproducible, relevant, and acted on. A green pipeline can hide skipped tests, stale dependencies, unsafe permissions, flaky checks, or environment mismatch.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when changes are integrated through automated or repeatable build and validation workflows before release or deployment.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no integration, build, test, security, artifact, or change feedback claim is made and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use source, build, package, dependency, test, security, artifact, configuration, environment, and governance baselines. Identify branch, trigger, gate, and owner.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Change, branch, source, dependencies, workflow, runner, tests, scans, artifact, gates, secrets, environment, results, and acceptance criteria.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners define required gates, flaky policy, bypass, merge, and remediation. Humans review important failures and retain authority for protected branch and release decisions.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may summarize runs, diagnose failures, draft tests, and propose fixes. It must not mark checks passed, suppress failures, bypass gates, merge, or change workflow permissions without human authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

CI Engineer, Build Engineer, Tester, Security Auditor, Dependency Manager, Quality Engineer, Verifier, Implementer, Analyst, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Trigger on controlled change; build; test; scan; capture artifacts and evidence; report; triage; manage flaky or quarantined tests; enforce gate; allow controlled retry or bypass; restore; and improve.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show change, branch, workflow, runner, checks, failures, evidence, gate, actor, and limitation. Require human approval for bypass, merge, permission, quarantine, and release-impacting actions.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use isolated runners, repeatable toolchain, secure secrets, parallelism with resource limits, artifact identity, required checks, policy as code, and durable logs.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show run, change, checks, gate, failures, flakiness, artifacts, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized rerun, cancel, quarantine, bypass, merge, and reopen controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

CI workflow and policy; run record; build and test results; scan and artifact; gate and bypass record; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify change, ref, workflow, runner, toolchain, check, result, artifact, time, actor, and limitation. Green status proves only completed checks and their coverage.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify trigger, isolation, required checks, failure visibility, secret handling, artifacts, reproducibility, flaky policy, bypass audit, and recovery. Test malicious change and runner loss.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Green with skipped checks, unbounded bypass, flaky retry theater, shared runner privilege, secrets in logs, CI as release approval, and no owner for failing pipeline.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stop affected integration, preserve run, repair or rollback workflow or change, rotate secrets, rerun, and reassess gate. Reopen on tool, dependency, policy, source, or environment change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Triggers, changes, checks, runners, artifacts, gates, owners, evidence, bypass, recovery, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve workflow, runs, checks, artifacts, bypasses, findings, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to CD, Build, Release, Security, Platform, Testing, and Operations.
