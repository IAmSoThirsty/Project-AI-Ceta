SECTION 275 — Infrastructure Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Design, provision, secure, operate, observe, scale, recover, and retire computing, network, storage, facility, and service infrastructure that supports declared outcomes.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An infrastructure model with resources, dependencies, topology, identity, configuration, capacity, availability, security, cost, observability, recovery, and lifecycle ownership.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Infrastructure failures can affect many services at once. Manual state, common failure domains, insecure defaults, provider dependence, and undocumented capacity make recovery slow and evidence weak.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when physical, virtual, cloud, edge, network, storage, database, facility, or shared platform resources affect a system or service.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no infrastructure resource, dependency, configuration, capacity, security, or operational behavior changes and an approved exact baseline applies.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use architecture, identity, security, configuration, environment, IaC, cloud, network, data, capacity, continuity, and operations baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Outcome, topology, resources, environments, identity, configuration, dependencies, workload, capacity, availability, security, cost, telemetry, recovery, and retirement.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Infrastructure owners approve design, access, capacity, cost, maintenance, change, exceptions, outage, recovery, and retirement. Humans accept service and provider risk.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze authorized topology, telemetry, plans, and incidents; draft infrastructure code and tests; and propose optimization. It must not provision, modify, scale, destroy, or expose infrastructure without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Infrastructure Engineer, Architect, Platform Engineer, Security Auditor, Network Engineer, Database Administrator, Storage Administrator, Tester, Verifier, and Operations Engineer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define service and topology; select resources; secure identity and configuration; provision; validate; observe; capacity-plan; maintain; back up; recover; optimize; document; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show resource, environment, owner, identity, change, dependency, cost, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for production, privileged, destructive, security, cost, and provider changes.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use IaC, segmentation, redundancy, isolation, least privilege, immutable or versioned resources, observability, backup, restore, failover, and provider-independent recovery where warranted.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show topology, resource health, drift, capacity, cost, incidents, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized provision, scale, isolate, restore, rollback, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Infrastructure charter and topology; resource and dependency catalog; IaC and configuration; capacity and cost; security and observability; recovery, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify resource, version, environment, configuration, provider, method, time, actor, result, cost, and limitation. A plan or metric proves intended or observed state only.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify provisioning, identity, segmentation, configuration, capacity, availability, security, cost, monitoring, backup, restore, failover, rollback, and retirement.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Manual drift, shared credentials, common failure domain ignored, capacity by guess, provider lock-in without plan, no recovery path, and infrastructure metric treated as service outcome.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Isolate, preserve state, restore or rebuild, fail over, rotate credentials, communicate, and reassess service and data impact. Reopen on topology, provider, workload, dependency, or risk change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Resources, topology, owners, configuration, identity, capacity, cost, security, evidence, recovery, lifecycle, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve topology, IaC, state, changes, incidents, backups, recovery, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Cloud, Edge, Network, Database, Storage, Platform, Security, and Operations.
