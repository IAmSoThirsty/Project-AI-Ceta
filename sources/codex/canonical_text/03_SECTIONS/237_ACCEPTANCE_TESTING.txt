SECTION 237 — Acceptance Testing

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Enable authorized users, owners, operators, and affected stakeholders to determine whether a delivered capability meets its intended outcome and is acceptable for the declared scope.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An acceptance model with decision owner, user or stakeholder scenarios, criteria, evidence, exceptions, unresolved issues, accessibility, operational readiness, and explicit accept, reject, or conditional decision.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Technical pass status cannot decide whether the capability solves the right problem, fits actual work, or is acceptable to those accountable for consequences. Acceptance without authority or honest limitations creates false launch confidence.

APPLICABILITY

Apply before a product, service, process, system, or change is accepted for use, release, transition, funding, or operational ownership.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability only when the change has no user, owner, operational, contractual, or material outcome effect and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use requirements, user research, system and E2E results, quality, accessibility, security, operations, support, training, recovery, and owner authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Objective, acceptance owner, users, scenarios, criteria, evidence, test results, known defects, risks, exceptions, training, support, deployment, and recovery state.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Authorized humans perform or witness acceptance, interpret user and domain meaning, approve exceptions, and decide release or rejection. Users can report mismatch or harm; AI cannot accept on their behalf.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may organize criteria, facilitate scenarios, summarize evidence, and surface unmet conditions. It must not simulate stakeholder consent, declare acceptance, minimize defects, or make a release decision without accountable human authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

User Experience Reviewer, Tester, Verifier, Quality Assurance Reviewer, Researcher, Analyst, Operations Reviewer, Accessibility Reviewer, Governance Reviewer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Confirm authority and criteria; select representative users and scenarios; execute; observe comprehension and outcome; record evidence and defects; assess operations and recovery; resolve or accept exceptions; document decision and next review.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show criterion, actor, scenario, evidence, result, exception, impact, reviewer, and decision authority. Require accessible human participation and explicit approval or rejection. Preserve dissent and conditional acceptance.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Support acceptance environments, realistic data, user feedback, traceability, defects, configuration identity, training, support, rollback, and post-acceptance monitoring.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show criteria, scenarios, participants, results, findings, risks, exceptions, readiness, evidence freshness, and authorized accept, reject, condition, defer, remediate, and reopen controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Acceptance charter; criteria and scenario set; participant and authority record; evidence and findings; exception and conditional decision; readiness, support, recovery, decision, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify criterion, actor, scenario, version, environment, method, result, observer, decision, limitation, and dissent. A signature or survey response proves only its bounded participation and claim.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify authority, representative use, accessibility, outcome, defect visibility, operational readiness, support, recovery, evidence, and decision traceability. Confirm accepted limitations and review date.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Proxy acceptance, rushed sign-off, silent dissent, acceptance by technical team alone, hidden exceptions, unrealistic users or data, and conditional approval without owner or expiry.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause transition, preserve decision and evidence, support users, remediate mismatch, and reconvene the authority. Reopen on material defect, user harm, changed scope, or failed operation.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Authority, criteria, users, scenarios, evidence, defects, exceptions, decision, readiness, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending authorized acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve acceptance evidence, participant and decision records, exceptions, readiness, support, recovery, and open gaps. Hand off to Release, Operations, Support, Quality, Governance, and Product owners.
