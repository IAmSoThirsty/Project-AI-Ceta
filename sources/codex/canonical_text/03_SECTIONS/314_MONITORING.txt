SECTION 314 — Monitoring

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Continuously observe defined states, thresholds, outcomes, and signals and route meaningful deviations to accountable humans or controlled automation.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A monitoring model with targets, signals, thresholds, frequency, source, owner, alert action, escalation, privacy, retention, cost, and blind spots.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Monitoring fails when signals are noisy, thresholds arbitrary, actions unowned, or dashboards disconnected from users. No alert can mean healthy, blind, broken, or unmonitored.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when ongoing observation supports operations, security, safety, capacity, quality, compliance, or user outcomes.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no ongoing state or outcome needs observation and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use observability, metrics, logs, traces, service levels, incident, privacy, security, cost, and operations baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Target, outcome, signal, source, threshold, frequency, context, owner, alert, escalation, retention, privacy, and response.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners define what matters and response; operators tune and act; privacy and security owners approve collection; humans decide severity and action.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may summarize monitoring, identify anomalies, draft thresholds and alerts, and recommend investigation. It must not suppress, alter, attribute, or act on alerts beyond authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Monitoring Engineer, SRE, Operations Engineer, Security Auditor, Analyst, Observer, Tester, Verifier, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define targets; instrument; set thresholds; collect; validate; alert; triage; tune; protect and retain; test false positives and misses; review after change; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show target, signal, threshold, source, owner, action, evidence, privacy, and limitation. Require human approval for alert action, threshold, retention, and response.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use multi-signal health, business context, resilient collectors, time alignment, access, redaction, alert routing, and safe behavior when monitoring fails.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show target, signal health, threshold, alerts, owner, evidence freshness, cost, and authorized acknowledge, tune, mute with expiry, escalate, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Monitoring charter; target and signal inventory; threshold and alert catalog; routing and escalation; test, incident, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify target, signal, source, threshold, period, method, result, owner, action, and limitation. Alert absence proves only the monitored signal and coverage.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify signal, threshold, route, escalation, latency, privacy, retention, cost, false positive, false negative, collector failure, and action. Test detection of known scenarios.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Alert fatigue, mute without expiry, threshold by habit, no owner, collector single point, metric without action, and monitoring health confused with target health.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Restore collector, preserve evidence, use alternate signal, notify owners, and reassess blind period and impact. Reopen on target, signal, threshold, service, or incident change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Targets, signals, thresholds, owners, alerts, actions, evidence, privacy, retention, cost, blind spots, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve monitoring configs, alerts, incidents, gaps, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Observability, Logging, Tracing, Metrics, SRE, Security, and Operations.
