SECTION 402 — Post-Implementation Review

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Compare intended outcomes, assumptions, evidence, user experience, operations, cost, risk, and actual results after implementation, release, migration, or transition.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A review record with objective, baseline, implementation, expected and actual outcome, evidence, variance, user and operator impact, unresolved risk, actions, and decision.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Implementation completion is not outcome. Review exposes whether the work solved the intended problem, created new debt or harm, and produced lessons for future decisions.

APPLICABILITY

Apply after a material implementation, launch, migration, policy, process, or operational change when outcome or learning matters.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when the change is low consequence, reversible, and creates no material outcome, user, operational, financial, safety, or learning claim beyond existing evidence.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use objective, requirements, acceptance, validation, release, operations, metrics, feedback, incident, financial, risk, and continuity records.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Objective, baseline, implementation, expected outcome, actual data, users, operators, cost, risk, incidents, feedback, acceptance, and unresolved questions.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners provide honest result and context; reviewers challenge attribution; humans decide corrective action, continuation, compensation, and whether to reopen or retire.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may summarize authorized evidence, compare expected and actual, identify variance, and draft questions. It must not manufacture success, assign blame, infer causality, or close actions without human review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Verifier, Validator, Analyst, Researcher, Product Manager, Operations Engineer, User Experience Reviewer, Critic, Governance Reviewer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Restate objective; collect baseline and actual evidence; compare; seek user and operator feedback; analyze variance and harms; record dissent; assign action; decide; and schedule follow-up.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show objective, baseline, evidence, outcome, variance, uncertainty, user impact, action, owner, and limitation. Require human interpretation and preserve disagreement.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Link implementation, version, metrics, users, incidents, cost, support, risk, and outcome; preserve pre-change comparison, evidence lineage, and correction path.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show objective, expected and actual, evidence freshness, variance, risks, user impact, owner, actions, and authorized accept, remediate, compensate, reopen, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Review charter; baseline and actual; variance and impact; feedback and lessons; action and decision; evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify objective, baseline, implementation, metric, period, source, method, result, user or operator, uncertainty, and limitation. Post-launch metric alone does not prove causality or value.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify objective, baseline, actual outcome, users, operations, cost, security, privacy, accessibility, incidents, support, actions, and follow-up.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Celebratory report, success by launch, blame, cherry-picked evidence, no baseline, user voice omitted, action without owner, and review used to close rather than learn.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Correct record, mitigate harm, assign remediation, communicate, preserve evidence, and revisit decision. Reopen on new evidence, variance, incident, user harm, or objective change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Objective, baseline, actual outcome, evidence, variance, impact, actions, owners, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve review, data, feedback, incidents, decisions, actions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Product, Operations, Quality, Risk, Governance, and Lessons Learned.
