SECTION 081 — UX Architecture

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define the structural organization of user experience across journeys, capabilities, channels, states, patterns, content, accessibility, and operating contexts.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

 - A scoped UX landscape connecting user outcomes to journeys, interaction structures, content, systems, services, and governance.
 - Stable experience models, principles, patterns, evidence, controls, measures, and lifecycle handoffs.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Without an experience architecture, teams optimize isolated screens and create inconsistent states, terminology, accessibility, and recovery. Architecture makes cross-product experience coherence inspectable.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to products, platforms, services, suites, multi-channel ecosystems, design systems, and complex user or operator journeys.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No material journey, pattern, content, state, accessibility, channel, or governance effect; or an unchanged approved UX architecture with impact recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Strategy, research, journeys, information and application architecture, domain rules, design system, content, accessibility, privacy, security, operations, and decision authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Strategy, research, journeys, information and application architecture, domain rules, design system, content, accessibility, privacy, security, operations, and decision authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve experience principles, boundaries, populations, inclusion, consistency, tradeoffs, and acceptance. They decide exceptions and retain authority over user impact and commitments.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may map journeys, identify pattern drift, and propose structures. It must preserve evidence, context, and dissent and must not impose a universal user model or silently change standards.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

UX Strategist, Architect, Researcher, User Experience Reviewer, Interaction Designer, Content Designer, Analyst, Critic, Governance Reviewer, Accessibility specialist, Tester, Verifier, Implementer, Documentation Writer, and Memory Curator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define experience scope, principles, journey and state taxonomy, channels, patterns, content relationships, accessibility, ownership, dependencies, exceptions, measurements, validation, and handoff.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show journey, state, pattern, actor, authority, automation, evidence, accessibility, and recovery. AI suggestions remain proposed and do not bypass human review or design-system governance.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Connect journeys, states, information, interactions, components, services, content, identity, permissions, analytics, accessibility, localization, error, and lifecycle. Keep architecture separate from implementation.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show UX baseline, journeys, pattern versions, users, dependencies, evidence, exceptions, accessibility status, and controls to approve, correct, supersede, restrict, or reopen.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

UX architecture charter; journey and state models; experience principles; pattern and channel map; ownership and dependency records; accessibility, content, evidence, decision, exception, and handoff package.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify population, context, journey, version, source, method, result, uncertainty, and limitation. A pattern inventory or consistency score proves only bounded structural coverage.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify journey completeness, state consistency, pattern reuse, accessibility, content, localization, error and recovery, cross-channel continuity, and impact of exceptions with representative users.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Forcing uniformity where context differs; pattern libraries without behavior; ignoring backstage systems; visual consistency over comprehension; inaccessible states; and architecture that cannot represent exception or recovery.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Quarantine harmful patterns, preserve prior versions and evidence, provide alternate path, and obtain owner review. Reopen when products, populations, journeys, platforms, policies, or evidence change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Scope, principles, journeys, states, patterns, ownership, accessibility, evidence, controls, recovery, open issues, and acceptance authority are explicit. Remain CONTENT-COMPLETE pending reconciliation, hostile review, and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve models, versions, rationale, exceptions, evidence, research, decisions, and next action. Hand off to Interaction, UI, Visual, Content, Design Systems, Application, Accessibility, Testing, and Operations.
