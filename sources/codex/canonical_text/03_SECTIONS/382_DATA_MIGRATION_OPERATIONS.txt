SECTION 382 — Data Migration Operations

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Move, transform, reconcile, validate, cut over, and retire data stores or schemas while preserving meaning, integrity, privacy, availability, lineage, and recovery.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A data-migration model with source, target, mapping, scope, quality, privacy, compatibility, rehearsal, cutover, dual-run, rollback, reconciliation, and owner acceptance.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Migration can lose, duplicate, transform, expose, or silently reinterpret data. A completed job or equal row count does not prove semantic correctness or downstream continuity.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when data, schema, database, warehouse, lake, archive, provider, format, or location changes through movement or transformation.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no data, schema, store, lineage, privacy, consumer, or lifecycle behavior changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use data architecture, quality, privacy, security, database, storage, records, backup, restore, application, and operations baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Source, target, schema, mapping, data scope, quality, consumers, privacy, access, rehearsal, cutover, rollback, reconciliation, and retention.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Data owners approve mapping, scope, privacy, downtime, cutover, rollback, deletion, and acceptance. Domain owners judge semantic meaning and downstream use.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inspect authorized metadata and bounded samples, draft mappings and tests, and identify anomalies. It must not expose raw data, execute migration, delete source, or declare reconciliation without human authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Data Migration Engineer, Data Architect, Database Administrator, Data Quality Analyst, Privacy Reviewer, Security Auditor, Tester, Verifier, and Operations Engineer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Inventory source and target; map schema and meaning; classify data; rehearse; validate; back up; migrate; dual-run or cut over; reconcile; monitor; retain or delete; and hand off.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show source, target, schema, mapping, data scope, privacy, owner, action, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for migration, cutover, deletion, sensitive data, and rollback.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use versioned mapping, checksums, lineage, idempotence, dual-write or staged cutover, reconciliation, rollback, access, privacy, and downstream compatibility.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show migration phase, source, target, data quality, progress, consumers, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized pause, validate, cut over, roll back, reconcile, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Migration charter; source and target; mapping and quality; rehearsal and cutover; reconciliation and deletion; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify source, target, schema, mapping, population, data point, method, time, result, reconciliation, actor, and limitation. Row count equality proves neither meaning nor completeness.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify schema, mapping, meaning, quality, privacy, access, lineage, performance, consumers, rehearsal, cutover, rollback, reconciliation, retention, and deletion.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Copy without mapping, source deleted early, row count as correctness, sensitive data exposed, consumer not tested, one-way migration, and rollback without data plan.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause writes or cutover, preserve both states, roll back or restore, reconcile, notify consumers, correct data, and reassess retention and user impact. Reopen on source, target, schema, mapping, or consumer change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Source, target, mapping, scope, quality, privacy, cutover, rollback, reconciliation, evidence, owners, deletion, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve mappings, versions, rehearsals, cutovers, reconciliations, backups, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Data Operations, Database, Storage, Application, Privacy, Security, and Records.
