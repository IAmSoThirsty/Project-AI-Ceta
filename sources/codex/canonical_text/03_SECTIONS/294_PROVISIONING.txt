SECTION 294 — Provisioning

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Create and initialize authorized resources, identities, devices, accounts, environments, data, and configuration with known ownership, security, and lifecycle.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A provisioning model with request, identity, eligibility, allocation, initialization, secrets, configuration, inventory, validation, handoff, recovery, and deprovisioning.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Provisioning establishes authority and state. Unverified automation can create orphaned resources, excessive privilege, wrong tenant or region, exposed secrets, and no path to clean removal.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when resources, accounts, devices, tenants, environments, services, or data are created or initialized for use.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no resource, identity, allocation, initialization, access, or lifecycle behavior changes and an approved exact baseline applies.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use IAM, authorization, infrastructure, configuration, environment, secrets, data, policy, billing, security, support, and retirement baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Request, requester, owner, eligibility, resource, tenant, region, identity, quota, configuration, secret reference, validation, billing, and deprovisioning.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners approve eligibility, allocation, identity, access, cost, data, exceptions, and retirement. Humans review high-impact or privileged provisioning and retain appeal.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may validate requests, draft provisioning plans, identify missing metadata, and summarize state. It must not create identities or resources, grant access, choose region, expose secrets, or deprovision without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Provisioning Engineer, IAM Engineer, Infrastructure Engineer, Platform Engineer, Security Auditor, Data Engineer, Tester, Verifier, and Operations Engineer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Validate request and authority; allocate; create; initialize; configure; inject secrets; register; test; notify and hand off; monitor; reconcile; and deprovision or recover.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show requester, owner, resource, tenant, region, identity, cost, data, action, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for privileged, sensitive, billable, regional, and destructive actions.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use idempotence, least privilege, policy, quotas, ownership tags, secret injection, inventory, approval, audit, rollback, and clean deprovisioning.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show request, resource, owner, access, tenant, region, cost, status, evidence freshness, and authorized approve, reject, provision, restrict, recover, and deprovision controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Provisioning request and policy; resource and identity record; initialization and validation; cost and ownership; handoff, deprovisioning, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify request, requester, owner, resource, identity, tenant, region, configuration, method, time, result, and limitation. Resource creation proves neither correct authorization nor readiness.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify eligibility, ownership, identity, access, tenant, region, configuration, secret, quota, cost, inventory, validation, handoff, and deprovisioning. Test duplicate and partial request.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Orphaned resources, shared identity, wrong tenant, missing owner, secret in output, quota bypass, provisioning without billing, and no clean removal.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Restrict or deprovision unsafe resource, preserve state, rotate credentials, correct metadata, notify owner, and reassess data and cost. Reopen on request, policy, provider, resource, or owner change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Request, authority, owner, resource, identity, tenant, region, configuration, cost, evidence, handoff, recovery, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve request, resource, identity, configuration, secrets references, cost, handoff, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Installation, Environment, Platform, Security, Billing, Operations, and Retirement.
