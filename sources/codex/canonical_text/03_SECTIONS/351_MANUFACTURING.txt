SECTION 351 — Manufacturing

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Transform approved materials, components, instructions, software, labor, and equipment into consistent, traceable, safe, and accepted products or outputs.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A manufacturing model with process, inputs, equipment, people, quality, traceability, configuration, work instructions, safety, capacity, inspection, nonconformance, and release.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Manufacturing variation can turn a correct design into inconsistent, unsafe, counterfeit, or untraceable output. Schedule and yield cannot replace quality, safety, provenance, and worker protection.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when physical or repeatable production transforms materials, components, firmware, software, data, or assemblies into deliverables.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no production process, input, quality, traceability, safety, or output behavior changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use product, hardware, embedded, supply chain, quality, safety, procurement, facility, workforce, testing, and logistics baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Design, bill of materials, work instructions, inputs, equipment, process, operators, quality, safety, capacity, traceability, inspection, and acceptance.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Manufacturing and quality owners approve process, materials, substitutions, safety, release, nonconformance, and capacity. Workers can stop unsafe work and report defects.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze authorized production data, identify variation, draft instructions and inspections, and propose scheduling. It must not change process, approve lot, infer worker performance, or control equipment without qualified human authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Manufacturing Engineer, Quality Engineer, Hardware Engineer, Safety Reviewer, Supply Chain Manager, Planner, Tester, Verifier, Observer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define process and inputs; qualify equipment and operators; issue instructions; produce; inspect; trace; handle nonconformance; protect safety; measure yield and quality; release; and improve or retire process.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show product, lot, process, input, equipment, operator role, result, safety, evidence, and limitation. Require human review for substitution, quality release, safety, and worker-impact decisions.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use revision-controlled instructions, lot traceability, equipment calibration, segregated nonconformance, quality gates, safety interlocks, secure provisioning, and recall path.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show process, lot, input, equipment, quality, safety, capacity, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized hold, inspect, quarantine, release, rework, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Manufacturing charter; process and work instruction; input and lot; quality and safety; nonconformance and release; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify product, lot, input, process, equipment, revision, operator role, method, time, result, inspector, and limitation. Yield or inspection sample proves only its declared population and method.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify process, input, equipment, calibration, traceability, quality, safety, software or firmware, inspection, nonconformance, release, and recall. Test variation and equipment failure.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Unapproved substitution, traceability gap, unsafe work, calibration expired, sample theater, quality release by schedule, and defect or rework hidden.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stop or quarantine, preserve lot and process evidence, inspect or rework, notify owners, recall where needed, and reassess safety and downstream impact. Reopen on process, input, equipment, or design change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Process, inputs, equipment, people, safety, quality, traceability, release, evidence, owners, recovery, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending qualified acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve work instructions, revisions, lots, equipment, inspections, nonconformance, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Production Planning, Assembly, Certification, Provisioning, Logistics, Quality, and Operations.
