SECTION 327 — Disaster Recovery

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Restore essential systems, data, services, and control after a disaster or major loss of site, provider, infrastructure, people, or trust.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A disaster-recovery model with scenarios, priorities, recovery time and data objectives, dependencies, runbooks, authority, alternate environment, evidence, testing, and return to service.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Disaster recovery is an operational claim that must survive real dependency and authority loss. A backup or plan without a tested restore, staffing, access, or reconciliation path is not recovery capability.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when loss or unavailability of a system, facility, region, provider, data, identity, or operational capability could materially affect essential outcomes.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability only when no disaster scenario, essential service, data, dependency, or recovery claim is material and an approved exact baseline supports it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use continuity, backup, restore, failover, service, data, identity, security, facilities, supplier, communication, and operational readiness baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Disaster scenarios, services, priorities, RTO, RPO, dependencies, alternate site, data, access, runbooks, staff, communication, exercises, and acceptance.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Recovery owners and commanders approve declaration, priority, failover, data loss, communication, resource, and return-to-service. Humans accept residual impact and legal duties.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may map dependencies, draft recovery steps, analyze exercise evidence, and identify gaps. It must not declare disaster, fail over, restore, overwrite data, or communicate emergency status without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Disaster Recovery Planner, Incident Commander, Operations Engineer, SRE, Data Engineer, Security Auditor, Continuity Planner, Tester, Verifier, and Coordinator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define scenarios and priorities; establish RTO/RPO; map dependencies; prepare alternate environment and access; back up; exercise; declare; recover; reconcile; communicate; return; and improve.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show scenario, priority, service, data, authority, RTO/RPO, action, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for declaration, failover, restore, data loss, communication, and closure.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use alternate control path, independent credentials, replicated data, immutable backups, infrastructure rebuild, dependency substitution, reconciliation, staged return, and evidence preservation.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show disaster mode, service priority, recovery phase, data point, dependencies, owner, evidence freshness, and authorized declare, prioritize, fail over, restore, communicate, and return controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

DR charter; scenario and priority map; RTO/RPO; recovery architecture and runbooks; exercise results; data reconciliation; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify scenario, service, version, backup, restore, data point, time, authority, method, result, impact, and limitation. A tabletop or backup list proves only planning or inventory.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Exercise declaration, access, alternate environment, backup, restore, dependencies, data loss, reconciliation, communication, staffing, security, and return. Record actual RTO/RPO and gaps.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Recovery dependent on failed system, backup not restored, credentials unavailable, data reconciliation omitted, priority unclear, tabletop only, and recovery success declared before user outcome.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Protect people and essential service, preserve evidence, recover by priority, reconcile, communicate, monitor, and reassess residual impact. Reopen on scenario, dependency, data, provider, or objective change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Scenarios, priorities, dependencies, RTO/RPO, access, data, runbooks, evidence, exercises, owners, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve DR plans, backups, exercises, access, recoveries, decisions, communications, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Continuity, Backup, Restore, Failover, Security, Data, and Operations.
