SECTION 290 — Deployment Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Engineer repeatable, observable, secure, and reversible mechanisms for placing exact artifacts and configuration into declared targets.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A deployment mechanism with artifact, target, identity, strategy, configuration, migration, health, policy, rollback, audit, and recovery.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Deployment changes live state. A mechanism that cannot identify what changed, stop safely, observe user impact, or recover from partial execution cannot support trustworthy operations.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when software, firmware, infrastructure, configuration, data, models, or policies are placed or activated in an environment.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no target, artifact, configuration, data, or live state changes and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use artifact, environment, configuration, identity, testing, release, migration, backup, rollback, monitoring, and incident baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Artifact, target, version, identity, strategy, configuration, migration, health, gate, telemetry, rollback, and authorization.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Deployment owners approve target, strategy, gate, identity, migration, rollback, exception, and stop. Humans decide production and destructive deployment.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft manifests, analyze deployment state, and recommend pause or rollback. It must not deploy, widen target, change gates, or claim completion without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Deployment Engineer, Release Engineer, Platform Engineer, Tester, Verifier, Operations Engineer, Security Auditor, and Coordinator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Validate artifact and target; check permission and policy; stage; deploy; monitor; pause or roll back; reconcile configuration and data; record; and maintain the mechanism.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show artifact, target, identity, phase, health, evidence, impact, and rollback. Require human approval for production, migration, gate bypass, and destructive actions.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use progressive rollout, health and business metrics, immutable artifacts, idempotent steps, migration checkpoints, independent control, audit, and rollback.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show deployment phase, target, artifact, health, incidents, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized start, pause, stop, rollback, promote, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Deployment specification; target and identity; rollout and health record; migration and rollback; logs, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify artifact, target, phase, identity, configuration, method, time, result, impact, and limitation. Execution completion proves neither readiness nor user success.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify identity, target, artifact, health, gate, migration, rollback, audit, cleanup, and partial-failure behavior. Test stale health and control-plane loss.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

No target validation, broad rollout, health blind to user outcome, non-idempotent step, migration without rollback, and deployment success treated as acceptance.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause, roll back or restore, preserve state, communicate, repair data, and reassess impact. Reopen on artifact, target, config, dependency, or health change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Artifact, target, identity, strategy, health, migration, rollback, evidence, owners, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve manifests, logs, artifacts, rollout, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Release, Operations, Platform, Security, Support, and Incident Response.
