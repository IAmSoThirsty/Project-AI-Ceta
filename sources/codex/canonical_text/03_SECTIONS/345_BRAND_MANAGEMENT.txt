SECTION 345 — Brand Management

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Govern the identity, promise, expression, use, protection, accessibility, consistency, and evolution of a brand across products, services, channels, and relationships.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A brand model with purpose, audience, promise, identity, assets, usage, rights, accessibility, governance, evidence, reputation, change, and recovery.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Brand communicates expectation and trust. Inconsistent, inaccessible, misleading, or culturally careless use can damage people and relationships while making product claims harder to interpret.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a name, mark, visual or verbal identity, promise, public representation, or branded experience is created, changed, licensed, or retired.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no brand identity, promise, asset, audience, public representation, or trust expectation changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use product, user research, ethics, accessibility, IP, legal, marketing, localization, community, and communications baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Purpose, audience, promise, identity, assets, usage, rights, channels, locale, accessibility, reputation, approvals, and evidence.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Brand owners approve promise, identity, usage, rights, cultural context, public response, exceptions, and retirement. Humans resolve reputation, harm, and ownership decisions.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft variants, analyze authorized feedback, check asset use, and identify inconsistency. It must not impersonate people, make public commitments, infer cultural acceptance, or publish brand material without human review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Brand Manager, Product Marketer, User Experience Reviewer, Researcher, IP Analyst, Legal Advisor, Accessibility Reviewer, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define purpose and audience; create identity; verify rights and accessibility; govern use; localize; monitor reputation; respond; update; and retire or transfer assets.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show asset, promise, audience, locale, source, rights, evidence, and limitation. Require human review for public, cultural, legal, accessibility, and crisis communication.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use asset registry, rights, version, accessibility, localization, approval, publication, takedown, and correction paths. Keep brand promise traceable to actual capability.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show asset, usage, audience, promise, evidence freshness, rights, incidents, owner, and authorized approve, correct, restrict, withdraw, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Brand charter; audience and promise; identity and asset register; rights and accessibility; usage and reputation; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify asset, promise, source, audience, locale, rights, method, time, result, reviewer, and limitation. Awareness or sentiment is bounded evidence, not reputation truth.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify asset rights, consistency, accessibility, localization, product alignment, public use, cultural context, correction, and takedown. Test misleading or outdated claim.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Brand promise outruns product, inaccessible identity, unlicensed asset, cultural harm, inconsistent use, synthetic sentiment, and crisis response without accountable owner.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Withdraw or correct, communicate, protect affected people, preserve evidence, resolve rights, and reassess promise and use. Reopen on product, audience, culture, law, or reputation change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Purpose, audience, promise, assets, rights, accessibility, usage, evidence, owners, recovery, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve assets, approvals, rights, usage, incidents, communications, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Product Marketing, Technical Marketing, IP, Legal, Product, Support, and Governance.
