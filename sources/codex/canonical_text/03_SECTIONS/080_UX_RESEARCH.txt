SECTION 080 — UX Research

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define ethical, rigorous research that establishes what people need, do, understand, experience, and cannot do in a declared context.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

 - A research question, population, context, method, consent and protection plan, evidence record, analysis, limitation, and decision link.
 - Reproducible findings that distinguish observation, interpretation, hypothesis, and recommendation.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Research reduces uncertainty only when its methods fit the question and its limitations are visible. Weak sampling or leading interpretation can institutionalize the wrong problem.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to discovery, evaluation, accessibility, service, product, operational, safety, and post-release questions involving people or affected communities.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No human or affected-party claim, decision, behavior, need, burden, comprehension, or acceptance inference; or an unchanged research plan with impact recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Research question, domain context, population, recruitment, consent, privacy, safety, accessibility, method, instruments, incentives, analysis plan, and decision owner.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Research question, domain context, population, recruitment, consent, privacy, safety, accessibility, method, instruments, incentives, analysis plan, and decision owner.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve research purpose, participant protection, consent, inclusion, compensation, interpretation, disclosure, and use of findings. They decide when evidence is sufficient and retain authority over decisions.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may help recruit within approved criteria, transcribe, code, summarize, compare, and identify gaps. It must preserve participant privacy, quotes, context, uncertainty, and dissent; it must not fabricate participants, consent, findings, or universal claims.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Researcher, User Experience Reviewer, Analyst, Observer, Critic, Governance Reviewer, Accessibility specialist, Security and Privacy Auditors, Tester, Verifier, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, and Decision Arbiter Advisory.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Frame question; define population and method; protect and recruit participants; collect and preserve evidence; analyze with alternative interpretations; validate findings; state limits; link decisions; archive and hand off.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show participant protection, method, source, quote or observation, interpretation, uncertainty, and decision use. AI-generated coding remains reviewable; participants can withdraw where promised and human contact remains available.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate participant evidence, researcher interpretation, hypothesis, recommendation, and decision. Protect identity, consent, access, retention, provenance, translation, accessibility, and reproducibility.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show question, sample, method, status, findings, confidence limits, dissent, risks, consent, evidence freshness, and controls to restrict, correct, withdraw, or reopen research.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Research protocol, participant and consent records, instruments, raw and derived evidence, analysis, findings, limitations, accessibility and risk records, decisions, and handoff package.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify participant or population, context, method, sample, date, researcher, source, consent, result, uncertainty, and limitation. A small sample, quote, survey, or AI summary cannot support universal claims.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify recruitment and consent, method fit, inclusion, leading effects, data protection, transcription, coding, rival interpretations, member checking where appropriate, and decision traceability.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Leading questions; convenience samples presented as representative; persona invention; quote mining; coercive incentives; re-identification; treating silence as satisfaction; and erasing dissent or negative cases.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stop unsafe research, protect participants, restrict or delete data as authorized, preserve an audit trail, disclose limitations, and commission corrective research. Reconsider decisions when sampling, consent, context, or interpretation is challenged.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Question, population, method, protection, evidence, analysis, findings, limitations, decisions, and acceptance authority are explicit. Remain CONTENT-COMPLETE pending reconciliation, hostile review, and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve protocol, consent, instruments, evidence, analysis, dissent, decisions, retention state, and next action. Hand off to UX, HCI, Experience, Service, Accessibility, Privacy, Governance, Product, and Testing.
