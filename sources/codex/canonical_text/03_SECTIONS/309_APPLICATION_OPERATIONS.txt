SECTION 309 — Application Operations

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Operate applications and their runtime dependencies so user outcomes, data, security, support, performance, and recovery remain within declared boundaries.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An application-operations model with service owner, versions, configuration, dependencies, health, jobs, data, access, release, support, incidents, and recovery.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Application behavior is more than process health. Feature, data, dependency, user, support, and operational failure can occur while the application reports as running.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when an application or service is deployed, configured, monitored, supported, changed, recovered, or retired.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no application, service, user, data, configuration, dependency, or operational behavior changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use application architecture, runtime, release, configuration, data, identity, security, observability, SRE, support, and recovery baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Application, version, config, users, data, dependencies, health, jobs, metrics, logs, traces, incidents, support, SLO, and recovery.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Application owners approve release, config, access, data, support, incident, recovery, exception, and retirement. Humans decide user-impact and service-risk tradeoffs.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze authorized application signals, logs, traces, and incidents; draft diagnosis and runbooks; and recommend bounded action. It must not alter, access restricted data, suppress alerts, or close incidents without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Application Operations Engineer, Application Engineer, SRE, Operations Engineer, Security Auditor, Incident Responder, Tester, Verifier, and Support Reviewer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Operate runtime and jobs; monitor health and outcomes; manage config and releases; support users; respond; manage data and dependencies; back up; recover; reduce toil; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show app, version, config, user journey, data, signal, action, authority, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for production, data, security, recovery, and communication.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use health plus business indicators, structured logs, traces, feature control, graceful degradation, idempotent jobs, rollback, data recovery, and support diagnostics.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show application state, version, dependencies, health, incidents, user impact, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized pause, rollback, scale, restrict, recover, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Application operations charter; service and dependency map; runbooks; release and config; monitoring and support; incident, recovery, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify app, version, environment, user outcome, data, signal, source, method, time, result, actor, and limitation. Process health proves neither application correctness nor user success.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify release, config, access, data, health, job, dependency, alert, log, trace, support, backup, restore, rollback, and retirement. Test user-impacting failure.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Process-only monitoring, hidden job failure, config drift, log without context, support unprepared, recovery without data reconciliation, and application uptime treated as outcome.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stabilize, preserve evidence, pause change, rollback or fail over, restore data, communicate, support users, and reassess. Reopen on app, dependency, data, config, user, or incident change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Application, users, data, versions, config, dependencies, signals, support, recovery, evidence, owners, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve app baseline, runbooks, releases, configs, incidents, telemetry, backups, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Platform, SRE, Security, Data, Support, and Release.
