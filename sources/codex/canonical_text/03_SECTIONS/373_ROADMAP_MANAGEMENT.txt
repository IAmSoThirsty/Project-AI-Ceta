SECTION 373 — Roadmap Management

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Sequence outcomes, bets, capabilities, dependencies, options, commitments, and learning over time while preserving strategy, evidence, capacity, and change authority.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A roadmap model with objective, horizon, themes, initiatives, outcomes, evidence, dependencies, capacity, risk, decision gates, confidence, ownership, and change history.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Roadmaps influence investment and expectation but are not promises by default. False precision, feature lists without outcomes, and stale priorities can consume capacity while avoiding hard choices.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when work is sequenced, prioritized, forecast, committed, funded, deferred, or retired across a portfolio, product, program, or capability.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no priority, sequence, investment, dependency, outcome, or commitment changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use mission, strategy, product, portfolio, research, market, finance, capacity, risk, architecture, feedback, and delivery baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Objectives, outcomes, initiatives, evidence, dependencies, capacity, cost, risks, options, stakeholders, constraints, decisions, and review cadence.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Roadmap owners facilitate priorities; accountable humans choose tradeoffs, investment, commitment, de-scoping, and stop criteria. Stakeholders can challenge assumptions and evidence.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may summarize evidence, map dependencies, draft options, and identify inconsistency. It must not set priority, promise delivery, invent confidence, or remove work without accountable human decision.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Planner, Product Manager, Portfolio Manager, Analyst, Researcher, Architect, Financial Analyst, Critic, Verifier, and Coordinator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define objective and horizon; collect evidence; frame outcomes; identify options and dependencies; assess capacity and risk; prioritize; publish confidence and assumptions; review; change; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show objective, outcome, evidence, confidence, dependency, capacity, cost, risk, owner, and limitation. Require human approval for priority, commitment, funding, cancellation, and public roadmap claim.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Link roadmap item to outcome, requirements, architecture, teams, resources, dependencies, evidence, release, and retirement. Preserve option and decision history.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show roadmap horizon, outcomes, priorities, capacity, dependencies, risks, evidence freshness, owner, confidence, and authorized prioritize, defer, fund, stop, revise, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Roadmap charter; outcome and initiative; evidence and confidence; dependency and capacity; decision, commitment, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify objective, item, outcome, source, date, assumption, confidence, capacity, cost, risk, decision, and limitation. A roadmap position proves intent, not delivery or value.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify outcome, evidence, dependency, capacity, cost, risk, owner, stakeholder understanding, decision gate, confidence, and change traceability.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Feature list as strategy, false dates, priority by loudness, capacity ignored, dependency hidden, confidence invented, sunk-cost continuation, and roadmap as promise without authority.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Mark stale, re-prioritize, communicate, preserve decisions, stop or de-scope, and reassess outcomes and capacity. Reopen on mission, evidence, market, strategy, dependency, or risk change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Objectives, outcomes, items, evidence, confidence, capacity, cost, dependencies, owners, decisions, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve roadmap versions, decisions, evidence, commitments, assumptions, changes, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Product, Portfolio, Program, Architecture, Finance, Engineering, and Governance.
