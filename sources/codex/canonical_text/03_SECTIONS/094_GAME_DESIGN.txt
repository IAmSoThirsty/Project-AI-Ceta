SECTION 094 — Game Design

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define rules, goals, systems, player agency, feedback, challenge, progression, content, safety, accessibility, and fairness for games and game-like experiences.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

 - A declared player population, context, platform, mechanics, content, economy, social model, moderation, and lifecycle.
 - Coherent rule and content specifications with evidence, tests, safeguards, and acceptance criteria.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Games shape behavior through rules, rewards, uncertainty, and social systems. Engagement or retention is not proof of wellbeing, fairness, or meaningful outcome.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to digital, physical, educational, serious, social, competitive, casino-like, and interactive game systems.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No game rule, player behavior, content, reward, social, safety, accessibility, privacy, or monetization effect; or an unchanged approved game baseline with impact recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Goals, player research, rules, mechanics, content, platform, economy, identity, privacy, safety, accessibility, moderation, monetization, analytics, and authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Goals, player research, rules, mechanics, content, platform, economy, identity, privacy, safety, accessibility, moderation, monetization, analytics, and authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve purpose, audience, content, age bounds, fairness, monetization, safety, accessibility, moderation, and acceptance. They retain pause, refund, appeal, and retirement authority.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may generate content, balance proposals, and tests. It must disclose generation and preserve player safety and privacy; it must not manipulate, target vulnerability, or change live rules without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Game Designer, Level Designer, Narrative Designer, UX Reviewer, Researcher, Content Designer, Safety and Privacy Auditors, Tester, Verifier, Critic, Governance Reviewer, Implementer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define goals, rules, mechanics, progression, feedback, balance, content, accessibility, social interaction, moderation, economy, safety, analytics, failure, and recovery; prototype and test.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show rule, player state, automation, consequence, probability, content source, permissions, spending, moderation, and exit. AI content remains reviewable and player controls remain available.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate rules, state, content, economy, identity, matchmaking, moderation, telemetry, monetization, and lifecycle. Make randomness, consent, limits, and recovery inspectable.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show game and player state, content version, economy, moderation, evidence, risks, and controls to pause, restrict, report, refund, appeal, or retire.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Game design document, rules and economy model, player journeys, content and moderation plan, accessibility and safety assessment, tests, evidence, decisions, and handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify player population, platform, rules, version, context, method, result, uncertainty, and limitation. Engagement, playtime, or win rate is bounded evidence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify rules, balance, accessibility, privacy, spending limits, randomness, exploits, moderation, safety, recovery, platform compatibility, and representative player outcomes.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Dark patterns; targeting vulnerable players; opaque odds; pay-to-progress pressure; inaccessible controls; unmoderated abuse; and optimizing retention over wellbeing or fairness.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause or disable harmful mechanics, protect players, preserve records, refund or compensate where authorized, moderate, and review affected populations before resuming.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Goals, rules, players, content, economy, safety, accessibility, evidence, tests, recovery, open risks, and acceptance are explicit. Remain CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve rules, content, builds, economy, moderation, decisions, evidence, incidents, and next action. Hand off to Level, Narrative, UI, UX, Audio, Game Engineering, Safety, and Operations.
