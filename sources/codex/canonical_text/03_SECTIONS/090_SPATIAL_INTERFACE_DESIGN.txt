SECTION 090 — Spatial Interface Design

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define interaction in physical or three-dimensional space, including placement, scale, orientation, movement, embodiment, context, and safety.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

 - Scoped space, users, devices, environment, tasks, tracking, content, accessibility, privacy, and lifecycle.
 - Spatial behavior, anchors, controls, evidence, tests, safety, and recovery records.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Spatial interfaces affect orientation, attention, physical movement, and shared environments. Immersion or novelty does not prove comprehension or safety.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to 3D interfaces, room-scale systems, tangible interaction, spatial audio, robotics interfaces, and mixed physical-digital environments.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No spatial placement, tracking, movement, safety, accessibility, privacy, or user-control effect; or an unchanged approved spatial baseline with impact recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

User and environment research, device and tracking limits, task model, physical safety, accessibility, content, privacy, identity, performance, and acceptance authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

User and environment research, device and tracking limits, task model, physical safety, accessibility, content, privacy, identity, performance, and acceptance authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve spatial purpose, safety zones, representation, accessibility, privacy, and acceptance. They retain stop, physical override, and incident authority.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may propose spatial layouts or analyze tracking. It must expose uncertainty and environmental limits and must not place content or command physical action without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Spatial Designer, UX and Interaction Designers, Architect, Researcher, Robotics and Safety specialists, Accessibility specialist, Tester, Verifier, Critic, Governance Reviewer, Implementer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define space, anchors, scale, orientation, gestures, devices, environment, shared ownership, accessibility, privacy, performance, safety, failure, and recovery; prototype and test in representative spaces.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show spatial state, tracking confidence, actor, boundaries, automation, consequence, privacy, and safe exit. AI placements remain proposals and physical stop paths remain independent.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Define coordinate frames, anchors, occlusion, latency, calibration, collision and boundary handling, shared space, identity, data, accessibility, and degraded tracking behavior.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show space, device, user, tracking, boundaries, hazards, evidence, and controls to pause, clear, recalibrate, exit, stop, or recover.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Spatial model, interaction specification, environment and safety map, tracking and calibration record, prototypes, accessibility tests, evidence, decisions, and handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify space, device, tracking, calibration, user, task, version, method, result, and limitation. A virtual demo proves bounded behavior only.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify tracking loss, latency, scale, accessibility, shared space, privacy, collision boundaries, fatigue, motion sensitivity, calibration, interruption, and safe exit.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Ignoring physical context; stale anchors; inaccessible gestures; hidden boundaries; tracking confidence treated as truth; and immersion that prevents stop or orientation.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause or remove spatial content, provide visual or non-spatial fallback, preserve state and evidence, recalibrate or isolate equipment, and retest the environment.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Space, tasks, devices, tracking, safety, accessibility, evidence, tests, recovery, open risks, and acceptance are explicit. Remain CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve maps, anchors, calibration, versions, findings, decisions, evidence, incidents, and next action. Hand off to AR, VR, XR, Robotics, HCI, Accessibility, Safety, and Testing.
