SECTION 150 - Smart Contract Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define governed engineering of executable ledger contracts that encode state, rules, permissions, value transfer, events, and external effects under immutable or upgrade-controlled deployment.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A smart-contract baseline with specification, invariants, authority, keys, interfaces, gas or resource limits, upgrade policy, verification, monitoring, and compensation path.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Contract code can be permanently executable and externally valuable. Bugs, oracle errors, reentrancy, privilege mistakes, and upgrade ambiguity can create irreversible harm.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to on-chain programs, tokens, escrow, DAOs, governance, automated settlement, wallets, or ledger-connected contracts.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

May be non-applicable to ordinary application rules with no ledger execution, cryptographic signing, on-chain state, or irreversible external effect. Record scope, owner, evidence, and reopening trigger.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Requires formal purpose, state model, invariants, threat model, language and runtime, interfaces, oracle and key authority, upgrade, audit, deployment, monitoring, legal, and compensation authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Provide states, functions, roles, assets, permissions, invariants, events, external calls, oracle data, limits, fees, upgrade, pause, dispute, and retirement requirements.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve specification, authority, key custody, deployment, audit, upgrade, pause, assets, and acceptance. Humans retain authority over irreversible value and governance effects.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft code, tests, invariant checks, and audit questions. It must not sign or deploy, claim formal security from tests, or weaken access, pause, upgrade, or compensation controls.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Architect, Cryptography Engineer, Security Auditor, Researcher, Analyst, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Governance Reviewer, Red Team, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, and Decision Arbiter Advisory. Existing role contracts remain controlling.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Specify state, roles, invariants, calls, assets, events, authority, and external dependencies.
2. Review code, dependencies, oracle, gas, upgrade, pause, and key paths.
3. Verify statically, dynamically, adversarially, and formally where warranted before deployment.
4. Deploy staged, monitor, govern upgrades, respond, compensate, and retire through authorized transactions.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show contract address, version, code hash, signer, role, call, state, value, oracle, gas, evidence, and consequence. Human approval is required for deployment, signing, upgrades, and material calls; all decisions remain attributable.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Represent specification, code, compiler, bytecode, state, storage, roles, keys, oracle, external calls, events, upgrade proxy, pause, and compensation as linked versioned artifacts. Model reentrancy, replay, failure, and partial effects.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show contract, network, code, roles, balances, calls, events, alerts, oracle freshness, pause state, evidence, and controls to pause, revoke, upgrade, migrate, or compensate within authority.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Contract specification, state and invariant model, source and bytecode provenance, threat assessment, audit and formal evidence, deployment and key records, monitoring, incident runbook, and compensation or retirement plan.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify network, contract, address, source, compiler, bytecode hash, call, signer, state, value, oracle, method, result, and limitations. Audit, test, formal, runtime, and owner-acceptance evidence remain distinct.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify access control, invariants, reentrancy, arithmetic, replay, oracle failure, denial of service, upgrade, pause, gas, key rotation, event integrity, deployment identity, and compensation paths.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Unaudited deployment; proxy confusion; privileged upgrade; unsafe external call; missing pause; oracle trust; hidden admin key; assuming tests prove safety; and proposing destructive rollback for immutable state.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause or narrow authorized calls, preserve chain evidence, revoke or rotate keys, migrate or compensate through linked transactions, communicate residual loss, and reopen specification, audit, and governance decisions.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Specification, code, authority, invariants, evidence, deployment, monitoring, compensation, and retirement are explicit. Keep CONTENT-COMPLETE until independent verification and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve specification, source, compiler, bytecode, address, keys by reference, transactions, audits, decisions, incidents, evidence, and next safe action. Hand off to Blockchain, Cryptography, Security, Key Management, Testing, Operations, and Governance.
