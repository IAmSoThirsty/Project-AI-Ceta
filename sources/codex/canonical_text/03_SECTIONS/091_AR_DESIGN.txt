SECTION 091 — AR Design

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define augmented-reality experiences that place digital information or controls in relation to the physical world while preserving comprehension, safety, privacy, and user control.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

 - A scoped user, physical environment, device, tracking, content, task, interaction, safety, privacy, and lifecycle model.
 - AR behavior, anchors, occlusion, accessibility, evidence, tests, fallback, and acceptance records.

WHY THIS MATTERS

AR can obscure real hazards, misalign information, expose surroundings, and overload attention. Alignment and novelty are not proof of safe use.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to headsets, mobile AR, industrial overlays, navigation, training, maintenance, medical, retail, and collaborative physical-digital experiences.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No overlay, tracking, physical task, privacy, safety, accessibility, or user-control effect; or an unchanged approved AR baseline with impact recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Environment and user research, device and tracking limits, spatial model, content, task, physical safety, privacy, identity, accessibility, performance, and acceptance authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Environment and user research, device and tracking limits, spatial model, content, task, physical safety, privacy, identity, accessibility, performance, and acceptance authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve overlay purpose, safety, privacy, representation, accessibility, physical boundaries, and acceptance. They retain emergency stop and physical control authority.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may generate overlays or analyze spatial context. It must disclose tracking and recognition uncertainty and must not place consequential guidance or command action without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

AR Designer, Spatial Designer, UX and Interaction Designers, Robotics and Safety specialists, Researcher, Accessibility specialist, Security and Privacy Auditors, Tester, Verifier, Critic, Implementer, and Governance Reviewer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define context, anchors, recognition, overlay, occlusion, field of view, input, warnings, accessibility, privacy, safety, failure, and recovery; prototype and test with representative environments and users.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show physical context, tracking confidence, overlay source, actor, authority, hazard, uncertainty, and safe exit. AI-generated content remains proposed; visual and physical fallbacks remain available.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Define coordinate frames, anchors, calibration, latency, occlusion, depth, data capture, identity, permissions, safe rendering, and degraded or no-tracking behavior.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show environment, device, overlay, confidence, hazards, privacy, evidence, and controls to hide, pause, recalibrate, exit, stop, or recover.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

AR concept and behavior model, environment map, content and anchor specifications, safety and privacy assessment, accessibility tests, prototypes, evidence, decisions, and handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify environment, device, tracking, content, user, task, version, method, result, uncertainty, and limitation. A lab overlay proves bounded conditions only.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify alignment, latency, occlusion, tracking loss, physical hazard visibility, privacy, accessibility, motion sensitivity, input, shared space, fallback, and safe exit.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Overlay hiding hazards; stale or false anchors; attention capture; unannounced recording; inaccessible controls; and recognizing a physical object as permission to act.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Hide overlays, return control to physical environment, preserve evidence, stop related actions, notify users, and recalibrate or fall back before resuming.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Environment, device, overlays, authority, safety, privacy, accessibility, evidence, tests, recovery, open risks, and acceptance are explicit. Remain CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve maps, anchors, content, versions, calibration, findings, decisions, evidence, and next action. Hand off to Spatial, VR, XR, HCI, Robotics, Safety, Privacy, Accessibility, and Testing.
