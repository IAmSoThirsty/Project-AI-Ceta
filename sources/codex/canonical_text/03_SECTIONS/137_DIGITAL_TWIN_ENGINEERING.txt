SECTION 137 - Digital Twin Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define governed engineering of a digital representation linked to a physical, organizational, or operational counterpart through identity, telemetry, state, models, controls, and lifecycle evidence.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A digital-twin baseline with counterpart identity, synchronization, provenance, fidelity, authority, safety boundaries, commands, model drift, and retirement conditions.

WHY THIS MATTERS

A twin can be stale, incomplete, or wrong while appearing live. Confusing representation with authority can cause unsafe commands, privacy exposure, or bad maintenance decisions.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to physical assets, facilities, processes, infrastructure, devices, organizations, or environments represented and linked to a digital system.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

May be non-applicable to an unlinked simulation, dashboard, or static model with no counterpart identity, synchronization, operational use, or command path. Record scope, owner, evidence, and reopening trigger.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Requires counterpart authority, identity, telemetry, data ownership, time synchronization, model purpose, command permissions, safety, privacy, security, calibration, operations, and retirement authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Provide asset identity, sensors, states, telemetry, models, commands, latency, freshness, calibration, users, decisions, safety limits, and failure or offline requirements.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve counterpart identity, data collection, command authority, safety, privacy, calibration, and acceptance. Humans retain authority over physical or material actions.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may detect anomalies, estimate state, and propose maintenance or model updates. It must distinguish telemetry from inference, never issue unapproved commands, and expose stale or missing state.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Architect, Domain Expert, Researcher, Analyst, Safety Auditor, Security Auditor, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Governance Reviewer, Observer, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, and Red Team. Existing role contracts remain controlling.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Identify counterpart, scope, identity, authority, sensors, models, and lifecycle.
2. Define synchronization, provenance, state, calibration, command, safety, privacy, and offline behavior.
3. Validate representation against observed counterpart evidence and test command boundaries.
4. Operate, monitor drift, update, quarantine, and retire with physical owner approval.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show counterpart identity, live or stale state, source, timestamp, model versus measurement, authority, command consequence, and evidence. Human approval is required for actuation and material maintenance decisions.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate counterpart, telemetry, state estimation, simulation, command, policy, and physical effect. Bind data to identity and time; make freshness, uncertainty, calibration, and safe offline state explicit.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show asset, sync health, freshness, data quality, model drift, alerts, commands, approvals, incidents, and controls to pause, isolate, revoke, revert, or retire.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Twin charter, counterpart catalog, identity and telemetry map, model and calibration records, command authority, safety and privacy assessment, validation evidence, runbook, and retirement plan.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify counterpart, sensor, timestamp, model, data, configuration, command, authority, environment, result, uncertainty, and limit. Preserve stale, missing, contradictory, and corrected observations.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify identity, synchronization, freshness, calibration, sensor loss, model drift, unauthorized command, stale state, network loss, safe mode, physical comparison, recovery, and retirement.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Treating a dashboard as a twin; stale telemetry as live; inferred state as measurement; shared identity; unapproved actuation; and hiding model drift or sensor gaps.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stop commands, isolate twin and counterpart, preserve telemetry, use local safe controls, reconcile state, compensate effects, notify owners, and reopen calibration and authority decisions.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Identity, sync, freshness, model, authority, evidence, safety, recovery, and retirement are explicit. Keep CONTENT-COMPLETE until independent verification and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve counterpart identity, telemetry, models, commands, approvals, incidents, evidence, decisions, and next safe action. Hand off to IoT, Embedded, Simulation, Control, Safety, Security, Data, and Operations.
