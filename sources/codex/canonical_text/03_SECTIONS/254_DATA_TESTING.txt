SECTION 254 — Data Testing

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Verify that data is accurate enough for its declared purpose, complete within scope, consistent, timely, traceable, protected, transformed correctly, and recoverable.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A data-test model with contracts, schema, grain, quality dimensions, samples, lineage, privacy, reconciliation, freshness, failure handling, evidence, and owner acceptance.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Data can be syntactically valid and still be stale, duplicated, biased, incomplete, misjoined, unauthorized, or semantically wrong. Downstream dashboards or models can amplify a small upstream defect.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when data is collected, transformed, stored, exchanged, reported, modeled, archived, deleted, or used to make or support a decision.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no data meaning, quality, lineage, access, retention, transformation, or consumer behavior changes and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use data contracts, information and data architecture, privacy, security, quality, warehouse, lake, model, records, and operations baselines. Identify data owner and quality authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Source, schema, grain, definitions, population, lineage, transformations, quality rules, thresholds, samples, privacy, access, freshness, reconciliation, consumers, and recovery.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Data owners define meaning, quality, criticality, access, retention, and acceptance. Domain owners judge semantic correctness; humans approve quarantines, corrections, deletion, and downstream use.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inspect authorized metadata and bounded samples, draft quality rules, identify anomalies, and propose reconciliation. It must preserve privacy, source, grain, time, and uncertainty and must not expose raw data, change authoritative values, infer sensitive attributes, or declare quality without human review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Data Tester, Data Engineer, Data Quality Analyst, Data Architect, Privacy Reviewer, Security Auditor, Analyst, Researcher, Verifier, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define quality and grain; map source and lineage; test schema, completeness, validity, uniqueness, consistency, timeliness, accuracy, reconciliation, privacy, access, transformation, retention, and recovery; quarantine and correct; notify consumers; and retest.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show source, grain, population, time, rule, sample, result, privacy boundary, owner, and limitation. Require human review for semantic rule, correction, quarantine, deletion, and downstream decision.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Support contracts, schema evolution, quality gates, quarantine, lineage, versioned transformations, reconciliation, privacy-preserving fixtures, freshness, rollback, and downstream impact notification.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show data source, grain, quality, freshness, lineage, affected consumers, evidence, incidents, owner, and authorized quarantine, correct, restrict, backfill, restore, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Data-test strategy; contracts and quality rules; source and lineage map; samples and fixtures; results and defect register; quarantine and correction; decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify source, schema, grain, population, time, rule, transformation, sample, method, result, actor, and limitation. Row count, null rate, or passing rule proves only its declared slice.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify schema, grain, definitions, quality dimensions, lineage, freshness, reconciliation, access, privacy, transformation, correction, backfill, deletion, recovery, and consumer interpretation.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Quality rule without meaning, row count as accuracy, hidden filters, stale data accepted, sensitive samples copied, quarantine bypassed, and corrected data without lineage or consumer notice.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Quarantine affected data or outputs, preserve versions, notify consumers, correct or backfill, reconcile, restore, and reassess downstream decisions. Reopen on source, schema, definition, transformation, population, or quality change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Source, grain, meaning, quality, lineage, privacy, owners, tests, corrections, evidence, recovery, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending independent review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve data and schema versions, rules, samples, results, quarantine, corrections, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Data Engineering, Quality, Warehousing, Analytics, AI, Privacy, Security, Testing, and Operations.
