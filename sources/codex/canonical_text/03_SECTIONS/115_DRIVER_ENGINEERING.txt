SECTION 115 — Driver Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define the design, implementation, verification, deployment, operation, update, and retirement of software that controls or exposes hardware devices within explicit safety, security, performance, compatibility, and recovery boundaries.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A versioned driver contract covering hardware assumptions, discovery, permissions, I/O, timing, errors, isolation, compatibility, tests, update, recovery, and evidence.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Drivers cross the hardware and privileged software boundary. A fault can corrupt data, bypass isolation, damage equipment, or make recovery unavailable.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to kernel, user-space, firmware-adjacent, virtual, bus, device, sensor, actuator, and compatibility drivers.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Using an unchanged supported driver without altering behavior, configuration, trust boundary, or support claim is not driver engineering; record the baseline.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Requires hardware specification, protocol, timing, privilege, threat, safety, lifecycle, test equipment, platform, update, and recovery requirements.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Device behavior, discovery, initialization, I/O, concurrency, errors, timeouts, power, permissions, data integrity, diagnostics, compatibility, and failure model.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve hardware access, privilege, safety, release, field update, residual risk, and retirement. Operators retain isolation and recovery authority.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze protocols, draft code and tests, and inspect traces. It must not invent electrical behavior, claim hardware safety, or release a driver without qualified human review and evidence.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Architect, Researcher, Analyst, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Security Auditor, Red Team, Critic, Governance Reviewer, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, and Optimizer. Existing role contracts remain controlling.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Define device, bus, platform, privilege, timing, safety, and recovery scope.
2. Specify discovery, commands, data, synchronization, errors, power, update, and compatibility contracts.
3. Implement isolation, validation, timeouts, diagnostics, and safe defaults.
4. Test normal, malformed, disconnected, noisy, overloaded, hostile, upgraded, and failed hardware.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Identify human, AI, automation, device, and provider actors. Require human review for safety, privilege, field update, and external effects; preserve manual and out-of-band recovery.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate driver, firmware, hardware, configuration, calibration, permissions, logs, and signed artifacts. Enforce least privilege, input validation, timeout, safe state, compatibility, and rollback or restore.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show device, driver, firmware, platform, permission, health, telemetry, evidence, update, and recovery state with authorized isolate, reset, restore, and quarantine controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Driver contract, hardware matrix, source, build and signing records, protocol tests, fault injection, compatibility, security and safety review, deployment, field recovery, and retirement records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify hardware revision, driver, firmware, configuration, test equipment, inputs, results, reviewer, and limitations. Emulation does not prove field behavior.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Test discovery, permissions, I/O, timing, disconnect, corruption, concurrency, power loss, hostile input, hardware variance, update, recovery, and safe-state behavior.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Unsafe defaults, unchecked device data, privileged access, ignored disconnects, untested hardware revisions, field updates without recovery, and erasing diagnostics during reset.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Isolate device, preserve logs and state, enter approved safe state, use prior driver or manual control, replace hardware if needed, compensate effects, and reopen after independent review.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Hardware and driver contracts, permissions, safety, compatibility, evidence, support, update, recovery, and retirement are explicit for declared devices and platforms.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Hand off hardware matrix, source, signed artifacts, tests, calibration, incidents, decisions, recovery, and open gaps to Kernel, OS, Embedded, Hardware, Security, Testing, Operations, and Maintenance sections.
