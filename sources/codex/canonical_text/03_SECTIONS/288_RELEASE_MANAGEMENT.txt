SECTION 288 — Release Management

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Coordinate the decision, readiness, timing, communication, risk, approvals, deployment, support, rollback, and follow-up for changes released to people or operations.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A release governance model with candidate, scope, readiness, decision rights, dependencies, communication, support, rollout, rollback, metrics, incident handling, and closure.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Release is a cross-functional commitment, not a button. A technically complete change can fail through poor timing, unsupported users, unresolved risk, unprepared operations, or misleading communication.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a version, capability, policy, model, data, configuration, or infrastructure change is made available beyond its authoring context.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no consumer, environment, behavior, commitment, support, or operational risk changes and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use acceptance, testing, artifact, deployment, change, support, operations, security, privacy, communication, and rollback baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Release candidate, scope, artifact, tests, findings, dependencies, readiness, approvals, risk, support, communication, rollout, rollback, and owner decision.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Release authority approves readiness, timing, risk, communication, exception, rollout, rollback, and closure. Contributors provide truthful evidence; affected owners can hold the release.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may assemble readiness, draft notes, identify missing evidence, and summarize impacts. It must not approve, publish, deploy, communicate commitments, or close a release without accountable human authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Release Manager, Release Engineer, Product Manager, Tester, Verifier, Operations Engineer, Support Reviewer, Security Auditor, Coordinator, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define scope and audience; baseline candidate; review evidence and risks; confirm compatibility, migration, support, and communication; approve; roll out; monitor; rollback or pause; collect outcome; and close or reopen.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show candidate, scope, evidence, readiness, owner, risk, dependencies, support, communication, authority, and rollback. Require human approval for release and external commitments.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use release registry, immutable artifacts, staged rollout, feature flags, readiness gates, dependency tracking, rollback, support hooks, and post-release evidence.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show release state, approvals, readiness, dependencies, incidents, users, evidence freshness, support, rollout, rollback, and authorized approve, hold, publish, pause, rollback, and reopen controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Release charter; candidate and readiness record; risk and dependency assessment; communications and support; rollout and rollback; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify candidate, artifact, scope, environment, tests, approval, actor, time, result, impact, and limitation. Release approval proves a decision, not post-release success.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify evidence, approvals, audience, compatibility, support, communication, deployment, monitoring, rollback, migration, and closure. Exercise partial and failed release.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Approval theater, hidden breaking change, release without support, notes that overclaim, no rollback, and release frequency treated as value.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Hold or withdraw, preserve artifacts and decisions, roll back or patch, support users, communicate, and reassess impact. Reopen on incident, audience, artifact, or risk change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Scope, candidate, evidence, approvals, support, communication, rollout, rollback, owner, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve release records, artifacts, approvals, notes, communications, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Deployment, Operations, Support, Product, Security, and Governance.
