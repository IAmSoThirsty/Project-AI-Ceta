SECTION 318 — Alerting

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Turn meaningful deviations from protected service, security, safety, capacity, quality, or data conditions into attributable, actionable, and proportionate notification.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An alerting model with signal, condition, threshold, owner, severity, deduplication, routing, escalation, suppression, expiry, action, privacy, evidence, and feedback.

WHY THIS MATTERS

An alert is a request for attention, not proof of incident. Noisy, duplicated, stale, unowned, or un-actionable alerts create fatigue and make true danger easier to miss.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a signal should prompt a human or controlled automation to investigate, decide, communicate, or act.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no deviation, response, or operational decision requires notification and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use monitoring, metrics, logs, traces, service levels, incident, security, privacy, staffing, and operations baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Signal, condition, threshold, context, owner, severity, route, escalation, suppression, action, evidence, schedule, and recovery.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners define actionable conditions, severity, recipients, escalation, mute authority, and response. Humans decide whether an alert represents incident, risk, or expected variation.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may correlate authorized signals, group alerts, draft tuning, and recommend investigation. It must not suppress, close, escalate, or execute action without authority and must surface uncertainty and missing context.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Alert Engineer, SRE, Operations Engineer, Security Operations Analyst, Analyst, Observer, Incident Responder, Tester, Verifier, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define outcome and action; configure; route; deduplicate; test; monitor delivery; tune; document runbook; review noise and misses; manage mute expiry; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show signal, condition, threshold, owner, severity, context, action, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for mute, threshold, escalation, and consequential automation.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use multi-signal context, deduplication, rate limits, routing redundancy, escalation, expiry, audit, privacy, and safe behavior when alerting fails.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show alert, source, severity, owner, action, age, evidence freshness, mute, escalation, and authorized acknowledge, suppress with expiry, escalate, tune, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Alert charter; alert catalog; routing and escalation; runbook; noise and miss analysis; test, incident, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify signal, condition, threshold, source, context, time, route, actor, result, and limitation. Alert receipt proves delivery, not diagnosis or response.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify trigger, threshold, context, deduplication, routing, escalation, mute expiry, privacy, delivery, false positive, missed event, and response. Exercise notification outage.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Alert fatigue, no owner, permanent mute, severity inflation, no runbook, alert as incident verdict, and automation that acts on an unverified signal.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Restore notification, use alternate channel, preserve signal and blind period, notify owners, and reassess missed impact. Reopen on target, threshold, owner, route, or incident change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Signals, conditions, thresholds, owners, actions, routes, evidence, privacy, mute, escalation, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve alert definitions, routes, mutes, incidents, evidence, decisions, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Monitoring, Incident, Security, SRE, Operations, and Support.
