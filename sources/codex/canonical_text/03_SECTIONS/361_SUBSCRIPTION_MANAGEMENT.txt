SECTION 361 — Subscription Management

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Manage recurring entitlement, plan, term, renewal, upgrade, downgrade, pause, cancellation, billing, payment, access, and customer communication accurately and fairly.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A subscription model with customer, plan, term, entitlement, price, usage, renewal, notice, payment, status, cancellation, refund, support, evidence, and lifecycle.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Subscription state controls access and recurring financial obligation. Ambiguous renewal, failed payment, entitlements, or cancellation can create customer harm and legal exposure.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a product or service grants recurring or term-based access, billing, usage, or commitment.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no recurring entitlement, term, price, renewal, cancellation, payment, or customer obligation changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use commerce, billing, payments, identity, product, legal, privacy, support, usage, and records baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Customer, plan, price, term, entitlement, usage, renewal, notice, payment, status, cancellation, refund, support, and evidence.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Subscription owners approve plans, price, terms, renewal, grace, cancellation, refund, access, exception, and customer communication. Humans resolve disputes and legal obligations.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze authorized subscription state, identify anomalies, and draft customer explanations. It must not alter entitlement, renew, cancel, charge, refund, or disclose customer data without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Subscription Manager, Commerce Manager, Billing Engineer, Payments Engineer, Customer Support, Legal Advisor, Analyst, Tester, Verifier, and Governance Reviewer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define plan and entitlement; create; activate; bill; renew; handle payment failure; upgrade or downgrade; pause; cancel; refund; notify; reconcile; and retire plan.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show customer, plan, term, price, entitlement, status, payment, action, evidence, authority, and limitation. Require human approval for refund, exception, disputed cancellation, and high-impact access.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use explicit state machine, immutable history, idempotence, entitlement and billing linkage, notice, grace, access, privacy, audit, and reconciliation.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show subscription, plan, term, renewal, entitlement, payment, cancellation, dispute, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized change, pause, cancel, refund, restore, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Subscription charter; plan and entitlement; term and price; lifecycle and payment; notice, cancellation, refund, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify customer, subscription, plan, term, price, entitlement, payment, state, notice, method, time, result, and limitation. State transition proves only recorded system state.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify create, activate, renew, grace, payment failure, upgrade, downgrade, pause, cancel, refund, entitlement, notice, privacy, support, and reconciliation.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Unclear renewal, access after cancel, charge after failure, cancellation maze, entitlement mismatch, hidden term, refund without ledger, and subscription count as value.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Correct state, stop charge, restore or restrict access, refund or credit, communicate, preserve evidence, and reassess customer and legal impact. Reopen on plan, price, term, payment, or policy change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Customer, plan, term, price, entitlement, payment, lifecycle, notices, evidence, owners, recovery, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve subscription state, plans, notices, payments, billing, cancellations, disputes, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Commerce, Billing, Payments, Support, Legal, Revenue, and Records.
