SECTION 079 — UX Strategy

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define how user experience contributes to approved outcomes, strategic choices, differentiated value, inclusion, risk treatment, and measurable long-term product or service direction.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

 - A declared experience ambition, users, business or mission outcomes, constraints, portfolio choices, measures, and horizon.
 - Prioritized experience principles, evidence, investment decisions, risks, governance, and acceptance criteria.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Experience strategy prevents local design activity from outrunning mission, user need, operational capacity, or ethical obligations. It makes tradeoffs and omissions visible.

APPLICABILITY

Applies when experience is a material source of value, adoption, safety, trust, inclusion, service quality, differentiation, or change priority.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No material experience outcome, population, prioritization, investment, policy, journey, or measure effect; or an unchanged approved UX strategy with impact recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Mission and product strategy, stakeholder and user evidence, market or domain context, journeys, accessibility, brand, architecture, operations, portfolio constraints, risks, and decision authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Mission and product strategy, stakeholder and user evidence, market or domain context, journeys, accessibility, brand, architecture, operations, portfolio constraints, risks, and decision authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans choose strategic outcomes, target populations, inclusion commitments, investment, tradeoffs, acceptable burden, and acceptance. They own commitments and decide when evidence supports reprioritization.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may synthesize evidence, identify patterns, compare strategic options, and draft hypotheses. It must expose evidence quality and opportunity cost and must not define user values or make portfolio commitments.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

UX Strategist, Architect, Researcher, Analyst, User Experience Reviewer, Product and Service owners, Governance Reviewer, Critic, Planner, Coordinator, Verifier, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, and Decision Arbiter Advisory.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define experience outcomes and principles; segment populations; map journeys and pain; assess current capability; generate options; prioritize by evidence, impact, risk, and cost; define measures; secure decisions; review and replan.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show evidence, population, goal, assumption, confidence limit, tradeoff, owner, investment, and expected consequence. AI recommendations remain advisory and do not bypass product, governance, accessibility, or user authority.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Link strategy to journeys, capabilities, processes, architecture, design system, content, operations, measures, experiments, and lifecycle. Define leading and outcome measures and invalidation conditions.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show experience goals, target populations, evidence freshness, roadmap choices, risks, measures, investment, dependencies, open dissent, and controls to approve, defer, stop, narrow, or reopen work.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Experience strategy, principles, population and journey map, opportunity register, evidence assessment, prioritization rationale, investment and dependency map, measures, decision record, and handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify source, population, context, method, date, sample, result, uncertainty, and limitation. Market signals, analytics, interviews, or AI synthesis are bounded evidence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify strategic traceability, population inclusion, outcome measures, accessibility, feasibility, opportunity cost, dependency, ethical risk, and whether decisions change under plausible evidence updates.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Chasing engagement over outcome; treating personas as facts; copying competitors; ignoring non-users; confusing aesthetic preference with value; and making commitments without owner, capacity, or evidence.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Record dissent, pause unsupported investment, preserve prior decisions, commission missing research, and re-evaluate affected populations and commitments. Reopen when mission, market, evidence, technology, policy, or user needs change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Outcomes, populations, principles, evidence, priorities, measures, tradeoffs, owners, risks, decisions, and reopening rules are explicit. Remain CONTENT-COMPLETE pending reconciliation, hostile review, and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve strategy version, evidence, assumptions, decisions, dissent, portfolio links, dependencies, measures, and next action. Hand off to Product, Service, UX, Research, Design Systems, Analytics, Operations, and Governance.
