SECTION 255 — Test Automation

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Automate repeatable test execution, data preparation, observation, reporting, and cleanup while preserving truthful scope, maintainability, human judgment, and safe control.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An automation model with target tests, framework, fixtures, environments, triggers, ownership, flakiness, artifacts, security, observability, maintenance, quarantine, and human exit decision.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Automation increases speed and repeatability but can hide stale tests, weak assertions, data leaks, environment drift, and false confidence. Automation is a tool for evidence, not evidence by itself.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when tests, checks, simulations, scans, or verification steps are executed automatically or are candidates for automation.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when automation adds no repeatability or safety value and manual or exploratory judgment is the declared appropriate method.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use test strategy, source control, CI or scheduler, environments, fixtures, secrets, data, observability, artifact storage, access, and maintenance ownership.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Tests, assertions, fixtures, target, framework, trigger, environment, data, dependencies, artifact policy, failure handling, owner, and acceptance criteria.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners approve automation scope, credentials, data, triggers, quarantine, release gating, and maintenance. Humans interpret exploratory or ambiguous results and retain stop control.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft tests and harnesses, analyze failures, reduce duplication, and propose maintenance. It must not run with unauthorized access, inject uncontrolled data, weaken assertions, hide flakiness, or change release gates without approval.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Test Automation Engineer, Tester, Implementer, Quality Engineer, Security Auditor, Analyst, Verifier, Researcher, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Select stable and valuable cases; design fixtures and isolation; secure credentials; implement and review; execute; capture artifacts; detect flakiness; maintain; quarantine transparently; report coverage and limits; and periodically retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show test, assertion, fixture, environment, credential scope, trigger, result, flakiness, and limitation. Require human approval for generated code, credentials, gate changes, quarantine, and release.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Provide deterministic reset, artifact identity, secure secret injection, isolated runners, rate limits, cleanup, retry policy, reproducible environments, and clear distinction between test and production authority.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show suite, trigger, target, environment, pass or fail, flakiness, artifacts, owner, evidence freshness, and authorized run, pause, quarantine, repair, accept, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Automation strategy; test and fixture inventory; runner and environment record; artifact and retention policy; flakiness register; results, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify test, code version, fixture, runner, environment, dependency, execution, result, artifact, time, actor, and limitation. Automation completion or coverage proves only the recorded execution.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify assertions, isolation, credentials, data, cleanup, reproducibility, artifact integrity, retry, flakiness handling, failure visibility, access, and gate behavior.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Brittle automation, retries hiding failures, secrets in logs, test data leakage, assertions too weak, quarantine without owner, and pipeline green while critical tests are skipped.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause unsafe runner, rotate exposed credentials, preserve artifacts, quarantine and repair, rerun, and reassess release evidence. Reopen on target, framework, environment, test, data, or risk change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Scope, tests, assertions, fixtures, environment, credentials, artifacts, ownership, maintenance, flakiness, evidence, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve automation code, runner, fixture, environment, artifacts, failures, decisions, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Testing, Quality, Release, Security, Platform, Operations, and Maintenance.
