SECTION 241 — Stress Testing

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Evaluate behavior beyond expected operating conditions and determine how the system degrades, protects people and data, recovers, and returns to service.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A stress-test model with overload or adverse condition, safety boundaries, observable thresholds, degradation expectations, recovery, resource exhaustion behavior, evidence, and owner decision.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Systems often fail in the transition between healthy and unavailable. Stress testing reveals queue collapse, retry storms, memory or resource exhaustion, unsafe fallback, data corruption, and recovery that was never exercised.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when overload, dependency loss, resource exhaustion, failure combination, extreme input, or environmental boundary can affect safety, continuity, integrity, or service.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability only when no adverse condition or recovery claim is material and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use performance, load, reliability, resilience, safety, security, recovery, capacity, and operations baselines. Establish isolation, monitoring, stop, restore, and communication authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Stress scenario, limit, trigger, workload, environment, data, dependencies, safety constraints, expected degradation, recovery objective, instrumentation, and acceptance criteria.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners approve adverse conditions, safety, data, production-like scope, stop, recovery, and residual risk. Operators control the exercise and can halt it immediately.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft stress scenarios, analyze telemetry, and identify degradation patterns. It must not create unsafe conditions, continue after stop, alter recovery, or claim resilience from a simulation without human authority and evidence.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Resilience Engineer, Performance Engineer, Tester, Safety Reviewer, Architect, Analyst, Observer, Operations Engineer, Verifier, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define adverse condition and safe bounds; prepare isolation and restore; establish baseline; apply controlled stress; monitor safety, integrity, resources, and users; stop; recover; analyze degradation and causes; remediate; rerun; record limits.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show scenario, trigger, rate, target, safety state, evidence, expected degradation, actual impact, and stop control. Require human supervision throughout stress and recovery.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Design overload shedding, backpressure, circuit breaking, isolation, graceful degradation, data integrity, checkpointing, restore, failover, and operator visibility. Prevent retry or recovery mechanisms from amplifying harm.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show stress phase, condition, resources, safety, user impact, recovery state, evidence freshness, and authorized pause, stop, isolate, restore, scale, rollback, accept, and reopen controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Stress charter; scenario and safety plan; environment and data record; telemetry; degradation and recovery results; remediation; decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify condition, trigger, workload, version, environment, resource, time, method, result, user impact, recovery, and limitation. A successful recovery in one failure mode proves only that mode.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify stop, isolation, degradation, data integrity, alerting, operator response, recovery, restore, failover, cleanup, and return-to-service. Reproduce material results independently.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Stress without isolation, no stop path, destructive load, retry amplification, recovery not observed, data corruption ignored, and resilience claimed from uptime before failure.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stop and stabilize, preserve evidence, restore or fail over under authority, protect users, communicate, and reassess residual risk. Reopen on architecture, workload, dependency, recovery, or safety change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Condition, safety, workload, degradation, recovery, evidence, owner, limits, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending qualified review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve stress and recovery traces, actions, findings, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Reliability, Resilience, Capacity, Operations, Incident Response, and Testing.
