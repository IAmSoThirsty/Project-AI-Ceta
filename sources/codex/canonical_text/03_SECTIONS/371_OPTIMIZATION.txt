SECTION 371 — Optimization

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Improve a defined outcome or constraint through measured change while protecting other required outcomes, uncertainty, safety, fairness, maintainability, and reversibility.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An optimization model with objective, constraints, baseline, variables, method, tradeoffs, guardrails, evidence, experiment, decision, rollback, and monitoring.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Optimization can improve a proxy while worsening the actual goal or shifting cost and harm elsewhere. Local improvement is not system improvement without constraints and outcome evidence.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a system, process, product, resource, model, or operation is deliberately tuned to improve performance, cost, quality, growth, reliability, or another outcome.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no objective, variable, tradeoff, or measurable outcome changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use requirements, metrics, experiments, performance, cost, safety, privacy, ethics, quality, reliability, and change baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Objective, constraints, baseline, variables, metric, data, method, alternatives, guardrails, evidence, owner, and rollback.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners define objective and protected constraints; specialists assess domain, safety, security, and user impact; humans approve tradeoffs, deployment, and residual risk.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze authorized data, propose candidates, simulate bounded changes, and identify tradeoffs. It must expose objective and limitations and must not alter production, optimize sensitive outcome, or accept collateral harm without human authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Optimizer, Analyst, Researcher, Architect, Experimenter, Performance Engineer, User Experience Reviewer, Safety Reviewer, Verifier, and Critic.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define objective and constraints; baseline; identify variables; propose options; test or experiment; assess tradeoffs and harm; select; deploy; monitor; rollback; and review.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show objective, constraint, baseline, metric, candidate, evidence, uncertainty, tradeoff, and limitation. Require human approval for high-impact optimization and production change.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use feature flags, experiment isolation, guardrails, rollback, multi-objective measurement, change traceability, safe bounds, and independent monitoring.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show objective, baseline, candidate, metric, constraints, harm signals, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized test, deploy, pause, rollback, constrain, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Optimization charter; objective and constraint; baseline and candidate; experiment and result; tradeoff and guardrail; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify objective, baseline, candidate, data, metric, method, period, result, tradeoff, uncertainty, and limitation. Local metric improvement proves neither global outcome nor no harm.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify baseline, constraints, metric, candidate, experiment, guardrails, user impact, security, privacy, cost, rollback, and outcome. Test adverse and boundary conditions.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Proxy optimization, goal drift, local maximum, harm externalized, metric gaming, no baseline, unbounded automation, and improvement without rollback.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause or roll back, protect affected parties, preserve evidence, correct metric or objective, and reassess tradeoffs. Reopen on outcome, constraint, data, population, system, or risk change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Objective, constraints, baseline, variables, evidence, tradeoffs, guardrails, decision, owners, limits, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve candidates, experiments, metrics, decisions, incidents, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Growth, Product, Analytics, Performance, SRE, Quality, and Operations.
