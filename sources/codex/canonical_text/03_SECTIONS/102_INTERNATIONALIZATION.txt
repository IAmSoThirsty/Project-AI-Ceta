SECTION 102 — Internationalization

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define product and system foundations that allow locale, language, culture, legal, accessibility, and regional variation without unsafe rewrites or hidden assumptions.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

 - A scoped locale model, language, data, formatting, content, architecture, workflow, testing, ownership, and lifecycle baseline.
 - Explicit variation points, defaults, fallback, evidence, and acceptance criteria.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Internationalization failures surface late as broken layouts, incorrect calculations, exclusion, or legal error. Early variation modeling reduces systemic rework and hidden regional assumptions.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to products, platforms, data, content, APIs, services, operations, and systems expected to support more than one locale or cultural context.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No locale, language, format, culture, legal, accessibility, or regional behavior effect; or an unchanged approved foundation with impact recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Target locales, language and script requirements, data and format rules, content, architecture, legal, privacy, accessibility, testing, deployment, and authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Humans approve supported locales, defaults, regional obligations, data boundaries, accessibility, legal fit, tradeoffs, and acceptance.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

AI may identify hard-coded assumptions and generate locale tests. It must preserve evidence and uncertainty and must not select regional policy or infer cultural meaning without authority.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inspect code, content, schemas, and tests for locale assumptions; propose variation points; generate representative fixtures; and report unsupported scripts, formats, parsing paths, or fallback behavior. It must preserve locale and regional scope, data meaning, privacy boundaries, accessibility, precision, timezone, currency, and uncertainty, and must separate detection from implementation and acceptance. It must not silently change global defaults, normalize or reinterpret user data, select legal or regional policy, treat one locale as proof of general support, or merge architecture changes without authorized review and recorded test evidence.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Architect, Analyst, Localization Specialist, Technical Writer, Content Designer, UX Reviewer, Tester, Verifier, Accessibility specialist, Security and Privacy Auditors, Governance Reviewer, Implementer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Inventory locale assumptions; define variation points; externalize strings and formats; support scripts, direction, plural, units, sorting, data, time, identity, accessibility; test locales; document and hand off.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show locale, source, default, fallback, format, actor, data boundary, uncertainty, and user control. AI findings remain recommendations pending qualified review.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use locale-aware schemas, formatting, collation, parsing, resource loading, content keys, test fixtures, timezone, currency, accessibility, and deployment without global mutable assumptions.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show supported locales, coverage, defaults, fallback, format rules, defects, evidence, dependencies, and controls to approve, restrict, switch, or recover.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Internationalization requirements, locale model, resource and format contracts, test matrix, hard-coded assumption inventory, evidence, decisions, and handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify locale, platform, data, format, version, test method, result, uncertainty, and limitation. Passing one locale proves bounded support only.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify scripts, direction, plural, dates, numbers, units, sorting, timezones, currency, parsing, accessibility, fallback, data integrity, and locale switching.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Hard-coded strings; locale as decoration; incorrect numeric or date parsing; global defaults; untestable formatting; and falling back to a language that hides critical meaning.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Restrict unsupported locale, preserve data, use approved fallback, correct formatting, notify owners, and retest affected locales before expansion.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Locale model, variation, data, formats, accessibility, tests, evidence, fallback, recovery, risks, and acceptance are explicit. Remain CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve locale rules, resources, fixtures, decisions, defects, evidence, and next action. Hand off to Localization, Content, Data, Application, UI, Accessibility, Testing, and Operations.
