SECTION 143 - Video Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define governed engineering of captured, encoded, processed, delivered, decoded, and displayed moving-image content with explicit provenance, quality, accessibility, privacy, security, and lifecycle controls.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A video baseline with source, rights, codecs, timing, quality, captions, delivery, storage, processing, evidence, recovery, and retirement.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Video can mislead through edits, missing context, stale frames, inaccessible delivery, or provenance loss. Availability and resolution do not prove truth or comprehension.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to capture, editing, encoding, playback, conferencing, surveillance, broadcast, computer vision input, and video delivery systems.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

May be non-applicable to systems with no video capture, processing, delivery, display, or video-dependent outcome. Record scope, owner, evidence, and reopening trigger.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Requires purpose, sources, consent, rights, codecs, timing, quality, captions, privacy, security, storage, delivery, accessibility, and retention authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Provide source, frame rate, resolution, color, audio relation, edits, metadata, captions, latency, network, device, retention, and failure requirements.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve content, consent, rights, surveillance, publication, accessibility, safety, and retention. Humans decide whether video is fit for evidence or decision use.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may transcode, caption, summarize, detect defects, or analyze supplied video. It must preserve provenance, expose uncertainty, and never publish or infer consequential facts without review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Video Engineer, Architect, Researcher, Privacy Reviewer, Security Auditor, Accessibility specialist, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Governance Reviewer, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, and Red Team. Existing role contracts remain controlling.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Define purpose, source, rights, consent, quality, accessibility, pipeline, and lifecycle.
2. Build capture, edit, encode, deliver, playback, caption, and archive paths.
3. Verify integrity, quality, timing, accessibility, privacy, security, and recovery.
4. Operate, monitor, correct, withdraw, and retire video assets and services.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show source, edit history, model or tool, timestamp, consent, caption state, quality, and evidence. Human approval is required for publication and consequential interpretation; alternative access remains available.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate capture, storage, edit, encode, manifest, delivery, playback, caption, analytics, and archive boundaries. Preserve hashes, time, lineage, access, deletion, and safe fallback.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show asset, source, version, delivery state, latency, quality, captions, permissions, incidents, and controls to stop capture, restrict access, withdraw, restore, or delete.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Video charter, source and rights catalog, media pipeline, edit and provenance record, caption and accessibility plan, quality evidence, delivery manifest, support, and retention package.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify source, asset, edit, codec, build, timestamp, device, method, result, and limits. Separate media quality, integrity, accessibility, privacy, security, and interpretation evidence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify consent, integrity, edit lineage, timestamps, codecs, captions, quality, sync, network loss, authorization, malformed media, retention, deletion, and recovery.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Unmarked edits; missing captions; capture without consent; insecure manifests; inaccessible controls; codec lock-in; and treating clear video or detection output as truth.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stop capture or publication, preserve chain of custody, restrict or withdraw content, provide alternate access, compensate affected decisions, and reopen provenance and retention review.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Purpose, source, rights, consent, provenance, accessibility, evidence, delivery, recovery, and lifecycle are explicit. Keep CONTENT-COMPLETE until independent verification and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve asset hashes, source, edits, captions, permissions, decisions, incidents, evidence, and next safe action. Hand off to Media, Streaming, Search, Privacy, Security, Accessibility, Testing, and Operations.
