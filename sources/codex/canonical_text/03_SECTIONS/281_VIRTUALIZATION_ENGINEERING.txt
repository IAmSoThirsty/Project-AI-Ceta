SECTION 281 — Virtualization Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Design and operate virtual compute, networks, storage, images, hypervisors, tenancy, isolation, performance, backup, and recovery.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A virtualization model with host, guest, hypervisor, image, identity, resource, network, storage, tenancy, migration, monitoring, security, and lifecycle ownership.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Virtualization increases utilization and flexibility while concentrating risk. Host compromise, noisy neighbors, image drift, resource overcommit, or shared management can cross guest boundaries.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when virtual machines, hypervisors, images, virtual networks, or virtual storage support a workload or service.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no virtual resource, host, image, tenancy, migration, or management behavior changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use infrastructure, cloud, server, storage, network, identity, image, security, capacity, backup, recovery, and operations baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Hosts, hypervisor, guests, images, resources, identity, network, storage, tenancy, migration, telemetry, backup, recovery, and retirement.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners approve host and guest access, overcommit, migration, image, backup, maintenance, recovery, exceptions, and retirement. Humans accept shared-failure risk.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze authorized inventory, metrics, images, and plans; draft policy and tests; and propose optimization. It must not access hypervisor control, migrate, resize, or delete guests without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Virtualization Engineer, Infrastructure Engineer, Security Auditor, Server Engineer, Storage Administrator, Network Engineer, Tester, Verifier, and Operations Engineer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Inventory hosts and guests; secure management; define isolation and resource; manage images; provision; monitor; migrate; patch; back up; recover; test failure; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show host, guest, image, identity, resource, tenancy, action, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for privileged, migration, data, host, and destructive action.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use management isolation, least privilege, image provenance, resource quotas, network and storage boundaries, live migration controls, host diversity, snapshot policy, and recovery.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show host and guest health, resource, image, tenancy, incidents, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized migrate, resize, isolate, restore, roll back, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Virtualization charter; host and guest catalog; image and resource model; isolation and migration; backup and recovery; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify host, guest, hypervisor, image, resource, tenant, method, time, result, actor, and limitation. Host metric or snapshot proves only its captured state.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify management access, isolation, image, resource, network, storage, migration, backup, restore, host loss, guest failure, and recovery.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Shared admin, guest isolation assumed, image without provenance, overcommit without evidence, snapshots as backup, host failure domain ignored, and migration without rollback.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Isolate host or guest, preserve state, restore or migrate, rotate credentials, reconcile data, and reassess shared impact. Reopen on host, hypervisor, image, workload, or provider change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Hosts, guests, images, resources, isolation, ownership, evidence, recovery, migration, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve host and guest inventory, images, configurations, migrations, backups, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Servers, Storage, Network, Security, Cloud, Platform, and Operations.
