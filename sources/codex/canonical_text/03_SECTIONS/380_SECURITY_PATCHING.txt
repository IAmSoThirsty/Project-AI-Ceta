SECTION 380 — Security Patching

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Reduce known security exposure through qualified, prioritized, tested, authorized, and verified patching of software, firmware, infrastructure, devices, and dependencies.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A security-patching model with advisory, asset, exposure, severity, owner, deadline, compatibility, rollout, mitigation, verification, exception, and evidence.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Patching competes with availability and compatibility but delay leaves known exposure. A patch applied to one asset or reported by a tool does not prove fleet remediation or safe operation.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a security fix, mitigation, configuration, or compensating control is required for a known vulnerability or security weakness.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no vulnerability, asset, security property, update, or mitigation changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use vulnerability, threat, asset, update, release, compatibility, change, testing, operations, and incident baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Advisory, vulnerability, asset, version, exposure, severity, owner, deadline, patch, mitigation, test, rollout, verification, and exception.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Security and asset owners prioritize and approve patch, mitigation, outage, exception, deadline, communication, and closure. Humans accept residual exposure with expiry.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may correlate advisories to authorized inventory, draft plans, and analyze patch results. It must not exploit, patch, suppress finding, declare remediated, or accept exception without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Security Engineer, Vulnerability Analyst, Update Engineer, Systems Administrator, Tester, Verifier, Security Auditor, Operations Engineer, and Governance Reviewer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Identify exposure; map assets; prioritize; choose patch or mitigation; test; schedule; deploy; verify; handle failure; manage exception; report; and retire obsolete exposure.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show advisory, asset, version, exposure, severity, patch, mitigation, owner, evidence, deadline, and limitation. Require human review for production, emergency, exception, and closure.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use asset inventory, signed artifact, staged rollout, test, rollback, compensating control, exposure monitoring, fleet reconciliation, and evidence linked to exact version.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show vulnerability, assets, exposure, priority, patch, mitigation, evidence freshness, owner, deadline, and authorized patch, isolate, defer, accept, remediate, and reopen controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Patching charter; advisory and asset map; priority and plan; test and rollout; verification and exception; decision, evidence, incident, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify vulnerability, asset, version, source, patch, mitigation, method, time, result, owner, coverage, and limitation. Scanner or patch status proves only its inventory and check.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify advisory, affected version, asset coverage, patch, mitigation, compatibility, rollout, rollback, verification, exception expiry, and incident. Test unsupported or offline asset.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Patch everything blindly, patch one asset as fleet proof, exception forever, scanner mismatch, rollback absent, unsupported asset ignored, and security score as remediation.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause, roll back or mitigate, preserve evidence, protect assets, communicate, reassess exposure, and reopen invalid closure. Reopen on advisory, exploit, asset, patch, dependency, or environment change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Advisory, vulnerability, assets, priority, patch, mitigation, owners, evidence, verification, exception, deadline, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve findings, asset coverage, patch versions, exceptions, incidents, decisions, evidence, and open gaps. Hand off to Vulnerability, Security Operations, Update, Dependency, Release, Testing, and Operations.
