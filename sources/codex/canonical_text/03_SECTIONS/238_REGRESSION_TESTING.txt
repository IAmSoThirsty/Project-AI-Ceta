SECTION 238 — Regression Testing

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Detect unintended change to previously working behavior, protected characteristics, interfaces, data, security, performance, operations, and user outcomes after modification.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A risk-based regression suite and review process tied to change impact, versions, environments, defects, coverage, exclusions, results, and owner decision.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Change can break behavior outside the edited area through shared dependencies, data, configuration, timing, policy, or user journeys. A large suite can still miss the changed risk or hide flaky and obsolete cases.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when code, hardware, data, configuration, dependency, policy, model, provider, environment, or operational change may affect existing behavior.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability only when impact analysis shows no behavior, contract, risk, user, or operational change and an approved exact baseline supports it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use change impact, requirements, architecture, defect history, unit, integration, system, E2E, performance, security, and acceptance baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Change, affected paths, dependencies, prior defects, critical journeys, suite, fixtures, environment, versions, exclusions, risk, and exit criteria.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Change and test owners define impact and scope; humans approve exclusions, quarantine, release, and residual risk. Domain owners decide whether behavior remains meaningful.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze diffs and dependency graphs, propose regression cases, prioritize suites, and summarize failures. It must not declare “no impact,” remove tests, hide flakiness, or approve release without human review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Tester, Test Analyst, Architect, Implementer, Quality Engineer, Verifier, Researcher, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Classify change; map affected risks and journeys; select and update cases; run representative suites; analyze new, missing, and flaky failures; retest fixes; review exclusions; and record decision and limits.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show change, impact evidence, selected cases, exclusions, versions, result, and limitation. Require human review for impact classification, test removal, exception, and release.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Make dependencies, contracts, versions, feature flags, migrations, configuration, and telemetry traceable. Support selective and full regression, deterministic reset, and safe rollback.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show change, impact, suite, coverage, failures, flaky state, exclusions, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized run, quarantine, expand, accept, reject, and reopen controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Impact analysis; regression selection; suite and fixture versions; results and defect record; exclusion and exception record; retest, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify change, baseline, case, version, environment, fixture, execution, result, time, method, owner, coverage, and limitation. A green rerun proves only the selected suite and conditions.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify impact analysis, critical path coverage, prior defect coverage, changed dependency behavior, exclusions, flakiness, performance, security, recovery, and independent reproduction.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Run everything without impact reasoning, run nothing for a “small” change, obsolete cases, flaky tests ignored, exclusions hidden, and regression pass treated as full acceptance.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Preserve failure, halt affected release, expand scope, correct code or test, and rerun. Reopen on further change, new defect, dependency drift, environment change, or user impact.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Change, impact, cases, fixtures, results, failures, exclusions, coverage, owners, evidence, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve baseline and suite versions, impact, results, defects, exceptions, decisions, evidence, and open gaps. Hand off to Release, Quality, Unit, Integration, System, E2E, Performance, and Operations.
