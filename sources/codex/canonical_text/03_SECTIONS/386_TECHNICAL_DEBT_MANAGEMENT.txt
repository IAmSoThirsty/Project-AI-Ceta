SECTION 386 — Technical Debt Management

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Identify, describe, prioritize, fund, reduce, contain, and retire technical debt and its interest without dismissing it as harmless or merely pre-existing.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A debt model with item, cause, affected outcome, interest, risk, owner, evidence, priority, remediation, containment, decision, review, and explicit acceptance or closure.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Debt is a known problem that actively consumes reliability, security, speed, quality, and human capacity. Untracked debt compounds and makes future change more expensive.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a shortcut, obsolete design, deferred maintenance, missing control, workaround, unsupported dependency, or known defect creates ongoing cost or risk.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no known structural weakness, deferred work, workaround, or compounding risk affects the scope and an approved exact baseline supports it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use architecture, requirements, incidents, vulnerabilities, quality, maintenance, cost, roadmap, risk, user, and operations evidence.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Debt item, cause, evidence, interest, risk, outcome, owner, affected users, remediation, containment, cost, priority, and review condition.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners record and actively manage debt; accountable humans prioritize remediation, fund work, accept bounded residual risk with expiry, and cannot erase debt by relabeling it.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may identify patterns in authorized code, incidents, findings, and work, draft debt records, and estimate bounded impact. It must not minimize, close, prioritize by score alone, or claim remediation without human decision and evidence.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Refactorer, Architect, Analyst, Researcher, Quality Engineer, Security Auditor, Operations Engineer, Planner, Verifier, and Governance Reviewer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Discover and evidence; classify cause and interest; assess risk and users; assign owner; contain; prioritize; plan remediation; track; verify; review aging; and retire or escalate.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show debt, cause, evidence, interest, risk, owner, priority, containment, and limitation. Require human review for priority, acceptance, funding, and closure.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Link debt to components, dependencies, incidents, tests, users, cost, roadmap, and risk. Make interest visible in capacity and change decisions; preserve safe containment.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show debt inventory, aging, interest, risk, owner, remediation, evidence freshness, and authorized prioritize, contain, fund, remediate, accept with expiry, and reopen controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Debt charter; item and evidence; interest and risk; remediation and containment; roadmap and review; decision, exception, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify item, source, component, cause, effect, period, method, result, interest, owner, and limitation. A ticket or estimate proves only its bounded record.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify debt identity, evidence, cause, interest, risk, owner, containment, remediation, regression, closure, expiry, and escalation. Test whether debt remains present after claimed fix.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Debt dismissed as pre-existing, backlog burial, score without impact, permanent acceptance, no owner, remediation without test, and debt interest hidden from roadmap.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Escalate aging or compounding debt, contain risk, preserve evidence, fund or de-scope, remediate, and reassess user and system impact. Reopen on recurrence, changed risk, new evidence, or invalid closure.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Debt, cause, evidence, interest, risk, owner, priority, containment, remediation, decision, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve debt history, evidence, decisions, remediation, exceptions, incidents, and open gaps. Hand off to Architecture, Maintenance, Refactoring, Security, Quality, Roadmap, and Operations.
