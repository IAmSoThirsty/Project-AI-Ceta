SECTION 367 — Product Analytics

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Measure and understand product behavior, adoption, journeys, outcomes, quality, and opportunity to inform responsible product decisions.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A product-analytics model with question, outcome, event, metric, population, cohort, funnel, experiment, data quality, privacy, decision, and limitation.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Product telemetry can show what happened but not always why, whether it helped, or who was excluded. Optimizing clicks or retention can harm comprehension, safety, or trust.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when product usage, journey, feature, quality, outcome, or experiment data informs product or operational decisions.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no product data, event, metric, journey, decision, or customer impact changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use product, user research, data, privacy, metrics, feature, experimentation, support, and governance baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Question, product, user, journey, event, metric, cohort, source, period, feature, outcome, privacy, and evidence.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Product owners define outcome and decision; data and privacy owners approve collection and use; humans validate interpretation with research and domain judgment.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze authorized aggregate product data, identify patterns, and draft hypotheses. It must not infer user intent, expose individuals, make product decisions, or claim causal effect without human review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Product Analyst, Data Analyst, Product Manager, Researcher, Statistician, Privacy Reviewer, Experimenter, Verifier, and Critic.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define question and outcome; instrument events; validate data; analyze journeys and cohorts; combine qualitative evidence; assess harm; recommend; experiment; monitor; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show question, event, metric, cohort, source, period, evidence, uncertainty, privacy, and limitation. Require human review for high-impact product, targeting, and retention decisions.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use event taxonomy, identity and consent, data lineage, metric versioning, privacy, aggregation, quality, experiment separation, and feedback to product decisions.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show product question, journey, metric, cohort, freshness, data quality, findings, owner, and authorized instrument, correct, restrict, experiment, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Product-analytics charter; event and metric catalog; journey and cohort; analysis and outcome; decision, evidence, privacy, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify question, event, user or cohort, source, metric, period, method, result, reviewer, and limitation. Usage or funnel change proves neither cause nor value.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify events, identities, consent, data quality, metrics, cohorts, journey, privacy, causality limits, experiment, and decision traceability.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Instrument everything, metric drift, event semantics unclear, retention as value, cohort bias, personal data exposure, and product change driven by dashboard alone.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Correct or withdraw data, preserve versions, notify consumers, revise product decision, and reassess user impact. Reopen on event, metric, feature, population, privacy, or product change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Question, outcome, events, metrics, cohorts, privacy, evidence, owners, decisions, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve event and metric versions, analyses, experiments, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Product, Customer Analytics, Data, Experimentation, Feedback, and Governance.
