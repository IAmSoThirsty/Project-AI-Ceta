SECTION 340 — Developer Relations

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Build two-way relationships with developers and technical communities through enablement, feedback, events, documentation, examples, support, and transparent stewardship.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A developer-relations model with audience, goals, channels, programs, content, community, feedback, safety, accessibility, evidence, commitments, and handoff.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Developer relations can create trust and learning or become marketing theater. Reach, attendance, and sentiment do not prove technical usefulness, truthful claims, or community health.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when an organization engages developers, technical users, contributors, partners, or communities around a product, platform, standard, or practice.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no developer audience, engagement, enablement, feedback, event, support, or public technical claim changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use product, developer experience, community, documentation, education, support, privacy, legal, IP, marketing, and governance baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Audience, goals, product, technical content, channels, events, community, feedback, support, accessibility, privacy, commitments, and evidence.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Developer-relations owners represent product truth, protect community, moderate, communicate limits, and route feedback. Humans approve technical commitments, public claims, sponsorship, and sensitive responses.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft examples, summarize authorized feedback, personalize content, and identify recurring questions. It must not invent capabilities, impersonate staff, expose community data, make commitments, or moderate high-impact issues without human review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Developer Relations Lead, Developer Experience Engineer, Community Manager, Technical Writer, Product Manager, Researcher, Support Reviewer, Verifier, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Understand audience; define program; create and review content; host events; support community; collect feedback; communicate roadmap and limits; measure technical outcome; escalate risk; and preserve continuity.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show audience, source, product version, claim, feedback, privacy, evidence, and limitation. Require human review for public technical claims, moderation, commitments, and safety.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Support feedback capture, content versioning, community safety, accessible events, support routing, consent, analytics, moderation, and durable knowledge transfer.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show audience, program, content, questions, feedback, incidents, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized revise, clarify, escalate, moderate, communicate, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Developer-relations charter; audience and channel; content and event; community and support; feedback, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify audience, claim, product version, source, channel, event, method, time, result, and limitation. Reach, attendance, or social sentiment proves only its bounded signal.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify technical accuracy, examples, accessibility, community safety, privacy, support routing, feedback, claim limits, and handoff. Test outdated content and difficult question escalation.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Marketing disguised as technical guidance, invented roadmap, community data exposure, no moderation, engagement as usefulness, and feedback collected without ownership or response.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Correct public material, notify audience, protect participants, preserve feedback, and reassess claim or program. Reopen on product, audience, community, policy, or safety change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Audience, goals, content, channels, feedback, safety, accessibility, evidence, owners, commitments, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve content, events, feedback, questions, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Product, Documentation, Education, Community, Support, Marketing, and Governance.
