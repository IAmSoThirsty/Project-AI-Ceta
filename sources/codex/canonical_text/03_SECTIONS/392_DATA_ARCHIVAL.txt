SECTION 392 — Data Archival

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Move inactive data and records to a durable, governed, retrievable, cost-aware, secure, and lifecycle-appropriate archive without losing meaning or rights.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An archival model with eligibility, authority, metadata, format, integrity, location, access, retrieval, retention, hold, migration, cost, and disposition.

WHY THIS MATTERS

An archive must remain understandable and trustworthy over time. Cheap storage can preserve unreadable, unindexed, inaccessible, or unauthorized copies and can complicate deletion or rights.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when data, records, artifacts, evidence, or content leaves active operation but must remain available for a defined purpose or period.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no inactive data, record, evidence, retrieval, retention, or lifecycle state changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use records, retention, privacy, legal, data, storage, backup, preservation, security, access, and deletion baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Data or record, eligibility, purpose, authority, metadata, format, integrity, location, access, retrieval, retention, hold, migration, and disposition.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Archive owners approve eligibility, format, metadata, access, migration, hold, retention, retrieval, and disposition. Humans decide authenticity, rights, and destruction.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inspect authorized metadata, identify archive candidates, draft indexes, and detect integrity or format issues. It must not archive, alter, disclose, migrate, or delete content without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Archivist, Records Manager, Data Engineer, Storage Administrator, Privacy Reviewer, Legal Advisor, Security Auditor, Verifier, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define eligibility; classify; preserve metadata and integrity; package and transfer; index; secure; test retrieval; migrate format; apply hold; review; and dispose when authorized.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show item, class, purpose, authority, metadata, format, integrity, access, action, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for archive, access, migration, hold, and disposition.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use durable format, fixity, metadata, catalog, access, encryption, geographic or media strategy, migration, retrieval, hold, retention, and deletion propagation.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show archived item, class, age, integrity, format, location, access, hold, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized retrieve, restrict, migrate, preserve, and dispose controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Archive charter; eligibility and authority; package and metadata; fixity and catalog; retrieval and migration; retention, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify item, source, class, authority, format, metadata, integrity, location, method, time, result, owner, and limitation. Archive presence proves neither readability nor authenticity without fixity and provenance.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify eligibility, metadata, format, fixity, access, retrieval, privacy, hold, migration, retention, deletion, cost, and disaster recovery. Retrieve representative and critical records.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Archive as dump, metadata lost, format obsolete, fixity absent, access uncontrolled, archive confused with backup, deletion blocked forever, and retrieval never tested.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Preserve alternate copy, restore catalog or metadata, migrate format, restrict access, apply hold, notify owners, and reassess authenticity and rights. Reopen on format, medium, law, purpose, or integrity change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Eligibility, authority, data, metadata, format, integrity, access, retrieval, retention, migration, evidence, owners, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending qualified acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve archive catalog, packages, fixity, migrations, retrievals, holds, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Records, Retention, Preservation, Storage, Legal, Privacy, Security, and Operations.
