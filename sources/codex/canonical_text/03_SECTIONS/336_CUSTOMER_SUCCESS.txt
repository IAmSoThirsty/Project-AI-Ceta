SECTION 336 — Customer Success

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Help customers achieve their intended outcomes through adoption, enablement, measurement, risk identification, support coordination, and honest feedback.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A customer-success model with customer goals, success criteria, adoption, health, value evidence, risks, plan, support, escalation, renewal or continuation, and feedback.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Usage or renewal can hide whether customers achieve value, understand risk, or feel able to leave. Success claims must reflect customer outcomes rather than account activity alone.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when an owner or team helps customers adopt, operate, measure, expand, renew, or recover value from a product or service.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no ongoing customer outcome, adoption, value, support, or relationship decision changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use product, user research, service, support, analytics, privacy, security, legal, training, and roadmap baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Customer goals, success criteria, users, use case, adoption, health, outcomes, risks, support, incidents, feedback, renewal, and evidence.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Customer-success owners represent customer outcomes, communicate honestly, escalate risk, protect data, and avoid making commitments they cannot authorize. Humans decide remedy, scope, and relationship action.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may summarize authorized customer signals, identify risk, draft enablement and review questions, and suggest next steps. It must not infer sentiment or intent as fact, expose data, promise outcome, or make account decisions without human review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Customer Success Manager, User Researcher, Product Manager, Analyst, Technical Support Engineer, Privacy Reviewer, User Experience Reviewer, Verifier, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Understand goals; define success; plan adoption; enable; measure outcome; monitor risk; coordinate support; communicate; review value; address gaps; capture feedback; and decide continuation or change.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show customer, goal, use case, evidence, health, risk, recommendation, privacy, and limitation. Require human review for customer communication, commitment, risk, renewal, and remedy.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Support customer outcome instrumentation, privacy, role separation, accessible enablement, feedback, support escalation, status, and data export or exit.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show customer goals, outcome, adoption, health, risks, incidents, support, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized intervene, escalate, communicate, compensate, and close controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Success charter; customer goal and health; adoption and outcome; enablement and support; risk, feedback, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify customer, goal, use case, metric, source, period, method, result, uncertainty, and limitation. Usage, satisfaction, or renewal proves only its bounded signal.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify goal, success criterion, user outcome, enablement, accessibility, support, risk, privacy, feedback, and customer understanding. Test failure and recovery journey.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Adoption as value, renewal as success, hidden churn risk, unsupported promise, customer data overcollection, and success narrative that ignores dissent or harm.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Protect customer, acknowledge mismatch, coordinate support, provide remedy, preserve feedback, and reassess outcome. Reopen on goal, product, user, risk, or relationship change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Customer, goals, outcomes, evidence, adoption, risk, support, owner, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending customer and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve success plans, feedback, risks, incidents, support, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Customer Support, Product, Sales, Solutions, Training, and Operations.
