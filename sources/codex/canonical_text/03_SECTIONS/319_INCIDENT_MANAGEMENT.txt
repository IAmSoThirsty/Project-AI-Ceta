SECTION 319 — Incident Management

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Coordinate the lifecycle of operational incidents from detection and declaration through command, communication, stabilization, recovery, closure, and learning.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An incident-management model with classification, commander, roles, timeline, evidence, impact, communications, actions, dependencies, recovery, closure, and review.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Incidents require coordinated authority under uncertainty. Unclear command, parallel unsafe action, missing timeline, or premature closure can increase harm and prevent recovery learning.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when an event disrupts or threatens service, safety, security, privacy, data, operations, users, property, or commitments.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability only for events with no material impact and a routine path that preserves evidence, escalation, and user support.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use alert, monitoring, service, security, continuity, communication, support, legal, privacy, records, and recovery baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Alert or report, impact, scope, severity, commander, contacts, evidence, actions, dependencies, communications, recovery objective, and owner authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Incident commander coordinates and can stop unsafe action. Owners approve containment, communication, service reduction, legal escalation, recovery, and closure. Humans decide material severity and harm.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may summarize signals and timeline, draft hypotheses and status, and track authorized actions. It must not declare, contain, communicate, delete evidence, attribute, or close without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Incident Commander, Incident Responder, SRE, Security Operations Analyst, Forensic Analyst, Coordinator, Operations Engineer, Legal Advisor, Privacy Reviewer, Verifier, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Prepare; intake; declare; assign command; preserve evidence; assess impact; communicate; contain; stabilize; recover; monitor; close; review; and track corrective action.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show incident, severity, impact, scope, commander, evidence, action, authority, communication, and uncertainty. Require human approval for containment, notification, recovery, and closure.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use case record, immutable timeline, evidence store, command channel, status page, action authority, privacy, audit, and recovery independent of the failed service where possible.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show incident state, commander, severity, scope, impact, actions, evidence freshness, dependencies, communication, residual risk, and authorized escalate, contain, communicate, recover, reopen, and close controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Incident charter; timeline; impact and severity; command and communications; evidence; actions and recovery; post-incident review; decision, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify event, source, asset, user, time basis, action, actor, result, impact, confidence, and limitation. Confirmed, suspected, disproved, and unknown states remain distinct.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Exercise declaration, command, paging, evidence, communication, containment, recovery, user support, legal and privacy escalation, closure, and post-incident follow-up.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

No commander, severity by volume, parallel conflicting action, lost timeline, status theater, premature closure, blame, and communication that overclaims.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stabilize, preserve evidence, recover, communicate, support users, and reassess impact and residual risk. Reopen on new evidence, scope, root cause, recovery failure, or harm.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Command, severity, scope, evidence, actions, communication, recovery, closure, lessons, owners, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve case, timeline, evidence, communications, actions, decisions, postmortem, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Problem, Security, SRE, Support, Legal, Privacy, Records, and Governance.
