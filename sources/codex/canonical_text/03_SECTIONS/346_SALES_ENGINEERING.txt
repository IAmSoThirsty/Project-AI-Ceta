SECTION 346 — Sales Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Translate customer technical needs into truthful solution fit, architecture, demonstration, risk, integration, security, deployment, and implementation expectations.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A sales-engineering model with customer context, requirements, proposed solution, assumptions, evidence, demo, integration, security, constraints, commitments, and handoff.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Sales discussions can create technical commitments before architecture and delivery review. A convincing demo may hide scale, security, data, support, or implementation limits.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when technical specialists support a customer or partner decision involving product fit, architecture, integration, security, deployment, or operational outcome.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no technical customer decision, solution claim, demo, integration, or commitment changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use product, solution, architecture, security, legal, IP, licensing, support, customer success, documentation, and roadmap baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Customer need, context, constraints, architecture, data, security, integration, deployment, scale, evidence, demo, assumptions, and approval authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Sales engineers document facts and assumptions and escalate gaps. Product, architecture, security, legal, and delivery owners approve commitments, exceptions, and customer-specific claims.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may summarize authorized customer context, draft diagrams and questions, and compare approved capabilities. It must not invent fit, guarantee performance, expose customer data, make commitments, or impersonate technical authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Sales Engineer, Solutions Engineer, Architect, Researcher, Analyst, Security Auditor, Product Manager, Legal Advisor, Verifier, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Understand need; qualify constraints; map requirements; design and review solution; demonstrate; validate integration, security, data, scale, operations, and support; state assumptions; obtain approvals; and hand off.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show customer, need, capability, version, evidence, assumption, limitation, reviewer, and commitment status. Require human approval for customer claim, architecture, pricing, legal, security, and delivery promise.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Link customer requirement to product capability, architecture, data, security, integration, deployment, support, and evidence. Separate demo state from production state.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show opportunity, customer need, solution, assumptions, evidence freshness, risks, owners, commitment status, and authorized approve, qualify, escalate, correct, and handoff controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Technical discovery; requirement and constraint map; solution architecture; demo and evidence; assumption and risk; security, deployment, support, decision, and handoff records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify customer, need, capability, version, environment, demo, source, method, result, assumption, and limitation. Demo success proves neither production fit nor customer outcome.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify requirement fit, architecture, integration, security, data, performance, deployment, support, licensing, assumptions, and handoff. Test customer scenario and failure path.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Demo as proof, unapproved promise, customer data in demo, architecture hand-waving, hidden assumption, security claim without evidence, and technical debt transferred silently.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Correct customer record, qualify or withdraw claim, notify stakeholders, preserve evidence, and reassess solution and commitment. Reopen on product, customer, architecture, security, or delivery change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Customer, need, solution, evidence, assumptions, risks, approvals, limitations, commitment, owners, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve discovery, diagrams, demos, commitments, assumptions, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Solutions, Product, Architecture, Engineering, Delivery, Support, Legal, and Customer Success.
