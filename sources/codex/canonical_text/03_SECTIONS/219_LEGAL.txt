SECTION 219 — Legal

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Identify, interpret, obtain advice on, and preserve the legal obligations, rights, authorities, liabilities, contracts, disputes, and jurisdictional constraints that affect the work.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A legal-obligation map with jurisdictions, parties, claims, contracts, notices, approvals, privacy, safety, IP, records, regulatory, dispute, and escalation requirements, clearly marked as legal advice or unresolved counsel questions.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Legal risk is context-specific and can turn on facts, jurisdiction, timing, contract language, or procedural detail. A confident summary, generic compliance statement, or AI-generated interpretation is not a substitute for qualified counsel or accountable decision-making.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when law, regulation, contract, rights, litigation, investigation, licensing, intellectual property, employment, safety, privacy, public communication, or jurisdiction affects the scope or consequences.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability only when qualified review confirms no legal, contractual, rights, jurisdictional, or dispute effect for the exact scope, version, parties, and time. “Not legal work” is not sufficient.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Identify jurisdictions, entities, parties, activities, data, products, contracts, regulators, counsel, deadlines, records, and decision owners. Preserve legal holds and confidentiality requirements.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Objective, parties, jurisdictions, facts, contracts, policies, data, product or service, claims, notices, deadlines, approvals, prior advice, disputes, source identity, and unresolved legal questions.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Qualified counsel or designated legal authority interprets law, privilege, litigation, disclosure, contract, and regulatory strategy. Business owners provide truthful facts, implement approved obligations, and accept non-legal operational tradeoffs only within authority.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may organize authorized legal sources, compare clauses, extract obligations, draft questions and issue registers, and summarize supplied advice with provenance. It must not claim to be counsel, create attorney-client privilege, infer jurisdiction, provide definitive legal advice, conceal adverse facts, or approve a legal position or disclosure.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Legal Advisor, Researcher, Analyst, Governance Reviewer, Privacy Reviewer, Records Manager, Documentation Writer, Verifier, Critic, and Memory Curator. Legal advice remains with qualified human authority.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define facts and scope; identify jurisdictions and parties; inventory obligations and contracts; obtain counsel review; map duties to controls and owners; track deadlines and notices; preserve holds; record assumptions and conflicts; verify implementation; and escalate unresolved questions.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Label source, jurisdiction, date, authority, privilege or confidentiality, fact, interpretation, question, recommendation, and decision. Keep sensitive advice in approved channels. Require counsel or authorized human review for material legal conclusions, notices, claims, and disclosures.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Represent obligations, parties, jurisdictions, contracts, rights, deadlines, evidence, holds, approvals, and controls as versioned records with access boundaries. Prevent automated workflows from silently changing legal position or destroying held records.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show obligation, jurisdiction, source, counsel status, owner, deadline, hold, risk, evidence, unresolved question, and authorized assign, approve, restrict, notify, escalate, preserve, and close controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Legal issue and obligation register; jurisdiction and party map; contract and notice inventory; counsel questions and advice record; hold and deadline record; control mapping; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify source, jurisdiction, version, party, fact, date, authority, method, reviewer, conclusion or question, and limitation. A legal summary, template, policy, or AI output is not proof of legal sufficiency.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify source and jurisdiction, party and contract scope, deadline, privilege handling, hold, implementation, notice, approval, disclosure, and escalation. Obtain qualified review for material claims and retain the basis for acceptance.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

AI presented as counsel, law copied without jurisdiction, stale advice, hidden facts, privilege in uncontrolled systems, contracts ignored as operational inputs, and compliance asserted from a checklist.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause affected action, preserve records, escalate to counsel, correct the factual record, notify authorized owners, and reassess deadlines and exposure. Reopen on new facts, jurisdiction, contract, law, regulator, dispute, or product change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Facts, jurisdictions, parties, obligations, sources, counsel status, owners, deadlines, controls, evidence, unresolved questions, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending qualified review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve source, advice status, privilege, holds, deadlines, decisions, notices, contracts, evidence, and open gaps. Hand off only through authorized channels to Compliance, Policy, Privacy, Records, IP, Licensing, Security, and accountable owners.
