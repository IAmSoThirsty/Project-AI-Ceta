SECTION 239 — Performance Testing

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Measure responsiveness, throughput, resource use, latency, capacity behavior, and user or operator experience against declared performance objectives and conditions.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A performance-test model with workload, scenarios, service levels, environment, instrumentation, baselines, variability, bottleneck analysis, evidence, and limits.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Performance is workload and environment dependent. A fast sample or average can hide tail latency, contention, resource exhaustion, degraded user experience, or a provider bottleneck.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when response time, throughput, resource use, capacity, cost, or user experience is material to the capability.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability only when performance has no material user, operator, safety, cost, service, or scale consequence and an approved exact baseline covers the change.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use performance objectives, architecture, workload, environment, telemetry, data, capacity, cost, reliability, and operations baselines. Define safe test authority and stop conditions.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Workload, scenarios, users, data, service levels, latency percentiles, throughput, resource limits, environment, dependencies, instrumentation, baseline, and acceptance criteria.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners define meaningful performance and tradeoffs; testers control load and safety; operators approve production-like testing; humans accept performance risk and cost.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft workloads, analyze traces, identify bottlenecks, and propose experiments. It must not generate unsafe load, claim generality from one environment, alter production, or optimize a metric while harming user outcomes without human review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Performance Engineer, Tester, Architect, Analyst, Observer, Optimizer, Verifier, Quality Engineer, Operations Engineer, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define objectives and workload; baseline; prepare data and environment; execute normal and boundary tests; capture latency distribution, throughput, resource, cost, and user evidence; analyze bottlenecks; remediate; rerun; document limits.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show workload, environment, version, dependency, metric definition, percentile, resource, cost, result, and limitation. Require human approval for load generation, production testing, resource changes, and acceptance.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Provide observability, correlation, resource isolation, realistic data, capacity controls, backpressure, graceful degradation, caching semantics, and safe test cleanup.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show workload, environment, latency, throughput, resource, cost, bottleneck, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized start, pause, stop, tune, rollback, accept, and reopen controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Performance objectives; workload and scenario model; environment and instrumentation record; baseline and results; bottleneck and remediation record; decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify version, workload, data, environment, dependency, metric, percentile, method, time, result, variability, and limitation. An average or one run proves only its observed slice.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify workload realism, instrument calibration, repeatability, latency distribution, throughput, resource, cost, user impact, degradation, recovery, and independent reproduction.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Benchmark theater, average-only reporting, synthetic workload unlike use, warm-cache bias, ignored tail latency, unsafe load, and optimization that shifts cost or harm elsewhere.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stop unsafe test, preserve traces, recover resources, isolate bottleneck, roll back harmful tuning, and reassess targets. Reopen on workload, version, environment, dependency, user, or objective change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Objectives, workloads, environments, metrics, results, variability, bottlenecks, owners, evidence, limits, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve workload and environment versions, traces, baselines, results, tuning, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Capacity, Load, Stress, Scalability, Reliability, Release, and Operations.
