SECTION 271 — GitOps

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Use versioned, reviewed, declarative source as the controlled expression of environment state, with reconciliation, drift visibility, safe promotion, and human authority.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A GitOps model with desired state, repository, controller, identity, policy, environment, reconciliation, drift, secrets, promotion, rollback, audit, and recovery.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Declarative control improves traceability but can make a bad commit or controller rapidly widespread. Desired state is not automatically safe, current, authorized, or complete.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when versioned source drives infrastructure, platform, application, policy, configuration, or environment reconciliation.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no declarative source, reconciler, drift, or automated environment change is used and an approved exact baseline applies.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use source control, configuration, identity, policy, secrets, build, artifact, environment, platform, security, and operations baselines. Define repository and reconciler authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Desired state, repository, review, controller, target, identity, policy, artifact, secrets, drift, health, rollback, and acceptance criteria.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners approve repository, protected changes, controller permissions, promotion, emergency override, drift reconciliation, and rollback. Humans decide whether desired state is safe and correct.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft declarative changes, compare desired and actual state, explain drift, and propose remediation. It must not commit, merge, reconcile, change controller permissions, expose secrets, or declare health without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

GitOps Engineer, Platform Engineer, Source Control Manager, Configuration Manager, Security Auditor, Tester, Verifier, Implementer, Analyst, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define desired state; protect repository; validate and scan; review and promote; configure controller; reconcile; observe drift and health; pause or roll back; handle emergency changes; restore; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show commit, desired state, target, controller, permissions, drift, health, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for protected change, reconcile, production, emergency, and destructive action.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate desired source, controller, target, secrets, policy, observed state, health, and recovery. Use least privilege, progressive promotion, drift policy, reconciliation pause, immutable artifacts, and independent control path.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show desired and actual state, commit, target, controller, drift, health, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized approve, pause, reconcile, rollback, override, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

GitOps charter; repository and controller model; desired-state definitions; policy and secret boundaries; promotion and drift records; health, rollback, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify commit, desired state, target, controller, identity, time, observed state, method, result, drift, and limitation. A commit proves intended state, not actual state or safe reconciliation.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify repository protection, policy, controller permission, secret handling, drift, reconciliation, health, pause, rollback, recovery, and audit. Test malicious commit, controller loss, and conflicting manual change.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Controller overprivilege, automatic broad reconciliation, secrets in repository, drift hidden, manual change overwritten, desired state mistaken for reality, and no emergency stop.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause reconciliation, preserve commit and state, restore known-good desired and actual state, rotate credentials, notify owners, and reassess blast radius. Reopen on source, controller, target, policy, or environment change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Desired state, repository, controller, target, identity, policy, drift, health, rollback, evidence, owners, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve repositories, commits, controller state, desired and actual state, promotions, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Platform, Configuration, Build, Release, Security, and Operations.
