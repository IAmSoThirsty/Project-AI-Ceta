SECTION 101 — Localization

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define adaptation of content, interaction, data, behavior, and support for a declared locale while preserving meaning, functionality, accessibility, legal fit, and cultural respect.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

 - A scoped source, locales, audiences, cultural context, content, formats, legal, accessibility, workflow, and lifecycle model.
 - Traceable translations, locale rules, review, tests, fallback, evidence, and acceptance records.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Literal translation can break meaning, layout, dates, numbers, safety, humor, consent, or legal disclosure. Locale coverage is not cultural validity.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to products, services, content, interfaces, support, data, voice, games, documentation, and operations used across locales.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No locale, translation, formatting, cultural, accessibility, legal, or audience effect; or an unchanged approved localization baseline with impact recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Source content, locale requirements, terminology, translators, cultural research, legal and policy rules, format constraints, accessibility, testing, and authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Humans approve locale scope, meaning, cultural fit, legal disclosures, accessibility, publication, and acceptance. They resolve untranslatable or sensitive content.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

AI may translate or identify variants, but must preserve source, context, uncertainty, and human review. It must not invent cultural authority or publish high-consequence translation alone.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may prepare locale-specific drafts, detect inconsistent terminology, flag truncation or cultural risk, and assemble traceable comparison and test evidence from approved source material. It must preserve source intent, locale scope, formatting rules, accessibility requirements, legal meaning, uncertainty, and reviewer attribution; distinguish translation from adaptation; and identify content that requires qualified cultural, legal, domain, or lived-experience review. It must not invent local authority, infer user identity or culture, silently replace approved terminology, expose sensitive source content, or publish, withdraw, or materially alter localized content without the required human decision and recorded evidence.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Localization Specialist, Translator, Content Designer, UX Reviewer, Researcher, Cultural or domain Expert, Accessibility specialist, Tester, Verifier, Governance Reviewer, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define locales and priority; inventory source; establish glossary; translate and adapt; review culturally and legally; test layout, data, voice, accessibility, and behavior; publish, monitor, correct, and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show source and locale, translation status, reviewer, uncertainty, actor, consequence, fallback, and correction. AI translations remain proposed until qualified review.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Support locale metadata, plural and gender rules, scripts, direction, dates, numbers, units, sorting, search, content, identity, accessibility, caching, and fallback without silently mixing locales.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show locale, source version, translation, reviewer, freshness, evidence, missing strings, defects, and controls to approve, correct, fall back, or withdraw.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Localization plan, locale and terminology register, translated assets, cultural and legal review, accessibility and layout tests, evidence, decisions, and handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify source, locale, translator or model, context, version, method, result, uncertainty, and limitation. Back-translation or fluency is bounded evidence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify meaning, terminology, pluralization, direction, dates, numbers, layout, accessibility, legal disclosure, cultural fit, fallback, and representative locale behavior.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Machine translation without review; English fallback for critical content; truncated scripts; cultural stereotypes; wrong units; and treating fluency as correctness.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Use approved fallback, remove harmful translation, preserve source and review history, notify owners, commission qualified review, and retest affected locales.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Locales, source, meaning, review, accessibility, evidence, tests, fallback, recovery, risks, and acceptance are explicit. Remain CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve source and locale versions, glossaries, translators, reviews, decisions, evidence, defects, and next action. Hand off to Internationalization, Content, UX, Accessibility, Testing, and Operations.
