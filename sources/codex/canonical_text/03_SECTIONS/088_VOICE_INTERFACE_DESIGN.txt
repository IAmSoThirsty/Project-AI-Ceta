SECTION 088 — Voice Interface Design

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define spoken interaction, input, recognition, prompts, turn-taking, feedback, confirmation, privacy, and recovery for voice-enabled systems.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

 - A scoped speaker, listener, context, language, device, task, authority, privacy, and lifecycle model.
 - Voice flows, prompts, intents, disambiguation, fallback, evidence, accessibility, testing, and acceptance records.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Voice is affected by noise, accent, language, privacy, interruption, and ambiguity. Fluent recognition does not prove correct intent or safe action.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to voice assistants, IVR, hands-free controls, accessibility tools, vehicles, devices, services, and conversational products with spoken input or output.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No voice input, output, command, privacy, accessibility, or task effect; or an unchanged approved voice baseline with impact recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Tasks, users, languages, accents, devices, acoustic context, speech and intent models, content, identity, permissions, privacy, security, fallback, and acceptance authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Tasks, users, languages, accents, devices, acoustic context, speech and intent models, content, identity, permissions, privacy, security, fallback, and acceptance authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve commands, permissions, disclosures, confirmations, recording, accessibility, language, risk, and acceptance. They retain stop, correction, appeal, and human-support authority.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft prompts, intents, tests, and summaries. It must preserve transcript, speaker, language, confidence, and privacy context; it must not infer intent or execute consequential actions from uncertain speech.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Voice Interface Designer, Conversational Designer, UX Reviewer, Researcher, Accessibility specialist, Security and Privacy Auditors, Architect, Tester, Verifier, Critic, Governance Reviewer, Implementer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define tasks, invocation, turn-taking, prompts, recognition, intent, confirmation, interruption, errors, fallback, privacy, language, accessibility, and safe action; test with representative speakers and environments.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show transcript or interpretation, confidence, actor, permission, consequence, confirmation, recording state, and recovery. AI proposals remain advisory; keyboard, visual, and human alternatives remain available.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate audio capture, recognition, intent, policy, confirmation, execution, response, logging, and recovery. Enforce identity and authorization independently of recognition confidence.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show voice session, speaker, language, device, transcript, confidence, permission, action queue, recording, evidence, and controls to cancel, mute, correct, escalate, or recover.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Voice architecture, intents and utterances, prompt and dialogue specs, language and accessibility coverage, privacy and permission model, test evidence, fallback, decisions, and handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify speaker population, language, acoustic context, device, model, version, transcript, method, result, uncertainty, and limitation. Recognition accuracy is not task or safety acceptance.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify accents, noise, silence, interruption, ambiguity, spoofing, permissions, confirmation, privacy, accessibility, localization, fallback, latency, and cancellation.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Executing on low confidence; recording without clear control; no transcript or correction; language exclusion; voice-only critical action; and treating recognition score as intent truth.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Cancel or suspend action, preserve transcript and evidence, mute or revoke capture, use alternate input or human support, and reassess affected speakers before resuming.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Tasks, speakers, languages, authority, privacy, dialogue, evidence, tests, fallback, recovery, open risks, and acceptance are explicit. Remain CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve dialogue versions, prompts, models, transcripts, permissions, findings, decisions, evidence, and next action. Hand off to Conversational, Audio, Speech, Accessibility, Localization, Security, Privacy, and Testing.
