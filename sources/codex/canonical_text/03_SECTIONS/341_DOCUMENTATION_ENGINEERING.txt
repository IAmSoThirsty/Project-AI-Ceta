SECTION 341 — Documentation Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Engineer documentation as a versioned, tested, accessible, source-grounded product that helps its audience understand, decide, operate, recover, and maintain continuity.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A documentation model with audience, purpose, source, structure, terminology, examples, version, accessibility, review, publication, feedback, retirement, and evidence.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Documentation is an operational and governance interface. Stale, ambiguous, inaccessible, or untested instructions can create defects, unsafe action, support load, and false expectations.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when instructions, reference, explanation, decision records, policies, runbooks, training, or public technical content affects people or systems.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no user, operator, decision, support, governance, or continuity documentation is created or changed and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use user research, glossary, source, product, architecture, operations, accessibility, localization, legal, IP, and knowledge baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Audience, purpose, source, version, terms, task, procedure, examples, limits, accessibility, localization, review, publication, and feedback.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Documentation owners and subject experts verify accuracy, scope, safety, and approval. Humans decide normative language, public claim, legal text, and retirement.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft, restructure, summarize supplied sources, identify gaps, and propose examples. It must not invent facts, citations, behavior, policy, or user evidence, and must not publish authoritative material without human review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Documentation Writer, Technical Writer, Researcher, Subject Matter Expert, User Experience Reviewer, Accessibility Reviewer, Verifier, Critic, and Memory Curator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define audience and task; gather authoritative sources; write; review; test instructions and examples; validate accessibility and localization; publish; collect feedback; update; version; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show source, version, audience, claim, uncertainty, example, reviewer, and limitation. Require human review for accuracy, safety, legal, public, operational, and normative content.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use docs-as-code or equivalent versioning, source links, metadata, search, accessibility, localization, publication gates, redirects, feedback, and archival.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show document status, source freshness, audience, owner, review, feedback, evidence, accessibility, and authorized edit, approve, publish, correct, deprecate, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Documentation charter; audience and content map; source and glossary; drafts and examples; review and publication; feedback, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify claim, source, version, audience, task, method, reviewer, time, result, and limitation. Publication or search ranking proves neither correctness nor usefulness.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify source, procedure, examples, links, version, accessibility, localization, search, task success, safety, support, and retirement. Test clean reader and failure path.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Stale docs, invented example, citation without source, inaccessible format, no owner, duplicate authority, broken link, and documentation that hides uncertainty or recovery.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Correct or withdraw, notify readers, preserve previous version, provide support, and reassess affected action. Reopen on product, policy, source, user, locale, or operational change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Audience, purpose, source, version, content, review, accessibility, publication, evidence, owner, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending subject-matter and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve source, versions, reviews, links, feedback, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Knowledge, Training, Support, Developer Relations, Product, and Operations.
