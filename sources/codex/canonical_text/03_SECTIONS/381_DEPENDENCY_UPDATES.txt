SECTION 381 — Dependency Updates

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Update direct and transitive dependencies to maintain security, compatibility, support, license, performance, and functionality with controlled evidence and rollback.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A dependency-update model with graph, current and target version, reason, advisory, license, compatibility, test, rollout, artifact, owner, and recovery.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Dependency updates can change APIs, licenses, performance, transitive behavior, and supply-chain trust. Automated update volume can hide important semantic or security change.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a package, library, provider, image, model, module, API, or external component is updated.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no dependency version, source, behavior, license, vulnerability, or support condition changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use dependency, package, vulnerability, license, source, build, compatibility, testing, release, and operations baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Graph, current and target version, reason, advisory, license, source, compatibility, tests, artifact, rollout, rollback, and owner.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners approve update reason, version, license, security, compatibility, rollout, exception, and rollback. Humans review transitive and customer impact.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inspect authorized graph and advisories, draft update, test, and release notes, and identify impact. It must not install, merge, approve, or claim safe update without human authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Dependency Manager, Security Auditor, Build Engineer, Tester, Verifier, License Analyst, Release Engineer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Inventory; identify reason and scope; review advisory and license; update; resolve; build; test; scan; release; monitor; roll back or pin; and record.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show dependency, version, reason, advisory, license, graph, test, artifact, owner, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for security, license, release, and exception.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use lockfiles, provenance, compatibility, isolated build, SBOM, staged rollout, version pinning, rollback, provider substitution, and transitive impact visibility.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show current and target version, graph, findings, tests, evidence freshness, owner, rollout, and authorized approve, hold, update, rollback, pin, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Dependency-update charter; graph and version; advisory and license; build and test; rollout and rollback; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify dependency, current and target, source, graph, advisory, license, method, time, result, artifact, and limitation. Passing build proves neither license, security, nor runtime compatibility.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify graph, source, version, advisory, license, API, behavior, security, performance, build, artifact, rollout, rollback, and provider loss.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Automated merge, transitive impact missed, license conflict, advisory ignored, floating version, update without test, and build pass treated as safe deployment.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pin or roll back, quarantine artifact, preserve evidence, remediate or substitute, communicate, and reassess consumers. Reopen on advisory, version, license, provider, or behavior change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Graph, current and target, reason, tests, license, advisory, owners, rollout, evidence, recovery, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve graphs, versions, advisories, licenses, builds, tests, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Dependency, Security, Build, Package, Release, Testing, and Operations.
