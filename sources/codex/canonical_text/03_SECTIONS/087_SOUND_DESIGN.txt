SECTION 087 — Sound Design

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define the intentional use of sound, silence, speech, acoustics, and audio feedback to communicate, orient, warn, and support an experience.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

 - Scoped listener, environment, message, device, hearing access, cultural context, performance, and lifecycle.
 - Audio specifications, assets, provenance, alternatives, tests, controls, evidence, and handoff.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Sound can convey urgency and state but can also exclude, startle, distract, expose private information, or create physical risk. Audio quality is not comprehension.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to interfaces, games, products, vehicles, environments, alerts, media, accessibility cues, and systems where audio affects action or understanding.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No material audio message, alert, accessibility, privacy, safety, or interaction effect; or an unchanged sound baseline with impact recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Interaction and content requirements, audience and environment, devices, acoustics, hearing access, localization, privacy, safety, rights, and acceptance authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Interaction and content requirements, audience and environment, devices, acoustics, hearing access, localization, privacy, safety, rights, and acceptance authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve meaning, urgency, audience, accessibility, privacy, rights, safety, publication, and acceptance.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft audio, classify recordings, and propose cues. It must preserve provenance, consent, context, and uncertainty and must not publish or infer comprehension without review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Sound Designer, Voice Designer, UX and Interaction Designers, Researcher, Accessibility specialist, Content Designer, Tester, Verifier, Critic, Governance Reviewer, Implementer, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define audio purpose, hierarchy, timing, volume, spatialization, interruption, silence, captions or visual alternative, recording rights, environment, and tests.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show cue meaning, urgency, actor, privacy, consequence, volume, caption or visual alternative, and user control. AI-generated audio remains proposed.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Specify audio identity, format, loudness, latency, mixing, priority, spatial behavior, accessibility alternative, localization, caching, privacy, and degraded operation.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show sound baseline, cue, environment, device, audience, accessibility, privacy, evidence, and controls to mute, caption, replace, or restore.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Audio brief, cue catalog, assets and rights, specifications, captions or alternatives, accessibility and environmental tests, evidence, decisions, and handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify recording, source, rights, listener, environment, device, version, method, result, and limitation. A studio sample or loudness metric proves bounded quality only.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify meaning, audibility, non-audio alternative, privacy, startling risk, localization, latency, mixing, noisy environment, assistive use, and device variation.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Audio-only critical status; excessive loudness; unlicensed recordings; hidden privacy exposure; culturally misleading cues; and sound that cannot be paused or replaced.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Mute, caption, replace, or disable harmful cues, preserve state and evidence, communicate through alternate channel, and retest affected environments.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Purpose, cues, audience, alternatives, rights, accessibility, evidence, tests, recovery, open risks, and acceptance are explicit. Remain CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve assets, rights, cue IDs, versions, findings, decisions, evidence, alternatives, and next action. Hand off to Voice, UI, Interaction, Accessibility, Localization, Testing, and Operations.
