SECTION 195 — Network Security

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Protect network paths, boundaries, protocols, names, routing, wireless, remote access, and communications against unauthorized use, disclosure, disruption, and manipulation.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

Network assets, zones, flows, owners, identities, policy, encryption, monitoring, availability, segmentation, incident, recovery, and evidence are explicit.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Networks carry trust and control between otherwise protected components. A route, name, certificate, or management path can bypass local controls or make recovery impossible.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to enterprise, cloud, data center, wireless, edge, internet, operational, partner, service-mesh, and management networks and their protocols and providers.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Only an approved exact topology, flow, protocol, identity, policy, environment, and change baseline can justify non-applicability.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Sections 039-040, 045-046, 051-052, 193-206, 284, 301, 305, 313-318, and 326-330 provide network, security, identity, resilience, operations, observability, and recovery context.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Topology, zones, flows, protocols, addresses, names, devices, identities, keys, firewall policy, dependencies, traffic baselines, providers, threats, telemetry, and recovery paths.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Network owners approve topology, policy, privileged access, monitoring, exceptions, outage, segmentation, and recovery with security, privacy, safety, and operations review.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may map authorized flows, detect anomalies, and propose policy or tests. It cannot alter routes or rules, infer safe traffic, suppress alerts, or declare segmentation effective without evidence.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Network Architect, Network Engineer, Security Auditor, Identity Engineer, Analyst, Observer, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Red Team, Governance Reviewer, and Operations Engineer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Inventory zones, flows, protocols, names, devices, identities, and trust boundaries.
2. Define least-access policy, segmentation, encrypted transport, management isolation, logging, detection, and recovery.
3. Review changes and providers; test unauthorized paths, spoofing, denial, partition, failover, and restore.
4. Baseline evidence, exceptions, and downstream owner impact.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show observed traffic, inferred behavior, proposed policy, and enforced state separately. Human approval is required for broad rules, isolation, emergency access, and outage-risk changes.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use deny-by-default boundaries, authenticated protocols, segmentation, egress control, secure naming, independent management, resilient routing, time-bounded exceptions, and telemetry protected from tampering.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show topology, zones, flows, rules, identities, anomalies, dependencies, evidence freshness, blast radius, and authorized block, isolate, reroute, restore, or retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Network map, flow inventory, threat model, rule and identity matrix, protocol standards, monitoring design, change records, tests, incident and recovery evidence, exceptions, and handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify topology, rule version, capture period, population, method, tool, result, owner, and limits. A diagram, scan, or flow sample proves only its bounded view.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Exercise spoofing, unauthorized routes, lateral movement, DNS failure, certificate expiry, wireless compromise, partition, congestion, provider loss, failover, and evidence replay.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Flat networks, broad firewall rules, shared admin access, unmanaged wireless, unauthenticated protocols, hidden tunnels, no egress control, alert suppression, and recovery dependent on the failed network.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Contain or isolate paths, preserve captures and configuration, revoke credentials, invoke approved alternate communications, restore known-good policy, validate workloads, and reopen after topology or threat change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Flows, owners, policies, evidence, tests, recovery, exceptions, and acceptance are explicit; status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending independent review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve topology, rules, identities, providers, incidents, decisions, evidence, dissent, and next action. Hand off to Cloud, Platform, Infrastructure, Identity, Communications, Security Operations, Incident, Testing, and Recovery.
