SECTION 141 - Animation Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define governed engineering of time-varying visual, spatial, or state transitions with explicit timing, interpolation, interaction, accessibility, performance, provenance, and interruption behavior.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A baseline with animation state, timing, assets, controls, transitions, cancellation, reduced-motion behavior, device support, evidence, and lifecycle.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Animation communicates state and can cause motion harm, confusion, performance loss, or inaccessible interaction when timing and interruption are not designed.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to UI, games, film, visualization, avatars, motion graphics, simulations, and procedural or generated animation.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

May be non-applicable to static output with no temporal behavior, transition, sequence, or motion claim. Record scope, owner, evidence, and reopening trigger.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Requires motion purpose, state model, timing, input, assets, platform, reduced-motion, accessibility, performance, safety, and content-rights requirements.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Provide states, triggers, duration, easing, keyframes, procedural rules, interruption, reduced motion, input, device, and acceptance conditions.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve motion meaning, accessibility, safety, content, and release. Humans decide when motion is optional, prohibited, or consequential.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may propose sequences, identify timing defects, and generate tests. It must not ignore reduced-motion needs, copy protected style, or ship generated animation without review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Architect, Motion Designer, User Experience Reviewer, Accessibility specialist, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Critic, Governance Reviewer, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, and Red Team. Existing role contracts remain controlling.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Define motion purpose, state, trigger, timing, interruption, and audience.
2. Implement asset and procedural paths with reduced-motion and low-resource behavior.
3. Verify semantics, timing, accessibility, performance, input, cancellation, and recovery.
4. Release, monitor, and retire animation assets and rules.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show state, motion purpose, source, duration, user setting, evidence, and consequence. Human review is required for accessibility and safety; users must control or bypass nonessential motion.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Model animation state separately from business state; make triggers, cancellation, interruption, time scaling, reduced motion, frame loss, and resource fallback explicit.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show active animation, state, timing, reduced-motion setting, performance, errors, and controls to pause, skip, slow, disable, or restore.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Animation state and timing model, asset and provenance record, accessibility and reduced-motion plan, test fixtures, performance evidence, release record, and fallback guidance.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify animation, asset, build, state, device, setting, method, result, and limits. Separate timing, semantic, accessibility, performance, and user-outcome evidence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify state transitions, interruption, reduced motion, keyboard or alternative input, frame loss, low power, long duration, localization, and safe fallback.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Motion without purpose; no skip; flashing or vestibular risk; animation blocking state; timing tied to frame rate; and treating visual smoothness as usability.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Disable or reduce motion, preserve state, use a static representation, communicate status, compensate incomplete transitions, and reopen accessibility and safety review.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Purpose, state, timing, control, accessibility, evidence, fallback, and lifecycle are explicit. Keep CONTENT-COMPLETE until independent verification and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve animation and asset revisions, settings, tests, incidents, decisions, evidence, and next safe action. Hand off to Motion, UI, Game, Graphics, Accessibility, Testing, and Operations.
