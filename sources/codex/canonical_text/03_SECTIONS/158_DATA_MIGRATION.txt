SECTION 158 — Data Migration

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Move or transform data between versions, systems, stores, regions, or ownership boundaries without losing meaning, authority, integrity, continuity, or evidence.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A migration plan with inventory, mapping, reconciliation, controls, rehearsal, cutover, coexistence, rollback or compensation, validation, communications, and retirement.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Migration changes historical truth and future behavior. A completed transfer can still lose records, alter meaning, duplicate identity, or leave unauthorized residual copies.

APPLICABILITY

Apply to material movement, conversion, consolidation, separation, modernization, provider change, schema change, region change, or retention transition.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No material data, meaning, authority, continuity, lifecycle, or evidence changes; exact baseline covers it and the decision records why migration analysis is unnecessary.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Source and target models, inventories, mappings, owners, quality, access, retention, volume, downtime, recovery, legal, security, privacy, and acceptance authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Migration scope, records, schemas, mappings, transformations, counts, checksums, exceptions, cutover, coexistence, reconciliation, rollback, communications, and unknowns.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

The owner approves scope, timing, risk, downtime, mapping, cutover, residual copies, rollback or compensation, communications, and acceptance; assigns source, target, security, privacy, and operations authority.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze authorized migration inventories, draft mappings, transformations, rehearsal plans, test cases, reconciliation logic, cutover criteria, communications, and evidence packages. It must preserve provenance, source and target versions, exceptions, uncertainty, residual-copy implications, and the distinction between count, checksum, and semantic equivalence. It must not infer authority or equivalence, expose records, execute transfer, cutover, deletion, destructive cleanup, or compensation, or declare migration success without authorized validation. Any migration mutation or cutover requires the applicable policy evaluation, capability scope, immutable decision record, human approval, and append-only audit linkage; rollback must use an approved compensation path rather than destructive reversal.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Architect, Planner, Implementer, Analyst, Researcher, Tester, Verifier, Observer, Governance Reviewer, Security Auditor, Critic, Coordinator, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, and Red Team.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Inventory and classify; map and rehearse; validate quality; secure and transfer; reconcile; execute controlled cutover; preserve evidence; verify consumers; retire or quarantine the source.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

AI may draft mappings, scripts, test cases, and reconciliation queries from authorized data. It must not execute destructive migration, infer equivalence, expose records, or declare success from counts alone.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Preserve source, target, versions, mappings, lineage, checkpoints, idempotency, audit, compatibility, reconciliation, compensation, and residual-copy semantics. Prefer reversible stages and explicit cutover gates.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show migration baseline, phase, counts, exceptions, quality, owners, approvals, source and target state, cutover readiness, residual copies, and authorized pause, abort, reconcile, compensate, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Migration charter, inventory, mapping, transformation, rehearsal, security, runbook, reconciliation, cutover, validation, rollback or compensation, communication, evidence, and handoff records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify source, target, scope, version, population, method, counts, exceptions, environment, authority, result, and limitation. A checksum does not prove semantic equivalence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify inventory completeness, mapping, transformation, authorization, counts, checksums, semantics, referential integrity, performance, cutover, coexistence, recovery, and deletion of residual copies.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Big-bang cutover, untested rollback, hidden source writes, count-only validation, lost exceptions, duplicate identities, unbounded downtime, and destructive cleanup before acceptance.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause writes or isolate phases on mismatch; preserve both states and evidence. Use reconciliation, replay, prior version, coexistence, controlled forward repair, or compensation; never silently erase the source.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Scope, authority, mappings, tests, cutover, reconciliation, residual copies, evidence, gaps, and next decision are recorded. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending later gates.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Hand off to Database, Storage, Data Engineering, Integration, Application, Security, Privacy, Testing, Operations, Data Migration Operations, Retention, and Retirement.
