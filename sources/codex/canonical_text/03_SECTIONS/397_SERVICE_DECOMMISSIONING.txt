SECTION 397 — Service Decommissioning

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Stop and remove a service safely after users, data, dependencies, access, contracts, operations, and continuity obligations are resolved.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A service-decommissioning model with owner, consumers, replacement, dependencies, data, access, DNS, certificates, billing, support, records, shutdown, evidence, and recovery.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Service shutdown can strand users, leave credentials or routes active, lose data, break dependencies, or create a false completion claim. Decommissioning is a controlled lifecycle transition.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a service, API, application, platform capability, background job, endpoint, or customer-facing offering stops operating or accepting work.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no service, consumer, data, access, dependency, contract, or operating state ends and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use sunset, deprecation, account, data, records, DNS, certificate, billing, support, security, operations, and continuity baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Service, owner, consumers, replacement, endpoints, data, dependencies, access, DNS, certificates, billing, support, records, and shutdown plan.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Service owners approve shutdown, consumer migration, data retention, access revocation, communication, contract, billing, and final completion. Humans resolve exceptional users and dependencies.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inventory authorized consumers, dependencies, and endpoints and draft shutdown checklists. It must not disable service, revoke access, delete data, or declare complete without human authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Service Manager, Architect, Operations Engineer, Migration Engineer, Security Auditor, Data Engineer, Support Reviewer, Legal Advisor, Verifier, and Coordinator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define reason and scope; inventory consumers and dependencies; notify and migrate; close work; preserve or delete data; revoke access; remove endpoints, DNS, certificates, jobs, billing, monitoring; shut down; verify; and archive.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show service, consumers, dependencies, data, access, endpoints, authority, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for notice, disablement, deletion, access, billing, and final closure.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use dependency discovery, drain, queue closure, data export, access revocation, DNS and certificate cleanup, billing state, monitoring retirement, and rollback or recovery window.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show service phase, consumers, dependencies, data, access, endpoints, support, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized drain, migrate, pause, disable, preserve, and close controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Decommissioning charter; consumer and dependency map; migration and communication; data and access; shutdown and verification; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify service, version, consumer, dependency, endpoint, data, access, method, time, result, owner, and limitation. Shutdown command proves neither consumer migration nor cleanup.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify consumer inventory, drain, replacement, data, access, DNS, certificates, billing, jobs, monitoring, support, records, security, and no unintended endpoint.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Shutdown before migration, hidden consumer, active credential, DNS or certificate left, data deleted early, monitoring gap, billing continues, and completion by process stop.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause shutdown, restore or reopen service where possible, protect data, communicate, migrate, revoke, and reassess. Reopen on consumer, dependency, data, contract, or incident discovery.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Service, consumers, dependencies, migration, data, access, endpoints, support, evidence, owners, cleanup, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve service inventory, migrations, shutdown, access, incidents, communications, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Infrastructure, Data, DNS, Certificates, Billing, Support, Security, and System Retirement.
