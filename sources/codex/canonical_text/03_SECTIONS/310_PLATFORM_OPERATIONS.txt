SECTION 310 — Platform Operations

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Operate shared platforms, control planes, clusters, services, guardrails, self-service paths, resources, and support so consumer workloads remain secure, reliable, and governable.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A platform-operations model with capabilities, consumers, ownership, health, access, capacity, cost, policy, change, support, incidents, recovery, and retirement.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Platform failure has multiplier effect. A control-plane outage, bad template, policy error, capacity issue, or hidden dependency can affect many teams and make local recovery impossible.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a shared platform or service is provisioned, changed, monitored, supported, secured, recovered, or retired.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no shared platform, consumer, guardrail, resource, service, or operational behavior changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use platform architecture, cloud, infrastructure, identity, security, configuration, DevOps, SRE, support, cost, and recovery baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Platform capabilities, consumers, services, resources, policy, access, health, capacity, cost, incidents, support, dependencies, and recovery.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Platform owners approve service, guardrails, access, change, cost, support, incident, recovery, exceptions, and retirement. Consumers retain workload responsibility and escalation rights.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze authorized platform state, consumer signals, and incidents; draft runbooks; and recommend bounded operations. It must not change shared policy, grant access, provision, or hide consumer impact without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Platform Operations Engineer, Platform Engineer, SRE, Security Auditor, Cloud Engineer, Support Reviewer, Incident Responder, Tester, Verifier, and Coordinator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Operate platform services; monitor health; manage access and capacity; support consumers; deploy and change; handle incidents; maintain guardrails; recover; communicate; and retire or migrate capabilities.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show platform, capability, consumer, policy, resource, action, evidence, authority, and limitation. Require human approval for platform-wide, privileged, destructive, cost, and recovery actions.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use tenant isolation, policy, service boundaries, self-service guardrails, control-plane redundancy, observability, backup, rollback, escape hatch, and consumer impact visibility.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show platform health, consumers, resources, capacity, cost, policy, incidents, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized restrict, scale, rollback, recover, migrate, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Platform operations charter; capability and consumer map; health and support; access, capacity, cost, incident, recovery, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify capability, platform, consumer, resource, policy, method, time, result, cost, owner, and limitation. Platform health proves neither consumer outcome nor workload correctness.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify platform service, consumer isolation, access, guardrails, capacity, cost, support, observability, change, rollback, recovery, and provider loss.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Shared control plane without recovery, guardrail drift, overprivileged support, consumer impact hidden, platform metric theater, no escape hatch, and platform outage treated as consumer fault.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stabilize platform, protect consumers, preserve evidence, restore or fail over, provide alternate path, communicate, and reassess. Reopen on capability, consumer, policy, provider, or architecture change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Platform, capabilities, consumers, owners, policy, health, capacity, cost, evidence, support, recovery, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve platform state, consumer map, policies, incidents, changes, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Platform Engineering, SRE, Security, Cloud, Support, Governance, and Operations.
