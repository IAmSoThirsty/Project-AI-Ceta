SECTION 159 — Data Warehousing

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Design governed analytical stores that preserve historical meaning, dimensional context, lineage, quality, access, performance, retention, and decision evidence.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A warehouse model with source contracts, grain, dimensions, facts, history, refresh, quality, lineage, security, ownership, recovery, and lifecycle controls.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Warehouses can make stale or transformed data appear authoritative. A report that runs successfully does not prove its grain, lineage, freshness, or interpretation.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when historical, integrated, subject-oriented, or decision-support data is consolidated for reporting, analysis, compliance, or operations.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No material analytical meaning, source, grain, refresh, access, quality, retention, or consumer behavior changes and an exact baseline covers the work; record the reason.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Business definitions, source systems, grain, history, dimensions, measures, refresh, quality, lineage, users, access, retention, performance, and acceptance authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Source inventory, model, mappings, snapshots, schedules, late data, corrections, consumers, reports, controls, privacy, security, recovery, assumptions, and gaps.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

The owner approves definitions, metrics, sources, refresh, access, quality, retention, exceptions, and acceptance; assigns data, reporting, privacy, security, and operations authority.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inspect authorized schemas, lineage, load histories, quality results, query plans, metric definitions, and warehouse metadata; identify inconsistencies; draft mappings, tests, documentation, and bounded analysis; and propose model, refresh, quality, access, cost, or recovery improvements.

AI must distinguish source facts, transformed values, inferred relationships, metric definitions, recommendations, and verified results. It must preserve grain, time window, source identity, transformation version, freshness, privacy classification, and known limitations in every material output.

AI must not silently redefine a metric, merge incompatible grains, infer sensitive attributes, publish consequential reports, change production schemas or loads, widen access, delete history, approve retention exceptions, or declare data quality or business truth without delegated authority and human acceptance.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Architect, Analyst, Researcher, Data Engineer, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Governance Reviewer, Security Auditor, Observer, Critic, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, and Red Team.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define grain and history; map sources; build conformed dimensions and facts; establish refresh, quality, lineage, access, reconciliation, observability, recovery, and consumer validation.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

AI may analyze authorized warehouse metadata and propose models or queries. It must preserve metric definitions and provenance and must not publish consequential reports or infer authority.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate source, staging, warehouse, mart, cache, report, and evidence. Preserve slowly changing history, late-arriving data, correction, reconciliation, access, retention, and reproducible query semantics.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show warehouse version, grain, freshness, source status, quality, metric owner, consumers, access, costs, incidents, evidence, and pause, correct, restrict, restore, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Warehouse charter; dimensional and lineage models; metric catalog; source and refresh contracts; quality, access, retention, recovery, report, test, decision, evidence, and handoff records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify source, grain, transformation, window, refresh, model, query, environment, actor, result, and limitation. A report or row count proves only its declared slice.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify grain, joins, history, late data, refresh, reconciliation, access, performance, report definitions, correction, recovery, retention, and independent reproduction.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Metric drift, double counting, stale dimensions, hidden filters, untracked corrections, unrestricted marts, snapshot confusion, and dashboards presented as decisions or truth.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Quarantine affected loads or reports, preserve versions and evidence, notify consumers, and use approved correction, backfill, prior snapshot, restricted mode, or compensation.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Definitions, grain, sources, owners, refresh, quality, access, recovery, tests, evidence, gaps, and review cadence are recorded. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Hand off models and metric contracts to Data Engineering, Data Quality, BI, Analytics, Database, Storage, Governance, Security, Testing, Operations, and Migration.
