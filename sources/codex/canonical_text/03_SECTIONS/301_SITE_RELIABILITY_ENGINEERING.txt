SECTION 301 — Site Reliability Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Engineer and operate reliable services through service-level objectives, error budgets, automation, observability, incident learning, capacity, and sustainable human work.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An SRE model with service ownership, user outcomes, indicators, objectives, budgets, alerts, runbooks, toil, incidents, reliability work, capacity, and recovery.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Reliability requires tradeoffs between change and stability, and between automation and human judgment. Uptime alone can hide correctness, latency, safety, privacy, support, or operator harm.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a team owns ongoing service reliability, availability, performance, resilience, incident response, capacity, or operational improvement.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no ongoing service, operator, reliability, or operational outcome exists and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use operational architecture, service levels, observability, incident, capacity, performance, reliability, continuity, security, support, and staffing baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Service, users, critical journeys, indicators, objectives, budget, dependencies, alerts, runbooks, incidents, capacity, toil, staffing, and recovery.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Service owners define user outcomes, objectives, budgets, risk, on-call, change, incident, and staffing. Humans decide when to spend budget, pause change, accept risk, or protect operators.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may correlate authorized telemetry, summarize incidents, draft runbooks, identify toil, and recommend bounded action. It must not suppress alerts, change production, close incidents, or accept reliability risk without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Site Reliability Engineer, Architect, Operations Engineer, Observer, Analyst, Incident Responder, Capacity Engineer, Tester, Verifier, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define user outcomes and SLOs; instrument; alert; operate; respond; manage error budget; plan capacity; reduce toil; test failure and recovery; review changes; learn from incidents; and improve.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show service, journey, indicator, objective, budget, evidence, incident, action, authority, and uncertainty. Require human control for production and incident decisions and preserve on-call stop and escalation.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use meaningful indicators, multi-window alerts, runbooks, automation with guardrails, graceful degradation, redundancy, rollback, capacity, dependency visibility, and independent recovery.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show service health, user outcome, SLO, budget, incidents, dependencies, toil, capacity, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized pause, rollback, scale, escalate, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

SRE charter; service and ownership map; SLI/SLO and budget; alert and runbook; capacity and toil; incident, recovery, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify service, journey, indicator, objective, period, source, method, result, uncertainty, owner, and limitation. Availability or dashboard health proves only the measured slice.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify indicators, objectives, alerts, runbooks, on-call, error budget, capacity, dependency, incident, recovery, automation, and operator workload. Exercise failures and false signals.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Uptime-only SLO, alert fatigue, budget ignored, toil hidden, automation without rollback, on-call burnout, incident blame, and service owner without authority.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stabilize, preserve evidence, reduce change, fail over or roll back, communicate, protect operators, and reassess user outcome and SLO. Reopen on service, dependency, workload, or objective change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Service, user outcome, indicators, objectives, budget, owners, alerts, runbooks, incidents, capacity, evidence, workload, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve SLO history, runbooks, telemetry, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, toil, capacity, and open gaps. Hand off to Production, Operations, Incident, Capacity, Reliability, Support, and Governance.
