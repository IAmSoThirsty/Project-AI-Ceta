SECTION 129 - Integration Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define governed engineering of connections among systems, services, people, devices, data, organizations, and suppliers, including transformation, orchestration, synchronization, identity, failure, evidence, and lifecycle.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An integration baseline with participants, contracts, mappings, ownership, ordering, reliability, security, observability, reconciliation, migration, and retirement conditions.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Integration defects cross boundaries and amplify quickly. Missing ownership or reconciliation can create duplicate effects, inconsistent records, silent loss, and impossible recovery.

APPLICABILITY

Applies whenever two or more systems, services, teams, suppliers, devices, data stores, or processes exchange information, commands, events, or responsibility.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

May be non-applicable to isolated computation with no boundary exchange or shared lifecycle consequence. Record scope, owner, evidence, and reopening trigger.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Requires participant inventory, domain and data contracts, identity, trust boundaries, mappings, timing, failure and reconciliation policy, ownership, tests, observability, and change authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Provide source and target, purpose, data and commands, schemas, transformations, frequency, ordering, duplicates, retries, security, privacy, reconciliation, recovery, and retirement needs.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve cross-boundary authority, data use, side effects, supplier commitments, risk, release, exceptions, and retirement. Humans decide reconciliation outcomes and material compensation.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may map schemas, identify dependency gaps, draft adapters, analyze traces, and suggest reconciliation. It must not execute external mutations, approve mappings, or suppress mismatches without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Architect, Analyst, Researcher, Security Auditor, Privacy Reviewer, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Governance Reviewer, Observer, Coordinator, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, Critic, and Red Team. Existing role contracts remain controlling.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Inventory participants, ownership, trust, data, commands, events, and boundaries.
2. Define contracts, mappings, transformation, ordering, delivery, retry, idempotency, reconciliation, and evidence.
3. Implement with isolation, observability, staged migration, and tested compensation.
4. Reconcile, monitor, change, deprecate, and retire integrations with consumer notice.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show source, target, identity, authority, payload, transformation, side effect, status, evidence, and reconciliation state. Human review is required for mappings and consequential writes; chat cannot bypass boundary controls.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Model synchronous and asynchronous paths, queues, schemas, adapters, identity, secrets, transactions, retries, deduplication, ordering, dead letters, reconciliation, tracing, and compensation.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show integration health, lag, failures, duplicates, dead letters, mismatches, credentials, versions, data freshness, incidents, and controls to pause, replay, reconcile, isolate, or compensate.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Integration catalog, context and sequence models, contracts and mappings, trust and data-flow assessment, runbooks, reconciliation design, test evidence, migration plan, and retirement record.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify participants, contract versions, message or request identity, data, transformation, environment, trace, result, date, and limitations. Preserve successful, failed, duplicate, delayed, and unmatched evidence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify delivery, ordering, duplicate and replay handling, partial failure, timeout, dead letter, schema change, credential loss, reconciliation, backfill, migration, audit, and compensation.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Point-to-point sprawl; silent transformation; no reconciliation; retrying non-idempotent effects; shared credentials; ignoring dead letters; and treating message delivery as business completion.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause affected flow, preserve queues and evidence, isolate bad data, revoke credentials, reconcile against authoritative records, compensate external effects, and reopen mappings and contracts.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Participants, contracts, mappings, controls, evidence, reconciliation, recovery, ownership, change, and retirement are explicit. Keep CONTENT-COMPLETE until independent verification and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve participant and contract identity, mapping versions, traces, queues, incidents, decisions, evidence, and next safe action. Hand off to Integration Architecture, API, Event, Data, Security, Testing, Operations, and Migration sections.
