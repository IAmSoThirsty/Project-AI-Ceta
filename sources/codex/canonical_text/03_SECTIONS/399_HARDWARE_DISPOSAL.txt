SECTION 399 — Hardware Disposal

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Retire, transfer, reuse, recycle, or dispose of hardware while protecting data, keys, identity, safety, environmental obligations, chain of custody, and evidence.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A hardware-disposal model with asset identity, owner, data, keys, wipe or destruction, chain of custody, vendor, condition, reuse, environmental method, certificate, and verification.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Hardware can retain data and trust after service ends. Informal disposal can expose people and organizations, create environmental harm, or leave an asset appearing active.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when hardware, device, media, component, battery, appliance, or equipment is sold, returned, reused, recycled, destroyed, or abandoned.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no hardware, media, data, key, identity, custody, or environmental disposition changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use device provisioning, hardware security, keys, secrets, records, privacy, legal, logistics, secure destruction, environmental, and inventory baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Asset, serial, owner, data, keys, identity, condition, disposition, wipe, destruction, vendor, custody, certificate, and environmental requirement.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners approve disposition, data and key treatment, transfer, reuse, vendor, destruction, environmental method, and certificate. Humans verify custody and final status.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inventory authorized assets and metadata and draft disposal checklists. It must not wipe, expose, transfer, destroy, or certify hardware without authority and controlled procedure.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Hardware Engineer, Asset Manager, Security Auditor, Key Manager, Logistics Manager, Environmental Reviewer, Records Manager, Verifier, and Governance Reviewer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Identify and secure asset; revoke identity; handle data and keys; choose disposition; wipe or destroy; transfer custody; verify; obtain certificate; reconcile inventory; and record environmental outcome.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show asset, serial, owner, data, identity, method, custody, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for wipe, key destruction, transfer, resale, destruction, and environmental disposal.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use asset identity, inventory, secure wipe, key destruction, chain of custody, vendor control, certificate, access revocation, and environmental traceability.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show asset, status, data, key, identity, custody, disposition, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized quarantine, wipe, transfer, destroy, recycle, and close controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Disposal charter; asset and data; identity and key; method and custody; vendor and certificate; environmental, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify asset, serial, data, key reference, owner, method, vendor, custody, time, result, certificate, and limitation. Vendor receipt proves only its stated scope.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify asset, identity revocation, data and key treatment, wipe or destruction, custody, vendor, certificate, inventory, environmental method, and no active access.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Hardware sold with data, key retained, identity active, asset untracked, vendor certificate vague, disposal without hold, and recycling treated as secure destruction.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stop transfer, preserve custody evidence, recover asset where possible, revoke identity, rotate keys, notify owners, and reassess exposure. Reopen on asset discovery, hold, vendor, or method failure.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Asset, owner, data, keys, identity, custody, disposition, method, evidence, certificate, environmental result, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending qualified acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve asset, wipe, custody, vendor, certificate, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Secure Destruction, Environmental Disposal, Records, Security, Logistics, and System Retirement.
