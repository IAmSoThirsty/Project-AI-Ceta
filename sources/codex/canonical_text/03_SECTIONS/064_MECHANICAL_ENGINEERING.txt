SECTION 064 — Mechanical Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define the governed engineering of structures, mechanisms, materials, motion, loads, interfaces, enclosures, thermal paths, manufacturability, serviceability, and physical safety for a bounded system across its lifecycle.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A traceable mechanical design with loads, materials, tolerances, joints, motion limits, interfaces, environmental ratings, human access, hazard controls, manufacturing, inspection, maintenance, recovery, and evidence for the declared use conditions.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Mechanical defects can cause collapse, pinch, impact, leakage, overheating, loss of alignment, unsafe motion, or inaccessible maintenance. A CAD model or static calculation does not prove behavior under fatigue, abuse, tolerance, environment, assembly variation, or field repair.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to structures, mechanisms, enclosures, mounts, rotating or moving parts, thermal and fluid interfaces, materials, packaging, tooling, facilities, devices, vehicles, and physical service environments.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Non-applicability requires no material load, motion, material, enclosure, thermal, fluid, vibration, human-access, manufacturing, service, safety, or lifecycle effect, with baseline, authority, evidence, and reopening trigger recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Functional and load requirements; operating modes; materials; geometry; tolerances; environments; interfaces; human factors; hazards; thermal, vibration, corrosion, fluid, acoustic, manufacturing, assembly, maintenance, accessibility, transport, and disposal constraints. Sections 019-063 provide upstream context.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Boundary, loads, motion, materials, geometry, interfaces, tolerances, environmental profile, human access, hazards, thermal and fluid conditions, manufacturing process, inspection, transport, maintenance, recovery, and disposal constraints.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

The human mechanical authority approves design basis, materials, safety factors, critical tolerances, prototypes, substitutions, field modifications, hazard acceptance, manufacturing release, and final acceptance. Human operators decide safe use, isolation, repair, and retirement.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze CAD metadata, load cases, tolerances, simulations, inspection records, and alternatives; draft calculations and tests; and identify inconsistencies. It must preserve units and assumptions and must not certify structural safety, invent material properties, or authorize operation.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Architect, Researcher, Analyst, Critic, Implementer, Refactorer, Tester, Verifier, Security Auditor, Red Team, Planner, Coordinator, Observer, Governance Reviewer, Documentation Writer, Optimizer, User Experience Reviewer, and Memory Curator under existing role contracts.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Establish boundary, loads, motions, environments, users, operators, hazards, production, service, and lifecycle.
2. Define geometry, materials, interfaces, joints, tolerances, fits, fasteners, seals, thermal paths, and motion limits.
3. Analyze static, dynamic, fatigue, fracture, buckling, vibration, shock, wear, corrosion, thermal, fluid, acoustic, and abuse cases as applicable.
4. Allocate physical interfaces and controls to electrical, electronic, software, human, facility, and maintenance boundaries.
5. Review manufacturability, assembly, inspection, transport, accessibility, maintenance, replacement, recovery, and disposal.
6. Validate with qualified specialists; baseline calculations, drawings, prototypes, tests, deviations, and evidence.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

AI-generated geometry, material selections, simulations, and optimization remain proposals. Human review must inspect loads, assumptions, margins, manufacturing and field conditions, and affected users. AI must not approve lifting, motion, pressure, structural, or safety operation; stop and manual inspection remain available.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Represent geometry, materials, loads, joints, interfaces, tolerances, motions, hazards, environmental ratings, manufacturing revisions, installed configuration, inspection, maintenance, and lifecycle as controlled artifacts. Separate CAD intent, manufactured part, assembled unit, measured state, and field evidence.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show asset identity, configuration, load or motion state, guards, temperature, pressure, alarms, inspection, maintenance, deviations, evidence, and authorized stop, isolate, repair, recover, and retire actions. Keep manual release, emergency stop, and physical access controls visible.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Design basis; drawings and CAD; material and process register; load and tolerance analyses; hazard assessment; interface control; prototype and inspection plans; manufacturing and assembly instructions; maintenance and recovery procedures; test and deviation records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Evidence identifies part or assembly revision, material batch, manufacturing process, inspection instrument, calibration, load, environment, setup, result, reviewer, and limitations. Simulation, supplier data, inspection, and destructive testing are distinct.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify dimensions, materials, interfaces, fit, motion, load, fatigue, vibration, thermal, wear, corrosion, guards, pinch and impact hazards, pressure or fluid containment, assembly variation, maintainability, and recovery under declared conditions.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Uncontrolled material or fastener substitution; missing fatigue or tolerance analysis; unguarded motion; inaccessible maintenance; hidden corrosion; CAD-only acceptance; ignored assembly variation; and recovery dependent on unsafe access or failed controls.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stop motion or isolate energy, secure the area, preserve configuration and evidence, identify the hazard and authority, and use approved inspection, repair, replacement, containment, rollback, or redesign. Reopen after load, material, geometry, manufacturing, environment, incident, or evidence change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Loads, materials, geometry, interfaces, hazards, manufacturing, inspection, service, lifecycle, tests, evidence, owner, and gaps are explicit for the declared use; no safety or durability claim exceeds verified conditions.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve drawings, CAD revision, material and batch data, as-built state, inspections, failures, maintenance, decisions, evidence, and next action. Hand off to Electrical, Electronics, Robotics, Embedded, Hardware Testing, Manufacturing, Safety, Reliability, Operations, and Disposal.
