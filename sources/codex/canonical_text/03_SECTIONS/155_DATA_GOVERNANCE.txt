SECTION 155 — Data Governance

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Govern the purpose, ownership, authority, access, quality, sharing, retention, correction, and retirement of data across its lifecycle.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A data-governance operating model with accountable owners, classifications, policies, decision rights, exceptions, evidence, review cadence, and enforcement paths.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Data governance determines who may use data and why. Unowned classification, undocumented purpose, or unenforced policy converts technical convenience into unauthorized consequence.

APPLICABILITY

Apply whenever data affects rights, decisions, safety, privacy, security, compliance, service, finance, models, records, or material operations.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

A bounded non-material change has no effect on purpose, ownership, access, classification, quality, retention, sharing, or lifecycle; record the exact baseline and reopening trigger.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Authority, purpose, data inventory, owners, subjects, classifications, jurisdictions, policies, contracts, retention, access, quality, risk, and acceptance rules.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Data domains, purposes, lawful or organizational basis, owners, custodians, consumers, classifications, access roles, decisions, exceptions, incidents, evidence, and unknowns.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

The human owner appoints data owners and stewards, resolves conflicting rights and purposes, approves policy, exceptions, sharing, retention, deletion, risk, and acceptance. Authority is not inferred from technical access.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inventory authorized data, compare governance sources, identify missing ownership or control evidence, and draft classifications, policies, registers, decision records, and review questions. It must preserve provenance and uncertainty, distinguish recommendation from authorization, and use only approved sources. It must not infer lawful basis, ownership, consent, access rights, retention authority, or policy exceptions; approve sharing or access; alter authoritative records or controls; or conceal conflicts, dissent, or unresolved gaps. Any governance mutation or consequential recommendation requires the applicable policy evaluation, capability scope, immutable decision record, human authority, and append-only audit linkage before execution or acceptance.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Governance Reviewer, Architect, Researcher, Analyst, Security Auditor, Verifier, Tester, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, Critic, User Experience Reviewer, Implementer, and Red Team.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Inventory data and assign ownership, purpose, classification, and lifecycle.
2. Define policy, access, quality, sharing, retention, correction, deletion, exception, and escalation rules.
3. Bind rules to systems, contracts, evidence, monitoring, reviews, and enforcement points.
4. Review changes and incidents; record decisions, dissent, evidence, and reopening conditions.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

AI may inventory, compare, and draft governance artifacts from authorized sources. It must not infer lawful basis, approve access, classify sensitive data conclusively, waive policy, or conceal uncertainty.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Represent policies, owners, classifications, permissions, decisions, exceptions, evidence, expiries, and supersession as immutable attributable records with enforceable controls and append-only audit.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show purpose, owner, classification, authority, policy status, expiry, consumers, exceptions, evidence, and controls to approve, deny, restrict, revoke, quarantine, correct, retain, or delete.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Governance charter; data inventory; ownership and classification register; policy and decision records; access and exception register; retention and deletion records; audit, evidence, review, and handoff package.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Each decision identifies authority, scope, version, purpose, subjects, policy, evidence, date, expiry, dissent, and limitation. A policy document alone does not prove enforcement.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify ownership, purpose, classification, access, policy enforcement, exception expiry, audit integrity, subject rights, retention, deletion, and independent decision reproduction.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Implied consent, ownerless data, blanket access, policy without enforcement, permanent exceptions, hidden secondary use, mutable audit, and treating a dashboard or attestation as compliance proof.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Suspend affected use on authority, classification, policy, access, or evidence conflict. Preserve records; use restriction, quarantine, human adjudication, correction, revocation, or compensation, then review downstream effects.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Scope, authority, owners, policies, controls, evidence, decisions, exceptions, review, gaps, and next action are recorded. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending reconciliation and acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Hand off enforceable rules and records to Data, Security, Privacy, Legal, Records, Application, Testing, Operations, AI Governance, and Retirement sections with reopening triggers.
