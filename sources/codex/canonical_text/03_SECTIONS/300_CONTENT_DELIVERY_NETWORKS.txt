SECTION 300 — Content Delivery Networks

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Deliver cached or proxied content with controlled origin, identity, cache, integrity, privacy, availability, invalidation, observability, and recovery.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A CDN model with content, origin, audience, cache policy, headers, certificates, access, invalidation, regions, provider, metrics, cost, and rollback.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Caching changes what users receive and for how long. Stale, private, wrong-region, unauthorized, or poisoned content can persist after origin correction and may be hard to withdraw.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a CDN, edge cache, reverse proxy, or content acceleration service stores or serves content, APIs, data, or artifacts.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no cache, origin, content, access, certificate, invalidation, or delivery behavior changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use content, domain, certificate, network, privacy, security, artifact, release, distribution, and operations baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Content, origin, cache key, TTL, headers, audience, identity, region, certificate, invalidation, provider, metrics, cost, and recovery.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Owners approve origin, content, cache, private-data handling, access, provider, invalidation, cost, and emergency purge. Humans accept stale and provider risk.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze authorized CDN metadata, cache behavior, headers, and metrics; draft policy and tests; and identify anomalies. It must not publish, purge, change origin, expose content, or alter cache policy without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

CDN Engineer, Network Engineer, Cloud Engineer, Security Auditor, Release Engineer, Content Engineer, Tester, Verifier, and Operations Engineer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define content and origin; design cache and access; configure certificates and headers; deploy; validate; monitor; invalidate; handle origin or provider failure; control cost; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show content, origin, cache, audience, region, identity, certificate, action, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for private content, origin, purge, routing, and production change.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use correct cache keys, private-content isolation, signed or authenticated requests, origin protection, TTL, purge, versioned URLs, integrity, WAF or rate controls, and fallback.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show origin, cache health, content version, freshness, regions, access, incidents, evidence freshness, cost, owner, and authorized purge, route, restrict, rollback, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

CDN charter; origin and cache model; content and access policy; certificate and header; metrics and purge; incident, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify content, origin, cache key, region, request, policy, method, time, result, actor, and limitation. Cache hit or latency proves neither freshness nor authorization.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify cache key, privacy, headers, access, integrity, TTL, invalidation, certificate, origin failover, regions, cost, and recovery. Test stale and poisoned content.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Private data cached publicly, stale security content, purge not tested, origin exposed, cache poisoning, wildcard policy, and performance treated as delivery correctness.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Purge or restrict affected content, protect origin, preserve evidence, restore or replace, rotate credentials, notify consumers, and reassess copies. Reopen on content, origin, provider, policy, or certificate change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Origin, content, cache, access, identity, regions, certificates, freshness, purge, evidence, recovery, owners, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve content and cache policy, origins, certificates, purges, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Distribution, DNS, Network, Security, Release, Cloud, and Operations.
