SECTION 146 - Search Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define governed engineering of systems that ingest, index, retrieve, rank, filter, explain, and correct information under explicit relevance, authority, access, freshness, provenance, and user-control constraints.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A search baseline with sources, indexing, schemas, ranking, permissions, freshness, recall and precision evidence, explanations, abuse controls, and retirement.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Search can omit, expose, distort, or stale information. Ranking and relevance metrics can hide minority needs, unauthorized disclosure, or poor task outcomes.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to keyword, semantic, federated, enterprise, media, code, geospatial, log, and AI-assisted search systems.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

May be non-applicable to systems with no retrieval, discovery, ranking, filtering, or search-dependent decision. Record scope, owner, evidence, and reopening trigger.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Requires information architecture, source ownership, schemas, permissions, indexing, ranking objectives, freshness, evaluation sets, privacy, security, accessibility, and correction authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Provide queries, users, sources, relevance goals, filters, ranking, access, freshness, synonyms, language, citations, latency, feedback, and failure requirements.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve source inclusion, access, ranking objectives, protected populations, evaluation, publication, and acceptance. Humans decide disputes over relevance, omission, and disclosure.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may propose ranking, summarize results, classify sources, and generate queries. It must preserve permissions and citations, expose uncertainty, and never equate rank with truth.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Architect, Information Architect, Researcher, Analyst, Security Auditor, Privacy Reviewer, User Experience Reviewer, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Governance Reviewer, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, and Red Team. Existing role contracts remain controlling.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Define users, sources, scope, access, vocabulary, relevance, freshness, and lifecycle.
2. Design ingest, index, query, ranking, filtering, explanation, correction, and deletion paths.
3. Evaluate recall, precision, task success, accessibility, privacy, security, latency, and bias.
4. Monitor drift, feedback, abuse, and source change; correct and retire safely.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show query, user, source, ranking, permission, timestamp, citation, uncertainty, and evidence. Human review is required for access, ranking changes, sensitive sources, and generated results.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate source, index, query, ranker, filter, cache, authorization, result, citation, and feedback boundaries. Enforce access before ranking or caching and preserve deletion propagation.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show index version, freshness, source coverage, query, results, ranking rationale, access decisions, quality metrics, incidents, and controls to reindex, restrict, correct, or roll back.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Search charter, source and permission catalog, schema and index design, ranking policy, evaluation sets, relevance and bias evidence, provenance and correction model, runbook, and retirement record.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify source, index, query, ranker, user context, permissions, timestamp, method, result, and limits. Separate retrieval, ranking, citation, task success, fairness, and security evidence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify access isolation, freshness, deletion, ranking, recall, precision, synonyms, language, adversarial queries, prompt injection, cache, source loss, accessibility, and recovery.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Ranking by engagement only; indexing unauthorized data; stale caches; missing citations; hiding no-result states; evaluation on convenient queries; and treating relevance as truth.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Restrict or disable affected index or ranker, preserve source and query evidence, reindex from authority, correct results, notify users, compensate decisions, and reopen evaluation and access review.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Sources, permissions, ranking, freshness, evidence, accessibility, correction, recovery, and lifecycle are explicit. Keep CONTENT-COMPLETE until independent verification and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve source and index identity, evaluation sets, queries, decisions, traces, incidents, evidence, and next safe action. Hand off to Information, Knowledge, AI, Data, Privacy, Security, Testing, and Operations.
