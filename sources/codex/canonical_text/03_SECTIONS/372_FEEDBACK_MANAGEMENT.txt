SECTION 372 — Feedback Management

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Collect, protect, understand, prioritize, respond to, and learn from feedback, complaints, requests, observations, and dissent across the lifecycle.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A feedback model with source, participant, context, consent, topic, severity, triage, owner, response, action, evidence, privacy, and closure or reopening.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Feedback is signal, not automatic truth or noise. Ignoring dissent, over-weighting loud voices, exposing reporters, or closing without response damages product and trust.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when feedback from users, customers, operators, communities, staff, suppliers, tests, incidents, or stakeholders informs decisions or improvement.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no feedback, participant, issue, decision, or improvement workflow changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use research, support, customer success, community, trust and safety, privacy, analytics, product, governance, and records baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Feedback, source, participant, context, consent, issue, severity, topic, evidence, owner, response, action, and retention.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Feedback owners protect participants, triage fairly, respond, escalate harm, and close transparently. Humans decide priority, action, compensation, and interpretation.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may classify authorized feedback, summarize themes, identify contradictions, and draft response or research questions. It must not expose participants, infer intent, erase dissent, promise action, or close high-impact feedback without review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Researcher, User Experience Reviewer, Customer Support, Community Manager, Product Manager, Analyst, Privacy Reviewer, Trust and Safety Reviewer, Verifier, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Collect and protect; authenticate or anonymize as needed; classify; triage; acknowledge; investigate; respond; route; act; communicate disposition; analyze trends; and retain or delete.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show source, context, participant protection, theme, evidence, action, owner, and limitation. Require human review for harm, privacy, public response, policy, and closure.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Provide consent, identity or anonymity, secure intake, case and topic, deduplication, provenance, response, appeal, access, retention, and feedback-to-decision traceability.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show feedback queue, topic, severity, source, owner, response, evidence freshness, privacy, action, and authorized assign, protect, respond, escalate, close, and reopen controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Feedback charter; source and participant; taxonomy and triage; response and action; trend and dissent; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify feedback, source, participant or segment, context, method, time, theme, action, result, and limitation. Volume or sentiment proves only a bounded signal.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify intake, privacy, accessibility, classification, triage, response, escalation, action, dissent, retention, and user understanding. Test abusive, duplicate, anonymous, and conflicting feedback.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Feedback theater, reporter exposure, loudest voice, sentiment as truth, no response, closure by categorization, and feedback collected without action owner.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Protect participant, correct record, respond, escalate, preserve evidence, remedy harm, and reassess decision. Reopen on new feedback, harm, population, product, or policy change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Source, participant, context, privacy, taxonomy, triage, owner, response, evidence, action, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve feedback, participant protections, responses, actions, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Product, Research, Support, Community, Analytics, Privacy, and Governance.
