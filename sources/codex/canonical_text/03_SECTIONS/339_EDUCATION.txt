SECTION 339 — Education

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Build durable understanding, judgment, and shared language about a domain, system, practice, or capability beyond one immediate task.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An education model with audience, learning purpose, concepts, sources, progression, inquiry, practice, accessibility, assessment, feedback, and continuity.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Education shapes future decisions and agency. Simplified material can become misleading doctrine, while inaccessible or one-sided instruction can reproduce blind spots.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when the goal is durable conceptual understanding, professional development, public literacy, community capability, or shared practice.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no learning, shared understanding, public or professional capability, or decision quality outcome changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use research, domain analysis, ethics, accessibility, documentation, training, legal, source, and knowledge baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Audience, goals, concepts, source basis, perspectives, sequence, examples, practice, assessment, accessibility, instructor, feedback, and limitations.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Educators and subject experts choose sources, represent uncertainty, protect learners, and assess understanding. Humans decide what is contested, required, or suitable for a population.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may explain approved concepts, generate examples, compare perspectives, and adapt practice. It must not fabricate sources, erase disagreement, certify understanding, or teach unsafe or contested claims as settled fact.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Teacher, Researcher, Domain Analyst, Documentation Writer, User Experience Reviewer, Accessibility Reviewer, Critic, Verifier, and Memory Curator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define learners and goals; inventory sources; structure concepts; present examples and uncertainty; enable inquiry and practice; assess; collect feedback; update; and preserve continuity.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show source, perspective, certainty, learner, exercise, feedback, and limitation. Require human review for source, safety, rights, cultural context, and assessment.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use versioned content, source links, accessible formats, learner control, safe practice, privacy, feedback, assessment integrity, and content retirement.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show curriculum, source freshness, perspectives, learner progress, accessibility, evidence, owner, and authorized revise, annotate, challenge, accommodate, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Education charter; audience and learning map; source and concept structure; examples and assessment; feedback, decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify claim, source, version, perspective, learner, method, time, result, and limitation. Completion or quiz score proves only bounded participation and recall.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify source, accuracy, uncertainty, accessibility, comprehension, perspective, safety, feedback, assessment, and content update. Test outdated or conflicting source.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Source-free lesson, false certainty, one perspective, inaccessible material, assessment as understanding, and education that hides its own limits.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Correct content, notify learners, preserve version, provide clarification, and reassess affected understanding. Reopen on source, domain, audience, policy, or risk change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Audience, goals, concepts, sources, perspectives, accessibility, evidence, assessment, owners, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending subject-matter and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve curriculum, sources, versions, learner feedback, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Training, Documentation, Knowledge, Community, Product, and Governance.
