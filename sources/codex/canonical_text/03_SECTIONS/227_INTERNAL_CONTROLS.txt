SECTION 227 — Internal Controls

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Design and operate controls that prevent, detect, correct, and evidence errors, fraud, unauthorized action, material misstatement, policy failure, and loss of accountability.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A control framework with objectives, risks, control owners, preventive and detective activities, segregation of duties, evidence, frequency, exceptions, testing, remediation, and management review.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Controls can exist on paper while failing in operation, creating false assurance. Overly manual, duplicated, unowned, or untested controls increase cost and still miss errors or abuse.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when governance, financial, operational, security, privacy, safety, quality, compliance, or trust outcomes require repeatable control.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability only when no material risk, obligation, protected outcome, decision, transaction, or evidence need requires a control and an approved exact baseline documents the conclusion.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use risk, requirements, policy, process, architecture, audit, compliance, security, privacy, quality, records, and operations baselines. Identify control objectives, owners, operators, evidence, and independent testing authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Objectives, risks, obligations, processes, systems, transactions, roles, controls, evidence, frequency, thresholds, exceptions, prior findings, incidents, users, and acceptance criteria.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Control owners design and operate controls; management approves risk, control objectives, exceptions, compensating controls, and remediation. Segregation, independent review, and escalation are assigned according to consequence. Humans attest only to evidence they can support.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may map risks to controls, inspect authorized evidence, identify gaps or duplicates, draft procedures and tests, and summarize exceptions. It must preserve control, population, period, owner, and evidence boundaries. It must not self-certify, fabricate evidence, approve exceptions, alter control operation, or close findings without human authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Controls Analyst, Auditor, Risk Analyst, Governance Reviewer, Security Auditor, Privacy Reviewer, Researcher, Tester, Verifier, Critic, Documentation Writer, and Memory Curator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define objectives and risks; design controls; assign owners and segregation; establish evidence and cadence; implement; train; monitor; test design and operation; manage exceptions; remediate; report; review changes; and retire obsolete controls.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show risk, objective, control, owner, population, period, evidence, result, exception, and limitation. Require human review for control design, attestation, exception, compensating control, remediation, and closure. Preserve independent challenge.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Link risk, obligation, process, system, control, evidence, finding, exception, and remediation. Prefer preventive and detective controls with least privilege, segregation, immutable evidence, change impact, and safe manual fallback.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show control objective, risk, owner, status, frequency, evidence freshness, test result, exception, due date, and authorized approve, operate, override, remediate, escalate, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Control framework; risk and control matrix; owner and segregation map; procedures and evidence plan; test and exception records; management report; decision, remediation, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify risk, objective, control, owner, population, period, system, method, evidence, reviewer, result, exception, and limitation. A procedure, configuration, or sample proves only the bounded claim and period.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify design, implementation, operation, evidence, frequency, segregation, override, exception, remediation, change impact, and independent testing. Sample normal and boundary cases and test control failure and recovery.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Controls without owners, evidence theater, self-review, permanent exceptions, manual workarounds hidden from the control, duplicate controls, and green status based on completion rather than outcome.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Preserve evidence, assess affected population and period, apply compensating control, notify owners, remediate, and reopen unsupported assertions. Reassess after process, system, risk, regulation, personnel, or supplier change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Objectives, risks, controls, owners, segregation, evidence, testing, exceptions, remediation, decisions, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending independent verification and management acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve risk-control matrix, evidence, tests, findings, exceptions, attestations, decisions, remediation, and open gaps. Hand off to Audit, Compliance, Legal, Security, Privacy, Quality, Operations, Finance, Records, and Governance.
