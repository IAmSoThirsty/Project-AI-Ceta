SECTION 267 — Continuous Delivery

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Keep validated, identified artifacts ready for controlled release through promotion, approval, environment preparation, rollback, and evidence-preserving delivery.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A delivery model with artifact identity, readiness gates, environments, approvals, configuration, data migration, communication, release strategy, rollback, and owner decision.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Delivery is not deployment. A ready artifact can still be unsuitable for an environment, user population, data migration, support capacity, or commitment. Automation must preserve human authority where consequence requires it.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when artifacts are promoted or prepared for release across environments, customers, operators, devices, or services.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no artifact, environment, user, data, support, or release readiness changes and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use build, artifact, configuration, environment, testing, acceptance, operations, support, data migration, security, and rollback baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Artifact, provenance, tests, findings, environment, configuration, migration, readiness, approvals, support, communication, rollback, and acceptance criteria.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Release owners approve readiness, risk, timing, communication, migration, exception, and rollback. Humans decide whether evidence and support capacity justify release.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may assemble readiness evidence, draft plans, identify gaps, and summarize changes. It must not declare ready, approve, promote, communicate commitments, or execute release without delegated authority and human control.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Release Engineer, Delivery Engineer, Architect, Tester, Verifier, Operations Engineer, Security Auditor, Support Reviewer, Coordinator, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Validate artifact and evidence; assess impact; prepare environment and configuration; plan migration and communication; obtain approvals; promote; monitor; rollback or pause; record outcome; and hand off operations.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show artifact, environment, change, evidence, readiness, approval, dependency, user impact, communication, and rollback. Require human approval for release, data migration, exception, and external commitment.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use immutable artifacts, promotion stages, gates, feature flags, canary or phased release, migration checkpoints, rollback, observability, support readiness, and access separation.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show release candidate, readiness, environment, approvals, dependencies, incidents, evidence freshness, communication, rollback, and authorized promote, pause, rollback, restrict, notify, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Delivery plan; readiness checklist; artifact and environment record; migration and communication plan; approvals; monitoring and rollback; decision, evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify artifact, environment, configuration, test, approval, actor, time, method, result, impact, and limitation. Readiness evidence does not prove post-release outcome.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify artifact identity, gates, approvals, environment, data, support, communication, migration, monitoring, rollback, and recovery. Exercise failed promotion and partial rollout.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Deploy without readiness, artifact drift, approval theater, migration without backup, rollback that misses data, support unprepared, and delivery status treated as user success.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause or roll back, preserve state and evidence, communicate, restore or compensate, and reassess users, data, commitments, and residual risk. Reopen on artifact, environment, data, or support change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Artifact, readiness, environment, approvals, migration, support, communication, rollback, evidence, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve release identity, artifacts, approvals, environment, telemetry, incidents, communications, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Deployment, Operations, Support, Product, Security, and Governance.
