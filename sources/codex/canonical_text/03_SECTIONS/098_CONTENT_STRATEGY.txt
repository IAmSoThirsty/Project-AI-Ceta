SECTION 098 — Content Strategy

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define the strategic purpose, portfolio, governance, investment, measurement, and lifecycle of content across audiences, channels, products, and services.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

 - A declared content mission, audiences, channels, outcomes, governance, resources, risks, and horizon.
 - Prioritized content system, ownership, standards, measures, evidence, decisions, and retirement model.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Content strategy prevents fragmented publication, contradictory claims, inaccessible information, and unmanaged maintenance burden.

APPLICABILITY

Applies when content is a material product, service, operational, marketing, instructional, support, or records capability.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

No material content outcome, audience, channel, claim, governance, resource, or lifecycle effect; or an unchanged approved strategy with impact recorded.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Mission, product and service strategy, information architecture, research, content inventory, channel data, policies, legal, accessibility, localization, staffing, and decision authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Mission, product and service strategy, information architecture, research, content inventory, channel data, policies, legal, accessibility, localization, staffing, and decision authority.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans set content purpose, priorities, claims, audiences, resources, risk, governance, and acceptance. They approve commitments and resolve conflicting stakeholder needs.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may inventory content, synthesize evidence, and propose priorities. It must expose evidence quality and opportunity cost and must not set editorial policy or publish claims.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Content Strategist, Content Designer, Technical Writer, Copywriter, UX Strategist, Researcher, Analyst, Governance Reviewer, Legal, Accessibility, Localization, Verifier, Critic, and Documentation Writer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define mission, audiences, inventory, lifecycle, channels, taxonomy, ownership, standards, workflow, measures, risks, resourcing, governance, and review cadence.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show content objective, audience, source, owner, status, evidence, risk, and decision. AI recommendations remain advisory and cannot bypass publication or records controls.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Connect strategy to information, product, service, workflow, localization, accessibility, legal, records, search, analytics, and retirement architecture.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show portfolio, gaps, freshness, owners, evidence, measures, risks, approvals, and controls to prioritize, pause, correct, withdraw, or retire.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Content strategy, inventory, audience map, governance model, lifecycle, standards, measurement plan, resource and risk register, decisions, and handoff.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify source, audience, channel, period, method, result, uncertainty, and limitation. Traffic, reach, or engagement is bounded evidence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify strategic traceability, audience inclusion, accessibility, claim accuracy, freshness, channel fit, governance, resource feasibility, and retirement capacity.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Publishing without owners; optimizing volume; treating traffic as value; ignoring maintenance; contradictory channels; inaccessible archives; and no retirement path.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Pause unsupported publication, preserve decisions, correct or withdraw content, assign owners, commission research, and rebaseline affected channels.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Mission, audiences, portfolio, governance, resources, evidence, measures, risks, recovery, open gaps, and acceptance authority are explicit. Remain CONTENT-COMPLETE pending review and acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve strategy, inventory, standards, decisions, evidence, owners, dependencies, and next action. Hand off to Information, Content, UX, Technical Writing, Localization, Records, and Operations.
