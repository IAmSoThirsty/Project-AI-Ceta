SECTION 220 — Regulatory Compliance

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Translate applicable regulatory obligations into owned, testable, evidenced, and continuously reviewed controls without confusing compliance documentation with actual protection or lawful operation.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A regulatory register mapping jurisdiction, authority, obligation, scope, control, owner, evidence, cadence, reporting, exception, remediation, and change trigger.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Regulation varies by jurisdiction, sector, activity, data, product, and time. A certification, policy, audit, or control description can be stale or incomplete and does not itself prove ongoing compliance.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a regulator, statute, rule, license, order, standard adopted by contract, or public obligation affects the system, organization, product, data, or process.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability only after qualified review of jurisdiction, entity, activity, data, population, product, and time. Preserve the reason, source, reviewer, date, and reopening condition.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Identify legal entities, jurisdictions, regulators, sector, data, contracts, products, controls, counsel, compliance owner, evidence systems, reporting deadlines, and audit or inspection authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Regulatory sources, scope, obligations, definitions, controls, policies, procedures, assets, owners, evidence, assessments, incidents, findings, reports, exceptions, and change notices.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Compliance and legal owners interpret obligations with qualified counsel, approve applicability, control mapping, reports, exceptions, and regulator communication. Control owners implement and evidence obligations. Humans accept residual risk and attest only to supported facts.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may organize authorized sources, extract obligations, compare control evidence, identify gaps, draft questions, and track changes. It must preserve jurisdiction, source, date, definition, evidence, and uncertainty. It must not declare compliance, provide legal advice, sign an attestation, conceal gaps, or communicate to a regulator without authorized human approval.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Compliance Analyst, Legal Advisor, Researcher, Governance Reviewer, Auditor, Security Auditor, Privacy Reviewer, Analyst, Verifier, Documentation Writer, Critic, and Memory Curator.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Identify scope and authorities; inventory obligations; map controls; assign owners and cadence; collect evidence; assess design and operation; track findings and exceptions; prepare reports; monitor regulatory change; test; obtain approval; and preserve continuity.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show authority, jurisdiction, obligation, source date, control, evidence, owner, status, limitation, and required decision. Require qualified human review for interpretation, applicability, attestation, exception, report, and regulator communication.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Represent obligations, controls, evidence, owners, attestations, findings, deadlines, and source versions as traceable records. Preserve segregation of duties, evidence integrity, access restrictions, legal holds, and change impact across systems.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show obligation, authority, scope, control, owner, evidence freshness, status, finding, deadline, exception, and authorized assign, attest, remediate, escalate, report, and reopen controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Regulatory applicability and obligation register; control map; evidence and cadence plan; assessment and findings; exception and remediation record; report and approval; decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify authority, jurisdiction, source version, obligation, scope, control, environment, method, time, owner, result, and limitation. A policy, certificate, or audit report proves only its stated scope and period.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify applicability, source currency, control implementation and operation, evidence integrity, sampling, segregation, exceptions, reporting, deadlines, and change monitoring. Obtain qualified acceptance for material conclusions.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Checklist compliance, stale register, unsupported attestation, control description treated as operation, one jurisdiction generalized to another, evidence manufactured for the audit, and exceptions without expiry or owner.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Mark unsupported claims, preserve evidence, notify compliance and legal owners, remediate or disclose through approved channels, and reassess scope and deadlines. Reopen on regulatory, product, data, entity, jurisdiction, contract, or control change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Authority, applicability, obligations, controls, owners, evidence, cadence, findings, exceptions, reports, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending qualified review and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve source and obligation versions, control mappings, evidence, findings, attestations, reports, communications, holds, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Legal, Policy, Audit, Security, Privacy, Records, Operations, and Governance.
