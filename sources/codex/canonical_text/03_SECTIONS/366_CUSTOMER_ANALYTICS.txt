SECTION 366 — Customer Analytics

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Analyze customer behavior, needs, outcomes, support, adoption, retention, value, and risk with respect for privacy, context, uncertainty, and human interpretation.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A customer-analytics model with purpose, population, data, consent or authority, definitions, segments, measures, cohorts, privacy, action, evidence, and limitations.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Customer data can inform service and product improvement while exposing people to surveillance, inference, unfair treatment, or misleading segmentation. Correlation and activity are not customer truth.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when customer data or feedback informs product, service, support, marketing, pricing, risk, retention, or customer-success decisions.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no customer data, segment, decision, metric, or customer-impacting use changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use data, privacy, consent, security, product, customer success, support, metrics, research, legal, and governance baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Purpose, customers, data, source, authority, population, segment, metric, cohort, timeframe, action, privacy, and evidence.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Customer and data owners approve purpose, use, segmentation, access, retention, action, and high-impact interpretation. Humans protect customer agency and resolve ambiguity.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze authorized aggregate or bounded data, identify patterns, and draft hypotheses. It must not infer sensitive traits, expose individuals, make customer decisions, or claim causality without authority and review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Customer Analyst, Data Analyst, Researcher, Product Manager, Privacy Reviewer, Statistician, User Experience Reviewer, Verifier, and Critic.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define question and purpose; inventory and minimize data; define metrics and segments; analyze; validate with customer and qualitative evidence; assess harm; recommend; monitor; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show purpose, population, data source, segment, metric, period, evidence, uncertainty, privacy, and limitation. Require human review for personal, sensitive, high-impact, and customer-facing action.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use purpose-bound data, aggregation, access, privacy, lineage, cohort and metric versioning, suppression, retention, correction, and separation of analysis from action.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show question, population, metrics, segments, data freshness, privacy, findings, owner, and authorized restrict, correct, annotate, act, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Customer-analytics charter; purpose and data; metric and segment; analysis and evidence; action and privacy; decision, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify question, population, source, data, metric, segment, period, method, result, reviewer, uncertainty, and limitation. Activity, sentiment, or retention proves only its bounded signal.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify authority, data quality, privacy, metric, segment, cohort, bias, causality limits, customer validation, access, retention, and action. Test missing and contradictory data.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Surveillance by default, sensitive inference, segment as identity, correlation as cause, dashboard as customer voice, data reuse without purpose, and metric gaming.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Restrict or delete unauthorized data, correct analysis, notify owners, remedy affected decisions, preserve evidence, and reassess purpose and harm. Reopen on data, population, purpose, model, policy, or customer change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Purpose, customers, data, authority, metrics, segments, evidence, privacy, actions, owners, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve datasets, definitions, analyses, decisions, evidence, privacy assessments, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Product Analytics, Customer Success, Data, Privacy, Marketing, Support, and Governance.
