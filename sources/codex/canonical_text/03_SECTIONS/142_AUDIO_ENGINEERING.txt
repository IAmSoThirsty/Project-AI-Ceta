SECTION 142 - Audio Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Define governed engineering of recorded, synthesized, spatial, interactive, or streamed sound with explicit content provenance, timing, accessibility, performance, privacy, and user control.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A sound baseline with sources, assets, mixing, spatialization, timing, device support, captions or alternatives, privacy, evidence, and recovery.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Audio can convey essential state, expose private context, harm hearing, or fail silently. Audio quality alone does not establish comprehension or accessibility.

APPLICABILITY

Applies to games, applications, devices, media, voice interfaces, spatial audio, alerts, synthesis, recording, and audio pipelines.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

May be non-applicable to systems with no audio generation, capture, playback, or audio-dependent outcome. Record scope, owner, evidence, and reopening trigger.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Requires audio purpose, assets and rights, timing, device, volume, hearing accessibility, captions, recording consent, privacy, performance, security, and lifecycle policy.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Provide sources, formats, levels, channels, spatial context, triggers, latency, accessibility alternatives, permissions, recording, retention, and failure requirements.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Humans approve content, rights, recording, privacy, safety, accessibility, volume, and release. Humans decide whether audio is essential or optional.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may generate, classify, mix, transcribe, or test audio. It must preserve consent and provenance, expose uncertainty, and never publish or record without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Audio Engineer, Architect, User Experience Reviewer, Accessibility specialist, Security Auditor, Privacy Reviewer, Implementer, Tester, Verifier, Governance Reviewer, Documentation Writer, Memory Curator, and Red Team. Existing role contracts remain controlling.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

1. Define sound purpose, sources, rights, timing, devices, levels, accessibility, and privacy.
2. Build capture, processing, mixing, spatial, playback, and alternative paths.
3. Verify content, levels, latency, intelligibility, captions, permissions, performance, and recovery.
4. Release, monitor, correct, and retire audio assets and pipelines.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show source, consent, asset, device, level, spatial state, transcript or alternative, evidence, and consequence. Human approval is required for recording, publication, and safety-impacting alerts.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Separate capture, processing, storage, mixer, output, spatial context, device, transcript, and consent boundaries. Apply limits, mute, fallback, and data deletion controls.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show audio source, session, levels, devices, consent, latency, errors, captions, incidents, and controls to mute, stop capture, lower level, switch output, or delete recordings.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Audio charter, asset and rights catalog, signal path, consent and privacy record, level and safety plan, accessibility alternatives, test evidence, build package, and retention or retirement record.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify source, asset, license, build, device, signal settings, consent, method, result, and limits. Separate fidelity, intelligibility, accessibility, safety, privacy, and user outcome evidence.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify capture consent, levels, mute, device loss, latency, spatial cues, malformed media, captions, alternative signals, privacy, offline, and deletion behavior.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Recording without consent; unsafe levels; audio-only critical information; unlicensed assets; hidden microphone use; inaccessible alerts; and treating loudness or fidelity as comprehension.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stop capture and playback, preserve required evidence, delete or quarantine unauthorized recordings, use visual or haptic alternative, compensate affected parties, and reopen consent or safety review.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Purpose, source, rights, consent, controls, accessibility, evidence, recovery, and lifecycle are explicit. Keep CONTENT-COMPLETE until independent verification and owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve assets, licenses, consent, signals, builds, incidents, decisions, evidence, and next safe action. Hand off to Game, Media, Voice, Accessibility, Privacy, Security, Testing, and Operations.
