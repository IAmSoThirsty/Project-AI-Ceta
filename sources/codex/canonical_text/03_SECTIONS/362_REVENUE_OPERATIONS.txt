SECTION 362 — Revenue Operations

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Coordinate the data, process, systems, people, controls, forecasting, pipeline, billing, customer, and finance workflows that produce trustworthy revenue operations.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A revenue-operations model with lifecycle, source systems, customer and product data, pipeline, pricing, quote, order, billing, payment, subscription, forecast, reconciliation, controls, and ownership.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Revenue data crosses teams and systems with different definitions and incentives. A polished forecast or dashboard can hide timing, attribution, churn, billing, data, or recognition error.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when commercial process, customer lifecycle, revenue data, forecasting, billing, payments, subscriptions, sales operations, or financial reporting is designed or operated.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no commercial lifecycle, revenue data, financial process, customer, forecast, or control behavior changes and an approved exact baseline covers it.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use commerce, product, sales, billing, payments, subscriptions, finance, legal, data, privacy, audit, controls, and service baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Customers, products, prices, pipeline, quotes, orders, usage, subscriptions, invoices, payments, adjustments, forecast, definitions, controls, and evidence.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Revenue owners define lifecycle, definitions, controls, forecast, approvals, exceptions, reconciliation, and reporting. Finance, legal, sales, product, and customer owners approve their domains.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may analyze authorized commercial data, identify anomalies, draft forecasts and process improvements, and summarize evidence. It must not alter financial records, make commitments, infer customer intent, approve transactions, or attest without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Revenue Operations Manager, Financial Analyst, Commerce Manager, Billing Engineer, Payments Engineer, Data Analyst, Sales Engineer, Auditor, Verifier, and Governance Reviewer.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define lifecycle and terms; align systems and definitions; manage pipeline and quote; order and entitlement; bill and collect; reconcile; forecast; report; control access; investigate; and improve.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show customer, product, lifecycle, source, definition, period, forecast, action, authority, evidence, and limitation. Require human approval for forecast, financial adjustment, commitment, report, and exception.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Link CRM, product, commerce, billing, payment, subscription, usage, ledger, support, and finance with identity, lineage, immutable history, reconciliation, and separation of duties.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show pipeline, customer, product, order, subscription, invoice, payment, forecast, reconciliation, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized correct, approve, hold, reconcile, report, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Revenue-operations charter; lifecycle and system map; definitions and controls; pipeline, forecast, billing, payment, subscription, reconciliation, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify customer, product, transaction, source, definition, period, method, result, actor, forecast, and limitation. Forecast or revenue report proves only its source, period, and assumptions.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify lifecycle, definitions, access, quote, order, entitlement, billing, payment, subscription, usage, forecast, reconciliation, controls, audit, privacy, and corrections.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Conflicting definitions, forecast as fact, pipeline inflation, revenue dashboard without lineage, manual adjustment hidden, customer data overexposure, and metric gaming.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Freeze affected report or adjustment, preserve records, reconcile, correct, communicate, and reassess financial, customer, legal, and operational impact. Reopen on source, definition, price, product, contract, or period change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Lifecycle, systems, definitions, customers, products, transactions, controls, owners, evidence, reconciliation, forecasts, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve lifecycle data, definitions, transactions, reports, forecasts, adjustments, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Commerce, Billing, Payments, Subscriptions, Finance, Sales, Product, Audit, and Records.
