SECTION 369 — Experimentation

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Run bounded, hypothesis-driven experiments that reduce uncertainty while protecting people, data, service, fairness, safety, and decision integrity.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

An experiment model with hypothesis, objective, population, intervention, control, metric, assignment, duration, power or uncertainty, guardrail, consent or authority, analysis, stop, and decision.

WHY THIS MATTERS

An experiment can produce a precise answer to the wrong question or create harm through spillover, biased sample, peeking, interference, or unmonitored guardrail failure.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a controlled change or comparison is used to learn causal or comparative evidence about product, process, policy, system, or user outcome.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no experiment, causal claim, intervention, population, or decision is planned and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use research, analytics, product, privacy, ethics, safety, statistics, feature, and governance baselines. Define experiment owner and stop authority.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Question, hypothesis, population, assignment, intervention, control, metric, sample, duration, guardrail, data, consent, analysis, and decision.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Experiment owners approve purpose, population, intervention, risk, consent, guardrails, stop, and interpretation. Humans judge ethical, practical, and customer meaning.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may draft hypotheses, design cases, analyze authorized aggregate results, and surface uncertainty. It must not assign sensitive participants, alter experiment, peek and conclude, expose data, or decide rollout without human review.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Experimenter, Statistician, Researcher, Product Analyst, Product Manager, Privacy Reviewer, Ethics Reviewer, Tester, Verifier, and Critic.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Define question and hypothesis; assess harm; design; assign; instrument; run; monitor guardrails; stop if needed; analyze; inspect limitations; decide; communicate; and preserve reproducibility.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show hypothesis, population, assignment, intervention, metric, guardrail, authority, evidence, uncertainty, and limitation. Require human review for sensitive population, harm, stop, and rollout decision.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use randomization or declared alternative, isolation, exposure control, feature flag, event integrity, privacy, guardrails, stopping rule, analysis version, and rollback.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show experiment state, population, intervention, metric, guardrail, incidents, evidence freshness, owner, and authorized pause, stop, analyze, extend, rollout, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Experiment charter; hypothesis and design; population and assignment; metrics and guardrails; analysis and decision; evidence, exception, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify hypothesis, population, assignment, intervention, control, metric, period, method, result, uncertainty, and limitation. Statistical significance alone proves neither value nor safety.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify assignment, exposure, contamination, sample, metric, guardrail, consent, data quality, analysis, stopping, reproducibility, and decision. Test missing data and adverse subgroup.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Peeking, underpowered study, biased sample, metric substitution, control contamination, harm ignored, experiment without stop, and statistically significant proxy treated as success.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Stop or narrow, protect users, preserve data, correct analysis, communicate, and reassess. Reopen on data, population, intervention, metric, guardrail, or external event change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Hypothesis, design, population, metrics, guardrails, evidence, analysis, decision, owners, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve design, assignments, feature state, data, analysis, decisions, incidents, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to A/B Testing, Product Analytics, Growth, Optimization, Privacy, and Governance.
