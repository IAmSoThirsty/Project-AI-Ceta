SECTION 287 — Release Engineering

STATUS: CONTENT-COMPLETE

PURPOSE

Engineer repeatable, traceable, safe, and reversible release processes that turn accepted changes into communicated, supportable, and governed versions.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

A release model with version, artifact, scope, readiness, notes, dependencies, approvals, packaging, communication, rollout, rollback, support, evidence, and retirement.

WHY THIS MATTERS

A release is a social, technical, and operational event. A package can be correct while communication, support, migration, compatibility, or rollback is not ready.

APPLICABILITY

Apply when a product, service, model, firmware, data, documentation, policy, infrastructure, or configuration version is made available to users, operators, or downstream systems.

NON-APPLICABILITY CONDITIONS

Record non-applicability when no version, consumer, artifact, deployment, communication, support, or operational expectation changes and an approved exact baseline covers the scope.

PREREQUISITES AND DEPENDENCIES

Use acceptance, delivery, deployment, artifact, configuration, compatibility, support, operations, security, legal, documentation, and rollback baselines.

REQUIRED INPUTS

Version, artifact, changes, requirements, tests, findings, dependencies, compatibility, approvals, notes, migration, support, communication, rollout, rollback, and acceptance.

HUMAN RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

Release owners approve version, readiness, notes, support, communication, exceptions, timing, rollout, rollback, and retirement. Humans decide whether users and operators can safely receive the release.

AI RESPONSIBILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

AI may assemble change summaries, draft release notes and checklists, identify missing evidence, and propose rollout. It must not invent changes, disclose sensitive details, approve, publish, deploy, or communicate commitments without authority.

APPLICABLE EXISTING AGENT ROLES

Release Engineer, Delivery Engineer, Product Manager, Documentation Writer, Tester, Verifier, Operations Engineer, Security Auditor, Support Reviewer, Coordinator, and Critic.

REQUIRED ACTIVITIES

Baseline version and artifact; inspect changes; validate tests and dependencies; assess compatibility and risk; prepare notes, migration, support, and communication; approve; package and distribute; monitor; roll back or patch; and retire.

HUMAN-AI INTERACTION REQUIREMENTS

Show version, artifact, audience, change, evidence, risk, dependency, communication, owner, and limitation. Require human approval for release, public notes, security or legal content, migration, and rollback.

ARCHITECTURE AND SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS

Use immutable version and artifact, signed provenance, staged distribution, compatibility and migration, feature flags, rollback, release registry, support hooks, and audit.

OWNER COCKPIT AND USER-CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

Show release candidate, readiness, change, evidence freshness, audiences, support, incidents, rollout, rollback, and authorized approve, hold, publish, promote, roll back, and retire controls.

REQUIRED OUTPUTS AND ARTIFACTS

Release charter; version and artifact record; change and compatibility summary; readiness and approval; notes, migration, support, communication, rollout, decision, evidence, and continuity records.

EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

Identify version, artifact digest, source, tests, findings, approval, audience, environment, method, time, result, and limitation. Release publication proves availability, not successful adoption or operation.

VERIFICATION AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS

Verify version and artifact, notes, compatibility, security, support, migration, approvals, distribution, rollback, monitoring, and retirement. Test partial, failed, and withdrawn release.

FAILURE STATES AND ANTI-PATTERNS

Release from unverified source, notes that overclaim, hidden breaking change, support unprepared, migration without rollback, no withdrawal, and release count treated as product success.

RECOVERY AND RECONSIDERATION

Hold or withdraw, preserve artifact and communications, roll back or patch, support affected users, reconcile data, and reassess commitments. Reopen on artifact, audience, dependency, incident, or compatibility change.

COMPLETION CRITERIA

Version, artifact, changes, readiness, approvals, audiences, support, communication, rollout, rollback, evidence, limitations, and reopening conditions are explicit. Status remains CONTENT-COMPLETE pending owner acceptance.

HANDOFF AND CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

Preserve release records, artifacts, approvals, notes, communications, incidents, decisions, evidence, exceptions, and open gaps. Hand off to Delivery, Deployment, Operations, Support, Documentation, Security, and Product.
