# Shared terminology

**Intelligence Operating System.** The CETA runtime coordinating observation, memory, reasoning, permissions, effects and continuity on the host OS.

**Companion.** Partner-facing reasoner with a technical continuity identity and bounded tool proposals.

**Governance kernel.** Deterministic owner of admissibility and positive-authority transitions.

**Canonical history.** Accepted per-scope ordered records from which current authoritative state is reconstructed.

**Projection.** Derived, replaceable view; never an independent grant or truth issuer.

**Observation.** Attributed evidence about a source/resource with method, time, completeness and uncertainty.

**Claim.** A proposition with scoped evidentiary status, not made true by authority or repetition.

**Standing grant.** Finite current permission for defined classes of activity; not a reusable consumed effect permit.

**Operation permit.** Single-use exact action admission bound to actor, resource, input, candidate, policy and time.

**Read set.** Specific state/resource revisions relevant to a decision; unrelated telemetry is excluded.

**Linearization point.** Defined ordering boundary used to resolve races such as revocation versus effect admission.

**Fencing generation.** Durable generation that prevents an obsolete worker from acting after reassignment.

**Prepared request.** Exact pinned model payload with sources, tokenization, validity and authority binding.

**Galahad.** Review responsibility for identity, consent, purpose and agency.

**Cerberus.** Review responsibility for boundaries, security, survivability and recovery.

**Codex Deus Maximus.** Constitutional interpretation/review responsibility; remembered policy does not create new law.

**The Fates.** Clotho candidate creation, Lachesis relevance, and Atropos authorized retention/resolution.

**TAAR.** Source roles for governed agent operation and adaptation; import actual qualified capabilities only.

**Shadow Thirst.** Governed proposal IR and isolated pre-effect evaluation.

**Sovereign Interior Vault.** Isolated speculative/reflection workspace, distinct from secret custody.

**Defensive Vault.** Governed object custody and scoped release, never a general launcher.

**CBCC.** Distributed proof-bearing cryptographic authorization with independently administered domains.

**NIRL.** Integrity/risk monitoring design label; its expansion remains unresolved in source.

**Chimera.** Visible isolated execution/interaction environment without inherited production authority.

**IIMP.** Interpretive-integrity and meaning-preservation source standard; audit drift/no-drift/unresolved symmetrically.

**Qualification.** Evidence-bound approval of a named capability under an exact configuration and scope.

**Indeterminate effect.** An action may have executed and lacks sufficient evidence for its outcome; blind retry is unsafe.

**Recovery.** New governed observation/settlement process preserving original uncertainty and evidence.

**Package validation.** Checks of these documents, IDs, references and bytes; no runtime or product qualification.

**Source adoption.** A legitimate decision to apply source requirements, separate from presence, formatting and implementation.
